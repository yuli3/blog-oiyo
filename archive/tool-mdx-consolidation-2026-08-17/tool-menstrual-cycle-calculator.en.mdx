## Cycle Basics

**Average cycle**: 21–35 days (average 28 days)  
**Period duration**: 3–7 days  
**Ovulation**: approximately 14 days before the next expected period

| Phase | Days (28-day cycle) | Key Features |
|---|---|---|
| Menstruation | Days 1–5 | Uterine lining sheds, hormones at lowest |
| Follicular | Days 6–13 | Rising estrogen, increasing energy |
| Ovulation | Around day 14 | Fertility peak, LH surge |
| Luteal | Days 15–28 | Rising progesterone, PMS may begin |

<Callout type="tip">
**Fertile window**: Approx. 5 days before ovulation + ovulation day + 1 day after = ~7 days total. Sperm can survive 3–5 days in the body, so the window begins before the ovulation day.
</Callout>

## Irregular Cycle Reference

| Condition | Definition |
|---|---|
| Normal range | Cycle 21–35 days, variation ±7 days |
| Frequent periods | Cycle under 21 days |
| Infrequent periods | Cycle over 35 days |
| Amenorrhea | No period for 3+ months |
| Heavy bleeding | Excessive flow (soaking a pad within an hour) |

<Callout type="warning">
This calculator provides **average-cycle-based estimates**. Stress, weight changes, and hormonal imbalances can shift actual ovulation. If you are trying to conceive or need contraception, combine this with an ovulation test and consult a gynecologist.
</Callout>

## PMS (Premenstrual Syndrome) Management

PMS commonly occurs in the luteal phase (days 15–28) after ovulation.

- **Physical symptoms**: Breast tenderness, bloating, headaches, fatigue
- **Emotional symptoms**: Irritability, low mood, difficulty concentrating
- **Management**: Regular aerobic exercise, reducing caffeine/sodium intake, adequate sleep, magnesium supplementation
