# Body Fat Calculator

Two people can share the same BMI while having completely different health profiles — one may be muscular, the other carrying excess fat. **Body fat percentage** reflects actual body composition far better than BMI alone.

## Body Fat Percentage Reference Chart

| Classification | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| Essential fat | 2–5% | 10–13% |
| Athletes | 6–13% | 14–20% |
| Fitness | 14–17% | 21–24% |
| Acceptable | 18–24% | 25–31% |
| Overweight | 25–30% | 32–37% |
| Obese | 31%+ | 38%+ |

<Callout type="tip">
**Body fat % vs. BMI**: BMI only considers height and weight. Body fat percentage measures actual fat ratio. A muscular athlete may have a BMI above 25 (classified "overweight") while their body fat is below 12%.
</Callout>

## Calculation Method (U.S. Navy Formula)

This calculator uses the **U.S. Navy body fat formula**, the most accurate measurement approach that doesn't require special equipment.

**Men**: 86.01 × log10(waist − neck) − 70.041 × log10(height) + 36.76  
**Women**: 163.205 × log10(waist + hip − neck) − 97.684 × log10(height) − 78.387

## Body Fat Reduction Strategy

| Goal | Strategy |
|---|---|
| Lose 1 kg of fat | ~7,700 kcal deficit (~500 kcal/day × 15 days) |
| Minimize muscle loss | 1.6–2.2 g protein per kg of body weight |
| Combine cardio + strength | Strength training preserves basal metabolic rate |
| Healthy pace | 0.5–1% reduction per week |
