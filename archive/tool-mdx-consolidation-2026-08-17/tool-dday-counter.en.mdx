# D-Day Counter

Track all your important dates in one place. Check at a glance how many days until an exam, how many days since a relationship began, or when your next trip departs.

This D-Day counter lets you register multiple events and saves them in your browser. Your list persists across refreshes, and each event automatically toggles between days remaining (D−N) and days elapsed (D+N).

**Ideal for:**
- Students preparing for university entrance exams, certifications, or public service exams
- Couples who don't want to miss milestone anniversaries (100 days, 200 days, 1 year)
- Travelers counting down to departure with excitement
- Teams that want to share a project deadline

## D−N vs. D+N

| Notation | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **D−30** | 30 days until the target | 30 days before an exam |
| **D−0** | Today is the day | The event is today |
| **D+100** | 100 days have elapsed since the start | 100-day relationship milestone |

<Callout type="tip">
**Origin of D-Day**: The Normandy landings on June 6, 1944, were called "D-Day." What started as military jargon is now universally used to mark any personally significant date.
</Callout>
