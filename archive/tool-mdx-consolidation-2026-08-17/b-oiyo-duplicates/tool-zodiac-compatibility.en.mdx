# Zodiac Compatibility Test

In Western astrology, compatibility between two people goes beyond simple attraction — it comes down to how the elemental (Element) and modality energies of each sign interact. Signs sharing the same element understand each other intuitively, while complementary element pairings create synergy by filling each other's gaps.

The 12 signs are grouped into four elements: Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius), and Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces). Same-element pairs connect naturally, while Fire-Air and Earth-Water are classic complementary combinations.

Compatibility scores are calculated by combining element compatibility, modality relationship, and aspect theory. Use the result as a reference — remember that in any real relationship, the most important factor is always mutual understanding and effort.

## How Zodiac Compatibility Works: Elements & Modalities

Compatibility theory in Western astrology rests on **4 Elements** and **3 Modalities**.

### Elemental Compatibility

| Element | Signs | Core Traits |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 Fire | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius | Passion, leadership, spontaneity, drive |
| 🌍 Earth | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn | Stability, practicality, patience, realism |
| 💨 Air | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius | Intellect, communication, ideas, freedom |
| 💧 Water | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces | Emotion, intuition, depth, empathy |

<Callout type="tip">
**Element summary**: Same-element pairs (Fire-Fire, Earth-Earth) feel the most natural. **Fire-Air** and **Earth-Water** are also excellent complementary matches. **Fire-Water** and **Earth-Air** are challenging but offer the greatest mutual growth.
</Callout>

## Compatibility by Relationship Type

### Love & Marriage
Elemental compatibility is most important for romantic relationships. Same-element pairs connect instinctively; complementary element pairings (Fire-Air, Earth-Water) fill each other's gaps. For long-term relationships, same or complementary elements with different modalities tend to work best.

### Friendship
Intellectual stimulation and shared interests matter most in friendships. Same-element friends build natural bonds; opposite-element friends broaden your perspective.

### Work & Business
Different strengths complementing each other is the key in professional settings. Fire (action) + Earth (realism), Air (ideas) + Water (empathetic leadership) make ideal business partners.
