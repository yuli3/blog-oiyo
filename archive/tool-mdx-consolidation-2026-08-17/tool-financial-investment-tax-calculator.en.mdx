# Financial Investment Income Tax Calculator

If you've made profits from Korean stocks, funds, or ETFs, use this calculator to check your financial investment income tax (금융투자소득세) liability.

## Tax Structure

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Taxable assets | Domestic stocks, funds, ETFs, bonds |
| Annual deduction | **₩50 million** |
| Tax rate (net profit ≤ ₩300M) | **20%** |
| Tax rate (net profit > ₩300M) | **25%** |
| Including local income tax | ×1.1 (effective: 22% / 27.5%) |
| Filing method | May of the following year (comprehensive income tax) |

<Callout type="tip">
**₩50M deduction**: All financial investment gains and losses in a calendar year are netted together. Only net profits above ₩50M are taxed. If you earned ₩51M net, only ₩1M is taxed.
</Callout>

## Gain/Loss Netting Example

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Stock A gain | +₩80M |
| Fund B loss | −₩10M |
| Net profit | ₩70M |
| Annual deduction | −₩50M |
| **Taxable amount** | **₩20M** |
| **Tax (20%)** | **₩4M** |

## Comparison: Before vs. After

| Category | Prior system (large shareholders only) | Financial investment tax (all investors) |
|---|---|---|
| Threshold | Holdings ≥ ₩1B per stock | All investors |
| Annual deduction | None | ₩50M |
| Tax rate | 20–30% | 20–25% |
| Loss offset | Not allowed | Allowed |

<Callout type="note">
The timing and specifics of Korea's financial investment income tax are subject to legislative change. Confirm the latest tax code or consult a certified tax accountant before making financial decisions.
</Callout>
