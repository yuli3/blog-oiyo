## Metronome BPM Reference Guide

| Tempo Marking | BPM | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Largo (very slow) | 40–60 | Slow ballads |
| Andante (walking pace) | 76–108 | Folk songs |
| Moderato (moderate) | 108–120 | Average pop song |
| Allegro (fast) | 120–156 | Dance music |
| Presto (very fast) | 168–200+ | Techno / drum & bass |
