# GA4 Review Checklist

## 1. Purpose

This checklist defines what should be reviewed in analytics as `blog-oiyo` grows through lectures, magazine pages, and Ahoxy migrations.

## 2. Baseline Questions

Review these first:

1. which landing pages actually bring search traffic
2. which pages lead readers to a second page
3. which internal links move users into deeper study
4. which Ahoxy-linked pages deserve richer expansion

## 3. Page Grouping to Review

Create or approximate these groups:

1. `academy`
2. `magazine`
3. `interactive`
4. qualification overviews
5. migrated Ahoxy pages

## 4. Core Metrics

### Traffic

1. landing page sessions
2. users by source/medium
3. organic search share
4. cross-site referral share

### Engagement

1. engaged sessions
2. scroll depth
3. average engagement time
4. pages per session

### Navigation

1. internal link click-through rate
2. chapter progression rate
3. track-to-track click movement
4. Ahoxy -> blog referral continuation

### Conversion-Like Behavior

1. calculator usage on article pages
2. island interaction starts
3. related-content clicks
4. qualification roadmap follow-through

## 5. Review by Track

### Academy

Check:

1. chapter entry pages
2. next chapter movement
3. series retention
4. whether readers bounce before reaching structured content

### Magazine

Check:

1. top landing essays
2. internal-link continuation
3. conversion into deeper study pages

### Interactive

Check:

1. whether people read before interacting
2. whether interaction improves retention
3. which islands are worth repeating as a pattern

## 6. Ahoxy Migration Review

For migrated candidates, compare:

1. source utility visits in Ahoxy
2. target article traffic in `blog-oiyo`
3. cross-site click behavior
4. whether the explanatory page creates deeper session depth

## 7. Decision Rules

### Expand

Expand when:

1. a page earns organic traffic
2. it leads well into deeper content
3. the topic supports a series or qualification path

### Refactor

Refactor when:

1. traffic arrives but readers leave fast
2. internal links are ignored
3. the island feels disconnected from the article

### Hold

Hold when:

1. topic fit is weak
2. editorial overhead is high
3. it performs only as a narrow utility

## 8. Reporting Cadence

Recommended cadence:

1. weekly quick scan for top landing pages
2. monthly track-level review
3. quarterly migration and taxonomy review
