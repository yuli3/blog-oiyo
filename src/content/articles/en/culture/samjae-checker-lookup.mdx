# Are You in Sam-Jae?

The "Three Disasters" (Sam-Jae) is a 3-year period of bad luck in Korean astrology. It cycles every 9 years for each zodiac sign.

In 2026 (Year of the Horse), the **Pig, Rabbit, and Sheep** are in their second year ("Staying Sam-Jae").

## Dealing with Sam-Jae
It's not just about bad luck; it's a period for caution and introspection. Use this time to avoid risky investments and focus on stability.
