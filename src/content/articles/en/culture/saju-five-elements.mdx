## The Cycle of Energy

Saju analysis relies heavily on the interaction between the Five Elements. These elements are not just static substances but phases of energy transformation.

### 1. Wood (Mok)
*   **Keywords**: Growth, creativity, expansion, benevolence.
*   **Season**: Spring.
*   **Direction**: East.

### 2. Fire (Hwa)
*   **Keywords**: Passion, dynamism, expression, courtesy.
*   **Season**: Summer.
*   **Direction**: South.

### 3. Earth (To)
*   **Keywords**: Stability, grounding, trust, transition.
*   **Season**: Between seasons (Indian Summer).
*   **Direction**: Center.

### 4. Metal (Geum)
*   **Keywords**: Structure, justice, refinement, decision.
*   **Season**: Autumn.
*   **Direction**: West.

### 5. Water (Su)
*   **Keywords**: Wisdom, adaptability, flow, intuition.
*   **Season**: Winter.
*   **Direction**: North.

## Generating and Overcoming Cycles

*   **Generating (Productive) Cycle**: Wood feeds Fire -> Fire creates Earth (ash) -> Earth bears Metal -> Metal collects Water (condensation) -> Water nourishes Wood.
*   **Overcoming (Controlling) Cycle**: Wood parts Earth -> Earth dams Water -> Water extinguishes Fire -> Fire melts Metal -> Metal chops Wood.

Understanding these relationships helps interpret how different aspects of your life (career, health, relationships) interact with each other.
