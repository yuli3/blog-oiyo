## The Four Elements of Astrology

The 12 zodiac signs are divided into four elements: **Fire, Earth, Air, and Water**. Understanding your element helps reveal your core energy and how you interact with others.

### 🔥 Fire Signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)
Passionate, dynamic, and temperamental. Fire signs light up the room but can burn out quickly. They are natural leaders and adventurers.

### 🌍 Earth Signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)
Grounded, practical, and loyal. Earth signs are the builders of the zodiac. They value stability, hard work, and material reality.

### 🌬️ Air Signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)
Intellectual, social, and communicative. Air signs are the thinkers and connectors. They value ideas, conversation, and freedom.

### 💧 Water Signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)
Emotional, intuitive, and sensitive. Water signs feel deeply and are highly empathetic. They value connection, privacy, and feelings.
