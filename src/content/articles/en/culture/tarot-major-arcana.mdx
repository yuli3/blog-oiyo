# The Major Arcana

The Major Arcana consists of 22 cards (numbered 0 to 21) that represent major life events, lessons, and archetypes. They often signify significant changes or deep spiritual insights.

## The Fool's Journey

The sequence of the Major Arcana is often called "The Fool's Journey."
-   It starts with **0. The Fool** (Beginnings, Innocence, Leap of Faith).
-   It travels through cards like **The Magician** (Manifestation), **The Empress** (Creation), and **Death** (Transformation).
-   It ends with **21. The World** (Completion, Achievement, Wholeness).

## Reading Tips

When a Major Arcana card appears in a reading, pay extra attention. It suggests that the issue at hand is of significant importance or is a major life lesson you are currently moving through.
