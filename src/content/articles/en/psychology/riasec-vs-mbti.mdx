# RIASEC vs MBTI: What's the Difference?

"My MBTI is INFP, what job should I get?"
"I got 'Artistic' on RIASEC. Is this a personality test too?"

When planning a career, you likely encounter two major tools: **RIASEC (Holland Codes)** and **MBTI (Myers-Briggs Type Indicator)**. While both help you understand yourself, their targets and goals are fundamentally different. Understanding this distinction is key to building a robust career map.

## 1. The Core Difference: "What" vs "How"

The biggest difference lies in the central question each test asks.

*   **RIASEC (Career Interest Test)**: **"WHAT do you like to do?"**
    *   Focus: **Interests, Topics, Preferred Activities**.
    *   Examples: "I like fixing machines", "I like teaching people".
    *   Outcome: Matches with specific job titles or industries.

*   **MBTI (Personality Test)**: **"HOW do you perceive and judge?"**
    *   Focus: **Communication Style, Energy Source, Processing Habits**.
    *   Examples: "I gain energy from solitude (I)", "I decide based on logic (T)".
    *   Outcome: Matches with work style and organizational culture.

**In summary:**
*   **RIASEC** tells you **"What subject matter"** will make you happy.
*   **MBTI** tells you **"What work style"** will make you comfortable.

## 2. Correlation Between RIASEC and MBTI

While not 100% aligned, there are common correlations. Knowing these overlaps can give you a 3D view of your profile.

| RIASEC Type | Common MBTI Traits | Shared Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Realistic (R)** | ISTP, ESTP, ISTJ | Tools, concrete facts, objective results |
| **Investigative (I)** | INTJ, INTP, ENTJ | Logical analysis, intellectual curiosity, theories |
| **Artistic (A)** | INFP, ISFP, ENFP | Creativity, expression, flexibility, individuality |
| **Social (S)** | ENFJ, ESFJ, ENFP | People-focused, empathy, harmony, helping |
| **Enterprising (E)** | ESTP, ESTJ, ENTJ | Action-oriented, goals, leadership, influence |
| **Conventional (C)** | ISTJ, ISFJ, ESTJ | Systems, order, rules, detail management |

*(Note: These are general trends, not absolute rules.)*

## 3. Combining Both for Career Success

Merging insights from both tests provides specific and actionable career advice.

### Example A: "I want to be a Developer"
*   **RIASEC**: Investigative (I) - You love solving complex puzzles. (Good fit!)
*   **MBTI**: **ENTP** (Extrovert+Intuition) vs **ISTJ** (Introvert+Sensing)
    *   **ENTP Developer**: Suited for fast-paced startups, R&D, and architecture design where novelty is key.
    *   **ISTJ Developer**: Suited for stable maintenance, financial/security backends where precision and reliability are key.
    *   *Conclusion*: Same job title, different **Role/Environment**.

### Example B: "I want to do Marketing"
*   **RIASEC**: Artistic (A) + Enterprising (E) - You want to sell creative ideas.
*   **MBTI**: **ENFP** vs **ESTJ**
    *   **ENFP Marketer**: Brand storytelling, creative content, social media (Using Intuition/Feeling).
    *   **ESTJ Marketer**: Performance marketing, data analytics, budget management (Using Sensing/Thinking).

## Conclusion: The Career Success Formula

The formula for optimal career satisfaction is:

> **(RIASEC Interest) + (MBTI Style) = Ideal Job Satisfaction**

1.  Use **RIASEC** to find the **Field** you will enjoy. This gives you your Job Title.
2.  Use **MBTI** to find the **Environment** you will thrive in. This gives you your Company Culture or Work Style.

To get the full picture, analyze yourself through both lenses today!
