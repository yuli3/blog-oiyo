# The Real Theory

Most people know MBTI as four letters (e.g., INFP), but the theory is actually based on "Cognitive Functions". These explain *how* your brain processes information.

# The 8 Functions

1.  **Se (Extroverted Sensing)**: Living in the moment, experiencing the physical world.
2.  **Si (Introverted Sensing)**: Relies on past experiences, tradition, and routine.
3.  **Ne (Extroverted Intuition)**: Exploring possibilities, brainstorming, connecting unrelated ideas.
4.  **Ni (Introverted Intuition)**: Predicting the future, seeing patterns, "gut insights".
5.  **Te (Extroverted Thinking)**: Efficiency, logic, organizing the external world.
6.  **Ti (Introverted Thinking)**: Internal logic, analyzing how things work, accuracy.
7.  **Fe (Extroverted Feeling)**: Social harmony, empathy, understanding group dynamics.
8.  **Fi (Introverted Feeling)**: Personal values, authenticity, inner emotional depth.

# Why it matters

Two types like INFJ and INFP might look similar, but they share *zero* cognitive functions!
-   **INFJ**: Ni-Fe-Ti-Se
-   **INFP**: Fi-Ne-Si-Te

Understanding functions gives you the true depth of personality analysis.
