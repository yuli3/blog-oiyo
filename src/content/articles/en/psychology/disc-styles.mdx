# What is DISC?

DISC is a behavior assessment tool that centers on four different personality traits. It's widely used in corporate settings to improve teamwork.

# The 4 Styles

-   **Dominance (D)**: Direct, firm, strong-willed, results-oriented.
    -   *Tip*: Be brief and focus on the bottom line.
-   **Influence (I)**: Outgoing, enthusiastic, optimistic, high-spirited.
    -   *Tip*: Let them share their ideas and build rapport.
-   **Steadiness (S)**: Even-tempered, accommodating, patient, humble.
    -   *Tip*: Be gentle and provide a stable environment.
-   **Conscientiousness (C)**: Analytical, reserved, precise, private.
    -   *Tip*: Provide data, facts, and time to think.

Adapting your style to the person you are talking to is the hallmark of emotional intelligence.
