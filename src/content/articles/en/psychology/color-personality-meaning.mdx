# The Spectrum of Emotion

Colors are wavelengths of light, but our brains translate them into feelings. Marketing uses this extensively (Red buttons, Trusted Blue logos).

# Personality Associations

-   **Red**: Energetic, passionate, aggressive, action-oriented.
-   **Blue**: Calm, reliable, analytical, sensitive.
-   **Yellow**: Happy, creative, optimistic, anxious.
-   **Green**: Harmonious, balance, growth, nature-loving.

While not a hard science like Physics, color choices often reflect our current emotional state and aspirations.
