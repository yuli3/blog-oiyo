# The Meeting Ecosystem

Every meeting is a micro-society. Understanding your role can help you navigate office politics and productivity.

# Common Archetypes

1.  **The Timekeeper**: Obsessed with usage. "We have 5 minutes left." Keeps things on track but can kill creativity.
2.  **The Dominator**: Talks for 80% of the time. Often passionate but can drown out others.
3.  **The Multitasker**: Laptop open, typing away. Physically present, mentally absent.
4.  **The Peacemaker**: Smooths over conflicts, ensures everyone is heard.

# Better Meetings

To improve meetings, you need a mix of styles. Too many Dominators lead to chaos; too many Silent Observers lead to zero decisions. Know your style and adapt.
