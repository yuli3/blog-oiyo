# What is DISC?

The DISC model is a behavioral assessment tool based on the theory of psychologist William Moulton Marston, which centers on four different personality traits.

## The 4 Traits

### 1. Dominance (D)
-   **Focus**: Results, bottom line, confidence.
-   **Strengths**: Direct, strong-willed, forceful.
-   **Challenges**: Can be impatient or insensitive.

### 2. Influence (I)
-   **Focus**: Relationships, influencing others, enthusiasm.
-   **Strengths**: Optimistic, magnetic, enthusiastic.
-   **Challenges**: Can be disorganized or impulsive.

### 3. Steadiness (S)
-   **Focus**: Cooperation, sincerity, dependability.
-   **Strengths**: Patient, good listener, calm.
-   **Challenges**: Can be overly accommodating or resistant to change.

### 4. Conscientiousness (C)
-   **Focus**: Quality, accuracy, expertise, competency.
-   **Strengths**: Analytical, systematic, precise.
-   **Challenges**: Can be overly critical or indecisive.

## Improving Communication

Understanding your DISC style and the styles of others can drastically improve how you communicate and work together.
