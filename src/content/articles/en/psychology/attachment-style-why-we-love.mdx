# The Blueprint of Love

Did you know your romantic patterns might have been set before you could even walk? **Attachment Theory**, developed by John Bowlby and Mary Ainsworth, suggests that our early bond with caregivers creates a "blueprint" for all future relationships.

## The 3 Main Styles

### 1. Anxious Attachment (The Pursuer)
- **Deep Fear**: Abandonment.
- **Behavior**: Cravies intimacy, needs constant reassurance, worries partner is pulling away.
- **Mantra**: "Do you still love me?"

### 2. Avoidant Attachment (The Distancer)
- **Deep Fear**: Enmeshment / Loss of Independence.
- **Behavior**: Pulls away when things get too serious, values autonomy over intimacy.
- **Mantra**: "I need space."

### 3. Secure Attachment (The Anchor)
- **Deep Fear**: Low fear.
- **Behavior**: Comfortable with both intimacy and independence. Trusts partner and self.

## The Anxious-Avoidant Trap

The greatest tragedy in dating is that Anxious and Avoidant types are magnetically attracted to each other.
- The Anxious person pushes for closeness creates the "engulfment" the Avoidant fears.
- The Avoidant pulls away, triggering the "abandonment" the Anxious fears.
- **Result**: A chaotic rollercoaster of highs and lows that feels like "passion" but is actually anxiety.

## Can you change?

Yes. This is called **"Earned Security"**. By recognizing your triggers and choosing secure partners (or healing with your current partner), you can rewire your brain for healthy love.
