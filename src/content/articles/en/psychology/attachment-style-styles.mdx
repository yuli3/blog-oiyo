# Attachment Theory

Attachment theory explores how the emotional bond formed with primary caregivers in childhood impacts relationships in adulthood.

## The Styles

### 1. Secure Attachment
-   Comfortable with intimacy and independence.
-   Relationships are generally open, trusting, and balanced.

### 2. Anxious-Preoccupied
-   Often fear rejection or abandonment.
-   Tend to be "clingy" or overly dependent on their partner for validation.

### 3. Dismissive-Avoidant
-   Equate intimacy with a loss of independence.
-   Tend to distance themselves when relationships get too close.

### 4. Fearful-Avoidant (Disorganized)
-   Crave connection but also fear it.
-   Often results from trauma; behavior can be confusing or erratic.

## Can It Change?

Yes! Attachment styles are not set in stone. Through self-awareness (and often therapy), people can move toward a more "earned secure" attachment style.
