# The Biology of Stress

Stress isn't just "being worried". It's a physiological response (Fight or Flight). But *how* we react varies wildly.

# Common Stress Archetypes

1.  **The Exploder**: Gets angry, yells, vents outwardly.
    -   *Strategy*: Needs to physically release energy (exercise, screaming into a pillow) before communicating.
2.  **The Imploder**: Withdraws, shuts down, internalizes everything.
    -   *Strategy*: Needs safe connection and gentle reassurance. Journaling helps.
3.  **The Fixer**: Immediately tries to solve the problem, ignoring emotions.
    -   *Strategy*: Needs to pause and acknowledge feelings before jumping to solutions.

Identifying your type is the first step to better mental health.
