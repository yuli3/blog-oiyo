# Decoding Your RIASEC 2-Letter Code

Did you finish the RIASEC test? Chances are, your result isn't just a single "Realistic (R)". Most people are a mix of a **Dominant (Primary) Type** and a **Supportive (Secondary) Type**.

Combining these two codes allows for a much more detailed and multi-dimensional career exploration. Check your **Top 2 Codes** below and discover what they mean for you.

## 1. High Consistency Combinations

These types are **adjacent to each other** on the Holland Hexagon model. They share many traits, leading to less internal conflict and clearer career paths.

### 🔧 **RI** (Realistic + Investigative) - "The Technical Analyst"
*   **Traits**: Loves machines/tools (R) but also enjoys digging into the principles behind them (I).
*   **Ideal Careers**: Mechanical Engineer, Software Developer, Medical Technician, Forensics Expert.
*   **Strength**: Solves complex technical problems with practical, hands-on logic.

### 👥 **SE** (Social + Enterprising) - "The Influential Leader"
*   **Traits**: Loves helping people (S) and enjoys leading/managing them (E).
*   **Ideal Careers**: School Principal, Social Entrepreneur, Politician, Sales Manager, HR Director.
*   **Strength**: Uses strong interpersonal skills to lead organizations and drive positive social change.

### 📋 **CE** (Conventional + Enterprising) - "The Administrator"
*   **Traits**: Systematic and detailed (C) while being goal and results-oriented (E). The classic business profile.
*   **Ideal Careers**: Accountant, Financial Manager, Bank Manager, Data Analyst, Auditor.
*   **Strength**: Efficiently manages organizational systems to achieve tangible business results.

## 2. Moderate Consistency Combinations

These types are **separated by one** on the hexagon. They mix slightly different interests, creating unique synergies.

### 🎨 **RA** (Realistic + Artistic) - "The Artisan"
*   **Traits**: Likes working with hands (R) but with a touch of creativity (A).
*   **Ideal Careers**: Architect, Landscape Designer, Chef, Carpenter/Woodworker, Fashion Technician.
*   **Strength**: Creates outputs that are both functional and aesthetically pleasing.

### 🔬 **IS** (Investigative + Social) - "The Expert Helper"
*   **Traits**: Combines cold logic (I) with a warm heart (S).
*   **Ideal Careers**: Physician (Doctor), Clinical Psychologist, University Professor, Dietitian.
*   **Strength**: Uses specialized expert knowledge to solve people's real-life problems.

## 3. Low Consistency Combinations (Opposites)

These types are **opposite each other** on the hexagon. Having opposing interests can cause internal conflict, but when integrated well, can lead to **unique and irreplaceable careers**.

### 🔧 **RS** (Realistic + Social) - "The Practical Helper"
*   **Traits**: Interests in things/machines (R) and people (S) - usually polar opposites.
*   **Ideal Careers**: Paramedic (EMT), Physical Therapist, Vocational Teacher, Sports Trainer.
*   **Synergy**: Uses physical strength or technical skills directly to assist others.

### 🎨 **AC** (Artistic + Conventional) - "The Digital Creator"
*   **Traits**: A free spirit (A) meets a stickler for rules and order (C).
*   **Ideal Careers**: Web Designer, UI/UX Designer, Librarian, Technical Writer, Editor.
*   **Synergy**: Delivers creative work within highly structured formats or rigorous systems.

---

## How to Use Your Code?

*   Don't just look at your #1 type. **Consider the "Flavor" your #2 type adds.**
*   Even for the same job like 'Developer (I)', an **IR (Realistic Mix)** might prefer embedded systems/hardware, while an **IA (Artistic Mix)** might thrive in frontend/game dev.
*   If your codes conflict (Opposites), consider satisfying one through your **Job** and the other through a **Hobby**.
