# What is MBTI?

The Myers-Briggs Type Indicator (MBTI) is an introspective self-report questionnaire indicating differing psychological preferences in how people perceive the world and make decisions.

## The 4 Dichotomies

1.  **Extraversion (E) vs. Introversion (I)**: Where you focus your attention.
2.  **Sensing (S) vs. Intuition (N)**: The way you take in information.
3.  **Thinking (T) vs. Feeling (F)**: How you make decisions.
4.  **Judging (J) vs. Perceiving (P)**: How you deal with the world.

## Why use it?

Understanding your type can help you appreciate your own strengths and gifts, and understand and appreciate those of others.
