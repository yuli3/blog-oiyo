# Money is Emotional

We like to think we are rational with money, but 90% of spending decisions are emotional.

# The 4 Money Personalities

1.  **The Spender**: "You only live once." Seeks instant gratification.
2.  **The Saver**: "Save for a rainy day." Finds security in accumulation, anxiety in spending.
3.  **The Avoider**: "I don't know, I don't look." Ignores bills and balances out of fear.
4.  **The Monk**: "Money is evil." Believes wealth corrupts; avoids negotiation or investing.

# Finding Balance

Healthy financial life is balancing enjoyment (Spender) with security (Saver), awareness (Anti-Avoider), and value (Anti-Monk).
