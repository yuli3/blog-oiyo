# Length matters more than complexity

A long phrase is often harder to brute-force than a short, complex one.
-   **Weak**: `Tr0ub4dor&3` (Complex but short)
-   **Strong**: `correct horse battery staple` (Long and random)

# Tips for Security

1.  **Use a Password Manager**: Don't rely on your memory for every site.
2.  **Avoid Personal Info**: Names, birthdays, and pet names are easy to guess.
3.  **Unique Passwords**: Never reuse passwords across important sites (Email, Banking).
4.  **Enable 2FA**: Two-Factor Authentication adds a critical layer of defense.
