# The Reason for Being

**Ikigai (生き甲斐)** is a Japanese concept referring to something that gives a person a sense of purpose, a reason for living. It is the intersection of four circles:
1.  What you love.
2.  What you are good at.
3.  What the world needs.
4.  What you can be paid for.

## The RIASEC Connection

While Ikigai gives us the *philosophy*, the **RIASEC (Holland Codes)** gives us the *data*.
- **Realistic (Doers)**: "What you are good at" often involves tools/machines.
- **Investigative (Thinkers)**: "What you love" is solving problems.
- **Artistic (Creators)**: Expressing self.
- **Social (Helpers)**: "What the world needs" - helping others.
- **Enterprising (Persuaders)**: "What you can be paid for" - leadership/sales.
- **Conventional (Organizers)**: Structure and order.

## How to Find Yours

Most people are unhappy because they only satisfy 2 or 3 circles.
- **Good at + Paid for** = Profession (but boring).
- **Love + World needs** = Mission (but you're poor).

Use our **RIASEC Test** to objectively identify your top 2 traits. Then look for careers that satisfy the remaining Ikigai circles for those specific traits.
