# The Problem with BMI

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple calculation based on height and weight. It works for the general population but fails for individuals.
-   **Muscle vs. Fat**: Muscle is denser than fat. A bodybuilder might have a "Obese" BMI despite having low body fat.
-   **Skinny Fat**: Someone with normal weight but low muscle mass might have a healthy BMI but unhealthy visceral fat levels.

# Enter Body Fat Percentage

Body fat percentage measures exactly what it says: the percent of your weight that is fat.
-   **Men**: 10-20% is generally healthy / athletic.
-   **Women**: 18-28% is generally healthy / athletic.

# Which should you use?

Use **BMI** for a quick general check.
Use **Body Fat Percentage** (via calipers, DEXA scan, or smart scales) for tracking fitness progress and true health.
