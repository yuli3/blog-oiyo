# Understanding BMI

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a person's weight in kilograms divided by the square of height in meters. It is an inexpensive and easy screening method for weight category—underweight, healthy weight, overweight, and obesity.

## BMI Categories

-   **Underweight**: < 18.5
-   **Normal weight**: 18.5 – 24.9
-   **Overweight**: 25 – 29.9
-   **Obesity**: 30 or greater

## Limitations

BMI does not measure body fat directly. As such, some people, such as athletes, may have a BMI that identifies them as overweight even though they do not have excess body fat.
