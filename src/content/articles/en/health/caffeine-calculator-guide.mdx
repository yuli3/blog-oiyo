## What is Caffeine Half-Life?

The "half-life" of a substance is the time it takes for the amount of that substance in your body to be reduced by half.
For the average adult, the half-life of caffeine is approximately **5 hours**.

### What does this mean?
If you drink a cup of coffee with 100mg of caffeine at 4:00 PM:
- 9:00 PM: You still have ~50mg in your system.
- 2:00 AM: You still have ~25mg in your system.

This implies that afternoon coffee can significantly disrupt your sleep, even if you feel "fine" by bedtime.

## Safe Limits

The FDA and other health organizations generally cite **400 milligrams a day** as an amount not generally associated with dangerous, negative effects for healthy adults.

### Approximate Caffeine Content
- **Brewed Coffee (8 oz):** 95 mg
- **Espresso (1 shot):** 63 mg
- **Black Tea (8 oz):** 47 mg
- **Coke/Pepsi (12 oz):** 30-40 mg
- **Energy Drinks:** 80-200+ mg (varies widely)

## Factors Affecting Metabolism
- **Age:** Metabolism slows with age.
- **Genetics:** Some people are "slow metabolizers" due to the CYP1A2 gene.
- **Pregnancy:** Caffeine metabolism slows significantly during pregnancy (half-life can extend to 10-18 hours).
- **Smoking:** Smokers metabolize caffeine twice as fast.
