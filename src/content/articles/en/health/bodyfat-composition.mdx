# Beyond BMI

While BMI is a quick screening tool, it doesn't distinguish between muscle and fat. Body Fat Percentage is a more accurate indicator of fitness and health risk.

## Essential Fat vs. Storage Fat

-   **Essential Fat**: Necessary for life functions (warmth, organ protection, hormone regulation).
    -   Men: ~2-5%
    -   Women: ~10-13%
-   **Storage Fat**: Accumulated energy reserves. Excess amounts correlate with health risks.

## Healthy Ranges

| Category | Men | Women |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Athletes** | 6-13% | 14-20% |
| **Fitness** | 14-17% | 21-24% |
| **Average** | 18-24% | 25-31% |
| **Obese** | 25%+ | 32%+ |

*Note: These ranges vary slightly by age and source.*

## Measuring It

Common methods include:
1.  **Skinfold Calipers**: Measures thickness of skin folds.
2.  **Bioelectrical Impedance (BIA)**: Smart scales (convenient but sensitive to hydration).
3.  **DEXA Scan**: The gold standard (medical imaging).
