# Negotiating Your Worth

Salary negotiation is a critical skill that can significantly impact your lifetime earnings.

## Key Strategies

1.  **Do Your Research**: Know the market rate for your role and experience level.
2.  **Focus on Value**: Articulate what unique value you bring to the company.
3.  **Practice**: Rehearse your pitch with a friend or mentor.
4.  **Be Willing to Walk Away**: Sometimes, the best negotiation tactic is knowing your bottom line.

## Common Mistakes

-   Accepting the first offer immediately.
-   Focusing on personal needs rather than market value.
-   Neglecting to negotiate benefits and perks.
