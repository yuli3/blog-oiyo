# Choosing a Mortgage Rate

When taking out a home loan, one of the biggest decisions is choosing between a fixed rate and a variable (adjustable) rate.

## Fixed Rate Mortgage

-   **Pros**: Stability. Your interest rate and monthly payments stay the same for the entire term.
-   **Cons**: Usually higher initial rate compared to variable.
-   **Best for**: People who plan to stay in their home for a long time and prefer predictable budgets.

## Variable Rate Mortgage

-   **Pros**: Lower initial rate. Potential to pay less interest if rates drop.
-   **Cons**: Uncertainty. Your payments can increase if market rates rise.
-   **Best for**: People who plan to move or refinance soon, or those who can tolerate some risk.
