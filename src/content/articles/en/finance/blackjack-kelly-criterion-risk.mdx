# The Formula for Fortune

In 1956 (a good year for science), Bell Labs physicist Claude Shannon and mathematician John Kelly developed a formula that would change gambling and investing forever.

## The Problem of Ruin

Imagine a coin toss game where you have an edge:
- **Heads (60%)**: You win double your bet.
- **Tails (40%)**: You lose your bet.

This is a great bet! But how much of your net worth ($1000) should you bet?
- **Too little ($1)**: Safe, but you'll get rich very slowly.
- **Too much ($1000)**: You have a 40% chance of going bankrupt immediately. **Game Over**.

## The Formula

The Kelly Criterion calculates the optimal bet size `f*` to maximize logarithm of wealth:
$$ f^* = \frac{bp - q}{b} $$

- `b` = odds received (1 to 1)
- `p` = probability of winning (0.60)
- `q` = probability of losing (0.40)

$$ f^* = \frac{(1 \times 0.60) - 0.40}{1} = 0.20 $$

**Result**: You should bet exactly **20%** of your bankroll. Not 19%, not 21%.

## Investment Lesson

This teaches us the iron law of investing: **Geometric Growth vs Arithmetic Loss**.
- If you lose 50%, you need a 100% gain just to get back to even.
- Therefore, **Survival** is the prerequisite to Wealth. Never bet the farm, even on a "sure thing."
