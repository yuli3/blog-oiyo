# Jeonse Safety Checklist

Jeonse fraud is a devastating issue. In 2026, new protections are in place, but your vigilance is the best defense.

## Must-Check Before Signing

### 1. 'Violating Construction' Status
Illegal extensions (unauthorized balconies, rooftop rooms) disqualify you from Jeonse Loans and HUG Insurance.
*   **Action:** Always check the **Building Ledger (건축물대장)** for "Violating (위반건축물)" tags.

### 2. The 126% Rule
HUG Insurance only covers up to **126%** of the Official House Price (Gongsi-ga).
*   **Warning:** If `Deposit > Gongsi-ga × 1.26`, **DO NOT SIGN**. It is high risk and likely uninsurable.

### 3. Tax Arrears
Landlords must show proof of paid taxes (Gukse/Jibangse Wan-nap).
*   **Risk:** Unpaid government taxes take priority over your deposit in auctions.

## New in 2026

-   **Mandatory Escrow (Pilot):** Deposits for certain high-risk villas may be held in escrow services until move-in to prevent "gap investment" fraud.
-   **Safe App 2.0:** Use the 'Ansim Jeonse' App to instantly check landlord blacklist status and real-time market price.
