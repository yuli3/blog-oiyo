# The Silver Economy Boom

Korea is entering a "Super-Aged Society" faster than any other nation in history. By 2026, over 20% of the population will be 65 or older. But these aren't the seniors of the past—they are the "New Seniors," armed with wealth and digital literacy.

## Top 3 Investment Sectors

### 1. Senior Housing & REITs
Traditional nursing homes are out. "Silver Towns" resembling 5-star hotels with medical care are in.
*   **Invest:** Look for REITs focusing on high-end senior residences and healthcare facilities.

### 2. Digital Healthcare
The focus has shifted from "lifespan" (living long) to "healthspan" (living healthy).
*   **Trends:** AI diagnosis, continuous glucose monitoring (CGM), and telemedicine platforms.

### 3. Care Robotics
With a shrinking workforce, humans can't fill all caregiving roles.
*   **Tech:** Wearable robots (exoskeletons) for mobility aid and nursing robots are moving from prototypes to commercial products.
