## What is the Financial Investment Income Tax?

The Financial Investment Income Tax (Geumtu Tax) is a system that imposes a 20% tax (25% for amounts exceeding 300M KRW) on the excess when income from financial investments such as stocks, bonds, funds, and derivatives exceeds a certain amount (50M KRW for domestic stocks).

*   **Implementation Date:** January 1, 2026 (scheduled)
*   **Tax Rate:** 22% ~ 27.5% including local income tax
*   **Basic Deduction:** 50M KRW for domestic listed stocks / 2.5M KRW for others (overseas stocks, etc.)

## How Should You Prepare?

1.  **Utilize ISA Accounts:** Profits generated in ISA (Individual Savings Account) can receive tax exemption benefits from the Financial Investment Income Tax.
2.  **Loss Offsetting:** Since taxes are calculated by combining profits and losses, you can reduce taxes by appropriately selling stocks with losses. (Year-end tax saving strategy)
3.  **Long-term Investment:** Holding quality stocks for the long term can be advantageous in terms of tax management and fees compared to frequent trading.
