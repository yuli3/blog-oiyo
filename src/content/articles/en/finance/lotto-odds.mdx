# The Odds

Winning the lottery is statistically improbable. For a typical 6/45 game, the odds of hitting the jackpot are 1 in 8,145,060.

# Randomness vs. Strategy

## The "Hot" and "Cold" Number Myth
Many players track "hot" numbers (frequently drawn) and avoiding "cold" numbers (rarely drawn). Mathematically, every draw is independent. The ball doesn't know it was picked last week.

## Lucky Numbers
Choosing birthdays (1-31) limits your range and increases the chance of splitting the pot if you do win, since many others use the same strategy.

# Responsible Play

The best strategy is to play for fun, not as an investment. Set a budget and stick to it.
