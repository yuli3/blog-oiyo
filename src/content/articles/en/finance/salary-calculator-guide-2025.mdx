## Understanding Your Take-Home Pay in 2025

When you receive a job offer of $60,000 (or 60 million KRW), that's not what hits your bank account. Your "Net Pay" is what remains after taxes and deductions.

### Key Deductions (2025 Update)

1.  **Income Tax**: Progressive rates based on your total annual income.
2.  **Social Security / National Pension**: Mandatory contributions for your future retirement.
3.  **Health Insurance**: Coverage for medical expenses.
4.  **Local Income Tax**: Often 10% of your federal/national income tax.

### How to Calculate Your Real Salary

Using our **Salary Calculator**, you can input your gross annual income. For 2025, tax brackets have adjusted slightly for inflation.
*   **Tax-Free Allowance**: Ensure you know the standard deduction for your region.
*   **Non-Taxable Income**: Meal allowances (often up to 200,000 KRW/month) are tax-free.

## Negotiating Your Salary

Reviewing your salary is crucial.
*   **Market Rate**: Use tools like Glassdoor or LinkedIn to benchmark.
*   **Inflation Adjustment**: If inflation is 3%, a 3% raise is just maintaining your purchasing power. Aim for 5-10% for real growth.
