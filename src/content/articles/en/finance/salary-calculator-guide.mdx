## Gross Pay vs. Net Pay

When you receive a job offer, the number discussed is almost always your **Gross Pay**. However, the amount you see on your payday notification is your **Net Pay**. Understanding the difference is crucial for personal budgeting.

### What is Gross Pay?
Gross pay is the total amount of money you earn before any deductions are subtracted. This includes:
- Base Salary
- Bonuses
- Overtime Pay
- Commissions

### What is Net Pay?
Net pay, often called "take-home pay," is what remains after all mandatory and voluntary deductions.
**Formula:** `Net Pay = Gross Pay - (Taxes + Benefits + Other Deductions)`

## Common Deductions

### 1. Income Tax
Governments levy tax on your income. The rate usually depends on your income bracket and marital status.

### 2. Social Security / National Pension
Contributions towards national retirement schemes. In many countries, this is matched by the employer.

### 3. Health Insurance
Premiums for medical, dental, and vision insurance are often deducted pre-tax.

## Why It Matters
When negotiating a salary, always estimate your Net Pay. A high Gross Salary might result in a lower-than-expected take-home amount if you move to a region with higher taxes.
