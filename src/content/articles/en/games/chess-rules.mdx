# The Goal

The goal of chess is to **Checkmate** the opponent's King. This means the King is under attack and has no way to maximize safety.

# The Pieces

-   **The King ♔**: Moves one square in any direction. Valuable but weak.
-   **The Queen ♕**: The most powerful piece. Moves any number of squares in any direction (straight or diagonal).
-   **The Rook ♖**: Moves any number of squares in a straight line (horizontal or vertical).
-   **The Bishop ♗**: Moves any number of squares diagonally. One bishop stays on white squares, the other on black.
-   **The Knight ♘**: Moves in an 'L' shape (2 squares one way, 1 square perpendicular). The only piece that can jump over others.
-   **The Pawn ♙**: Moves forward one square (or two on its first move). Captures diagonally forward.

# Special Moves

-   **Castling**: A defensive move involving the King and Rook.
-   **En Passant**: A special pawn capture rule.
-   **Promotion**: When a pawn reaches the other side, it can become a Queen, Rook, Bishop, or Knight.
