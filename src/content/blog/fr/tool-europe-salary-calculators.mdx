## Comparaison des Charges Salariales en Europe

| Pays | Cotisations salarié (~) | Cotisations employeur (~) | Net / Brut |
|---|---|---|---|
| **France** | 22–23% | 40–45% | ~75–78% |
| **Allemagne** | 20–21% | 20–21% | ~62–68% |
| **Espagne** | 6,4% | 30,9% | ~75–80% |
| **Belgique** | 13,1% | 27,5% | ~58–65% |
| **Pays-Bas** | 17–20% | 18–22% | ~65–75% |
| **Suède** | 7% | 31,4% | ~68–75% |
