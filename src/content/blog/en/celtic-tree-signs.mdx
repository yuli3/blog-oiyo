## Wisdom of the Trees

The Celtic Tree Calendar is a lunar system consisting of 13 moons. Each moon is associated with a sacred tree (like Birch, Oak, or Willow) that holds unique magical properties and personality traits. Your tree sign reveals your fundamental nature and how you grow through life.

Unlike solar astrology which focuses on the ego's outward expression, **Celtic Tree Astrology connects you to the rhythmic cycles of the earth**. It asks you not "Who am I?" but "What is my root?" and "How do I branch out into the world?"

## Ogham: The Language of Trees

Ogham is an ancient alphabet found carved on standing stones across Ireland and Britain. Each letter is linked to a tree. This connection suggests that language, knowledge, and nature are intricately woven together.

- **Beith (Birch)**: The Achiever
- **Luis (Rowan)**: The Thinker
- **Nion (Ash)**: The Enchanter

Your sign's Ogham symbol is a key to unlocking your intuitive powers. Meditating on your symbol can help you access the ancient wisdom stored within your lineage.

## Nature as a Mirror

For the Celts, the boundary between the physical and spiritual worlds was thin. Trees were seen as bridges between these realms. By understanding your tree sign, you learn to root yourself deeply in your reality while reaching high for spiritual wisdom, just as a tree bridges earth and sky.

### Living with the Seasons

Just as trees shed their leaves in autumn and bud in spring, your energy flourishes when aligned with nature's cycles. Embracing your "season" allows you to harness your natural power rather than fighting against the current of your destiny.
