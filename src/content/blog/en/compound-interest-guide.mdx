## Simple vs. Compound Interest — The 20-year Gap

What does ₩10M invested at 7% for 20 years become?

- **Simple interest**: ₩10M + (₩10M × 7% × 20 years) = **₩24M**
- **Compound interest**: ₩10M × (1.07)²⁰ = **₩38.7M**

Same principal, same rate, same period — yet compound interest produces **₩14.7M more**. That's the essence of compounding: the interest you earn becomes part of your principal, generating further interest in turn. **Interest earns interest**.

## The Rule of 72 — Time to Double

A quick rule for estimating how long it takes compound interest to double your money:

> **72 ÷ Annual Return = Years to Double**

- 6% annual return → 72 ÷ 6 = **12 years**
- 8% annual return → 72 ÷ 8 = **9 years**
- 12% annual return → 72 ÷ 12 = **6 years**

The rule works in reverse too. If inflation is 4% per year, your cash's purchasing power halves in 72 ÷ 4 = **18 years**.

## Practical Principles for Compound Investing

### 1. Never Stop — That's the Best Strategy

Compound interest's greatest enemy is withdrawing money partway through. Selling during a downturn or pausing contributions breaks the compounding chain. Use automatic transfers to link income directly to investing — remove willpower from the equation.

### 2. Minimize Costs

The impact of a 0.5% fee difference over 30 years is far larger than you'd expect. Fees also compound — but they compound against you, draining your returns every single year.

### 3. Use Tax Deferral

Tax-deferred accounts (pensions, ISAs, retirement savings plans, etc.) are compounding amplifiers. Money that would have gone to tax instead stays invested for decades — generating returns on those "saved" tax dollars year after year.
