Palmistry is a piece of shared human wisdom with a history stretching back thousands of years. In the West it is called **palmistry** or **chiromancy**; in East Asia it developed as **susang-hak (手相學)**. More than simple fortune-telling, palm reading is the study of a person's tendencies and energy through the lines and shape of the hand.

<Callout type="info">
**Which hand should you read?**: Traditionally, for a right-handed person the **left hand shows your potential at birth and the right hand shows your present state**. Comparing both hands allows a richer reading.
</Callout>

## The four major lines

### 1. The Life Line

The curve that runs from the wrist around the base of the thumb, between thumb and index finger.

<Callout type="warning">
**A common myth**: A short life line does not mean a short life. The life line reflects **vitality, energy, and quality of life** rather than lifespan.
</Callout>

| Life line feature | Meaning |
|-----------|------|
| **Long, deep line** | Strong vitality, good health, stamina |
| **Short, faint line** | Delicate health; energy needs managing |
| **Wide curve toward the wrist** | Active, loves travel |
| **Straight, hugging the palm's center** | Cautious and realistic |
| **Broken in the middle** | A major life change or shift in health |
| **Double life line** | Strong protection and resilience |

### 2. The Heart Line

The line that begins below the little finger and runs toward the index or middle finger.

| Heart line feature | Meaning |
|-----------|------|
| **Rises to below the index finger** | Idealistic, high expectations |
| **Ends below the middle finger** | Material, practical |
| **Curved** | Emotional, expressive |
| **Straight** | Rational, logical |
| **Short and straight** | Tends to be self-focused |
| **Long and deep** | Emotionally rich and devoted |

### 3. The Head Line

The line that begins between the thumb and index finger and runs toward the little finger.

| Head line feature | Meaning |
|-----------|------|
| **Long and straight** | Logical, analytical thinking |
| **Curving downward** | Creative, imaginative |
| **Short and straight** | Practical, down-to-earth |
| **Starts joined to the life line** | Prudent, careful |
| **Starts apart from the life line** | Independent, adventurous |
| **Forked at the end** | Versatile, with varied interests |

### 4. The Fate Line

The vertical line that begins near the center of the wrist and rises toward the middle finger.

<Callout type="info">
**No fate line?**: Having no fate line is not a bad omen. It is read instead as a free life — one you choose for yourself.
</Callout>

| Fate line feature | Meaning |
|-----------|------|
| **Starts at the wrist** | A direction set from childhood |
| **Starts mid-palm** | Direction settles after the thirties |
| **Starts from the life line** | A life where family or tradition matters |
| **Starts from the heart line** | A fresh start after the fifties |
| **Deep and long** | Strong sense of purpose, success |

## Personality by hand shape

### The four basic hand types

| Hand type | Features | Personality |
|--------|------|------|
| **Fire hand** (long palm, short fingers) | Passionate, intuitive | Full of energy, impulsive |
| **Earth hand** (square palm, short fingers) | Realistic, practical | Diligent, seeks stability |
| **Air hand** (square palm, long fingers) | Analytical, communicative | Intellectual, sociable |
| **Water hand** (long palm, long fingers) | Emotional, intuitive | Sensitive, artistic |

## Personality by finger

### The thumb

| Feature | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Long, strong thumb** | Willpower, leadership, self-control |
| **Short, weak thumb** | Dependent, acts on emotion |
| **Thick first phalange** | Logical, good judgment |
| **Thick second phalange** | Emotional, intuitive |

### The index finger — the finger of Jupiter

Represents dominance, ambition, and self-esteem.

- **Long, strong index finger**: leadership, confidence
- **Short index finger**: lower self-esteem, introversion

### The middle finger — the finger of Saturn

Represents responsibility, balance, and destiny.

### The ring finger — the finger of the Sun

Represents artistry, creativity, and self-expression.

### The little finger — the finger of Mercury

Represents communication, intelligence, and business sense.

- **Long, strong little finger**: excellent communication and persuasion
- **Short little finger**: difficulty with self-expression

## Special marks on the palm

### The star (★)

- **Star on the heart line**: an intense love experience
- **Star on the head line**: a creative achievement
- **Star on the fate line**: a remarkable success

### The island (○)

An island shape on a line generally points to difficulty or an obstacle, but it is not necessarily a bad sign.

### The cross (+)

- A cross in the center of the palm (the Mystic Cross): intuition, spiritual ability
- A cross between the heart and head lines: a trial concerning love

## A modern understanding of palmistry

Palmistry is not prophecy but **tendency**. The lines change with age, lifestyle, and mindset. This in itself implies what palmistry has always taught: destiny is not fixed.

**The right mindset for reading palms**:
1. Use it as a tool for self-understanding.
2. If you find a weakness, treat it as a chance to grow.
3. If you find a strength, develop it further.
4. Choices matter more than fate.
