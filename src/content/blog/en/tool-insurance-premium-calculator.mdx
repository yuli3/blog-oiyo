# Insurance Premium Calculator

Insurance premiums decrease when you're younger, healthier, and choosing shorter coverage terms. Getting a ballpark estimate upfront makes budgeting and portfolio planning much easier.

## Insurance Types at a Glance

| Type | Coverage | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Whole life** | Death benefit (lifetime coverage) | Primary earners with dependents |
| **Term life** | Death coverage for a fixed period; lower premiums | 30s–40s, cost-efficiency focused |
| **Cancer** | Cancer diagnosis payout + treatment | All ages, especially with family history |
| **Medical expense (실손)** | 80–90% of actual hospital bills | Everyone (considered essential) |
| **Annuity** | Monthly pension in retirement | 40s–50s planning for old age |

<Callout type="tip">
**Recommended premium budget**: Keep total insurance premiums within **7–10% of monthly income**. Prioritize in this order: medical expense → cancer → life → retirement/annuity.
</Callout>

## Premium Estimates by Age (Men, Korea market reference)

| Entry Age | Medical Expense / Month | Cancer Insurance / Month |
|---|---|---|
| 20s | ₩10,000–20,000 (~$8–16) | ₩20,000–40,000 (~$16–32) |
| 30s | ₩20,000–40,000 (~$16–32) | ₩40,000–80,000 (~$32–64) |
| 40s | ₩50,000–90,000 (~$40–72) | ₩80,000–150,000 (~$64–120) |
| 50s | ₩100,000–200,000 (~$80–160) | ₩150,000–300,000 (~$120–240) |

*USD approximations at ₩1,250/$. Premiums differ significantly by country and insurer.*

## Pre-Purchase Checklist

- Check for overlap with workplace group insurance
- Compare renewable vs. non-renewable plans
- Confirm waiting period and reduction period
- Look for auto-debit and preferred-health discounts

<Callout type="note">
This calculator provides **reference estimates only**. Actual premiums vary significantly by insurer, product, and health disclosure. Get an accurate quote from a licensed insurance agent.
</Callout>
