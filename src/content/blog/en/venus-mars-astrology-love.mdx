"If your Sun sign tells the world who you are, your Venus tells the world how you love — and your Mars tells it what you want."

Most people only check their Sun sign when they think about romantic compatibility. But in astrology, the two planets that most directly shape love and relationships are **Venus** and **Mars**.

## Venus — The Planet of Love

Venus represents **how you love, what you find beautiful, and what kind of relationship you want**.

You'll need your birth chart to find your Venus sign (free tools like Astro.com can calculate it for you).

### Love Style by Venus Sign

| Venus Sign | How Love Is Expressed | Ideal Relationship |
|-----------|------------|---------|
| **Aries** | Passionate, direct, impulsive | Thrilling, dynamic romance |
| **Taurus** | Sensual, steady, trust-seeking | Comfortable, stable relationships |
| **Gemini** | Intellectual, playful, changeable | Fun, stimulating conversation |
| **Cancer** | Nurturing, protective, loyal | Deep emotional safety |
| **Leo** | Dramatic, generous, devoted | Romantic gestures and expression |
| **Virgo** | Practical, attentive, of service | Everyday acts of care |
| **Libra** | Graceful, harmony-seeking, partnership-oriented | A balanced, equal partnership |
| **Scorpio** | Intense, all-in, craving depth | A soul-deep connection |
| **Sagittarius** | Free-spirited, adventurous, candid | A relationship built on shared growth and adventure |
| **Capricorn** | Careful, devoted, long-term-minded | Building a future together |
| **Aquarius** | Independent, unconventional, friendship-based | An intellectual partnership that respects freedom |
| **Pisces** | Romantic, empathetic, spiritually connected | A spiritual, deeply felt love |

## Venus–Mars Compatibility

### The Basic Principle

**How Venus and Mars interact**: attraction runs strong when someone's Mars aligns well with your Venus sign, and the reverse is just as true — when your Mars aligns with their Venus, you become magnetic to them.

**Examples**:
- Person A's Venus in Sagittarius + Person B's Mars in Aries → both fire signs resonate, producing strong attraction
- Person A's Venus in Taurus + Person B's Mars in Scorpio → opposite signs (an axis of tension) → intense attraction alongside real friction

### Venus–Mars Compatibility by Element

| Venus Element | Mars Element | Compatibility |
|---------|---------|------|
| Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) | Fire | Passionate, but egos can clash |
| Fire | Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) | Air feeds fire — a natural harmony |
| Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) | Earth | Stable, but can lack change |
| Earth | Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) | Water enriches earth — emotionally grounding |
| Air | Air | Intellectually connected, but can lack feeling |
| Air | Fire | A lively combination of ideas and energy |
| Water | Water | Deep emotional connection, but can lack practicality |
| Water | Earth | Emotion grounded by practicality — a stabilizing mix |

## A Case Study

**Person A (Venus in Scorpio, Mars in Aries) + Person B (Venus in Aries, Mars in Scorpio)**

→ A's Mars (Aries) is drawn to B's Venus (Aries): a strong initial spark
→ B's Mars (Scorpio) is drawn to A's Venus (Scorpio): a craving for deep connection
→ Overall: intense attraction, but with the potential for power struggles

This kind of pairing is the classic pattern of a couple who's crazy about each other — and fights just as hard.
