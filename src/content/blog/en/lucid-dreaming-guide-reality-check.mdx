## Reality Check

Ask yourself dozens of times a day. "Is this a dream or reality?"

- \*\* In dreams, fingers bend backward or the number changes.
- \*\* In dreams, numbers on a watch constantly change or are unreadable.
- \*\* In dreams, you can breathe even if you block your nose.

If this habit is imprinted in the unconscious, you will ask the same question in your dream and become lucid.

## Utilization of Lucid Dreaming

Lucid dreaming is not just play. You can turn nightmares into epic victories or simulate skills practicing in reality (sports, instruments) to strengthen neural networks.\*\*
