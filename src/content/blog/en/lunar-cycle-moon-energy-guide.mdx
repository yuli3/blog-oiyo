The moon is Earth's closest celestial neighbor, and in many traditions it's treated as one of the most powerful sources of energetic influence. Its gravitational pull moves the ocean's tides, and since the human body is roughly 70% water, it's a natural leap to imagine the moon affecting us too. For thousands of years, farmers have planted and harvested by the lunar calendar, and some healers have timed procedures around the moon's phase.

## The basics of the moon cycle

The moon orbits the Earth roughly every 29.5 days, moving through 8 major phases along the way. Each phase alternates between waxing (growing) and waning (shrinking) energy.

**Waxing Moon**: energy builds from new moon toward full moon
- A good window for beginnings, creation, growth, and setting intentions

**Waning Moon**: energy recedes from full moon back toward new moon
- A good window for tidying up, releasing, letting go, and healing

## The energy of each of the 8 moon phases

### 1. New Moon — New beginnings

**When**: the moon is invisible
**Energy**: seeds, possibility, fresh starts
**Colors**: black, silver

**Good activities for this phase**:
- Setting new intentions
- Starting a new project
- Writing a wish list of new goals
- Inward reflection through meditation

**A new moon ritual**:
1. Light a candle in a quiet space
2. Write down the five things you want to accomplish this month
3. Close your eyes and visualize them as already achieved
4. Declare: "I am ready to receive [what you want]"

---

### 2. Waxing Crescent — Reinforcing intention

**Energy**: hope, anticipation, planning
**Good for**: turning your new-moon intentions into a concrete plan

---

### 3. First Quarter — Challenge and breakthrough

**Energy**: decisiveness, challenge, overcoming obstacles
**Good for**: taking active steps toward your goal, confronting obstacles head-on

---

### 4. Waxing Gibbous — Fine-tuning

**Energy**: patience, refinement, final preparation
**Good for**: reviewing and adjusting your plan, finishing the details

---

### 5. Full Moon — Harvest and culmination

**When**: the moon is fully round
**Energy**: completion, harvest, peak, gratitude
**Colors**: white, silver, gold

**The full moon's intense energy**:
- Emotions run higher (both joy and sadness feel bigger)
- Intuition and dreams intensify
- Considered the best time for crystal cleansing

**Good activities for this phase**:
- Expressing gratitude for what you've accomplished
- Cleansing crystals in the moonlight
- A moon bath (meditating by a window or on a balcony, bathed in moonlight)
- Writing down what you want to release, then burning the paper

**A full moon ritual**:
1. Light a white candle
2. Take time to feel gratitude for what you've achieved this month
3. Write down what you no longer need (an emotion, a situation, a relationship)
4. Safely burn the paper as an act of release
5. Declare: "I release [what you're letting go of]. I am free."

---

### 6. Waning Gibbous — Beginning to release

**Energy**: gratitude, sharing, the start of letting go
**Good for**: clearing out what's unnecessary, gratitude journaling

---

### 7. Last Quarter — Cleansing

**Energy**: forgiveness, liberation, purification
**Good for**: processing old emotions, forgiveness meditation, cleaning and clearing your space

---

### 8. Waning Crescent — Rest and preparation

**Energy**: rest, recharging, reflection
**Good for**: getting real rest, meditating, reflecting ahead of the next cycle

---
