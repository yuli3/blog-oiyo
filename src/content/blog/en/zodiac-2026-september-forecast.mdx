## Key Astro Events — September 2026

September is a highly unusual month with **both Venus and Mercury in retrograde simultaneously**. Venus retrograde calls for a review of relationships, beauty, and finances; Mercury retrograde demands caution around communication, contracts, and travel. Focus on **reviewing the past and reorganizing**, not on new beginnings.

| Event | Date | Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Venus retrograde begins | Early Sep–early Nov | Reassess relationships, finances & aesthetics |
| Mercury retrograde begins | ~Sep 9 | Caution with communication, contracts & travel |
| Autumn Equinox | Sep 22 | Sun enters Libra |
| Virgo → Libra transition | Sep 22 | Detail-oriented → balance & relationship energy |

<Callout type="warning">
**September Caution**: Venus + Mercury both in retrograde. Where possible, postpone new contracts, large purchases, and major relationship decisions until October. Review anything already in progress with extra care.
</Callout>

## Aries ♈ (Mar 21–Apr 19)

### Overall
**6th House (health, routine)** energy overlaps with retrograde energy. A good time to review health routines and reorganize long-standing habits. Focus on completing existing work rather than new starts.

### Love
Venus retrograde can bring an ex back into the picture. Rather than rushing to reconcile, use it as a chance to soberly assess why things ended.

### Money
Postpone large purchases or investments until October. A month to audit current spending patterns and clear unnecessary items.

### Career
Focus on wrapping up past projects and completing unfinished work. This is a month for endings, not beginnings.

## Taurus ♉ (Apr 20–May 20)

### Overall
**5th House (creativity, romance)** energy. Since Venus rules Taurus, the retrograde effect feels more pronounced. Reassessment is needed in romance and creative projects.

### Love
A month to reflect on what you truly want in a relationship. Check whether you're repeating old relationship patterns. Don't rush your emotions.

### Money
Avoid impulse buys and luxury spending. Approach financial decisions conservatively during the retrograde.

### Career
Focus on polishing and completing previous ideas in creative work. Revealing new ideas is better done after the retrograde ends.

## Gemini ♊ (May 21–Jun 20)

### Overall
Mercury rules Gemini, so the retrograde has a direct impact. Combined with **4th House (home, past)** energy, this is a month to revisit family relationships and home matters.

### Love
Old emotions or established relationship patterns may resurface. Look at how family dynamics affect your current love life.

### Money
Home-related contracts, moves, and renovations are safer after the retrograde ends. If already in progress, check all paperwork carefully.

### Career
Watch for communication errors and contract mistakes. Double-check emails and messages; read all contracts thoroughly before signing.

## Cancer ♋ (Jun 21–Jul 22)

### Overall
Jupiter in Cancer's luck energy continues, but Mercury retrograde affects **3rd House (communication, travel)**. Double-check important announcements and travel plans to prevent errors.

### Love
A single carelessly worded message can cause a misunderstanding. Face-to-face communication is best for emotional exchanges. Choose in-person over texts and social media.

### Money
Jupiter's financial luck holds, but be cautious with major investment decisions during the retrograde. Plan now, execute next month.

### Career
Mistakes are easy to make in presentations, contracts, and negotiation documents. Slow down, check carefully — that's the motto for this month.
