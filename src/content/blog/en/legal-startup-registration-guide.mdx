# Starting a Business: Structure, Registration & Tax

The business structure you choose affects your taxes, liability, funding options, and how much paperwork you face every year. There's no universal right answer — but there are clear tradeoffs.

---

## 1. Business Structure Comparison

| Structure | Liability | Tax | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sole Trader / Sole Proprietor** | Unlimited personal | Personal income tax on profits | Freelancers, side projects, low risk |
| **Partnership** | Unlimited (general) / Limited (LP) | Pass-through to partners | Professional services, small teams |
| **LLC (US) / LTD (UK)** | **Limited** | Pass-through (LLC) or corporate (UK LTD) | Most small-medium businesses |
| **S-Corporation (US)** | Limited | Pass-through + payroll tax savings | US businesses > ~$40K profit |
| **C-Corporation (US)** | Limited | Corporate tax + dividends | VC-backed startups, IPO track |
| **GmbH (Germany)** | Limited | Corporate tax ~30% | German operations |
| **SAS / SARL (France)** | Limited | Corporate IS or IR option | French businesses |

## 2. How to Register: Country by Country

### United States

| Structure | How to Register | Cost | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sole Proprietor | DBA filing with county/state (optional) | $0–$50 | 1–3 days |
| LLC | File Articles of Organization with state | $50–$500 | 1–4 weeks |
| Corporation (C-Corp) | File Articles of Incorporation | $100–$800 | 1–4 weeks |
| S-Corp election | File Form 2553 with IRS after incorporation | Free | 2–4 months |

<Callout type="tip">
**Delaware or your home state?** Delaware is preferred for VC-funded startups (investor-friendly law, Court of Chancery). For a Main Street business, your home state is usually simpler and cheaper. Wyoming and Nevada are popular for low-tax LLC registrations.
</Callout>

### United Kingdom

1. Register on **Companies House** (companies.gov.house.gov.uk) — £12 online, takes 24 hours
2. Register for Self Assessment (sole trader) or Corporation Tax (Ltd) with HMRC
3. Register for VAT if turnover exceeds **£90,000/year** (2024 threshold)

### Germany

1. Trade registration (Gewerbeanmeldung) at local Ordnungsamt — ~€30
2. Tax registration with Finanzamt (within 4 weeks of starting)
3. For GmbH: notarial deed, commercial register entry (~€500–1,500)

### France

1. Register via **Guichet unique** (formalites.entreprises.gouv.fr) — one-stop portal
2. Auto-entrepreneur (micro-enterprise): simplest, online in 24–48 hours
3. SAS/SARL: notarial deed or online at Infogreffe (~€300–600)

## 3. Tax Obligations at Startup

### Year 1 Essentials

| Obligation | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Register for income/corporate tax | At registration | Done automatically in most countries |
| Register for sales tax / VAT | At threshold | US: state-by-state; EU: country-by-country |
| Payroll tax / social contributions | On first paycheck | PAYE (UK), FICA (US), URSSAF (France) |
| Business bank account | Immediately | Keep strictly separate from personal |
| Bookkeeping | From day 1 | Even simple spreadsheets help |

<Callout type="warning">
**Mixing personal and business funds is a critical mistake.** In the US, co-mingling can "pierce the corporate veil" — meaning courts can hold you personally liable for business debts despite having an LLC. Always open a dedicated business account before making any business transactions.
</Callout>

## 4. Common Licenses and Permits

Not every business needs special licenses, but many do. Check before you open.

| Business Type | Typical License / Permit |
|---|---|
| Food service / restaurant | Food handler's permit, health inspection |
| Retail (alcohol) | State/national alcohol license |
| Professional services (legal, medical) | Professional board license |
| Financial services | FCA (UK), SEC/FINRA (US), BaFin (Germany) |
| Online retail (EU) | Consumer terms of service, GDPR compliance |
| Daycare / childcare | Local authority / state registration |

## 6. One-Person Business: Sole Trader vs LLC

For solopreneurs, the choice usually comes down to liability vs simplicity.

| Factor | Sole Trader | LLC/Ltd |
|---|---|---|
| **Setup** | Instant, nearly free | Few days, small fee |
| **Tax filing** | Schedule C (US) / Self Assessment (UK) | Separate corporate return |
| **Liability** | Your personal assets at risk | Limited to business assets |
| **Perception** | Less professional | More credible with clients |
| **Recommended when** | Low-risk consulting, testing an idea | Any ongoing business |
