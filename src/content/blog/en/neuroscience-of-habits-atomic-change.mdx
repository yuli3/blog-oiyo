## Don't Trust Willpower

The brain fundamentally wants to save energy (is lazy). To perform a new action, the frontal lobe uses a lot of energy, so the brain resists. But if this is repeated and stored in the **'Basal Ganglia'**, it is performed automatically without willpower. This is a habit.

## 4 Laws of Habit Design

- **Law 1 — make it obvious:** Make it obvious. (Put workout clothes by the bed)
- **Law 2 — make it attractive:** Bundle it with something attractive. (Listen to podcasts while working out - Temptation Bundling)
- **Law 3 — make it easy:** Make it easy. (Not 100 pushups, but just 1)
- **Law 4 — make it satisfying:** Give immediate satisfaction. (Check V on habit checklist)
