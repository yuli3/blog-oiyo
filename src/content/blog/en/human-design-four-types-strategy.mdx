# Human Design: Your Own Management Manual for Energy

**Human Design** is a unique self-awareness system created by synthesizing astrology, I Ching, Chakras, Kabbalah, and modern physics. Its core is understanding the 'energy blueprint' granted to us at birth and living accordingly.

Many people live against their true nature to fit the criteria of the world. Human Design proposes an **'Innate Strategy'** that you don't have to struggle by force to follow.

---

## I. Four Types of Human Design

1.  **Manifestor (Initiator)**: Approx. 9%.
    - **Characteristics**: Pioneers with a powerful driving force to start and inform something themselves.
    - **Strategy**: **'To Inform.'** They must reduce resistance by informing those around them before acting.
2.  **Generator (Builder)**: Approx. 70% (including Manifesting Generators).
    - **Characteristics**: Have infinite life energy and shine when doing work they love.
    - **Strategy**: **'To Respond.'** Rather than starting themselves, they should wait to see if their inner self responds "YES" to questions or opportunities the world throws at them.
3.  **Projector (Guide)**: Approx. 20%.
    - **Characteristics**: Innate guides and managers who read others' energy and suggest directions.
    - **Strategy**: **'Wait for the Invitation.'** Their talents are properly displayed only when there is a request from a place that recognizes their abilities.
4.  **Reflector (Mirror)**: Approx. 1%.
    - **Characteristics**: Mirror-like beings who reflect the surrounding energy exactly. They show the health of a society.
    - **Strategy**: **'Wait for the Cycle of the Moon.'** They must observe for a month (28 days) before making important decisions.

---

## II. Signatures and Not-Self

There are indicators to know if you are living according to your design:

- **Satisfaction vs. Frustration (Generator)**: Satisfaction comes when doing work you love; frustration comes when forcing it.
- **Success vs. Bitterness (Projector)**: Success is felt when acting upon invitation; bitterness (being ignored) is felt when forcibly intervening.
- **Peace vs. Anger (Manifestor)**: Peace comes when informing and acting; anger rises when working while being interrupted.
- **Surprise vs. Disappointment (Reflector)**: Surprise comes when discovering the wonders of the world; disappointment comes when engulfed in murky energy.

---

## III. Why is Human Design Important?

Life is the sum of numerous **'decisions.'** We try to decide with the head (logic), but the head is usually obsessed with social fears and desires. Human Design teaches us to trust the wisdom of the body, the **'Authority.'**

Waiting for an emotional wave to pass or trusting the gut feeling below the navel. When following the method suggested by your design, life's resistance disappears and life begins to flow by itself.

## Conclusion: You are Designed Differently from Anyone Else

We live in a world where we learn that we succeed only if we act like Manifestors. However, 90% of people are designed to discover true treasures at the end of waiting (response or invitation).

The reason you are currently struggling might be that you are wearing clothes that don't fit your design, rather than being lacking. Check your type today and experiment with the corresponding strategy just once. The perfect flow prepared by the universe for you is waiting.
