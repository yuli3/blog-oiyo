If there's a personal color type that looks like it was dipped in sunlight and handed back to the world, it's **Spring Warm Vivid**. Clear, warm, and brilliantly alive — this is the palette of coral reefs and California poppies, of a morning that promises heat and light without restraint. People with this coloring carry their best color as an internal glow that warm, vivid tones only amplify.

## What Is Spring Warm Vivid?

The Spring Warm Vivid type sits at the intersection of **warm undertone** (yellow-gold in the skin) and **high contrast + high clarity**. Unlike the softer Spring subtypes, Spring Vivid can handle colors with full saturation — bright, clear, luminous tones that would overwhelm softer palettes but on this type create a look of natural radiance.

**Characteristic features:**
- Skin: warm peach, golden, or warm ivory with yellow undertone and natural luminosity
- Eyes: bright amber, warm brown, golden hazel, or clear olive-brown
- Hair: natural warm blonde, copper-red, golden brown, or honey highlights
- Overall impression: a person who looks naturally bright, as if lit from within

**The key**: clarity. Spring Vivid is distinguished from Spring Mute by the absence of dusty or muted quality — the colors are clean and bright, not softened.

## Your Signature Colors

### Power Colors

**Coral Red** — the signature shade for Spring Vivid. Not the blue-toned red of winter, not the earthy red of autumn, but a warm, clear red with orange underlying it. This color creates instant radiance on Spring Vivid skin.

**Bright Orange** — bold and warm. A classic Spring color at its most vivid. Orange eyeshadow, an orange dress, an orange bag — these all work when your undertone is warm and your overall coloring is clear.

**Golden Yellow** — sunflower, dandelion, marigold. Not the muted mustard of autumn, but clear, bright yellow with warmth.

**Warm Lime Green** — yellow-green rather than blue-green. A color that surprises Spring Vivid wearers with how well it works when they try it for the first time.

**Peach** — the universal Spring Vivid color. Every shade of peach, from pale champagne to deep coral-peach, is flattering and natural on this type.

**Warm Turquoise** — the blue that contains enough yellow to warm it slightly. Clear, bright, and tropical.

### Neutrals

- **Warm Camel** — not dark brown, but a light, golden tan
- **Ivory** — never stark white; the yellow-toned off-white is softer and more natural
- **Warm Nude** — your version of nude is the golden-peach, not the pink-beige

### Colors to Approach Carefully

- **Black** — can work in small amounts (a black belt, black shoes) but a full black outfit can drain warmth from Spring Vivid skin; try dark warm brown or navy instead
- **Cool pinks** (fuchsia, hot pink with blue) — these pull against the yellow undertone
- **Dusty or muted colors** — these are the territory of Spring Mute; they make Spring Vivid appear tired

## Makeup

### Foundation and Base
Look for foundations with **warm, peach, or golden undertones**. Beige-neutral or pink-neutral foundations will look mismatched. The right shade has a warmth that makes skin glow rather than appear flat.

**Setting powder**: lightly warm-toned translucent; avoid stark white powder which creates an ashy effect.

### Eyes

**Eyeshadow**: Your strongest territory. Warm, bright tones are extraordinary — coral, copper, peach, champagne gold, warm terracotta, golden bronze. Eye looks that would appear theatrical on cooler or darker types read as natural and radiant on Spring Vivid.

**Eyeliner**: Warm brown is more natural than black for everyday. For drama, dark warm brown or warm navy. Gold or copper liner for creative looks.

**Mascara**: Brown-black to warm black; straight black also works and creates a beautiful contrast.

### Cheeks

Peach and coral blush are the signature Spring Vivid colors — they blend seamlessly with the natural warmth of the skin and create a glow that looks genuinely alive. Avoid cool pinks or deep berries.

**Highlighter**: Champagne gold, peachy pearl, warm bronze. Any highlight with warmth or yellow-gold reflects beautifully.

### Lips

- **Coral red** — the single most flattering lip color for this type; brightens the entire face
- **Peach-pink** — natural and radiant
- **Warm orange-red** — bold but flattering
- **Clear glosses** — over a warm nude base, these extend the natural-luminous look
- **Avoid**: deep wine, cool berry, dark plum — these pull the warmth from the face

## Fashion and Wardrobe

### Building the Spring Vivid Wardrobe

Spring Vivid has arguably the most obviously *alive* palette — in the right colors, everything is amplified. The wardrobe should reflect this: warm, bright, clear, and joyful.

**Core wardrobe colors:**
- Coral (in multiple shades, from pale to saturated)
- Peach (the most versatile neutral for this type)
- Warm camel (replacing traditional beige)
- Clear warm turquoise
- Ivory (replacing white)
- Warm green (yellow-green rather than blue-green)
- Golden yellow

**Pattern notes**: Spring Vivid can wear pattern beautifully — the type handles color saturation well. Florals in warm tones, geometric prints in vivid warm colors, tropical prints.

**Metal jewelry**: Gold is the clear winner — yellow gold, rose gold, copper. Silver can work in the right context but is less natural with Spring Vivid coloring.

## Hair Color

Natural Spring Vivid hair often has warm highlights that appear in sunlight. Supporting this:

**Most flattering:**
- Golden blonde
- Warm honey blonde
- Copper highlights
- Strawberry blonde
- Caramel highlights in brown hair

**Approach carefully:**
- Cool ash tones (blonde or brown) — pull against the warm undertone
- Very dark, blue-toned black — creates contrast that overwhelms rather than illuminates

- **Jessica Alba** — warm golden skin, clear features, naturally coral-compatible
