## Conspicuous Consumption

Why do people buy a $500,000 watch that tells time worse than a $10 Casio?
Because the point is not to tell time. The point is to tell people _you have $500,000_.

These are **Veblen Goods**. Their value comes _from_ their high price. If the price dropped, people would stop buying them because they would no longer signal status.

## The Status Game

We are social primates obsessed with hierarchy. Veblen goods act as costly signals of fitness. We waste resources to prove we have resources to waste.
Ask yourself: Are you buying this because you like it, or because you want others to know you can afford it?
