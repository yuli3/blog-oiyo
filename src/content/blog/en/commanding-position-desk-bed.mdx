## Commanding Position

The first rule of Feng Shui interior is "Be in a position to control the door". The door is the entrance for energy and potential danger (intruders).

## Bad Layout vs. Good Layout

- **Poor placement:** Sitting with back to the door, or right in front of the door. (Causes psychological anxiety)
- **Good placement:** Position where the door is visible diagonally and there is a solid wall behind the back. (Psychological stability and widest view)

If structured unavoidably, use a mirror to see behind you.
