Moving in Japan involves procedures that are quite different from other countries — especially the initial costs (shikkin, reikin, agent fees) that can add up to 4–6 months' rent before you even move in. This guide covers everything an English speaker or expat needs to know.

---

## 1. Before the Move — Japanese Rental Contracts

### Understanding Japanese Rental Costs
Japanese rentals are known for high upfront costs:

| Item | Explanation | Typical Amount |
|---|---|---|
| 敷金 (Shikkin) | Security deposit — refundable (usually) | 1–2 months' rent |
| 礼金 (Reikin) | "Key money" / gift to landlord — non-refundable | 1–2 months' rent |
| 仲介手数料 | Real estate agent fee | 1 month's rent + consumption tax |
| 前家賃 (Maeyachin) | First month's rent in advance | 1 month's rent |
| 火災保険 (Fire Insurance) | Required by most landlords | ¥10,000–¥20,000/year |

> **Tip**: Properties advertised as "礼金ゼロ (reikin zero)" skip the key money — these are increasingly common. Budget 4–6 months' rent total for move-in costs.

### Guarantors and Guarantee Companies
- Most landlords require a **guarantor (連帯保証人)** or approval from a **guarantee company (保証会社)**
- As a foreigner, finding a personal guarantor is often difficult
- Guarantee companies (e.g., JID, 日本セーフティー) accept foreigners — expect an upfront fee of 30–50% of one month's rent

### Renting as a Foreigner
- You must present your **Residence Card (在留カード)** at signing
- Short residency permits may result in rejection — try apartments marketed as 外国人OK (foreigner-friendly)
- Useful platforms: [SUUMO](https://suumo.jp), [Goodroom](https://www.goodrooms.jp), [Sakura House](https://www.sakura-house.com)

## 2. Preparing for the Move

### Choosing a Moving Company
- March (fiscal year-end) and April (new year) are **peak season** — prices can double or triple
- Major movers: アート引越センター (Art Moving), サカイ引越センター (Sakai), 日本通運 (Nippon Express)
- **単身パック (Tanshim Pack)**: Budget option for solo/studio moves — priced by container size
- Large item disposal: 大型ゴミ (large garbage) requires a paid disposal sticker from your local municipality — call the ward office or check their website

### Utilities and Services to Cancel/Transfer
- **Electricity**: Contact your power company (e.g., Tokyo Electric Power) online or by phone
- **Gas**: Cancel at old address + schedule new activation (a technician visit is required for turn-on)
- **Internet**: Cancel, then re-apply at new address — allow 2–4 weeks for installation
- **NHK fee**: Update address via [NHK's official portal](https://www.nhk.or.jp/otasuke)

## 3. Before Moving Day — Tenshutsu届 (Deregistration)

### Filing 転出届 (Tenshutsu-todoke) at Your Old Municipality
- Submit at the ward/city office (市区町村 役所) of your **current address**
- When to file: Up to 14 days before your move, or on moving day itself
- Documents needed: ID (Residence Card, passport), My Number Card if applicable
- You will receive a **転出証明書 (Tenshutsu Certificate)** — keep it for the move-in registration

> **My Number Card holders**: You can submit your tenshutsu online via [マイナポータル (MynaPortal)](https://myna.go.jp)

---

## 4. Moving Day — On-Site Checks

### At Your Old Address
- [ ] Full walkthrough — photograph walls, floors, and any existing damage
- [ ] Photograph gas and electric meter readings for final billing
- [ ] Return keys, mailbox key, and any access cards
- [ ] Confirm outdoor A/C unit piping is properly detached (requires a specialist)
- [ ] Confirm all oversized garbage has been properly disposed of

### At Your New Address
- [ ] Check all fixtures, closets, bathroom, windows — document pre-existing damage on the move-in inspection form
- [ ] Sign the move-in checklist and share a copy with your landlord
- [ ] Be present for **gas activation** (technician will visit — someone must be home)
- [ ] Confirm internet installation appointment

## 5. After Moving — Required Admin Tasks

### ① 転入届 (Tennyu-todoke) — Most Important
- File at the ward/city office of your **new address**
- **Deadline: Within 14 days of moving** (fines of up to ¥50,000 for late filing)
- Documents needed: 転出証明書 (from old municipality), Residence Card, My Number Card
- Your Residence Card address will also be updated at this same window

### ② Residence Card Address Update
- Done simultaneously with 転入届 — the clerk updates your card on the spot
- Same 14-day deadline applies

### ③ Driver's License Address Update
- Visit your nearest **police station (警察署)** or **Driver's License Center (運転免許センター)**
- Bring: Driver's license + 住民票 (Juminhyo / resident certificate) or updated Residence Card

### ④ My Number Card Address Update
- Handled at the ward office when you file 転入届 — no extra documents needed

### ⑤ Bank and Postal Mail
- Update address with each bank (via app or branch visit)
- **Japan Post mail forwarding**: [ゆうびんポータル](https://www.post.japanpost.jp/service/tenkyo) → forward mail to new address for up to 12 months

### ⑥ National Health Insurance
- Often updated automatically with 転入届, but confirm directly
- If you're covered by company health insurance (社保), notify your HR/admin team separately

### ⑦ Vehicle Registration
- Visit the **Land Transport Office (陸運局)** in your new address's jurisdiction
- **Deadline: Within 15 days**
- New garage certificate (車庫証明) may be required in some areas

## 6. Japan-Specific Notes

### 原状回復 (Genjo Kaifuku — Restoring Original Condition)
- When you leave, you're expected to return the apartment to its original state
- Normal wear and tear from aging is the **landlord's responsibility**
- Damage caused by your negligence is **your responsibility**
- Official guidelines: [Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism](https://www.mlit.go.jp/jutakukentiku/house/jutakukentiku_house_tk3_000020.html)

### Neighbor Greeting (挨拶 / Aisatsu)
- It's customary in Japan to visit immediate neighbors (above, below, left, right) on or shortly after moving day
- Bring a small gift — ¥500–¥1,000 value (cookies, tea, hand towels) is standard

### Garbage Rules
- Each municipality has its own strict sorting and collection schedule
- Get the local garbage guide from your ward office or ask your building manager immediately
