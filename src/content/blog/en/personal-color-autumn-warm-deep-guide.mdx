**Autumn Warm Deep** is the palette of the autumn forest at its most saturated moment — the hour when the light is low and every color deepens into something almost unreal. Deep burgundy leaves, forest-dark evergreens, the orange of embers, the warm near-black of late twilight in November. This type can carry depth and richness that lighter types simply cannot — and the result, when the colors are right, is a presence that fills a room without trying.

## What Is Autumn Warm Deep?

Autumn Warm Deep combines **warm undertone** (golden or olive in the skin) with **deep, rich coloring and higher contrast**. This is the darkest of the autumn types — the person with darker skin or deeper features that can support and in fact require rich, dark, warm tones.

**Characteristic features:**
- Skin: warm olive, deep warm brown, golden tan, or warm medium-dark complexion
- Eyes: dark warm brown, deep amber, or very dark hazel
- Hair: dark warm brown, dark auburn, near-black with warm tones, or very dark chestnut
- Overall impression: rich, warm, dramatic; the deep coloring gives this type a natural authority

**The key**: richness and warmth together. Autumn Deep colors are warm AND deep — they have both the yellow-gold undertone and the depth that lighter warm types cannot support.

## Your Signature Colors

### Power Colors

**Deep Burgundy** — not the cool wine of Winter, but a warm, brown-red burgundy. Dark red with orange undertone. The color of dark wine held up to firelight. This is perhaps the single most dramatic and flattering color for Autumn Deep.

**Forest Green** — deep, dark green with warmth; the green of old growth evergreens, of deep shade, of velvet moss. Not the vivid bright green of spring or the cool blue-green of winter — this is the warm, deep, earthy green.

**Burnt Orange** — the orange of turning maple leaves at their peak saturation; deeper and richer than the vivid orange of Spring Vivid, with more depth and warmth.

**Warm Chocolate Brown** — deep warm brown; the color of dark chocolate or strong coffee. One of the most elegant and versatile deep colors for this type.

**Dark Olive** — the darkest, richest version of olive; almost brown-green.

**Warm Copper** — rich, metallic orange-red; a jewel-tone adjacent color that this type carries beautifully.

### Neutrals

- **Warm Black** — not pure blue-black but a slightly warm, dark near-black
- **Dark Chocolate** — the warm brown that replaces black
- **Rich Camel** — a deeper, more saturated version of camel than Autumn Mute uses

### Colors to Approach Carefully

- **Cool colors** — as with all autumn types, cool undertones fight the warm skin
- **Pale pastels** — they disappear against the deep coloring; this type needs depth
- **High-contrast cool colors** (pure white with black) — the cool quality works against the warm undertone

## Makeup

### Foundation and Base
**Warm, deep-toned foundations** — golden, olive, or warm brown undertones at medium to full coverage. Autumn Deep skin typically has enough depth that light coverage foundations look too sheer. The right foundation tone is definitively warm, not neutral.

### Eyes

**Eyeshadow**: The full depth of warm autumn tones — deep terracotta, warm brown, burgundy, bronze, rich gold, warm copper, deep olive. The Autumn Deep eye is particularly stunning with deep, warm, smoky looks in earthy rich tones.

**Eyeliner**: Dark warm brown, warm black, deep bronze, or rich burgundy liner. The eye definition options are extraordinary with this depth of feature.

**Mascara**: Dark brown-black or warm black.

### Cheeks

Rich warm apricot, deep peach-bronze, or warm terracotta blush. This type can support more pigmented blush than lighter types — the depth of skin absorbs color beautifully. Deep plum-rose bronzer also works as a warming contour.

### Lips

- **Deep burgundy** — the Autumn Deep signature lip; dramatic, warm, sophisticated
- **Warm brick red** — orange-brown-red; earthy and deeply flattering
- **Burnt orange-red** — bold but natural on warm deep skin
- **Warm copper** — a metallic option for special occasions
- **Avoid**: cool berry, cool pink, blue-red, fuchsia

## Fashion and Wardrobe

### Building the Autumn Warm Deep Wardrobe

The Autumn Deep wardrobe is built on richness and warmth at full depth. The overall effect should be opulent, natural, and authoritative.

**Core wardrobe colors:**
- Deep burgundy
- Forest green
- Burnt orange
- Warm chocolate brown
- Dark olive
- Warm copper
- Rich camel
- Warm near-black
- Deep warm red
- Cognac / whiskey brown

**The Autumn Deep capsule wardrobe** has a jewel-tone richness that makes it extraordinary in autumn light — these colors were made for the long shadows and golden quality of afternoon in November.

**Pattern notes**: Rich prints, warm earth-tone florals at full saturation, animal prints (leopard, warm snake), ethnic textiles with warm palette. This type can carry bold, large-scale prints in warm tones.

**Metal jewelry**: Gold — rich, warm, possibly antique or hammered gold. Copper and bronze are extraordinarily beautiful on this type. Rose gold works well.

**Fabric suggestions**: Rich, textured fabrics — velvet, suede, leather, brocade, cashmere, tweed in warm tones. These textures enhance the richness of the palette.

## Hair Color

**Most flattering:**
- Natural warm dark brown
- Rich auburn
- Deep chestnut
- Dark warm mahogany
- Near-black with warm undertone

**Approach carefully:**
- Cool black (adds a blue-black sheen that fights the warm undertone)
- Platinum or light blonde — creates high cool contrast against deep warm features
- Vivid fashion colors in cool tones

Autumn Warm Deep is the palette of maturity and depth — not in terms of age, but in terms of *presence*. These are the colors that command attention without demanding it, that fill a room with warmth simply by being present in it.

The person who discovers they are Autumn Deep often has a particular moment: trying on a deep burgundy or a forest green for the first time and seeing how it not only doesn't overwhelm their features but somehow brings them into sharper, richer focus. The color doesn't cover anything. It reveals everything.

This is the promise of personal color done correctly: not transformation but revelation. Not adding something foreign but finding the colors that make you most completely, undeniably yourself.
