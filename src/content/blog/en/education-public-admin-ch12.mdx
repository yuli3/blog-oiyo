# Chapter 12: Budget Systems and Processes

Budget systems have evolved to reflect the values (control, management, planning, reduction, etc.) that the government pursues in response to environmental changes.

---

## 1. Trends in Budget System Reform

| System                      | Focus      | Features                             | Weakness                           |
| :-------------------------- | :--------- | :----------------------------------- | :--------------------------------- |
| **Line-Item (LIBS)**        | Control    | Limits on each expense item          | Difficult to grasp results, rigid  |
| **Performance-Based (PBS)** | Management | Unit cost × Workload                 | Difficulty in measuring workload   |
| **Program Planning (PPBS)** | Planning   | Linkage of long-term plans & budget  | Centralization of decision-making  |
| **Zero-Based (ZBB)**        | Reduction  | Ignore previous budgets (Start at 0) | Excessive time and effort required |
| **New Performance (OBB)**   | Outcomes   | Emphasis on results (outcomes)       | Linked to national fiscal plans    |

---

## 2. The Budget Cycle

1. **Budget Formulation (Government)**: Departments submit requests, and the Ministry of Finance adjusts and confirms the draft.
2. **Budget Deliberation (Legislature)**: Policy speech $\rightarrow$ Committee review $\rightarrow$ Special committee review $\rightarrow$ Floor vote.
3. **Budget Execution (Government)**: Allocation and re-allocation of funds. Specific mechanisms (Transfers, shifts, etc.) are used to maintain flexibility.
4. **Closing and Audit**: Post-audit of the legality of the execution. Performed by the Board of Audit and Inspection.

---

## 3. Flexibility Mechanisms in Execution

- **Transfer (利用)**: Shifting funds between legislative categories (Chapters/Sections). **Requires Legislative Approval**.
- **Shift (轉用)**: Shifting funds between administrative categories (Sub-sections/Items). **Possible with Government Approval**.
- **Reserve Fund**: Funds sets aside in advance to cover unpredictable expenditures.

---

## Key Checklist

- [ ] Which budget system re-examines the priorities of all projects without recognizing the previous year's budget as a vested right? (Answer: Zero-Based Budgeting, ZBB)
- [ ] What system involves shifting funds between legislative categories, requiring parliamentary approval? (Answer: Transfer)
- [ ] Which budget system aims for optimal resource allocation by linking planning and budgeting at the formulation stage? (Answer: Program Planning Budgeting System, PPBS)
