# The Moon Doesn't Change — the Side We See Does

The Moon doesn't produce its own light; it reflects sunlight. The shape we see isn't the Moon itself changing — it's the **relative positions of the Sun, Earth, and Moon** shifting, which changes which side of the Moon is lit.

## Why Don't Solar and Lunar Eclipses Happen Every Month?

A full moon rises every month, and a new moon passes every month too. Yet solar and lunar eclipses don't happen every month. That's because the Moon's orbit is tilted slightly relative to Earth's orbital plane, so the Sun, Earth, and Moon rarely line up in a perfectly straight line.
