If Summer Cool Light is the soft morning, **Summer Cool Mute** is the silver afternoon — more depth, more sophistication, the same cool undertone with a greyish veil that transforms every color into something understated and distinctly refined. This is the palette of dusty rose, of faded lavender, of the grey that is not grey but the ghost of every color at once. It is the most quietly sophisticated of all sixteen types.

## What Is Summer Cool Mute?

Summer Cool Mute combines **cool undertone** (pink or blue-rose in the skin) with **low saturation and medium contrast**. Unlike Summer Cool Light (pale, delicate, airy), Summer Cool Mute has a slightly deeper, greyer quality — the skin may have a more noticeable grey-rose or grey-beige tone, and the overall coloring has more depth with the same cool foundation.

**Characteristic features:**
- Skin: grey-rose, grey-beige, or cool ivory with slight ashy quality; natural, understated tone
- Eyes: grey, grey-blue, grey-green, cool hazel, or cool light brown; often with a veiled, slightly smoky quality
- Hair: ash brown, dark ash blonde, grey-brown, or silver-grey
- Overall impression: sophisticated, slightly cool and mysterious; features that are clear but not dramatically contrasted

**The key**: the grey veil. Summer Mute colors all have a slight greyish quality that makes them softer and more sophisticated than their clear equivalents. This greyness is not flatness — it is the specific quality that makes these colors look luxurious.

## Your Signature Colors

### Power Colors

**Dusty Rose** — the quintessential Summer Mute color. Not hot pink, not pastel pink, not warm blush, but the slightly grey, slightly dusty rose that is this type's most distinctive territory. Macarons and vintage wallpaper and the inside of a seashell.

**Muted Lavender** — greyed purple; slightly deeper and more sophisticated than Summer Light lavender. This color is unusually flattering on Summer Mute and looks genuinely distinctive.

**Grey-Blue** — the blue that contains grey; slate, steel, dove. These blue-greys are perhaps the most sophisticated neutrals in any personal color system, and they belong most naturally to Summer Mute.

**Mocha Rose** — a deeper, slightly rosewood-toned neutral that sits between brown and pink with cool undertone. Deeply flattering and versatile.

**Cool Periwinkle** — the blue-violet that is not quite blue and not quite purple; distinctly Summer Mute territory.

**Faded Teal** — teal with enough grey to mute it; the sophisticated version of turquoise.

### Neutrals

- **Greige (cool)** — the Summer Mute version of beige is always grey-beige, never warm
- **Soft White** — or cool off-white; warmer whites look discordant
- **Medium Grey** — a universally flattering neutral for this type

### Colors to Approach Carefully

- **Any warm color** — the cool undertone requirement means warm tones (orange, warm yellow, warm brown) will always fight the skin
- **High-saturation vivid colors** — these overwhelm the muted palette
- **Very light, barely-there colors** — Summer Mute has more depth than Summer Light; some of the palest, most delicate colors lack the depth to show up against this type's features

## Makeup

### Foundation and Base
**Cool-toned foundations** with neutral to pink-beige undertone. The grey-rose quality of Summer Mute skin makes it particularly sensitive to warm foundations, which can look orange or sallow. Look for descriptors like "cool," "rosy," "porcelain," or "neutral."

### Eyes

**Eyeshadow**: The Summer Mute eye palette is extraordinarily sophisticated — cool grey, dusty mauve, smoky taupe, muted lavender, grey-brown, soft navy. This is the type that can wear a grey-lavender smoky eye in the daytime and have it look entirely natural.

**Eyeliner**: Cool grey and soft navy are the signature choices. A grey liner creates definition without the harsh contrast of black. Dark navy is elegant and cool.

**Mascara**: Black or dark brown-black. The slightly smoky quality of Summer Mute features means mascara creates beautiful, naturally dramatic eyes.

### Cheeks

Dusty rose and soft mauve-pink blush are the Summer Mute signature. These muted, cool-pink shades blend seamlessly with the skin's natural undertone and create a sophisticated rather than girlish flush.

### Lips

- **Dusty rose** — the perfect Summer Mute lip; natural, sophisticated, effortlessly chic
- **Cool mauve** — deeper and more defined; ideal for evenings or when more intensity is wanted
- **Rose-brown nude** — a deeper nude with cool undertone; very sophisticated
- **Muted berry** — cool, slightly dusty berry; a low-key dramatic option
- **Avoid**: warm nudes (too orange), coral, warm red (looks discordant against cool skin)

## Fashion and Wardrobe

### Building the Summer Cool Mute Wardrobe

The Summer Mute wardrobe is built on cool sophistication. The overall effect should feel refined, effortless, and quietly distinctive.

**Core wardrobe colors:**
- Dusty rose (the anchor color)
- Muted lavender
- Grey-blue / slate
- Mocha rose
- Cool periwinkle
- Faded teal
- Cool greige
- Medium grey
- Soft cool white
- Deep dusty mauve (for evening)

**The Summer Mute capsule wardrobe** is one of the most luxurious-looking in personal color — everything coordinates naturally because all the colors share the grey undertone, and the overall effect is of effortless European sophistication.

**Pattern notes**: Fine stripes in grey-tones, soft abstract prints in muted cool colors, simple elegant patterns. Summer Mute is often best served by clean silhouettes in great colors rather than complex patterns.

**Metal jewelry**: Silver is the natural choice. The grey quality of silver complements the grey-cool palette perfectly. White gold, cool platinum. Rose gold can work in some combinations.

## Hair Color

**Most flattering:**
- Ash brown (the natural color for many Summer Mutes)
- Dark ash blonde
- Natural grey or silver-grey
- Light ash brown with cool highlights

**Approach carefully:**
- Warm golden highlights — the cool undertone makes warm hair color look slightly incongruous
- Very dark, blue-black — can create too much contrast against soft Summer Mute skin

---

Summer Cool Mute is the palette of restraint elevated to art. These are the colors that don't try to be beautiful — they simply are, in the way that a well-tailored grey coat or a room with honest natural light is beautiful. The sophistication is structural, not applied.

When you wear your colors, people will rarely say "what a beautiful dress." They will say "you look amazing today" — because the effect is of the person being remarkable, not the garment. That is the peculiar power of the right personal color: it doesn't draw attention to itself. It draws attention to you.
