## Will AI Bring a New "Electricity Moment"?

The steam engine in the 19th century. Electricity and computers in the 20th. Technological revolutions have historically driven explosive productivity gains and economic growth. Could AI be the next wave?

Optimists say yes — that AI could raise the productivity of knowledge work tenfold, even a hundredfold. But history shows that technological revolutions typically take decades to show up in the economic data.

### The Productivity Paradox

A similar debate played out during the PC and internet revolution of the 1990s. Nobel laureate Robert Solow famously said that you could see the computer age everywhere except in the productivity statistics. That's the "productivity paradox."

AI may well follow a similar path.
- **Short term:** rising AI investment, explosive growth for related companies
- **Medium term:** businesses adopting and adapting to AI
- **Long term:** effects actually showing up in the productivity data

### What It Would Take for AI to Be the Savior

For AI's economic impact to show up across the whole economy, three things need to happen.
1. Real AI adoption by businesses (already underway)
2. Labor market restructuring and the creation of new roles
3. Buildout of AI infrastructure (power, data centers)

The long-term picture is positive, but short-term investors should be wary of excessive expectations.
