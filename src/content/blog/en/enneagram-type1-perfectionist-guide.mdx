Enneagram Type 1 is known as **The Reformer** or **The Perfectionist**. They believe the world can be better and feel compelled to lead that change themselves. Deep in their core sits an intense conviction: *"I must live rightly."*

## Type 1 Core Profile

| Attribute | Description |
|------|------|
| Type Name | The Reformer / The Perfectionist |
| Basic Desire | Goodness, integrity, perfection |
| Basic Fear | Being corrupt, evil, or defective |
| Core Motivation | Making the world better; upholding principles |
| Center | Instinctive (Gut) Center |
| Worldview | "There is right and wrong in the world, and I must stand on the right side" |

## Personality Traits

### Healthy Type 1 (Levels 1–3)

Healthy Ones are among the most principled and courageous reformers in the world.

- **Ethical courage**: Confronts injustice fearlessly. Many social activists and whistleblowers are healthy Type 1s.
- **Mature self-acceptance**: Acknowledges their imperfections and can let go of the drive for perfection.
- **Objective critique**: Offers constructive criticism of systems and situations without personal attacks.
- **Balance of principle and flexibility**: Holds to principles while applying them flexibly to context.

### Average Type 1 (Levels 4–6)

Where most Type 1s spend their time.

- Applies high standards to self and others; becomes critical when standards aren't met.
- Constantly battles an inner voice ("inner critic") saying "you should" or "you shouldn't."
- Struggles to express anger directly — repressed anger leaks out as chronic resentment.
- Strong sense of responsibility makes them feel they must handle everything personally.

### Unhealthy Type 1 (Levels 7–9)

- Treats their own moral code as absolute truth and sits in judgment of others.
- Extreme perfectionism leads to paralysis — unable to decide or even start.
- Focuses only on others' faults to maintain their own self-image of righteousness.

<Callout type="info">
**Enneagram Health Levels**: On a scale of 1–9, levels 1–3 are healthy and 7–9 are unhealthy. The same type can look completely different depending on an individual's level of growth.
</Callout>

## Wing Types

### 9w1 (Nine Wing)

- Quieter and more introverted
- Idealistic but less outwardly assertive
- More hidden internal conflict
- Combines the pursuit of harmony and peace

### 2w1 (Two Wing)

- More extroverted and other-focused
- Wants to help others while staying principled
- Enhanced qualities of educator, counselor, and leader
- Emotionally warmer

## Stress & Security Directions

| State | Direction | Characteristics |
|------|------|------|
| Under Stress | → Type 4 (Individualist) | Becomes melancholy, emotionally volatile, self-critical |
| Feeling Secure | → Type 7 (Enthusiast) | More humorous, spontaneous, pleasure-seeking |

<Callout type="tip">
**Growth Point**: Real growth for Type 1 happens when they move toward Type 7 — allowing joy, spontaneity, and fun into their lives.
</Callout>

## Relationship Patterns

### Relationship Strengths

- Loyal and reliable partners
- Continuously work to improve the relationship
- Their principled nature builds deep trust

### Relationship Challenges

- Frequently points out a partner's flaws ("I'm saying this because I want the best for you...")
- Uncomfortable with spontaneity and imperfection
- Tends to evaluate relationships through logic and reason rather than emotion

### Ideal Partner

Someone who respects Type 1's principles while helping them laugh and lighten up. A partner who receives critique as care for growth — and shows them it's okay to be loved even when imperfect.

## Growth Guide for Type 1

### Practices to Start Today

1. **Practice "Good enough"**: A finished thing beats a perfect one.
2. **Be kind to yourself**: Tell your inner critic, "You're doing well enough."
3. **Allow yourself pleasure**: Set the rulebook aside and experience spontaneous joy.
4. **Acknowledge your anger**: Anger isn't a bad emotion. Recognize it and express it healthily rather than suppressing it.

### A Message to Type 1

Your longing for perfection is a beautiful force that can make the world better. But **you are already a good enough person right now, in this moment**. Learn to forgive yourself before you try to fix the world.
