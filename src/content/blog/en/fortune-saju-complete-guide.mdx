## 1. What Is Saju-Palja?

**Saju-palja (四柱八字)** is a core system of destiny interpretation in East Asian philosophy. "Saju" means the four pillars (四柱) and "palja" means the eight characters (八字): the **year, month, day, and hour** of a person's birth are each expressed as two Chinese characters, and your destiny is read from those eight characters in total.

The origins of saju trace back to China's Tang dynasty (618–907 CE), systematized by scholars such as Li Xuzhong and Xu Ziping. It was later refined through the Song dynasty into *myeongri* (命理, the study of destiny), and today it is widely used in Korea, China, Japan, and Taiwan.

The philosophical foundation of saju is the thought of **Yin-Yang and the Five Elements (陰陽五行)**. The theory holds that all things in the universe are composed of two energies, Yin (陰) and Yang (陽), and that the five elements — Wood (木), Fire (火), Earth (土), Metal (金), and Water (水) — circulate in endless relationships of generation and control. A person's destiny is seen as part of this vast cosmic flow.

## 2. Heavenly Stems (天干): The Ten Energies of the Sky

The **Heavenly Stems (天干, Cheongan)** are ten Chinese characters representing the energies of the sky. Each carries its own element and Yin-Yang attribute, symbolizing a person's character, talents, and social role.

### Personalities of the Ten Stems

**Gap (甲) – Yang Wood (陽木)**
Like a sprout breaking through the soil in spring, it has strong vitality and a pioneering spirit. Strong in leadership and willing to take on challenges, but at times it can be domineering and inflexible. Excellent at starting new things.

**Eul (乙) – Yin Wood (陰木)**
Like a climbing vine, it adapts flexibly to its environment. Delicate and richly sensitive, it values relationships. Rather than fighting head-on, it achieves goals through gentle means.

**Byeong (丙) – Yang Fire (陽火)**
Bright and warm like the sun. Outgoing and sociable, with the charm to brighten everything around it. Passionate, though at times impulsive.

**Jeong (丁) – Yin Fire (陰火)**
Steady, concentrated energy like a candle flame. Gifted with artistic sensibility, devoted, and capable of deep love. It holds an inner passion while appearing calm on the outside.

**Mu (戊) – Yang Earth (陽土)**
Stable and dependable like a high mountain. Strongly responsible and trusted, well suited to the role of mediator. It seeks stability over change.

**Gi (己) – Yin Earth (陰土)**
It has the embracing capacity to hold everything, like fertile ground. Practical and meticulous, sensitive to others' feelings, with a strong spirit of service.

**Gyeong (庚) – Yang Metal (陽金)**
Strong and decisive like an axe. It values principle, speaks plainly, and does not compromise with injustice. At times it can appear overly rigid.

**Sin (辛) – Yin Metal (陰金)**
Refined and perfectionistic like a jewel. It has an outstanding aesthetic sense and sharp analytical power, with an excellent ability to focus on detail.

**Im (壬) – Yang Water (陽水)**
Wise and flexible like a great river. Possessing deep thought and excellent memory, with strong philosophical leanings. Highly adaptable, though it can easily lose direction.

**Gye (癸) – Yin Water (陰水)**
Quiet, with a deep inner world like a drizzle. Highly intuitive, with a mysterious charm. Richly sensitive and often showing artistic talent.

## 4. The Four Pillars: Year, Month, Day & Hour

Saju-palja places a Heavenly Stem and an Earthly Branch on each of the year, month, day, and hour of birth, forming four pillars (四柱).

### Year Pillar (年柱) – Ancestors & Early Years
The stem-branch of your birth year. It represents **the blessings of ancestors, childhood (ages 0–15), and your relationship with your parents.** It also reflects your outward appearance and the first impression you give others. For example, someone born in 1990 (the year of Gyeong-O) has a year pillar of 庚午 (Gyeong-O).

### Month Pillar (月柱) – Parents & Youth
The stem-branch of your birth month. It represents **your relationships with parents and siblings, your youth (ages 15–30), and your career and social life.** The month pillar is one of the most powerful pillars in the chart, playing a key role in determining a person's environment and temperament.

### Day Pillar (日柱) – Yourself & Midlife
The stem-branch of your birthday. It represents **yourself, your relationship with your spouse, and midlife (ages 30–50).** In particular, the **Day Stem (日干, Ilgan)** is the single most important character, directly representing you. It is the reference point for all saju interpretation.

### Hour Pillar (時柱) – Children & Later Years
The stem-branch of your birth hour. It represents **your relationship with children, old age (after 50), and the fortunes of your later years.** The hour pillar shows the results of a life and hints at the circumstances in which you will live in old age.

## 5. The Five Elements (五行): Balance Is Everything

The Five Elements are **Wood (木), Fire (火), Earth (土), Metal (金), and Water (水)** — the basic energies that compose the universe and human beings. In saju, the balance of the five elements directly affects health, character, and destiny.

### Generation (相生) and Control (相剋)

**Generation (mutually nourishing relationships)**
- Wood generates Fire (木生火): wood makes fire
- Fire generates Earth (火生土): fire makes earth
- Earth generates Metal (土生金): earth makes metal
- Metal generates Water (金生水): metal makes water
- Water generates Wood (水生木): water makes wood

**Control (mutually restraining relationships)**
- Wood controls Earth (木剋土): wood pierces the earth
- Earth controls Water (土剋水): earth dams the water
- Water controls Fire (水剋火): water extinguishes the fire
- Fire controls Metal (火剋金): fire melts the metal
- Metal controls Wood (金剋木): metal cuts the wood

### Reading Excess and Deficiency of the Elements

Among the eight characters of a chart, having three or more of a particular element is read as **excess**, while having none at all is read as **void (空亡)** or **deficiency.**

- **Excess Wood (木)**: strong drive, but stubborn and apt to ignore others' opinions
- **Excess Fire (火)**: passionate, but with sharp mood swings and a tendency toward impulsive decisions
- **Excess Earth (土)**: stable, but resistant to change and slow to move
- **Excess Metal (金)**: decisive, but cold and capable of hurting others
- **Excess Water (水)**: wise and flexible, but indecisive and prone to anxiety

## 7. Luck Pillars, Annual & Monthly Luck: Reading the Flow of Time

Saju does not stop at static analysis. Through the **Luck Pillars (大運, Daeun), Annual Luck (歲運, Seun), and Monthly Luck (月運, Wolun)**, it predicts changes in fortune as time flows.

### Luck Pillars (大運) – The Big Flow in 10-Year Cycles

The Luck Pillars show the major flow of fortune in ten-year cycles. They start from the stem-branch following the month pillar, calculated forward for "Yang-male / Yin-female" and backward for "Yin-male / Yang-female."

The starting age of the Luck Pillars (the "luck number") is the number of days from your birth to the next solar term, divided by three. For example, if the luck number is 3, you enter a new Luck Pillar at ages 3, 13, 23, 33, and so on.

### Annual Luck (歲運) – The Yearly Flow

Annual Luck is the stem-branch of each changing year. 2025 is the year of Eul-Sa (乙巳), and 2026 is the year of Byeong-O (丙午). When the annual luck forms a favorable relationship with your chart, it is an auspicious year; when it forms clash, punishment, or control relationships, it is a year to be cautious.

### Monthly Luck (月運) – The Detailed Monthly Flow

Monthly Luck reads a finer flow through the stem-branch of each month. Even if the Luck Pillars and Annual Luck are good, a bad monthly luck calls for caution that month; conversely, even in a poor Luck Pillar, a good monthly luck can bring opportunity.

## 8. Clash, Punishment, Combination & Breaking: Relationships Between Characters

The eight characters of a chart influence one another.

**Combination (合) – Two characters merge to create a new element**
- Stem combinations: Gap-Gi combine into Earth (甲己合化土), Eul-Gyeong combine into Metal (乙庚合化金), etc.
- Branch triple-combinations: In-O-Sul form a Fire frame (寅午戌合火局), Sin-Ja-Jin form a Water frame (申子辰合水局), etc.

**Clash (沖) – Two characters collide and the energy is shaken**
- Branch clashes: Ja-O clash (子午沖), Chuk-Mi clash (丑未沖), In-Sin clash (寅申沖), etc.
- A strong clash hints at movement, change, separation, or accidents.

**Punishment (刑) – Three characters meet and generate punishing energy**
- The In-Sa-Sin triple punishment (寅巳申): conflict and trials
- The Chuk-Sul-Mi triple punishment (丑戌未): hardship and suffering

**Breaking (破) – Two characters break each other**
- Ja-Yu break (子酉破), O-Myo break (午卯破), etc.
- Hints at the rupture of relationships or the frustration of plans

## 9. The Useful God (用神): The Energy Your Chart Needs Most

The **Useful God (用神, Yongsin)** refers to the element your chart needs most. When a chart is imbalanced, it is the key energy that corrects it.

For example, if your chart overflows with Wood (木) and Fire (火) but has no Water (水), then Water becomes your Useful God. Knowing your Useful God lets you grasp which colors, directions, careers, names, and environments are favorable to you.

Methods for finding the Useful God include the **support-suppress method (억부법), the climate-adjustment method (조후법), the mediation method (통관법), the disease-medicine method (병약법), and the dominant-energy method (전왕법)** — and each myeongri scholar takes a different approach.

## 10. What to Know Before a Saju Reading

### An Accurate Birth Date and Time Are Essential

In saju-palja, the **Hour Pillar (時柱)** is determined by your birth time. Because it changes every two hours, even a one- or two-hour difference in birth time produces a different chart. Check your exact birth time on your birth certificate or with your mother.

### Saju Is a Map of Possibility

Saju is not an absolute verdict that decides your fate. Hundreds of thousands of people in the world share the same chart, yet live different lives. Saju is like a **map** that shows your innate temperament, environment, and possibilities. Looking at the map, you decide for yourself which road to take.

### How to Choose a Good Saju Reader

1. **Check qualifications and experience**: confirm their study of myeongri and their counseling experience.
2. **Counseling style**: avoid readers who use intimidation or ominous hints to push extra charges.
3. **Concrete advice**: choose a place that gives realistic, specific advice rather than vague prophecy.
4. **Transparent pricing**: a place that clearly explains fees in advance is more trustworthy.

### Saju and Naming

In saju, your **name** matters too. When the stroke count and elements of a name's Chinese characters match the chart's Useful God, the name is seen as a positive influence. People often request a saju-based name evaluation when naming a child or considering a name change.

## In Closing

Saju-palja is a sophisticated system of destiny interpretation, distilling thousands of years of East Asian philosophy. Although it faces criticism for being difficult to verify scientifically, it still gives meaning to many people as a tool for reflecting on one's strengths and weaknesses and contemplating the direction of life.

The wise way to use saju is to gain insights like "Ah, I have this tendency," or "In a period like this, I should be careful about that." Not being dragged along by fate, but understanding it and actively forging your own life — that is the true reason for studying saju-palja.
