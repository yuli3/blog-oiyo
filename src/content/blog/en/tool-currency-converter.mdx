## Smart Exchange Tips

<Callout type="tip">
**Best ways to exchange (Korea-based travel)**: ① Toss / KakaoBank app exchange (90–95% preferential rate) → ② Bank app exchange (70–90%) → ③ Airport kiosks as a last resort. Exchange 1–2 weeks ahead when rates are favorable.

- **USD**: $100 ≈ 135,000 KRW at a ₩1,350/USD rate
- **JPY**: ¥10,000 ≈ 90,000–100,000 KRW
- **EUR**: €100 ≈ 150,000 KRW at a ₩1,500/EUR rate
