Enneagram Type 3 is known as **The Achiever** or **The Performer**. They pursue success, recognition, and excellence. The belief *"I am valuable because of what I achieve"* becomes the engine of their life.

## Type 3 Core Profile

| Attribute | Description |
|------|------|
| Type Name | The Achiever / The Performer |
| Basic Desire | To feel valuable, successful, and excellent |
| Basic Fear | Being seen as worthless or a failure |
| Core Motivation | Gaining recognition and proving self-worth through success |
| Center | Feeling (Heart) Center |
| Worldview | "I must accomplish something to have worth" |

## Personality Traits

### Strengths

**Goal-Oriented**: Knows exactly what they want and finds ways to achieve it.

**Adaptability**: Shifts seamlessly to fit different audiences and situations — equally polished in the boardroom and at a party.

**Energy & Drive**: A seemingly inexhaustible energy aimed at their goals.

**Practical Efficiency**: Knows how to get maximum results with minimum resources.

**Inspiration**: Successful Type 3s inspire those around them with a sense of "I can do this too."

### Weaknesses

**Lack of Authenticity**: Becoming too absorbed in image and role can cause them to lose touch with who they truly are.

**Emotional Suppression**: Views feelings as obstacles to efficiency and tends to dismiss them.

**Transactional Relationships**: Evaluates relationships based on performance or usefulness.

**Burnout Risk**: Feels guilty resting, leading to chronic overwork.

## Wing Types

### 2w3 (Two Wing)

- More people-oriented
- Pursues success through relationships
- Warm and sociable
- Humanitarian tendencies

### 4w3 (Four Wing)

- More artistic and individualistic
- Values originality over image
- Emotionally more complex
- Pursues success in creative fields

## Stress & Security Directions

| State | Direction | Characteristics |
|------|------|------|
| Under Stress | → Type 9 (Peacemaker) | Becomes listless, indecisive, and avoidant |
| Feeling Secure | → Type 6 (Loyalist) | Collaborates with the team; values trust and loyalty |

## Relationship Patterns

### Relationship Strengths

- Proud of their partner and wants to show them off
- Puts real effort into dates and special occasions
- Works hard to make the relationship a "success"

### Relationship Challenges

- Focuses on the image of a "successful relationship" rather than genuine feelings
- Tends to prioritize work over a partner
- Withholds vulnerability, creating emotional distance

### Ideal Partner

Someone who celebrates Type 3's achievements while also showing they are loved apart from those achievements. A partner who makes them feel safe enough to reveal their true self — fears, insecurities, and all.

## Growth Guide for Type 3

1. **Practice resting without producing**: Train your body to know it has worth even when nothing is being achieved.
2. **Ask "Who am I?"**: When you strip away the roles and image, who is left?
3. **Allow yourself to be vulnerable**: Stop performing perfection and practice sharing real feelings.
4. **Find meaning in the process**: Seek significance in the journey, not only in the result.
