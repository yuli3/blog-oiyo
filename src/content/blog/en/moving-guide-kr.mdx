Moving in South Korea is more than just hauling boxes — there's a chain of admin tasks (lease review, mover selection, resident registration, utility transfers) that can cost you money if you miss them. Follow this checklist in order and you won't miss a step.

---

## 1. Two to Four Weeks Before Moving Day — Contract and Movers

### Reviewing Your Lease
- Check the registered title (등기부등본) at [Internet Registry Office](https://www.iros.go.kr) → verify no liens (근저당) or seizures (압류)
- Contract must clearly state: **balance payment date, move-in date, special clauses**
- For jeonse (전세) or monthly rent: Get a "확정일자" (certified date stamp) — at your local community center (주민센터) or via [Internet Registry Office](https://www.iros.go.kr)
- Consider jeonse deposit insurance through [Korea Housing Finance Corporation (HUG)](https://www.khug.or.kr)

### Hiring Movers
- Get at least 3 in-person quotes
- Verify the company is a **licensed freight/moving company** via [Fair Trade Commission](https://www.ftc.go.kr)
- Contract must specify: date, time, total cost, and damage compensation terms
- For small moves (studio / 1-room): a 용달 (small freight truck) is often sufficient and cheaper

### Timing
- Month-end (25th–31st) and month-start (1st–5th) are **peak season** — prices 20–30% higher
- Weekends and public holidays also carry a surcharge
- Book at least 2 weeks in advance

## 2. One Week Before — Sorting and Pre-Filings

### Sorting and Packing
- Sort items into 3 groups: throw away / sell (당근마켓/Carrot) / move
- Wrap fragile items yourself in newspaper or bubble wrap
- Turn off and empty your refrigerator the night before the move
- Confirm whether movers include packing service (extra cost)

### Pre-Filing Address Changes
- Post office mail forwarding: [Korea Post](https://www.epost.go.kr) → redirect mail to new address
- Banks and brokerage accounts: update via online banking or in person
- Subscription services: update delivery address

---

## 3. Moving Day

### At Your Old Address — Checklist
- [ ] Cross-check all furniture and appliances against your inventory list
- [ ] Photograph walls and floors — document any **pre-existing damage** (prevents disputes)
- [ ] Photograph utility meters (electric, gas, water) for your final reading
- [ ] Settle management fees (관리비) with the building office
- [ ] Return keys and access cards
- [ ] Check A/C outdoor unit pipes and washing machine drain hoses

### At Your New Address — Checklist
- [ ] Photograph all walls, floors, and ceilings **before** moving anything in
- [ ] Confirm water, gas, and electricity are working
- [ ] Confirm internet activation (apply in advance — installation takes a few days)
- [ ] Report any moving damage directly to the movers on-site

## 4. Within Two Weeks — Mandatory Administrative Tasks

### ① Jeonipshingo 전입신고 — Mandatory Within 14 Days
- How: Visit your local community center (주민센터) or apply at [Government 24](https://www.gov.kr)
- Documents: National ID, lease contract
- **Critical**: Jeonipshingo creates your legal "대항력" (standing against the landlord/new owner) — this protects your deposit
- If you haven't gotten your 확정일자 yet, request it at the same time

### ② National Health Insurance and Pension Address Update
- Often updated automatically via jeonipshingo linkage, but verify directly
- [National Health Insurance Service](https://www.nhis.or.kr) / [National Pension Service](https://www.nps.or.kr)

### ③ Vehicle Registration Address Change
- **Deadline: Within 15 days of moving**
- How: Visit the nearest vehicle registration office or use [Car Civil Affairs Portal](https://www.ecar.go.kr)

### ④ Utilities Transfer
- **Electricity**: [KEPCO Customer Service](https://cyber.kepco.co.kr) (dial 123) or their app
- **Gas**: Contact your regional city gas company (varies by area) — usually requires a technician visit
- **Water**: Contact your local water authority
- **Internet/TV**: Request transfer installation (confirm whether transfer fees apply)

### ⑤ Driver's License and Passport Address Update
- Driver's license: [Road Traffic Authority](https://www.safedriving.or.kr) online or at a police station
- Passport: Address updates automatically on renewal — not urgently required

## 5. Easy-to-Forget Items

| Item | How to Handle | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| School transfer (children) | Get school head's transfer approval, then enroll at new school | Before move |
- Medical aid recipients: Update at community center | Within 14 days |
| Pet registration address | [Animal Protection System](https://www.animal.go.kr) | Within 30 days |
| Regular delivery/subscription services | Update individually at each service | Ongoing |

## Deposit Dispute Protection

If your landlord refuses to return your deposit after moving out:
- **주택임차권 등기명령 (Tenancy Lien Registration)**: File with the court to register your tenancy right on the title — protects your claim even after you've moved out
- **내용증명 (Certified letter)**: Send a registered letter via post office formally demanding return of your deposit
- **Korea Legal Aid Corporation**: [Free legal consultation](https://www.klac.or.kr) — call 132 (no area code)

> This guide provides general procedural guidance. For specific legal situations, consult the relevant government agency or a legal professional.
