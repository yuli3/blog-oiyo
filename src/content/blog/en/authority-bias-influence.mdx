## Milgram Experiment

In the famous experiment, participants administered what they thought were lethal electric shocks to a stranger, simply because a man in a lab coat told them to "please continue."
65% of people went all the way to 450 volts.
We trust symbols of authority (uniforms, titles) more than we trust our own judgment.
