## Annual Leave Accrual Rules (Korea Labor Standards Act)

### First Year: Monthly Accrual

| Tenure | Days Earned | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|
| 1 month of full attendance | 1 day | 1 day |
| 2 months of full attendance | 1 day | 2 days |
| … | … | … |
| 11 months of full attendance | 1 day | **max 11 days** |

### After 1 Full Year: 15 Days Base per Year

| Years of Service | Annual Leave |
|---|---|
| 1 year | 15 days |
| 2 years | 15 days |
| 3 years | 16 days |
| 4 years | 16 days |
| 5 years | 17 days |
| … (+1 day every 2 years) | … |
| 21+ years | **max 25 days** |

<Callout type="tip">
**Accrual formula**: 15 days + ⌊(years of service − 1) ÷ 2⌋, capped at 25 days.
</Callout>

## Leave Encouragement Policy

Employers must prompt employees to use annual leave at least 6 months before it expires. If the employee still doesn't use it after proper notice, **the employer's obligation to pay unused-leave compensation is waived**.

<Callout type="warning">
**Unused leave payout**: If you resign with unused leave, you are entitled to those days × one day's regular wage. However, this may not apply if a formal leave-encouragement notice was issued.
</Callout>

## FAQ

**Q. Does part-time work under 15 hours per week qualify for annual leave?**  
If you work 15 hours or more per week, the same rules apply. Workers under 15 hours per week are excluded from the annual leave provisions.

**Q. How are half-days (half-leave) counted?**  
Two half-day leaves = 1 full day used. This calculator supports 0.5-day increments.
