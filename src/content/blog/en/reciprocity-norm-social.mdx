## The Hare Krishnas

They would give a flower to travelers in airports. The traveler would try to refuse, but they insisted: "It is a gift."
Moments later, they asked for a donation. The traveler, holding the unwanted flower, felt a social pressure to give money. They gave.

## Influence

To get what you want, give something first.

- A free sample.
- A compliment.
- A small favor.
  The other person will feel an unconscious debt to repay you.
