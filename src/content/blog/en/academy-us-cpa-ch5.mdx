## Discipline Section Comparison

### TCP (Tax Compliance and Planning)
**Best for**: Tax-focused careers, public accounting tax teams, tax consulting

**Content**: Individual/corporate/partnership tax planning; tax minimization strategies; international tax basics

**Choose if**: Targeting Big 4 Tax, regional CPA firm tax practice, or IRS-related work

---

### BAR (Business Analysis and Reporting)
**Best for**: Corporate finance, FP&A, investment banking, asset management

**Content**: Financial analysis and valuation; advanced accounting (derivatives, hedging); data analytics fundamentals

**Choose if**: Targeting Controller/CFO track, financial analysis, private equity

---

### ISC (Information Systems & Controls)
**Best for**: IT auditors, cybersecurity specialists, internal control professionals

**Content**: Information systems controls; IT audit methodology; SOC reports (SOC 1, 2, 3); cybersecurity risk basics

**Choose if**: Targeting IT audit, fintech, ERP implementation consulting

---

## Recommended Exam Order

**Path A (Public Accounting focus)**:
FAR → AUD → REG → BAR or TCP

**Path B (Efficiency-first)**:
AUD → REG → FAR → Discipline

**Why start with AUD**: Conceptual section = faster to pass = early momentum.

**Why FAR first**: FAR knowledge underpins every other section — studying it first reduces overall review burden.

## Study Strategies That Raise Pass Rates

### Practice-Focused Learning
- **60% concept review, 40% MCQ practice** is the minimum
- Wrong question analysis > completing more questions
- Aim for 75%+ accuracy on practice MCQs before testing

### Task-Based Simulations (TBS)
- TBS = 40% of your score — not optional
- TBS tests real-world application (spreadsheets, research, memos)
- Many candidates underperform on TBS despite strong MCQ scores
