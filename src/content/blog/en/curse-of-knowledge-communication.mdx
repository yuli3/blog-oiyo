## Tapping the Rhythm

In a study, "tappers" tapped the rhythm of a famous song (like Happy Birthday) on a table. "Listeners" had to guess the song.
Tappers predicted 50% would guess it. Real success rate: 2.5%.
Why? The tapper _hears the song in their head_. They cannot imagine that the listener hears only random taps.

## The Teacher's Burden

This is why brilliant professors are often terrible teachers. They assume context that the student does not have.
To communicate, you must unlearn your expertise and step into the beginner's mind.
