## Introduction: Compass of Life, Yongsin

When you get a Saju consultation, the most common thing you hear is "You need Water" or "Fire is your Yongsin." What on earth is **Yongsin (用神)** that Saju scholars are so obsessed with it?

Yongsin literally means **'Using (用) God (神)'**, or **'the core character that operates my Saju'**. It is the single most urgent and important character among the 8 characters. It is not just a lucky charm, but a **strategic weapon** to balance my life and break through crises.

## Core Concept Analysis

Characters in Saju are divided into Allies and Enemies.

### 1. Yongsin (用神, Useful God): My Weapon

Medicine (藥) that cures the sickness (病) of the Saju chart.

- **Eokbu (Suppress/Support) Yongsin:** Suppressing the too strong, or helping the weak.
- **Johu (Climate) Yongsin:** Regulating climate that is too cold or too hot.
- **Tonggwan (Flow) Yongsin:** Enabling communication between blocked energies.

### 2. Heesin (喜神, Supporting God): Yongsin's Assistant

Character that helps Yongsin. If Yongsin is the General, Heesin is the Lieutenant. When Heesin comes in fortune, Yongsin gets empowered and life flourishes.

### 3. Gisin (忌神, Opposing God): My Obstacle

Character that attacks or hinders Yongsin. The main culprit breaking the balance. Appears as trials in life.

### 4. Gusin (仇神, Enemy God): Gisin's Assistant

Character helping Gisin to make me harder. (Uses the character for 'Enemy/Revenge' 仇)

## Philosophical Meaning of Yongsin

### 1. Aesthetics of Junghwa (Harmony): Excess is Bad

Yongsin thought touches Confucian 'Golden Mean (Jungyong)' philosophy. Shedding the strong, Adding to the weak. This is the principle of the universe and healthy life.

- **Wangja Chungbal:** If you touch (clash) a guy who is too strong, he explodes. (In this case, you must not suppress but drain/channel the energy).

### 2. Deficiency is Drive

Yongsin is usually what I **'crave the most'**. If one is cold due to lack of Fire (deficiency), he wanders seeking warmth all his life. That desperation becomes the power moving him. Thus, Yongsin is my **deficiency and simultaneously my aspiration**.

### 3. Yongsin Changes with Situation

Many think Yongsin is fixed for life. But just as a hero in turbulent times becomes a traitor in peaceful times, the necessary weapon (Yongsin) changes when the situation (Great Fortune) changes.

- Fire (Climate Yongsin) is urgent in Winter fortune, but when Spring comes, Water and Earth to grow Wood might become more necessary than Fire.

## Deep Dive: Eokbu vs Johu Debate

The eternal theme of Saju academia: "Is Power Balance (Eokbu) first, or Climate Regulation (Johu) first?"

- **Eokbu School:** "We are starving to death, who cares about weather? Balance of power comes first."
- **Johu School:** "No matter how much money you have, if you are frozen solid, you can't use it. Temperature for life activity comes first."

**Modern Conclusion:**

- Social achievement (Wealth, Status) is deeply related to Eokbu (Power).
- Personal happiness (Health, Emotion) is deeply related to Johu (Climate).
- Rich but unhappy? Eokbu was right but Johu was broken.

## Practical Application: Using Yongsin (Opening Luck)

Let's take a person whose Yongsin is 'Wood (Tree)'.

1. **Material:** Growing plants, wood furniture, green clothes, hiking, East direction.
2. **Action:** Starting, planning, benevolence (In 仁), playing with children.
3. **Job:** Education, architecture, fashion, publishing.
4. **People:** Meeting a spouse or partner with strong Wood energy.

But the most important method is **Mindset**. If Yongsin is Wood, possessing a mind that is **straight, flexible, and growing** like a tree will open luck.

## Wrapping Up: You Are Your Own Yongsin

One might lack Yongsin or have a powerless one (Bulgeup). But no need to be disappointed.

Classics said "Broken structure without Yongsin," but modern perspective values the **"ability to bring what is missing from outside or create it oneself."**
Yongsin is not a savior coming from outside. Recognizing the imbalance within me and my **Will** to balance it myself—that is the true Yongsin.
