# Cost & Variance Interactive Lab

Cost variances don't appear all at once.  
**Price variance and efficiency variance** build up over time and shape the final result.

---

## 1. Variance Analysis Simulator

Adjust actual quantity (AQ), actual price (AP), standard quantity (SQ), and standard price (SP).  
You can see with your own eyes **exactly which path each variance takes to appear**.

<Callout type="tip">
**What to watch**: Price variance is a "price" problem; efficiency variance is an "input quantity" problem.  
Separating them is what makes your improvement strategy clear.
</Callout>
