## The Mug Experiment

Researchers gave people a coffee mug and asked how much they would sell it for. Average price: \$7.00.
They asked another group how much they would pay to buy that same mug. Average price: \$3.00.
Ownership doubles the perceived value. This is why it is so hard to declutter your house or sell your car for a fair price.
