## Future Shock

Toffler predicted "Future Shock": the psychological state of individuals and societies defined by "too much change in too short a period of time."

## Filter Failure

The problem is not lack of information; it is _filter failure_.
Attention is the scarce resource. You must aggressively curate your inputs, or you will drown in noise.
