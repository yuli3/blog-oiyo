## FAR: The Largest CPA Section

FAR (Financial Accounting and Reporting) is the broadest and most content-heavy section of the CPA exam. It carries the lowest pass rate and requires the most preparation time.

**FAR covers**:
- GAAP financial reporting (business entities)
- Governmental accounting
- Not-for-profit accounting
- IFRS vs. GAAP comparisons

---

## GAAP vs. IFRS Key Differences

| Item | US GAAP | IFRS |
|------|---------|------|
| Inventory | FIFO, LIFO, weighted average | FIFO, weighted average (no LIFO) |
| R&D costs | All expensed immediately | Development phase capitalization allowed |
| Investment property | Cost model only | Fair value model permitted |
| Revenue recognition | ASC 606 (5-step) | IFRS 15 (identical model) |

---

## ASC 606: Five-Step Revenue Recognition

1. **Identify the contract** with a customer
2. **Identify performance obligations** (distinct promises to transfer goods/services)
3. **Determine the transaction price** (including variable consideration, time value of money)
4. **Allocate** to performance obligations (based on standalone selling prices)
5. **Recognize revenue** when (or as) each obligation is satisfied

---

## ASC 842: Lease Accounting

**Lessee accounting**:
- **Operating lease**: Recognize ROU asset + lease liability; single straight-line lease expense
- **Finance lease**: Recognize ROU asset + lease liability; separate interest + amortization expense

**Lessor accounting**:
- Operating lease: Recognize straight-line rental income
- Direct financing/sales-type lease: Recognize a receivable

---

## Governmental Accounting

Governmental accounting is approximately 25–30% of FAR — high-stakes content.

**Two measurement focuses**:
1. **Fund accounting**: Resources segregated by purpose
2. **Government-wide reporting**: Full accrual basis

**Major fund types**:
- **General Fund**: Primary operating fund (pays for day-to-day government operations)
- **Special Revenue Fund**: Restricted for specific purposes
- **Capital Projects Fund**: For acquisition/construction of capital assets
- **Debt Service Fund**: For principal/interest payments on long-term debt
