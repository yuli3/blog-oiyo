## Three Credentials Positioned

| Feature | US CPA | ACCA | CFA |
|---------|--------|------|-----|
| Administrator | AICPA/NASBA (US) | ACCA (UK) | CFA Institute (US) |
| Core Focus | Audit, tax, reporting | Accounting, finance, management | Investment analysis, portfolio management |
| Time to Complete | 1.5–2 years | 3–5 years | 3–5 years |
| Pass Rate | ~45–55% per section | 40–80% per paper | ~10–20% overall |
| Total Cost | \$4,000–\$8,000 | £5,000–£10,000 | \$2,000–\$4,000 |

## Career Goal → Best Credential

### US/Canada Public Accounting or Employment
→ **US CPA first**
- Required for audit sign-off on US public company filings
- Mandatory for Big 4 promotion to manager in the US

### UK/Hong Kong/Singapore/Middle East
→ **ACCA first**
- Dominant in British Commonwealth countries
- Integrated with local professional licensing frameworks

### Asset Management / Investment Analysis / Hedge Funds
→ **CFA first**
- Global standard for portfolio management
- Required or strongly preferred for buy-side analyst roles

### Maximum Global Mobility
→ **CPA + CFA combination**
- Reporting expertise (CPA) + investment expertise (CFA)
- Opens Big 4 → asset management career transition path
