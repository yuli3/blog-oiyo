## Order Is Half the Battle

After a crash everyone panics. Knowing the order in advance limits your losses and disputes. Follow this checklist in sequence.

> General guidance only. For specific handling, follow your insurer, police, and local law. Emergency numbers below are for Korea — use your country's equivalents elsewhere.

---

## Step 1 — Secure Safety First

```
□ Turn on hazard lights
□ Move the vehicle somewhere safe (prevent a secondary crash) —
  but on serious/injury accidents, preserve the scene
□ Highway: get past the guardrail to a safe zone, place a warning triangle
□ Check for injuries → call emergency services if anyone is hurt
```

<aside>
**Secondary collisions are the deadliest risk.** On highways and in tunnels, do not stay on the road or in the car — get behind the guardrail first, then call.
</aside>

---

## Step 2 — Report (official numbers)

```
□ Injuries/fatalities/hit-and-run/major crash → Police, Ambulance
□ In Korea: 112 (police), 119 (fire/ambulance), 1339 (medical info)
□ File with your insurer (call center) — report from the scene
□ Korea expressway incidents: 1588-2504
□ Elsewhere: dial your local emergency number (e.g. 911, 112, 999)
```

---

## Step 3 — Secure Evidence (photos)

Photos are your key evidence. Capture multiple distances and angles.

```
□ Whole scene (so the vehicles' relative positions show) — from afar
□ Damage to both vehicles — close up
□ Tire direction, brake marks, signals, lane markings
□ Save dashcam footage (before it's overwritten)
□ Other party: plate number, contact, insurer, license
□ Witness contacts (if any)
```

---

## Step 4 — Insurance & Medical

```
□ Get the claim number and adjuster contact
□ Pain can appear later → see a doctor and get it on record if needed
□ Don't rush a settlement — wait until injuries and repairs are clear
```

---

## ⚠️ What NOT to Do

```
✗ On-the-spot cash settlement / written waiver (later disputes, hidden injury)
✗ Casually admitting fault (fault ratio is decided by investigation)
✗ Moving the car fully before any photos (even minor crashes: evidence first)
✗ Leaving an injured person / hit-and-run (criminal offense)
✗ Hiding a DUI or unlicensed status
```

---

## Key Takeaways

1. Order: **secure safety → report → photo evidence → insurance/medical.**
2. Preventing a secondary crash comes first — on highways, get behind the guardrail.
3. Photograph far and close, plus dashcam.
4. No on-the-spot cash deal, no casual fault admission, no moving the car before photos.
5. Core numbers (Korea): 112, 119, 1339; report to your insurer immediately.
