# Morning Pages: How to Start the Day with a Clear Mind

Many people think of creativity as the exclusive property of artists. However, author **Julia Cameron** says that all of us were born as creative artists, but that ability is blocked simply because of fear and social censorship.

The most powerful recovery tool she proposes is **'Morning Pages.'**

---

## I. What are Morning Pages?

The method is surprisingly simple. **Every morning, as soon as you wake up, write whatever comes to mind for three pages of A4 paper.**

- **Stream of Consciousness**: You don't need to have good writing skills or be logical. Pour out trivial and messy thoughts exactly as they are—"I'm hungry," "Yesterday's dream was weird," "I don't want to work."
- **Show Nobody**: This is not a work of art. No one other than you, not even you yourself for a while, should re-read it. Only then can you be honest.

---

## II. Why are Morning Pages Effective?

1.  **Silencing the Inner Censor**: Our brains have a censor who whispers, "This is not allowed," or "You'll be laughed at." If you keep writing without stopping, at some point the censor's voice fades and your true feelings begin to flow out.
2.  **Mental Drain**: By transferring stray thoughts and worries accumulated overnight onto paper, it secures clear space (RAM) for your brain to work.
3.  **Clear Insight**: As you fill three pages, you might start with complaints and then, toward the last page, gain a lightning-like realization: "Ah, this is what I actually wanted."

---

## III. Tips for Practice

- **Power of Handwriting**: I recommend writing by hand rather than typing on a computer. The connection between brain and hand stimulates a deeper unconscious.
- **Speed**: Do not think too deeply and keep your hand moving. If your thoughts are blocked, even write "I don't know what to write" repeatedly.
- **Abandon Sense of Obligation**: Filling three pages is important, but if it's too difficult, you can start from one page. What's important is the rhythm of doing it 'every morning.'

## Wrapping Up: The Answer is Within You

Morning Pages is like the act of wiping a mirror. As you wipe away the dust of the mind on paper every morning, at some point you will discover your true face reflected in that mirror—your dreams and passions.

Are you stifled because the direction of life is not visible? Your brain already knows all the answers. They are just hidden by stray thoughts and are not visible. Tomorrow morning, prepare three sheets of blank paper and a pen. And listen to the miracle-like story your hand writes for you.
