# Business Strategy Interactive Lab

SWOT isn't a checklist — it's **a map for strategy**.  
Build one yourself and you'll develop a genuine sense for separating "internal" from "external."

---

## 1. Build Structure with the SWOT Builder

Enter your Strengths (S), Weaknesses (W), Opportunities (O), and Threats (T) directly.  
The goal is to transform a list into a **strategic framework** you can actually use.

<SWOTBuilder client:load />

<Callout type="tip">
**What to watch**: SWOT isn't about "diagnosis" — it's about "choice."  
Connect the one or two most important elements to concrete strategies first.
</Callout>
