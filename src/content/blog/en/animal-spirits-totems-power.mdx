# Animal Spirits and Totems: Awakening the Inner Wild

To ancient humanity, animals were not merely prey. They were spiritual teachers with supernatural powers humans lacked, and **Totems** that protected the identity of tribes and individuals. In modern psychology, too, animals symbolize specific 'archetypal powers' latent in our unconscious.

Is there an animal you are uniquely attracted to, or one that frequently appears in your dreams? It may be a compass pointing toward the energy your soul needs right now.

---

## I. What is a Power Animal?

In shamanic traditions, a **'Power Animal'** is like an individual's guardian spirit. They grant us specific qualities and protect us in moments of crisis.

- **Eagle**: Insight and freedom to view the whole from a height.
- **Wolf**: Community bonding, sharp instincts, and solitary leadership.
- **Bear**: The power of healing by immersing inward, patience, and overwhelming presence.
- **Deer**: Strength hidden in gentleness, sensitivity, and elegance.
- **Snake**: Transformation and rebirth through shedding skin, and the wisdom of healing.

---

## II. How to Find Your Animal

Finding the animal that guides your soul is a matter of **'resonance,'** not a logical choice.

1.  **Observation**: Which animal makes your heart race or gives you a sense of deep intimacy when you see it? Or is there an animal you repeatedly encounter in unexpected places?
2.  **Dream Journaling**: Observe who is running with you in the night when the door to the unconscious opens.
3.  **Meditation and Journey**: Close your eyes in a quiet place and enter your 'inner garden.' See with your mind's eye which animal is waiting for you there.

---

## III. Applying Animal Wisdom to Daily Life

If you have found your animal, don't stop at merely knowing it; borrow its energy.

- **Ahead of a Presentation**: Imagine the lion's dignity and put strength into your voice.
- **When No Solution is Seen in Conflict**: Try to see through to the unseen truth with the owl's eyes.
- **When You Are Utterly Exhausted**: Regain your own rhythm slowly but surely like a sea turtle.

## Conclusion: The Path to Reconnecting with Nature

Humans have built civilizations, but our origins are still in contact with the earth. Respecting animal spirits is the act of re-embracing the alienated wild within us, and the path to reconnecting with the vital force of the entire universe.

Feel the breath of the invisible animal guarding your side today. You are not alone. Just as our ancestors did thousands of years ago, your totem is still standing firmly behind you.
