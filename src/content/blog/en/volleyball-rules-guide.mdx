## Volleyball is easy once you know rally scoring

Volleyball is fast and intense, but its rules are clear-cut. You really just need to understand how scoring works, the 3-touch rule, and rotation.

---

## The basics — players and sets

```
Players: 6 per team on the court. (Beach volleyball uses 2.)
Sets:    Best of 5 sets — first to win 3 sets wins the match.
Set score: First to 25 points, winning by a margin of at
         least 2. The deciding 5th set is played to 15.
Rally scoring: whoever wins the rally scores the point,
         regardless of who served.
```

---

## The key rule — 3 touches

```
A team may touch the ball up to 3 times before it must
cross back over the net.
- Typical sequence: receive (dig) → set (raise the ball) →
  spike (attack)
- The same player can't touch the ball twice in a row
  (except when blocking)
- A block touch doesn't count toward the 3-touch limit
```

---

## Common faults

```
- Double contact: one player touches the ball twice in a
  row (e.g. during a set)
- Four hits: a team touches the ball 4 times
- Net touch: touching the net during play
- Reaching over: crossing into the opponent's side to
  attack or interfere
- Foot fault / over the line: stepping on or over the
  line while serving
- Position fault: being out of the correct rotation order
  at the moment of serve
```

---

## Rotation and positions

```
Rotation: winning back the serve moves every player one
          position clockwise.
Positions: 3 front-row players (attack, block) + 3 back-row
          players (defense, receive).
Back-row players may not jump and attack from in front of
the 3-meter attack line.
```

---

## The Libero

```
- A defensive specialist (wears a different-colored jersey)
- Plays only in the back row — can receive and defend, but
  can never attack, block, or serve
- Can substitute in and out of the back row freely
  (no limit on the number of substitutions)
```

---

## Position names

```
Setter: runs the offense by setting the ball (the "quarterback")
Outside hitter (left side): the primary attacker
Middle blocker (center): blocking and quick attacks
Opposite (right side): back-row attacks, the "closer"
```

---

## Key takeaways

1. 6 players, rally scoring, sets go to 25 points (win by 2), first to 3 sets wins (the 5th set goes to 15).
2. A team gets **up to 3 touches** before sending the ball back over (receive-set-spike).
3. Double contacts, four hits, and net touches are all faults.
4. Winning the serve back triggers a clockwise rotation; back-row players can't jump-attack in front of the attack line.
5. The libero is a defense-only specialist — no attacking or serving, but unlimited substitutions.

> This guide follows International Volleyball Federation (FIVB) rules.
