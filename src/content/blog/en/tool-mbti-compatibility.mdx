## MBTI's Four Dimensions

| Dimension | One Side | Other Side | Key Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E/I** | Extrovert (E): energized by people | Introvert (I): energized by solitude | Energy direction |
| **S/N** | Sensing (S): concrete, realistic | Intuition (N): abstract, possibilities | Information intake |
| **T/F** | Thinking (T): logic and principles | Feeling (F): values and relationships | Decision-making |
| **J/P** | Judging (J): planned, systematic | Perceiving (P): flexible, spontaneous | Lifestyle approach |

<Callout type="tip">
**Compatible pairings**: Research suggests that types sharing the same N/S preference tend to communicate more naturally. T/F opposites (T+F) can balance each other well, each compensating for the other's blind spots.
</Callout>

## Romance Style by Type Group

| Type Group | Relationship Style |
|---|---|
| NF (INFJ·INFP·ENFJ·ENFP) | Emotional depth, idealistic love, deep connection |
| NT (INTJ·INTP·ENTJ·ENTP) | Intellectual exchange, independent, direct expression |
| SJ (ISTJ·ISFJ·ESTJ·ESFJ) | Stable, responsible, traditional relationship roles |
| SP (ISTP·ISFP·ESTP·ESFP) | Present-moment, spontaneous dates, freedom-loving |

<Callout type="note">
MBTI compatibility is an entertainment and self-awareness tool. In actual relationships, communication, mutual respect, and shared values matter far more than MBTI type alignment.
