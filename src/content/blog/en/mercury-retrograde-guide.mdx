Ever heard someone say "Mercury is in retrograde"? Known as a stretch of miscommunication, tech glitches, travel delays, and mixups, Mercury retrograde is one of the most talked-about phenomena in astrology. Here's a full breakdown of what it is and how to handle it.

## What Is Mercury Retrograde?

Mercury orbits the Sun roughly every 88 days. "Retrograde" refers to **an optical illusion**: because of the relative positions of Earth and Mercury, Mercury appears to move backward across the sky.

Mercury isn't actually moving backward. It's the same effect as when a faster car overtakes a slower one on the highway — the slower car appears, for a moment, to be moving in reverse.

**How often it happens**: about 3–4 times a year, each lasting roughly three weeks.

## What Mercury Governs

In astrology, Mercury rules:
- Communication, conversation, contracts, documents
- Travel and transportation
- Technology and electronics
- Information, learning, thinking
- Business transactions

During a retrograde, all of these areas are said to see more delays, errors, and misunderstandings.

## What to Avoid During Mercury Retrograde

### Avoid Starting New Things

- Signing new contracts (contracts already in negotiation are fine)
- Launching a new business
- Starting a new job or changing jobs
- Making a big electronics purchase

### Be Careful with Communication

- Double- and triple-check important emails and texts before sending
- Review the fine print of any contract or document carefully
- Clear up misunderstandings right away when they arise

### Be Careful When Traveling

- Push important trips to after the retrograde if you can
- Reconfirm reservations before you leave, and build in buffer time
- Back up your devices

## What Mercury Retrograde Is Actually Good For

Retrograde periods are ideal for anything with a "re-" prefix.

| Activity | Why |
|------|------|
| **Review** | Revisiting past decisions and plans |
| **Reconnect** | Reaching out to old friends or exes |
| **Revise** | Editing writing or reworking a project |
| **Reflect** | Meditation, journaling |
| **Rest** | Finishing what you've started rather than beginning something new |

<Callout type="tip">
**Making the most of it**: think of Mercury retrograde as the universe's invitation to look backward. It's an ideal window for tying up loose ends — unfinished business, unresolved relationships, projects you never quite wrapped up.
</Callout>

## Surviving Mercury Retrograde

### Checklist Before It Starts

- [ ] Finish signing important contracts before the retrograde begins
- [ ] Adjust any major travel plans
- [ ] Back up your devices
- [ ] Save email drafts and reread them before sending

### Mindset During the Retrograde

Thinking "nothing will work because Mercury is retrograde" can become a self-fulfilling prophecy. Stay extra careful during retrograde periods, but remember: not every bad thing that happens is actually because of it.

**A better approach**:
1. Be extra clear and kind in your communication
2. Slow down and double-check things
3. Clear up misunderstandings the moment they happen

## Mercury Retrograde and Tech Problems

Plenty of people report more device glitches during Mercury retrograde.

**Where the science stands**: no causal link between Mercury retrograde and device malfunctions has been scientifically established. It may simply be that people who are watching for it pay closer attention to glitches they'd otherwise overlook.

**Practical advice**: retrograde or not, backing up important files, reading contracts carefully, and confirming reservations before you travel are always good habits.
