## 3. The New Sovereignty: Digital Identity & Ownership

In Web3, you are not a product; you are a participant.

- **Digital Sovereignty**: You control your data and your assets. No platform can "ban" your wallet from the blockchain.
- **Incentive Alignment**: In a DAO, the "Shareholders" are also the "Workers" and the "Users." Everyone wins when the network succeeds.
- <mark><strong>"From Platforms to s"</strong></mark>: Instead of using Uber (as a platform), a Web3 future might see us using a "Rideshare " owned by the drivers and riders themselves.

<Callout type="note">
  **The Governance Paradox**: Fully decentralized voting can be slow and subject to "Whale
  Manipulation" (few rich people controlling the vote). Modern DAOs are experimenting with
  "Quadratic Voting" and "Delegation" to make systems more fair and efficient.
</Callout>

## 4. Conclusion: A New Social Contract

Web3 is not just about technology; it is a new <mark><strong>"Social Contract."</strong></mark> It represents a transition from a world of "Don't be evil" (Web2) to "Can't be evil" (Web3). By moving control from centralized humans to decentralized code, we are building a more resilient and equitable digital future.
