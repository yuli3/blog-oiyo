## 0. The Fool: Pure Beginning

The Fool standing at the edge of a cliff but looking at the sky without fear. This symbolizes not recklessness, but \*\* untainted by worldly prejudice. Every journey begins in this state of 'Not-knowing'.

## Stages of the Journey

- \*\* Forming the ego and adapting to the world by meeting the Magician, Empress, etc.
- \*\* Deconstructing the ego and exploring the inner self through Justice, Hermit, Death, etc.
- \*\* Resisting the Devil's temptation, passing the Star and Sun, and finally reaching completion in 'The World'.

## Where Are You?

If the card you drew now is 'Wheel of Fortune', you are in a time of change; if 'Hermit', you are in a time of inner exploration.
