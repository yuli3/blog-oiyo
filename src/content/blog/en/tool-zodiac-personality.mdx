# Zodiac Personality Analysis

The Western zodiac (황도 12궁) divides the year into 12 signs based on the Sun's position at birth, forming the foundation of Western astrology.

Zodiac personality theory traces back to the Hellenistic period (2nd century BC) and has evolved as a cultural tradition ever since. Carl Jung noted that astrology can serve as a symbolic system for exploring archetypal patterns in the human psyche. Today, zodiac signs function less as superstition and more as a **language for self-exploration**.

**What this analyzer shows:**
- Core personality keywords (strengths and weaknesses)
- Behavioral patterns in romance and work
- Element group (Fire, Earth, Air, Water) characteristics
- Compatible signs

Select your sign to begin.
