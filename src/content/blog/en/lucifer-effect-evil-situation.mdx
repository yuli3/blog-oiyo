## The Barrel, Not the Apple

We often ask "Who is the bad apple?"
Zimbardo asks "What is the bad barrel?"
If you put good people in a bad situation (unlimited power, anonymity, dehumanization), they will do evil things.

## Heroism

The antidote is to recognize the situational forces. Heroes are everyday people who resist the situation.
Most evil is not committed by psychopaths, but by ordinary people following orders.
