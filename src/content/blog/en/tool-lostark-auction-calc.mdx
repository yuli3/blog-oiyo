# Lost Ark Auction Calculator

When an auctioned item drops in a Lost Ark Legion Commander Raid or Abyss Dungeon, this tool instantly calculates the bid that keeps the loot fair for all party members.

## How the Calculation Works

**Fair-split bid formula:**
```
Bid price = (Market price after 5% fee) × (Party size − 1) ÷ Party size
```

**Max-gain bid formula:**
```
Bid price = Fair-split bid ÷ 1.1
```

The 5% marketplace fee is automatically deducted from the market price.

## Term Reference

<Callout type="note">
**Fair-split bid**: The price at which the winner and all non-winners end up with equal gold value. Use this when the party's primary goal is equity.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
**Max-gain bid**: The price that maximizes the winner's gold gain while still being below the fair-split amount. Only use this with prior party agreement. It will be lower than the fair-split bid.
</Callout>

### Example

Market price: 100,000 gold, 4-person party:
- Marketplace fee (5%): 5,000 gold
- Distributable amount: 95,000 gold
- **Fair-split bid: 71,250 gold** (all 4 members gain ~23,750 gold equally)
- **Max-gain bid: 64,772 gold** (winner maximizes gold profit)
