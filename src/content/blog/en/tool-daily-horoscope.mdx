# Daily Horoscope

Based on Western astrology's 12 zodiac signs, this horoscope offers today's overall fortune, love, money, and health readings for your sign.

Taking a moment before the day begins to check the energetic flow can provide a light directional nudge for daily planning. The overall fortune reflects the day's general energy; love covers relationships and emotions; money speaks to finances and decisions; and health addresses physical and mental condition.

**How to use your horoscope:**
- High score day → Take on important decisions, start new things
- Low score day → Move carefully, conserve energy
- Neutral score → Stay balanced, keep things calm
