## Introduction: The Times Have Changed

Do you remember "Aquarius" sung by hippies in the 60s?
Astrologically, Earth moves through a constellation approximately every 2,160 years (Precession of Equinoxes).
The last 2,000 years were the **Age of Pisces**. An era of believing and following great Gurus like Jesus and Buddha, hoping for sacrifice and salvation.
But now we have entered the **Age of Aquarius**.

## Core Concept: From Vertical to Horizontal

### Age of Pisces (Past)

- **Keywords:** Believe, Religion, Hierarchy, Sacrifice, Savior.
- **Structure:** God -> Priest -> Layman (Pyramid).
- "I am a sinner, save me."

### Age of Aquarius (Future)

- **Keywords:** Know, Science, Equality, Network, Individuality.
- **Structure:** Individual ↔ Individual (P2P Network).
- "I am a divine being. I Know myself."

## Deep Dive: Democratization of Spirituality

The biggest feature of the Age of Aquarius is the **'Disappearance of Intermediaries'**.
Now you don't need to go to church or temple to meet God. Just as you access global information via smartphone, you access the Cosmic Library (Akashic Record) directly through meditation and intuition.
The Internet (WWW) is the material manifestation of the Aquarian Age. A world where all information is shared and everyone is connected.
Now spirituality is not 'believing a specific religion', but **'Technology to understand and tune into Cosmic Principles'**.

## Practical Application: How to Live?

1.  **Do Not Blindly Believe:** Don't follow authorities or gurus blindly. Only accept what convinces you through your "Inner Verification System".
2.  **Love Uniqueness:** Aquarius is the sign of the 'Eccentric'. Living like others is a sin. Find your unique Vibe.
3.  **Solidarity:** Don't just live well alone, but contemplate what you can contribute to the Ascension of humanity's consciousness.

## Conclusion: You Are the Guru

Stop waiting for the Second Coming.
You are the Savior of your own life.
The Age of Aquarius is an era where we all put down our crosses and march into the cosmos hand in hand like friends.
