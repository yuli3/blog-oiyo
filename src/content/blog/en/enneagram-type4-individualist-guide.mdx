Enneagram Type 4 is known as **The Individualist** or **The Romantic**. They feel something fundamentally missing within themselves, and seek to fill that absence through beautiful expression and deep connection. At the center of their lives is an awareness: *"I am different and special — and that is both my gift and my curse."*

## Type 4 Core Profile

| Attribute | Description |
|------|------|
| Type Name | The Individualist / The Romantic / The Artist |
| Basic Desire | Identity, deep meaning, self-expression |
| Basic Fear | Having no identity or significance |
| Core Motivation | Understanding oneself and expressing a unique identity |
| Center | Feeling (Heart) Center |
| Worldview | "I am missing something that others have" |

## Personality Traits

### Strengths

**Deep Sensitivity**: Type 4 experiences the full spectrum of emotions. Both joy and sorrow are felt more intensely — that depth becomes the source of artistic creation.

**Pursuit of Authenticity**: Cannot tolerate the surface or superficial. Always seeks what is deeper and more truthful.

**Creativity & Aesthetic Sense**: Sees the world through a distinct lens. Originality comes naturally.

**Empathy**: Because they experience deep feelings themselves, they connect profoundly with others' pain.

**Meaning-Making**: Can transform even the most difficult experiences into meaningful stories.

### Weaknesses

**Excessive Self-Focus**: Can become overly absorbed in their own emotions and inner narrative.

**Romanticization**: Sees the present as incomplete and fixates on what is absent.

**Emotional Volatility**: Mood swings make them difficult to predict.

**Comparison Habit**: Envies what others have — relationships, talents, lives — and feels inherently deficient.

## Wing Types

### 3w4 (Three Wing)

- More extroverted and achievement-oriented
- Pursues success in a distinctive way
- Tension between image and authenticity
- Charismatic artist type

### 5w4 (Five Wing)

- More introverted and observational
- The philosophical artist type
- Loves solitude and deep reflection
- Original, innovative thinker

## Stress & Security Directions

| State | Direction | Characteristics |
|------|------|------|
| Under Stress | → Type 2 (Helper) | Becomes overly reliant on others or obsessively helpful |
| Feeling Secure | → Type 1 (Reformer) | Structures creativity through principles and discipline |

## Relationship Patterns

### Relationship Strengths

- Seeks deep, genuine connection
- Expresses romantic feelings in ways that move a partner
- Understands a partner's emotions profoundly

### Relationship Challenges

- Projects excessive ideals onto the relationship
- Yearns for an imagined perfect relationship more than the real one
- Emotional volatility can be exhausting for a partner

### Ideal Partner

Someone who understands Type 4's uniqueness and deep sensitivity, and can weather emotional storms while remaining grounded. A partner who genuinely sees their "I am different" as beautiful rather than a problem.

## Growth Guide for Type 4

1. **Stay in the present**: Dwell in this moment rather than in past wounds or future ideals.
2. **The ordinary holds meaning**: Everyday life can be beautiful even when it isn't dramatic.
3. **Express feelings through action**: Don't just feel — create, do, build.
4. **Stop comparing**: Instead of focusing on what others have, see what you already possess.
