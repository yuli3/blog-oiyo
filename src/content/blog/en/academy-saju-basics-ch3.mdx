## What Are the Earthly Branches?

The Earthly Branches classify **earth energy into 12 categories**. Positioned at the bottom of the four pillars, they represent **environmental, material, and subconscious energy**.

If the Heavenly Stems represent consciousness and psychology, the Earthly Branches symbolize **the actual environment of life, the body, and unconscious behavioral patterns**.

| Aspect | Heavenly Stems | Earthly Branches |
|---|---|---|
| Position | Top of the pillars | Bottom of the pillars |
| Energy | Conscious, psychological | Environmental, material |
| Level | Mind | Body, feet |
| Feature | Pure Five Element | Contains hidden stems (Jijanggan) |

## Complete Analysis of the 12 Earthly Branches

### 子 Ja — Rat 🐭

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Element | Water (水) · Yang (陽) |
| Season | Winter (Lunar 11th month) |
| Hour | 23:00–01:00 |
| Direction | North |

**Characteristics**: Ja-Water is a concentrated **pure Water energy**. Wise and sharp with excellent adaptability. Like a rat finding its way in the dark, has outstanding crisis response.

**Strengths**: Strategy, agility, adaptability, wit
**Watch out for**: Overly calculating tendency, anxiety

**Hidden stems (Jijanggan)**: Im/Gye Water (Im 20 days, Gye 10 days)

### 丑 Chuk — Ox 🐂

| Item | Content |
|---|---|
| Element | Earth (土) · Yin (陰) |
| Season | Late winter (Lunar 12th month) |
| Hour | 01:00–03:00 |

**Characteristics**: Chuk-Earth is **frozen earth in the cold of winter**. Like an ox, works silently and persistently moves toward goals. Slow but certain; once decided, sees it through.

**Strengths**: Patience, diligence, reliability, steadiness
**Watch out for**: Resistance to change, stubbornness, slow pace

**Hidden stems**: Gye/Sin/Gi (Gye 9 days, Sin 3 days, Gi 18 days)

## Major Combinations (合) and Clashes (沖)

### Six Combinations (六合) — mutually harmonizing pairs

| Combination | Elemental change |
|---|---|
| Ja-Chuk (子丑) | Water·Earth → Earth |
| In-Hae (寅亥) | Wood·Water → Wood |
| Myo-Sul (卯戌) | Wood·Earth → Fire |
| Jin-Yu (辰酉) | Earth·Metal → Metal |
| Sa-Sin (巳申) | Fire·Metal → Water |
| O-Mi (午未) | Fire·Earth → Fire·Earth |

### Three Combinations (三合) — powerful elemental unions

| Three-way combo | Resulting element |
|---|---|
| In-O-Sul (寅午戌) | Fire |
| Sin-Ja-Jin (申子辰) | Water |
| Sa-Yu-Chuk (巳酉丑) | Metal |
| Hae-Myo-Mi (亥卯未) | Wood |

### Six Clashes (六沖) — mutually conflicting pairs

| Clash | Influence |
|---|---|
| Ja-O (子午) | Relocation, job change, conflict |
| Chuk-Mi (丑未) | Relationship change, instability |
| In-Sin (寅申) | Accidents, collision, movement |
| Myo-Yu (卯酉) | Conflict, separation |
| Jin-Sul (辰戌) | Change, turmoil |
| Sa-Hae (巳亥) | Career change, relocation |

<Callout type="note">
**Understanding clashes**: A clash is not always negative. It is also an energy that sets stagnant things in motion. Sometimes a clash can dissolve something harmful.
</Callout>
