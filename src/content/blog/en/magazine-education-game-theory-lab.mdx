# Game Theory Interactive Lab

Game theory is not mathematics — it is the **structure of choice**.
In this lab, you'll see firsthand how strategies accumulate into outcomes.

---

## 1. Play the Prisoner's Dilemma

Alternate between cooperating and defecting to watch how the **cumulative score** shifts.
Switch the AI's strategy and observe how the outcome patterns change.

<PrisonersDilemma client:load />

<Callout type="tip">
**Observation point**: Tit-for-Tat is not revenge — it is **reflection**.
Mirroring the opponent's last move is a strategy with real power to sustain cooperation.
</Callout>
