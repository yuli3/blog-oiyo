Chakra is a Sanskrit word meaning **"wheel."** The concept comes from Ayurveda and yogic philosophy, which hold that the body contains seven major energy centers through which life force flows. Each chakra is linked to a specific area of the body along with particular emotions and psychological states.

## The Seven Chakras at a Glance

| Chakra | Location | Color | Element | Core Function |
|--------|------|------|------|---------|
| 1. Root (Muladhara) | Base of the spine | Red | Earth | Safety, survival, grounding |
| 2. Sacral (Svadhisthana) | Below the navel | Orange | Water | Creativity, emotion, pleasure |
| 3. Solar Plexus (Manipura) | Upper abdomen | Yellow | Fire | Willpower, confidence, personal power |
| 4. Heart (Anahata) | Center of the chest | Green/pink | Air | Love, empathy, connection |
| 5. Throat (Vishuddha) | Throat | Blue | Ether | Communication, truth, self-expression |
| 6. Third Eye (Ajna) | Between the eyebrows | Indigo | Light | Intuition, insight, wisdom |
| 7. Crown (Sahasrara) | Top of the head | Violet/white | Cosmic consciousness | Spirituality, connection, enlightenment |

---

## Chakra 1: The Root Chakra (Muladhara)

**Location**: Base of the spine, perineum
**Color**: Red
**Element**: Earth

### Function

The root chakra governs your survival instinct, sense of safety, and material stability. It's responsible for your feeling of being grounded, connected to the earth beneath you.

### Signs of Imbalance

| Overactive | Underactive |
|-----------|-----------|
| Greed, materialism | Anxiety, fear |
| Lethargy, laziness | Financial struggle |
| Excessive need for stability | Feeling disconnected from reality |

### How to Activate It

- **Connect with nature**: walk barefoot on the ground, garden
- **Crystals**: red jasper, hematite, black tourmaline
- **Foods**: red foods — apples, strawberries, beets
- **Mantra**: "LAM"
- **Yoga poses**: Mountain Pose, Child's Pose

---

## Chakra 2: The Sacral Chakra (Svadhisthana)

**Location**: About two inches below the navel
**Color**: Orange
**Element**: Water

### Function

This is the center of creativity, emotion, pleasure, sexuality, and relationships.

### Signs of Imbalance

| Overactive | Underactive |
|-----------|-----------|
| Extreme emotional swings | Emotional numbness |
| Tendency toward addiction | Lack of creativity |
| Over-dependence on others emotionally | Sexual repression |

### How to Activate It

- **Creative practice**: painting, music, dance, writing
- **Crystals**: carnelian, sunstone, moonstone
- **Mantra**: "VAM"
- **Yoga poses**: Butterfly Pose, hip openers

---

## Chakra 3: The Solar Plexus Chakra (Manipura)

**Location**: Upper abdomen
**Color**: Yellow
**Element**: Fire

### Function

This is the seat of personal power, confidence, willpower, and identity — the source of what people mean by "trusting your gut."

### Signs of Imbalance

| Overactive | Underactive |
|-----------|-----------|
| Domineering, aggressive | Low self-esteem |
| Perfectionism | Indecisiveness |
| Explosive anger | Chronic fatigue |

### How to Activate It

- **Physical training**: core exercises, martial arts, dance
- **Crystals**: citrine, tiger's eye, pyrite
- **Mantra**: "RAM"
- **Yoga poses**: Boat Pose, Warrior Pose

---

## Chakra 4: The Heart Chakra (Anahata)

**Location**: Center of the chest
**Color**: Green, pink
**Element**: Air

### Function

The center of love, empathy, compassion, and forgiveness. It bridges the three lower, more material chakras with the three upper, more spiritual ones.

<Callout type="info">
**Why the heart chakra matters so much**: sitting at the exact midpoint of the seven chakras, it's the chakra that has to open for your material self and spiritual self to integrate.
</Callout>

### Signs of Imbalance

| Overactive | Underactive |
|-----------|-----------|
| Clinginess, dependence | Emotional coldness |
| No sense of boundaries | Loneliness, isolation |
| Codependency in relationships | Inability to forgive |

### How to Activate It

- **Practice self-love**: self-care rituals, gratitude journaling
- **Crystals**: rose quartz, green aventurine, malachite
- **Mantra**: "YAM"
- **Breathwork**: heart breathing (hand on chest, deep breaths)

---

## Chakra 5: The Throat Chakra (Vishuddha)

**Location**: Throat
**Color**: Blue
**Element**: Ether/space

### Function

The center of communication, self-expression, honesty, and listening.

### Signs of Imbalance

| Overactive | Underactive |
|-----------|-----------|
| Talking excessively | Fear of speaking up |
| Gossip, dishonesty | Suppressed self-expression |
| Not listening to others | Throat or thyroid issues |

### How to Activate It

- **Self-expression**: journaling, singing, public speaking
- **Crystals**: blue lace agate, aquamarine, sodalite
- **Mantra**: "HAM"
- **Yoga poses**: Shoulder Stand, Fish Pose

---

## Chakra 6: The Third Eye Chakra (Ajna)

**Location**: Between the eyebrows
**Color**: Indigo
**Element**: Light

### Function

The center of intuition, insight, imagination, and wisdom — your inner knowing and spiritual vision.

### Signs of Imbalance

| Overactive | Underactive |
|-----------|-----------|
| Confusing fantasy with reality | Lack of intuition |
| Headaches | Rigid thinking |
| Escapism | Inability to see the bigger picture |

### How to Activate It

- **Meditation**: focus meditation on the point between the eyebrows
- **Crystals**: lapis lazuli, amethyst, fluorite
- **Mantra**: "OM"
- **Dream journaling**: record your dreams daily to develop intuition

---

## Chakra 7: The Crown Chakra (Sahasrara)

**Location**: Top of the head
**Color**: Violet, white
**Element**: Cosmic consciousness

### Function

The connection to universal consciousness, spiritual awakening, and a sense of purpose.

### Signs of Imbalance

| Overactive | Underactive |
|-----------|-----------|
| Loss of touch with reality | Spiritual emptiness |
| Excessive spiritual fixation | A sense that life lacks meaning |
| Rejection of the material world | Depression, cynicism |

### How to Activate It

- **Meditation**: silent meditation, focusing on pure being
- **Crystals**: amethyst, clear quartz, selenite
- **Mantra**: "OM" — resonates with every chakra
- **Nature**: stargazing to feel connected to the cosmos

---

## A Full Chakra-Balancing Meditation

1. Sit or lie down comfortably
2. Breathe in deeply, then breathe out
3. Start at the root chakra and move upward
4. Focus on each chakra's location, visualizing its color
5. Set the intention: "This chakra is fully open and balanced"
6. After reaching the crown chakra, move back down to close the practice

<Callout type="tip">
**Chakras and crystals**: placing the crystal associated with a chakra on your body during meditation can support its activation — for example, resting rose quartz on your chest during heart chakra meditation.
</Callout>
