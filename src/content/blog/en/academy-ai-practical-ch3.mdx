## Chapter 3 Overview: Why Read Financial Statements Alongside AI

Financial statements are a company's report card. Investors, founders, and employees alike need to read them, but dense accounting jargon and unfamiliar figures make that harder than it should be.

For financial statement analysis, AI acts as **an instant translator of what the numbers actually mean.**

<Callout type="info">
**Goal of this lecture**: use AI to quickly analyze the three core financial statements and build practical skills for diagnosing a company's profitability, stability, and growth potential.
</Callout>

---

## 1. The Three Core Financial Statements

| Statement | Key question | Main metrics |
|---|---|---|
| **Income Statement (P&L)** | "Is this company making money?" | Revenue, operating income, net income |
| **Balance Sheet (B/S)** | "What does this company own and owe?" | Assets, liabilities, equity |
| **Cash Flow Statement (C/F)** | "Is cash actually coming in?" | Operating, investing, and financing cash flows |

## 2. How to Analyze Financial Statements with AI

### 2-1. Income statement analysis prompt

```
Example prompt:
"Below is Company A's 2023 income statement.
Calculate profitability metrics (gross margin, operating margin,
net margin) and explain the key drivers behind the change
compared to the prior year (2022).

[Paste income statement figures]"
```

**Key profitability metrics AI can calculate for you:**

| Metric | Formula | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Gross margin | Gross profit ÷ Revenue × 100 | Profitability of the product/service itself |
| Operating margin | Operating income ÷ Revenue × 100 | Efficiency of core operations |
| Net margin | Net income ÷ Revenue × 100 | Bottom-line profitability |
| EBITDA margin | EBITDA ÷ Revenue × 100 | Cash-generating capacity |

### 2-2. Balance sheet analysis prompt

```
Example prompt:
"Based on the balance sheet below:
1. Calculate the debt-to-equity ratio and current ratio
2. Flag any warning signs from a financial-stability standpoint
3. Explain how this compares to the industry average

[Paste balance sheet figures]"
```

**Key stability metrics:**

| Metric | Formula | Healthy benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Debt-to-equity ratio | Total liabilities ÷ Equity × 100 | Under 200% |
| Current ratio | Current assets ÷ Current liabilities × 100 | Over 150% |
| Quick ratio | Quick assets ÷ Current liabilities × 100 | Over 100% |
| Equity ratio | Equity ÷ Total assets × 100 | Over 40% |

### 2-3. Cash flow statement analysis prompt

The cash flow statement has three sections. Ask AI to interpret the pattern across them.

```
Example prompt:
"Analyze the cash flow statement below. In particular, explain
why operating cash flow is negative and whether this company
looks like it's in a growth phase or a decline phase.

[Paste cash flow statement figures]"
```

**Interpreting cash flow patterns:**

| Operating CF | Investing CF | Financing CF | Company condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| + | − | − | Stable, mature company (ideal) |
| + | − | + | Investing for growth |
| − | − | + | Losing money, dependent on outside funding (risky) |
| + | + | − | Repaying debt by selling assets |

## 3. In Practice: A Quick Financial Health Check Before Investing

Before buying a stock, you can ask AI to analyze a company's last three years of financial statements in one pass.

```
Example prompt:
"Based on [Company]'s financial statements for the last three years,
analyze:
1. Profitability trend
2. Financial stability (debt levels)
3. Cash-generating capacity
4. Competitiveness versus peers
5. Potential investment risks"
```

<Callout type="warning">
AI's financial analysis is a **general analysis based on publicly available information**. It may not reflect insider information or the latest results, so always verify against official disclosure filings (SEC EDGAR, your market's equivalent regulator, etc.) before making an investment decision.
</Callout>

## 4. Using AI to Draft Financial Statements as a Founder

If you're in the early stages of starting a business, you can ask AI for a **simplified financial statement template**.

```
Example prompt:
"For a one-person startup with $4,000 in monthly revenue,
$1,600 in labor costs, $650 in rent, and $400 in other
fixed costs, create a simplified income statement template
and calculate the break-even point."
```

## 5. How to Ask AI to Explain Key Financial Terms

Whenever you hit a term you don't recognize while reading a financial statement, ask AI right away.

**Effective ways to ask:**

| Term | How to ask AI |
|---|---|
| EBITDA | "What exactly is EBITDA and why does it matter? Explain with a real example." |
| Depreciation | "Explain how depreciation affects cash flow, with a numeric example." |
| Deferred tax | "Explain in simple terms when deferred tax assets/liabilities arise." |
| Goodwill | "Explain why goodwill arises after an M&A deal and how impairment testing works." |
