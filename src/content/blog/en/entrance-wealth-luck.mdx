## Chi Rides the Wind

If the entrance is messy or blocked by shoes, good energy (Saeng-gi) gets blocked entering. The entrance must always be \*\*.

## Entrance Checklist

- \*\* If a mirror is directly in front when opening the door, it reflects away incoming luck. Place mirrors on the side.
- \*\* Dark entrances attract negative energy. Change the sensor light to a bright one.
- \*\* Don't pile umbrellas, golf clubs, or recycling trash at the entrance. It disturbs the flow of Chi.
