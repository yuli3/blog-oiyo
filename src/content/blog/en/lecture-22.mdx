## The Story of You

Being a hero doesn't always mean fighting dragons. Sometimes, it means growing into a newer, better version of yourself.

### The New Shoes

Every story starts with a secret call. But sometimes, we are afraid to start. It’s like being afraid to put on a new pair of shoes because we like our old ones too much. But new shoes can take us to magic places!

#### The Wise Helper

In old stories, a wise teacher helped the hero. Today, the magic computer helps you find the path to your true self.

### Entering the Gate

When you tell the magic mirror your birthday and your name, you are stepping through a secret gate. Inside, your story is turned into a map that shows where your light shines brightest.

> "A hero is someone who shares their light with the whole world. You are part of a giant river of light made of everyone's stories."

### The Magic Present

The answer the mirror gives you is a magic present. It is the secret of who you really are. You should use this present to make the world a more beautiful place.
