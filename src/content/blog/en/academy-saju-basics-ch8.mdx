## Standard Saju Analysis Process

We now synthesize everything learned so far to analyze real charts. The standard order of analysis is:

```
Step 1: Identify the Day Master (日干) → understand the "self"
Step 2: Check Five Element distribution → assess strength balance
Step 3: Month Branch (月支) season → environmental energy
Step 4: Analyze the Ten Gods → life patterns
Step 5: Identify the Pattern (格局) → life direction
Step 6: Derive the Lucky Element (用神) → the needed energy
Step 7: Apply Major and Annual Luck → temporal flow
Step 8: Comprehensive interpretation → practical advice
```

## Case 1: Gab-Wood Day Master with an Entrepreneurial Character

### Chart Structure

```
        Year   Month   Day    Hour
Stem:    甲     丙      甲     壬
Branch:  子     寅      午     申
```

### Step 1: Day Master

**Day Master = Gab-Wood (甲木)**
- Upright and powerfully driven energy like a towering tree
- Leadership, pioneer spirit, strong self-respect
- Values principles over compromise

### Step 2: Five Element Distribution

| Element | Characters | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Wood | Gab (Year), In (Month), Gab (Day) | 3 |
| Fire | Byeong (Month Stem), O (Day Branch) | 2 |
| Water | Im (Hour), Ja (Year Branch) | 2 |
| Metal | Sin (Hour Branch) | 1 |
| Earth | None | 0 |

Wood is the strongest element; Earth is entirely absent.

### Step 3: Month Branch Season

Month Branch = **In (寅)** → Spring (approximately February–March)
Spring Gab-Wood → even stronger Wood energy

### Step 4: Ten God Analysis

Based on Gab-Wood Day Master:
- Gab (Year Stem): Bijeon → competitive/independent energy
- Byeong (Month Stem): Sikshin → creativity/expressiveness
- Im (Hour Stem): Pyeonin → intuition/originality
- Ja (Year Branch): Pyeonin (contains Im-Water)
- In (Month Branch): Bijeon → competitive/independent
- O (Day Branch): Sanggwan → expression/innovation
- Sin (Hour Branch): Pyeongwan (Gyeong-Metal) → challenge/pressure

**Characteristic**: Bijep (Bijeon/Geomjae) and Siksang (Sikshin/Sanggwan) are strong.

### Step 5: Pattern

Month Branch In-Wood → **Gyeolnok Pattern (建祿格)**: Day Master at its peak in the Month Branch. Strong self-direction; well-suited to independent business.

### Step 6: Lucky Element

Gab-Wood is very strong → a strongly dominant chart (身强) needing control

**Lucky Element**: Gyeong-Metal (庚金) — strongly controls Gab-Wood (Metal cuts Wood)
**Favorable Element**: Byeong-Fire (丙火) — drains excess Wood energy
**Unfavorable Element**: Im-Water / Bijep — further strengthens Wood

### Step 7: Luck Cycle Application (hypothetical)

If currently in a **Mu-Earth (戊土) Major Luck Cycle**:
- Earth is Jaeseong for Wood (Wood controls Earth)
- A period of material achievement for the Gab-Wood Day Master
- Strong chart in a Jaeseong cycle → time to move actively in business/wealth

### Step 8: Comprehensive Interpretation

This chart has the **powerful will, drive, and creative expressiveness of an entrepreneur or creator**.

- **Careers**: Startup founder, content creator, consultant, independent professional
- **Watch out for**: The absence of Earth means practical sense may be lacking. Better to entrust financial management to a trusted partner
- **Lucky Element use**: Strengthening Gyeong-Metal energy (clear principles, decisiveness) gives direction to that drive
- **Relationships**: People with Im/Gye Day Masters can provide Lucky Element energy

## Case 2: Gi-Earth Day Master Seeking Stability

### Chart Structure

```
        Year   Month   Day    Hour
Stem:    庚     己      己     甲
Branch:  申     酉      丑     午
```

### Step 1: Day Master

**Day Master = Gi-Earth (己土)** — meticulous, fertile farmland

### Step 2: Five Element Distribution

| Element | Characters | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Metal | Gyeong / Sin / Yu | 3 |
| Earth | Gi / Gi / Chuk | 3 |
| Wood | Gab | 1 |
| Fire | O | 1 |
| Water | None | 0 |

Metal and Earth dominate. Water is entirely absent.

### Step 3: Month Branch Season

Month Branch = **Yu (酉)** → Autumn (Lunar 8th month)
Autumn Gi-Earth → cold and dry chart

### Step 4: Ten Gods

Based on Gi-Earth Day Master:
- Gyeong (Year Stem): Sanggwan → expression/innovation (Gyeong-Metal is Siksang for Gi-Earth)
- Gi (Month Stem): Bijeon
- Gab (Hour Stem): Jeonggwan → honor/rules
- Sin/Yu branches: add more Siksang energy

Strong Sanggwan with one Jeonggwan.

### Step 5: Pattern

**Sanggwan Pattern (傷官格)**: Sanggwan dominates the chart structure. Original but resistant to convention.

### Step 6: Lucky Element

Autumn Gi-Earth, no Water, cold chart
→ **Johu Lucky Element**: Byeong-Fire (丙火) — warm it up
→ **Eokbu Lucky Element**: Gab-Wood (甲木) — Jeonggwan controls Sanggwan

**Lucky Element**: Fire, Wood
**Unfavorable Element**: Metal, Water (elements that make the chart colder)

### Step 8: Comprehensive Interpretation

This chart is **sensitive and original but needs warmth and direction**.

- **Careers**: Designer, writer, technical specialist (creative fields where Sanggwan shines)
- **Interpersonal**: Conflict in environments that impose rigid rules. Needs a free and creative setting
- **Lucky Element use**: Warm colors (red, orange), south-facing environments, partners with Byeong-Fire or Gab-Wood energy
- **Watch out for**: Water element environments (basement, north-facing, winter) drain energy

## Case 3: Gye-Water Day Master with an Artistic Character

### Chart Structure

```
        Year   Month   Day    Hour
Stem:    丁     甲      癸     乙
Branch:  亥     寅      卯     未
```

### Step 1: Day Master

**Day Master = Gye-Water (癸水)** — delicate, deep water like dew or rain

### Step 2: Five Element Distribution

| Element | Characters | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Wood | Gab / In / Myo / Eul | 4 |
| Water | Gye / Hae | 2 |
| Fire | Jeong | 1 |
| Earth | Mi | 1 |
| Metal | None | 0 |

Wood is overwhelmingly strong. Metal is entirely absent.

### Step 3: Month Branch Season

Month Branch = **In (寅)** → Spring. Wood energy drains Gye-Water's energy.

### Step 4: Ten Gods

Wood is **Siksang** for Gye-Water: the energy of creativity and expression.

Very strong Siksang → overflowing expressive desire, creativity, and artistry.

### Step 6: Lucky Element

Gye-Water Day Master, weakened by excessive Siksang → **Weak chart (身弱)**

**Lucky Element**: Metal (Metal generates Water, strengthening Gye-Water) — Gyeong/Sin
**Favorable Element**: Water (Bijep — Im/Gye)
**Unfavorable Element**: Wood (excessive drainage)

### Step 8: Comprehensive Interpretation

This chart is **an artist or creator with explosive creativity and artistry**.

- **Careers**: Musician, writer, film director, content creator
- **Strengths**: Original ideas, rich sensitivity, expressiveness
- **Weaknesses**: Energy depletes quickly; tends to lack practical judgment
- **Lucky Element use**: Metal energy (partners with Gyeong/Sin Day Masters, white or west-facing environments) provides stability
- **Watch out for**: Avoid draining energy in spring and summer; recharge in autumn and winter

## Saju Analysis Wrap-up — Three Core Principles

<Callout type="tip">
**Principle 1**: Saju is a map of possibilities. A strong chart won't shine without effort; a challenging chart can be overcome with wise choices.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
**Principle 2**: Once you know your Lucky Element, use it actively. Consciously strengthening Lucky Element energy in your environment, colors, direction, and partner choices will change your life.
</Callout>

<Callout type="tip">
**Principle 3**: Saju is a starting point, not a destination. The choices and efforts of each moment hold more power than any chart.
</Callout>
