# Travel Budget Calculator

Setting a travel budget is the most important first step of trip planning. Breaking it down by category — instead of a vague "around $1,000" guess — prevents post-trip financial surprises and helps you make the most of every destination.

## Average Daily Budget by Destination (per person, USD)

| Destination | Budget | Mid-Range | Premium |
|---|---|---|---|
| Japan | $50–75 | $90–140 | $190+ |
| Thailand / Vietnam | $30–45 | $60–90 | $150+ |
| Europe | $75–115 | $150–230 | $380+ |
| United States | $90–135 | $190–300 | $520+ |
| Southeast Asia (Bali, etc.) | $35–55 | $75–120 | $200+ |
| Latin America | $40–60 | $80–130 | $220+ |

*Includes accommodation, food, and local transport — excludes international airfare*

## Budget-Saving Tips

<Callout type="tip">
**Flights**: Book 2–3 months in advance. Tuesday and Wednesday departures tend to be cheaper. Use Skyscanner's "whole month" view for the best rate.
</Callout>

| Category | How to Save |
|---|---|
| Flights | Book early, use miles, fly mid-week |
| Accommodation | Airbnb / hostels, stay slightly outside the city center |
| Food | Local markets, convenience stores, lunch set menus |
| Transport | Day transport passes, walking, cycling |
| Currency | Exchange via fintech apps, bank apps for best rates |

## Budget Allocation Guide (% of total)

| Category | Recommended % |
|---|---|
| Flights | 30–40% |
| Accommodation | 20–30% |
| Food | 15–20% |
| Local transport | 10–15% |
| Sightseeing / entry fees | 5–10% |
| Shopping / souvenirs | 5–10% |
| Emergency fund | 10% (essential!) |

<Callout type="warning">
**Travel insurance is non-negotiable.** It covers medical emergencies, lost baggage, and flight delays — and typically costs just $15–30 for a standard international trip.
</Callout>
