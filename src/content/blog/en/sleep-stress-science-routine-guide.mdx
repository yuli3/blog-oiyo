# Sleep isn't a willpower problem — it's a rhythm problem

When sleep won't come, it's easy to blame yourself. But sleep is shaped far more by circadian rhythm, light, caffeine, body temperature, and stress arousal than by willpower.

A sleep routine isn't a technique for engineering the perfect day. It's a technique for building a baseline you can return to, even on a day that fell apart.
