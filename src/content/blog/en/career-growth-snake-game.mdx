### Introduction: Career Management in an Era Where Standing Still Means Falling Behind

If the career of the past was a ladder — one rung at a time — the modern career resembles a **snake** that constantly changes direction in search of food. We must absorb new skills and knowledge to grow, but if that process moves too fast or veers in the wrong direction, we tangle ourselves up or crash into a wall and face a game over: burnout.

Today, through the classic snake game, let us explore how to grow in a healthy, sustainable way over the long arc of a career.

## 2. The Three Phases of Career Growth the Game Teaches

### ① Knowledge Accumulation (Learning Phase)
The first thing to do when the game starts is move toward knowledge. In the early stages of a career, the priority is building a broad base of capability by collecting a wide variety of "knowledge tiles." At this stage, your small size lets you change direction quickly — that **flexibility** is your greatest strength.

### ② Deepening Expertise (Specialization)
As your size grows, movement becomes heavier and there is more to consider. At the senior level, when real expertise has accumulated, the key is not simply consuming more knowledge — it is **optimizing your path** by predicting where the next valuable opportunity will appear. Undiscriminating growth can become the very chain that constrains you.

### ③ Risk Management and Breathing Room (Burnout Management)
The longer your tail, the more cramped the screen feels. As responsibilities compound in a career, stress management and creating space become essential. Rather than pushing to go faster, reading the whole board and moving steadily is the secret to a long run.

---

## 3. Tips for Growing Without Burning Out

Here is how to win the giant snake game known as professional life:

1.  **Direction over speed**: Rather than simply working harder, periodically check where you are actually heading.
2.  **Rest is a skill**: Before you feel the panic of near game over, deliberately slow your growth and recharge — schedule intentional "pause" time.
3.  **Network as board expansion**: Instead of growing in isolation, collaborate with colleagues to widen the space you have to move in.

## Conclusion: What Shape Is Your Career?

The most important thing in the snake game is to survive to the end and fill the board. We hope your career traces a beautiful arc — like the game you played today — sometimes turning sharply, sometimes moving with caution.

Wishing you growth all the way!
