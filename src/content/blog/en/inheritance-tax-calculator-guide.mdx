## What Is Inheritance Tax in Korea?

Korean inheritance tax (상속세) is levied on the total estate of the deceased — this is the **estate tax model**, taxing the entire estate before distribution. The heir(s) are responsible for payment.

<Callout type="info">
Korea's top inheritance tax rate of 50% is the second highest in the OECD, behind Japan (55%).
</Callout>

## How Korean Inheritance Tax Is Calculated

### Step 1: Calculate the Taxable Estate

> **Taxable Estate = Total Estate − Non-Taxable Assets − Debts − Funeral Expenses**

- **Non-Taxable**: Daily household items, ancestral rite items
- **Debts**: Deceased's outstanding liabilities
- **Funeral Expenses**: Minimum 5M KRW; actual costs up to 15M KRW

### Step 2: Apply Deductions

**Basic Deduction**: 2M KRW (always applied)

**Individual Deductions**:
| Deduction | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| Spouse Deduction | min(actual acquisition, 3B KRW) — minimum 500M guaranteed |
| Child Deduction | 50M KRW × number of children |
| Minor Child Deduction | (19 - age) × 10M KRW per child |

**Lump-Sum Deduction**: 500M KRW (choose whichever is larger: individual or lump-sum)

**Financial Asset Deduction**: 20% of financial assets, capped at 200M KRW

### Step 3: Apply Progressive Tax Rates

> **Tax Base = Taxable Estate − Total Deductions**

| Tax Base | Rate |
|----------|------|
| Up to 100M KRW | 10% |
| 100M to 500M | 20% |
| 500M to 1B | 30% |
| 1B to 3B | 40% |
| Over 3B | 50% |

## Legal Tax Planning Strategies

### 1. Lifetime Gifting

Korea aggregates gifts over 10-year periods. Strategic advance gifting reduces the taxable estate.
- Spousal gift deduction: 600M KRW per 10 years
- Direct descendant deduction: 50M KRW per 10 years

### 2. Maximize Spouse Deduction

The spouse deduction can reach up to 3B KRW based on actual acquisition amount. Structuring the estate so the spouse receives a larger share maximizes this deduction.

### 3. Family Business Deduction

If you operate a qualifying family business, a deduction of up to 50B KRW may apply. Requirements include 10+ years of operation and holding 50%+ equity.

<Callout type="warning">
Optimal tax planning varies significantly by individual circumstances. Always consult a qualified tax professional before making decisions.
