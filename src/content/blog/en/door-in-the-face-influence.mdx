## Reciprocity

Request 1: "Will you volunteer 2 hours every week for 2 years?" (No).
Request 2: "Will you volunteer just once for 2 hours?" (Yes).
By backing down from the large request, you appear to be making a concession. The other person feels obligated to reciprocate by agreeing to the smaller request.
