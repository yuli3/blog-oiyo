## 1. Introduction: The Monomyth

Joseph Campbell studied the myths of every culture—Native American, Greek, African, Norse. He found that they were all telling variations of the same story: **The Hero's Journey**.
It is not just a formula for movies; it is a psychological map for human growth.

---

## 2. Stage 1: The Call to Adventure

You are in the **Ordinary World**. Life is normal, boring, or stagnant.
Then, the **Call** comes.

- A job offer in a new city.
- A diagnosis.
- A breakup.
- A burning desire to write a book.

The Call disrupts your comfort. It demands you leave the Village.
Most people engage in **Refusal of the Call**. "I can't. I have a mortgage. It's too risky."

---

## 3. Stage 2: Initiation (The Belly of the Whale)

If you accept the call, you cross the **Threshold**.
You enter the **Special World** (the unknown). Rules are different here.

- **Tests and Enemies**: You face challenges. You fail.
- **Mentors**: You meet teachers (Obi-Wan, Yoda) who give you tools, but they cannot walk the path for you.
- **The Inmost Cave**: The deepest point of the journey. You must face your greatest fear (The Dragon/Shadow). You must die (metaphorically) to your old self.

---

## 4. Stage 3: The Return (Master of Two Worlds)

You defeat the dragon and seize the **Elixir** (Wisdom/Treasure).
Now you must return to the Ordinary World. This is hard. You have changed, but your old friends haven't. They don't understand what you've seen.
Your task is to integrate your new wisdom into your old life. You become the Mentor for others. You become the Master of Two Worlds.

---

## 5. Where Are You?

Look at your current struggles.

- Are you bored? You are waiting for a Call.
- Are you terrified and failing? You are in the Tests.
- Does it feel like everything is lost? You are in the Inmost Cave.
- Do you feel alienated from your old life? You are in the Return.

Knowing the map keeps you sane. "I am not lost; I am just in Chapter 2."

---

## 6. Conclusion: Follow Your Bliss

Campbell's advice: **"Follow your bliss."**
This doesn't mean "do what feels good." It means "follow the path that makes you feel alive."
The cave you fear to enter holds the treasure you seek.
