## TOEIC Score Level Guide

| Score Range | ETS Level | CEFR | Proficiency |
|---|---|---|---|
| 905–990 | Advanced | C1 | Advanced: understands and expresses complex content |
| 785–900 | High Intermediate | B2 | Upper-Intermediate: natural communication with native speakers |
| 605–780 | Intermediate | B1 | Intermediate: can handle familiar everyday topics |
| 405–600 | Low Intermediate | A2 | Elementary: basic expression possible |
| 205–400 | Elementary | A1–A2 | Beginner: limited communication |
| 10–200 | Beginner | A1 | Entry: very limited |

## Key Employment & Certification Thresholds (Korea)

| Organization / Role | Required Score |
|---|---|
| Samsung Electronics (open hiring) | 700+ (varies by position) |
| Hyundai Motor | 700–800 |
| SK Group | 700+ |
| Civil service (7th & 9th grade extra credit) | 700+ |
| Civil service (5th grade / diplomatic track) | Separate English test required |
| Average large corporation | **700–800** |
| Foreign companies | 850+ recommended |

<Callout type="tip">
**TOEIC vs. TOEIC Speaking / OPIc**: Speaking test requirements are growing in Korean hiring. More companies now require OPIc or TOEIC Speaking in addition to the written exam. Check requirements specific to your target employer.
</Callout>

## Score Validity

The official validity period is **2 years**. Most employers accept scores within 2 years; some public institutions require scores within 1 year.
