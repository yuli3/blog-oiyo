## IELTS Listening Strategy

### Listening Structure (30 min + 10 min transfer)

| Part | Scenario | Common Question Types |
|------|----------|----------------------|
| Part 1 | Everyday conversation (booking, enquiry) | Form/note completion |
| Part 2 | Public information monologue | Multiple choice, map labelling |
| Part 3 | Academic discussion (2–4 people) | Multiple choice, matching |
| Part 4 | Academic lecture (monologue) | Note/summary completion |

---

### Predictive Listening

**Use the pre-listening time (30 seconds) to**:
1. Identify question type (form fill, multiple choice, map, etc.)
2. Underline keywords in questions (proper nouns, numbers, capitals)
3. Predict what type of answer is needed (number, name, adjective, etc.)

**During the audio**:
- Write answers immediately as you hear them (answers appear in sequence)
- Mark uncertain answers; come back during transfer time
- Numbers and dates: write exactly what you hear — don't round or interpret

---

### Common Listening Traps

| Trap | Example |
|------|---------|
| Self-correction | "No wait, it's Tuesday, not Wednesday" → Tuesday is correct |
| Similar sounds | fifteen (15) vs. fifty (50) |
| Negation | "I don't think it works" → answer is the opposite |
| Distractor then correct answer | Speaker discusses wrong option, then confirms correct one |

## IELTS Reading Strategy

### Reading Structure (60 min, 40 questions)

- 3 passages × ~700–900 words
- Academic: scholarly texts from journals, books, newspapers
- General Training: notices, advertisements, practical texts + academic passage

---

### Scanning Technique

**Step-by-step**:
1. Scan title, headings, subheadings, and images (30 seconds)
2. Read the question — identify the key word or concept
3. Scan the passage for the keyword or a synonym
4. Read carefully only the relevant sentence(s) — then answer

---

### Question Type Strategies

| Type | Strategy |
|------|---------|
| True/False/Not Given | NOT GIVEN ≠ False. If topic not mentioned at all → NG |
| Matching Headings | Use first and last sentences of each paragraph |
| Summary Completion | Watch for paraphrasing — the text uses different words |
| Sentence Completion | Check grammar agreement (singular/plural, tense) |

### True/False/Not Given: The Critical Distinction

This is the question type most test-takers find hardest:
- **True**: The statement agrees with the passage
- **False**: The statement contradicts the passage (direct opposite stated)
- **Not Given**: The passage says nothing about this topic at all

Trap: Don't assume False just because you know the statement is factually wrong in the world — only the passage content matters.

---

## Time Management

**Recommended allocation (60 min)**:
- Passage 1 (easiest): 17 minutes
- Passage 2 (medium): 20 minutes
- Passage 3 (hardest): 23 minutes
