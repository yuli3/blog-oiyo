## What Is the GMAT?

The **GMAT (Graduate Management Admission Test)** is the world's leading assessment for business school admissions. Over 2,300 programs at universities worldwide accept GMAT scores, and it remains the preferred test for top MBA programs including Harvard Business School, Wharton, Stanford GSB, and INSEAD.

---

## GMAT Focus Edition: Current Structure

The **GMAT Focus Edition**, introduced in 2023, is shorter and more focused than its predecessor.

| Section | Questions | Time | Score Range |
|---------|-----------|------|-------------|
| Quantitative Reasoning | 21 | 45 min | 60–90 |
| Verbal Reasoning | 23 | 45 min | 60–90 |
| Data Insights | 20 | 45 min | 60–90 |
| **Total** | **64** | **2h 15m** | **205–805** |

**Total score range**: 205–805 (in 10-point increments)

**Section order**: Test-takers choose their preferred order from 3 options before exam day.

---

## Section Breakdown

### Quantitative Reasoning
Tests mathematical problem-solving — **no advanced math required**, but speed and accuracy matter.
- Question type: Problem Solving (5-choice multiple choice)
- Topics: Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, statistics
- No calculator allowed

### Verbal Reasoning
Tests **reading comprehension, logical reasoning, and critical thinking**.
- Reading Comprehension: Understand and draw inferences from passages
- Critical Reasoning: Analyze argument structure, evaluate premises and conclusions

### Data Insights
Combines the former Integrated Reasoning section with data analysis.
- Table Analysis, Graphics Interpretation, Multi-Source Reasoning
- **Calculator allowed** (built-in)

## GMAT vs. GRE for MBA Admissions

Most top MBA programs now accept both. Which should you choose?

| Factor | GMAT | GRE |
|--------|------|-----|
| Quant difficulty | Higher | Moderate |
| Verbal style | Logic-focused | Vocabulary-focused |
| MBA preference | Traditional favorite | Increasingly equal |
| Cost | ~$275 | ~$220 |

**Rule of thumb**: If you're strong in math and logical reasoning → GMAT. If vocabulary and reading are your strengths → GRE may suit you better.

---

## The GMAT Adaptive Algorithm

GMAT Focus Edition uses **computerized adaptive testing (CAT)**: each question's difficulty adjusts based on your previous answers. Getting difficult questions right matters more than easy ones. This means:
- Don't rush early — accuracy on harder questions boosts your score more
- Missing a question isn't fatal; how you respond to the next determines the trajectory
