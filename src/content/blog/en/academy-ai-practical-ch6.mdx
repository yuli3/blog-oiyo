## Chapter 6 Overview: AI Transforms Business Communication

The average office worker spends 2–4 hours a day writing emails and messages. Using AI to cut that time in half — while communicating more clearly and professionally — is entirely possible.

---

## 1. Writing Business Emails

### Prompts by purpose

```
Prompt (declining a proposal):
"Write an email politely declining a vendor's proposal.
- Reason for declining: [reason]
- Relationship: needs to stay intact
- Future possibility: keep the door open
- Tone: professional and warm"

Prompt (requesting collaboration):
"Write an email requesting collaboration from another team.
- What's being requested: [details]
- Expected contribution: [their role]
- Deadline: [date]
- Incentive: [mutual benefit]"
```

**Core principles by email type:**

| Type | Opening line | Key focus |
|---|---|---|
| Request email | Lead with the purpose | Spell out the benefit to them |
| Decline email | Open with thanks | Reason + alternative |
| Apology email | Apologize immediately | Concrete prevention plan |
| Status-update email | Conclusion first | PREP structure |

## 2. Automating Meeting Minutes

Paste a meeting recording or transcript into AI to auto-generate formal minutes.

```
Prompt:
"Turn the meeting content below into formal minutes.

Include:
- Date/time, attendees, agenda
- Summary of discussion (key points only)
- Decisions made (clearly stated)
- Action items (owner and deadline)
- Next meeting

[Paste meeting content]"
```

## 3. Optimizing Slack/Teams Messages

```
Prompt:
"Turn the content below into a Slack message.
- Urgency: [urgent/normal/low]
- Audience: [manager/team/everyone]
- Purpose: [announcement/request/status update]
- Length: 3 lines or fewer

Content: [what you want to convey]"
```

**Tone guide by channel:**

| Channel | Tone | Length |
|---|---|---|
| #urgent | Clear and concise | 1–2 lines |
| #general | Friendly and informative | 3–5 lines |
| #leadership-updates | Formal and structured | PREP format |
| #team channel | Casual and collaborative | Freeform |

---

## 4. Rehearsing Difficult Conversations

Before delivering sensitive feedback or handling a conflict, rehearse it with AI first.

```
Prompt:
"I need to deliver [message] to [person] in [situation].
Simulate the conversation so I can work toward a resolution
without reacting emotionally. Play the other person's role
too, and teach me how I should respond."
```
