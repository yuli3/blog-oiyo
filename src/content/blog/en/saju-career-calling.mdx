## Introduction: Why Do I Feel Empty Even When Working?

The biggest concern for modern people is "What work suits me?"
Even with a high salary or stable government job, people complain of emptiness. This is because **Job** and **Calling** do not match.
Saju clearly distinguishes these two.

## Core Concept: Survival vs Self-Actualization

### 1. Characters of Rice Bowl: Wealth (Jae) and Title (Gwan)

In a capitalist society, the first things we look at when choosing a job are Money (Jae) and Honor/Stability (Gwan).

- **Wealth (Jae):** "Does it make money?" (Result-oriented)
- **Title (Gwan):** "Do others recognize it? Is it stable?" (Signboard-oriented)
  Many choose jobs based on these two. But this is the realm of 'Survival'. It does not give fullness of soul.

### 2. Characters of Talent: Expression (Siksang)

- **Eating God/Wounded Officer:** "Am I having fun? Am I using my talent?"
- For people with strong Siksang, 'Fun of work' and 'Freedom' are more important than money. They get sick if trapped in an organization (Gwan).

### 3. Characters of Calling: Wisdom (Inseong) and Useful God (Yongsin)

- **Seal (Inseong):** "Is this work valuable? Does it contribute to the world?"
- **Useful God (Yongsin):** The nature that resolves the imbalance of my chart. When we use this, we feel "Alive".

## Deep Dive: Solving the Conflict (Job vs Calling)

### The Dilemma

"If I follow my calling, I'll starve. If I chase money, my soul will die."
Saju tells us the **Timing**.

- **Early Luck (Wealth/Title):** Time to build a social foundation. You must take care of the 'Rice Bowl'. Learn skills and save money even if you dislike it.
- **Mid-Life Luck (Yongsin):** Finally the time to live as yourself. If the foundation is laid, boldly switch to Calling. ("Second Life")

**Example:**
A person who needs 'Water (Wisdom)' in Saju but lived only using 'Fire (Passion)', burns out in their 40s. They must stop and switch to 'Water' work (studying, counseling) to heal.

## Practical Application: Utilizing My Saju

1.  **Wealth/Title Structure:** Organization, Business, Finance suit you. Don't look for 'Deep Meaning' here. Work professionally, and find Calling in hobbies after work. (Job = Job)
2.  **Expression/Wisdom Structure:** Cannot survive potential organization life. Must go as Freelancer, Artist, Educator, Counselor. Even if it doesn't make money right away, you succeed by building 'My Brand'. (Job = Self)
3.  **Change of Great Luck:** Career views change when Great Luck changes. The emptiness that comes then is a signal saying "Now seek Calling, not just Rice Bowl".

## Conclusion: Your Soul isn't Unemployed

Just because you don't have a workplace doesn't mean your soul is unemployed.
When the Universe sent you to this earth, it gave you a task (Calling).
Decode that code hidden in the eight characters. And beyond making a living, shoot for the stars.
