## Introduction: The Whole in the Part

Have you seen a Hologram?
A hologram made with laser light has a fascinating feature. If you cut the photo in half, you don't see half the picture, but **the whole picture, just smaller**. Even if cut again, down to a tiny fragment, it contains the information of the whole.
This is the principle of **"One is All, All is One"**.

## Core Concept: Brain and Universe are Holograms

Neuroscientist Karl Pribram and Physicist David Bohm proposed an amazing hypothesis.

- **Brain:** Memory is not stored in specific brain cells, but spread throughout the brain as waves (interference patterns).
- **Universe:** Space is not empty, but a Hologram Field full of information as waves.
  The 'material world' we see might be a **Projection** from the holographic field.

## Deep Dive: Indra's Net

In the ancient Buddhist scripture 'Avatamsaka Sutra' appears 'Indra's Net'.
In Indra's palace, there is an infinitely vast net, with a jewel at each knot.
Each jewel reflects every other jewel. **In one jewel, all other jewels (the entire universe) are reflected.**
This corresponds exactly to the principle of **Non-locality** in modern physics.
Two separated particles are instantly connected regardless of distance (Quantum Entanglement).
