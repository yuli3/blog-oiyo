## Eco-Driving Habits to Boost Efficiency

<Callout type="tip">
**Eco-driving** can improve fuel efficiency by up to 20%.
</Callout>

| Habit | Fuel Savings |
|---|---|
| Avoid aggressive acceleration/braking | +10–15% |
| Maintain correct tire pressure | +3–5% |
| Minimize A/C usage | +5–10% |
| Remove unnecessary trunk weight | +1–3% |
| Steady highway speed (100 km/h) | Optimal efficiency range |

## Ways to Cut Fuel Costs

| Method | How It Helps |
|---|---|
| Use self-service (pay-at-pump) stations | Significantly cheaper per liter / gallon |
| Fuel company credit card | Discount with partner cards |
| GasBuddy / Opinet equivalent | Find lowest nearby price |
| Fill up in the morning | Cooler fuel = slightly higher density |

<Callout type="note">
**Accurate efficiency measurement**: Fill the tank completely at the start, drive normally, then refill at the same pump. Record liters added and distance driven for a precise km/L calculation.
</Callout>
