# Japan & China Salary Calculators

When considering a job abroad, **net take-home pay** matters far more than the quoted gross salary. Japan and China have very different social insurance structures, so the same headline number can translate to very different monthly deposits. Use these calculators to see your net pay and compare it directly against a Korean salary in KRW.

## Tax & Social Insurance Comparison

| Item | South Korea | Japan | China |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top marginal income tax rate | 45% | 45% | 45% |
| Social insurance (employee share) | ~9.3% | ~14% | ~10.5% |
| Take-home ratio (average) | ~80–85% | ~75–80% | ~75–82% |
| Pension eligibility age | 62–65 | 65 | 60/55 |

<Callout type="tip">
**Japan salary benchmarks**  
- Entry level (graduate): ¥200,000–220,000/month  
- IT roles: ¥300,000–500,000/month  
- Senior corporate manager: ¥500,000–700,000/month  
- KRW estimate (¥100 ≈ ₩900): ¥200,000/month ≈ ₩1,800,000
</Callout>

<Callout type="note">
Tax calculations use each country's 2024 tax codes. Actual tax may vary depending on personal circumstances (dependents, deductions, etc.).
</Callout>
