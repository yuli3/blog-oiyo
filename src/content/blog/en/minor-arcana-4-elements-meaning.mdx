## 1. Wands: Energy of Fire

**Keywords:** Passion, Will, Action, Creation, Intuition
Wands are energy that starts and ignites something, like a magician's staff. If many of these cards appear, it is **a time to act**. You are busy with work or full of motivation to start a new project.

## 2. Cups: Energy of Water

**Keywords:** Emotion, Love, Relationship, Unconscious, Healing
Cups are vessels for the heart. They deal with all emotional experiences: falling in love, getting hurt, or receiving artistic inspiration. It is time to flow **where feeling leads** rather than logic.

## 3. Swords: Energy of Air

**Keywords:** Reason, Thought, Decision, Conflict, Stress
Swords are double-edged. You can solve problems with clear reason, but excessive thinking becomes pain that stabs us. Sword cards suggest you need **cool judgement** or need to escape from the prison of thoughts.

## 4. Pentacles: Energy of Earth

**Keywords:** Material, Reality, Money, Health, Stability
Pentacles deal with realistic problems of living with feet on the ground. They mean financial gain, physical health, and steady sincerity. It is time to stop dreaming and produce **something you can actually see**.
