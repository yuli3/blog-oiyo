## Monte Carlo, 1913

At a roulette table, black came up 26 times in a row. Gamblers lost millions betting on red, thinking "Red _must_ come up next!"
But the odds were always 50/50. (Or technically 48.6/48.6 due to the green zero).

## Independent Events

The dice have no memory. They do not know what happened on the last roll.
If you have 5 daughters, you are not "due" for a son. The chance is still ~50%.
