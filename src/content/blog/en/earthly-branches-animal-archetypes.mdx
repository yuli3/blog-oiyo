# The Secrets of Earthly Branches: The Soil of Reality

If the Heavenly Stems (天干) symbolize our pure thoughts and will, the **Earthly Branches (地支)** mean the ground we actually step on, the environment we are in, and our physical instincts. The Earthly Branches consist of twelve characters, which are **'segments of time'** symbolizing the 12 months of a year and the 12 hours of a day.

The Eastern sages compared these abstract energies of time to 12 species of animals to make them easier to understand. However, the essence lies not in animals but in the **'seasonal changes of the five elements'**.

---

## 1. The Beginning and Growth of Spring (Wood, 木)

- **In-Wood (寅木, Tiger)**: The powerful eruptive force of early spring. Like a sprout surging through the snow, it signifies fearless challenging spirit and independence.
- **Myo-Wood (卯木, Rabbit)**: Spring at its peak. Flexible generative power spreading in all directions. Has the power to decorate, arrange, and expand vitality.
- **Jin-Earth (辰土, Dragon)**: The intersection of spring and summer. Earth with ever-changing and grand ideals. Symbolizes the driving force connecting reality and ideals.

## 2. Diffusion and Heat of Summer (Fire, 火)

- **Sa-Fire (巳火, Snake)**: Brilliant changes of early summer. Signifies rapid information delivery, sharp intuition, and energy pursuing splendor.
- **Oh-Fire (午火, Horse)**: Summer at its peak. The purest passion and light surging unrestrainedly up and down. Symbolizes undisguised honesty and dynamism.
- **Mi-Earth (未土, Goat)**: The intersection of summer and autumn. Dry earth containing intense heat. Mature and patient energy that stops growth and prepares for fruition.

## 3. Decision and Fruition of Autumn (Metal, 金)

- **Shin-Metal (申金, Monkey)**: Hard condensation of early autumn. Signifies multifaceted talent, high adaptability, and a cold, agile energy focused on practical benefits.
- **Yu-Metal (酉金, Rooster)**: Autumn at its peak. A pure finished product with impurities removed. Meticulous and sharp character, like a jewel that clearly distinguishes right from wrong.
- **Sul-Earth (戌土, Dog)**: The intersection of autumn and winter. Dry earth storing fruition. Loyalty, responsibility, and conservative, reliable energy trying to protect the essence.

## 4. Condensation and Wisdom of Winter (Water, 水)

- **Hae-Water (亥水, Pig)**: Deep convergence of early winter. Signifies inclusive leadership accepting everything, wisdom containing the source of life, and artistic inspiration.
- **Ja-Water (子水, Rat)**: Winter at its peak. The seed of new life starting from the deepest and quietest place. Symbolizes precise thinking, strong survival instinct, and secret insight.
- **Chuk-Earth (丑土, Ox)**: The intersection of winter and spring. Wet earth preparing to break through the frozen ground. The crystallization of diligence, sincerity, and patience waiting silently for opportunity.

---

## Wrapping Up: Responding to the Rhythm of the Earth

Your Day Branch (the Earthly Branch of the day you were born) is the environment where you feel most comfortable and the practical attitude with which you face the world.

- If your Heavenly Stem is Fire but your Earthly Branch is Water, you may be a person who holds intense aspirations but moves very calmly and coolly in reality.
- Conversely, if your Heavenly Stem is Metal but your Earthly Branch is Wood, you may look cold on the outside but have a warmer heart than anyone else, dreaming of new beginnings.

The Earthly Branches are your **'rhythm of life'**. When we understand the seasonal wisdom shown by these twelve animals, we can finally stand on the ground and grow correctly toward the sky.
