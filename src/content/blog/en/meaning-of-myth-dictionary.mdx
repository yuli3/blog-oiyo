Myth is not an old tale. It is a **map of human psychology**. Zeus's hunger for power, Loki's mischief, Princess Bari's devotion — every mythological figure is a face of an archetype alive within us.
This dictionary organizes 100 figures from four civilizations with the same structure: core myth → archetypal reading.

## How to Read Through Archetypes

Jung called myths "dreams of the collective unconscious." If you feel unusually drawn to a certain figure, it may be a sign that the archetype is awakening in your life now.

- Notice whether the figure's **core desire** resembles your own.
- An uncomfortable figure often reflects the **shadow**: the self you have turned away from.
