## Model Portfolios by Investor Type

### Beginner (0–2 years): Index Core

**Allocation**:
- 70% VOO (S&P 500)
- 15% VEA (International Developed)
- 15% BND (US Bonds)

**Rules**:
- Monthly DCA (dollar-cost average)
- No individual stock picking
- Annual rebalancing only
- 5+ year minimum holding horizon

---

### Intermediate (2–5 years): Core + Satellite

**Core (70%)**:
- S&P 500 ETF (VOO or VTI)
- International ETF (VEA + VWO)

**Satellite (30%)**:
- 5–10 individual stocks based on fundamental analysis
- Each position ≤ 5% of total portfolio

**Selection criteria**:
- ROE > 15% consistently
- Positive free cash flow
- Competitive moat identifiable
- Reasonable valuation (PEG < 2, P/E below sector average)

---

### Advanced (5+ years): Multi-Strategy

**Allocation**:
- US Growth (AI/Tech/Healthcare): 30%
- US Value + Dividends: 25%
- International Developed: 15%
- Emerging Markets: 10%
- Bonds/Alternatives: 10%
- Cash (opportunistic): 10%

## 10 Rules of Successful Investing

1. **Not investing is the biggest risk** — inflation erodes uninvested cash
2. **Invest only in what you understand** — ignorance is never compensated by luck
3. **Diversification is required** — no single position >20% of portfolio
4. **Stop-losses protect capital** — discipline over ego
5. **Long holding periods beat frequent trading** — transaction costs and taxes compound
6. **Market timing is nearly impossible** — DCA removes the need to time
7. **Emotions are the market's most consistent enemy** — rules over feelings
8. **Compound interest rewards patience exponentially** — every year earlier matters
9. **By the time news breaks, the price has already moved** — don't chase
10. **Investing is a marathon, not a sprint** — think in decades

## Investment Buy Checklist

Before buying any stock or ETF:

**For ETFs**:
- [ ] Do I understand what this ETF tracks?
- [ ] Is the expense ratio ≤ 0.20%?
- [ ] Does it fit my asset allocation target?

**For Individual Stocks**:
- [ ] Can I describe this company's business in 2 sentences?
- [ ] Has the company grown earnings for 3+ consecutive years?
- [ ] Is the valuation reasonable relative to growth (PEG, P/E)?
- [ ] What could make me wrong? (Identify bear case)
- [ ] What is my stop-loss price?
- [ ] What is my target price (exit plan)?

---

## Sell Checklist

When to sell:
- [ ] Original investment thesis is no longer valid
- [ ] Fundamentals have materially deteriorated
- [ ] Stock has reached target price (partial trim)
- [ ] Stop-loss triggered (discipline over hope)
- [ ] Better opportunity identified and portfolio needs to fund it
- [ ] Rebalancing requires trimming overweight positions
