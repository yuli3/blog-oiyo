## Fake Rest vs. Real Rest

Lying on the sofa watching short-form videos for 3 hours is not rest. It is tremendous labor for the brain, constantly stimulating dopamine receptors. True rest is \*\*.

## Dopamine Detox Guide

Try quitting smartphones, processed foods, stimulating content, and shopping for just one day. At first, you might go crazy with boredom. That boredom is the signal that your brain is recovering. Enduring boredom sensitizes the brain's reward system, allowing you to find joy in simple things like the scent of flowers on a walk or a glass of water.

## Active Rest

Active rest is often more effective than passive rest (doing nothing). Analog activities that use hands and body, like light jogging, cooking, or drawing, wash away brain fatigue.\*\*
