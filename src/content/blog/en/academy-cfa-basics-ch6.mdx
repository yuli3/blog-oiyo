## CFA Level II: What Changes

Level II is widely considered the most difficult of the three CFA levels.

**Key differences from Level I**:
- **Item set (Vignette) format**: 6-question sets based on a case study. No standalone questions.
- **Depth**: Deeper quantitative analysis of topics introduced in Level I
- **Application**: Less memorization, more applying concepts to real-world scenarios
- **New topics**: Advanced equity valuation, fixed income analytics, derivatives pricing

**Pass rate**: Historically 40–50%, lower than Level I

---

## The Vignette Format

Each item set presents a 1–2 page case study followed by 6 questions. All questions in a set share the same case — but each question is largely independent.

**Reading strategy**: Read the questions first, then the vignette with the questions in mind. Mark which data points each question needs.

<Callout type="tip">
**The most common Level II mistake**: Misreading the vignette. A number buried in a footnote can be the key to the correct answer. Practice reading financial cases efficiently — identify and extract relevant data quickly.
</Callout>

## Ethics: Why It Matters More at Level II

CFA Institute's Code of Ethics and Standards of Professional Conduct becomes more nuanced at Level II. Questions present complex situations where multiple Standards conflict.

**Key areas**: Research objectivity, client priority, material non-public information (MNPI), performance presentation standards.

**Ethics strategy**: When in doubt, choose the option that most protects the client's interests and maintains market integrity.

## Level I to Level II: Study Roadmap

**After passing Level I**:
1. Rest 2–4 weeks (recovery matters)
2. Begin 6 months before exam date
3. Curriculum first, question bank second
4. Mock exams in final 2 months (simulate full exam conditions)
5. Emphasize Ethics in final weeks

**Study hours**: 300–350 hours recommended.

---

## Exam-Day Strategy

**Time management**: 4.5 hours, 88 questions (item sets). ~3 minutes per question.

**Don't abandon sets**: All 6 questions in an item set share the vignette. If you struggle with 1–2 questions, complete the remaining 4 from the same case before moving on.

**Educated guessing**: No penalty for wrong answers. Never leave a question blank.

**CFA Exam Ethics heuristic**: "If it feels wrong, it probably violates a standard." When multiple options seem compliant, choose the one that most actively protects client interests.

## Practice Quiz

**Q1. A CFA charterholder learns non-public information from a client about an upcoming merger. What is the correct course of action?**
> Do not trade on this information and do not tip others — this is material non-public information (MNPI). The appropriate action is to establish information barriers (a "firewall") and escalate to compliance. Even the appearance of insider trading violates Standard II(A) Material Nonpublic Information.

**Q2. Why does the item set format make Level II more challenging than Level I?**
> In Level I, each question is independent. In Level II, all questions in an item set reference a shared vignette — a multi-page case with many data points, some relevant and some distracting. Candidates must efficiently identify which data applies to each question while managing time across the lengthy case material. Misreading even one key detail can result in 2–3 wrong answers in a set.
