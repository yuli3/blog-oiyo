## Final Strategy Summary

| Section | Core Strategy | Common Mistake |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| Listening | Pre-read questions; answer in real time | Mishearing similar sounds |
| Reading | Scan for keywords; True/False/NG distinction | Confusing False and Not Given |
| Writing Task 1 | Overall trend first; specific data second | Including personal opinion |
| Writing Task 2 | Clear position + 2 body paragraphs | Under minimum word count |
| Speaking | IERI structure; conversational extension | Yes/No answers without elaboration |

## Band Score Improvement Roadmap

### 5.5 → 6.5
- Listening: Master Parts 1–2 (more predictable vocabulary)
- Reading: T/F/NG strategy intensive practice
- Writing: Hit minimum word counts consistently; basic essay structure
- Speaking: Part 1 fluency improvement

### 6.5 → 7.5
- Listening: Parts 3–4 academic vocabulary comprehension
- Reading: Matching headings and summary completion accuracy
- Writing: Varied vocabulary and grammar structures
- Speaking: Part 3 analytical opinions with specific examples

## IELTS One Skill Retake

Introduced in 2023, **One Skill Retake** allows you to:
- Retake ONE of the four sections within 60 days of your original test
- Combine the retake score with your original 3 section scores
- Receive a new certificate reflecting the combined result

**Strategic use**: If a single section (especially Writing or Speaking) fell below your target band while others were strong, One Skill Retake is far more cost-efficient than sitting the entire test again.

## Exam Day Playbook

### What to Bring
- Valid ID (passport for international testing)
- Pencil and eraser for Listening and Reading (where pencil answers are transferred)
- Earplugs if allowed at your test center

### Listening
- Verify headphones are working before the audio starts
- Use the 10-minute transfer time fully — recheck spellings and grammar

### Reading
- Never spend more than 5 minutes on a single question
- Mark uncertain answers, complete what you can, then return

### Writing
- Attempt Task 2 first (higher weighting)
- Count words at 150 and 250 marks to verify minimums

### Speaking
- Remember: this is a conversation, not a performance
- "Could you repeat the question?" is perfectly natural — use it if needed
- Extend every answer: reason + example + reflection

- [ ] Part 4 academic vocabulary refreshed

**Speaking**:
- [ ] 10 Part 2 topic cards practiced (1-min responses)
- [ ] 20 advanced vocabulary items used naturally in sentences
