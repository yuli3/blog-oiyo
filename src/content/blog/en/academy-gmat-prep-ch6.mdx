## GMAT Score Is the Floor, Not the Ceiling

A 750 GMAT score won't save a weak application. Conversely, compelling essays and a strong professional story can overcome a 680. The MBA application is a holistic evaluation: **GMAT + GPA + Essays + Recommendations + Interview**.

---

## The Why MBA Framework

Every essay ultimately answers: **"Why do you need an MBA — and why now?"**

**Career Narrative Structure**:
- **Past**: What have you accomplished, and what skills have you built?
- **Present**: Why are you at a career inflection point?
- **Future (short-term)**: Specific post-MBA role and industry (be concrete)
- **Future (long-term)**: Broader impact/goal
- **Why this school**: Specifically how this program bridges your past to your future

---

## School-Specific Essay Strategies

### Harvard Business School (HBS)
- Essay: "As we review your application, what more would you like us to know as we consider your candidacy?" (900 words)
- Strategy: This open prompt is intentionally personal. HBS wants to know the person behind the resume — choose a theme or story that no other part of your application covers.

### Wharton
- Essay 1: "How will you contribute to Wharton's collaborative learning model?" (500 words)
- Strategy: Name specific Wharton resources: clusters, labs, particular professors, relevant clubs. Generic essays are a red flag.

### Stanford GSB
- Essay 1: "What matters most to you, and why?" (650 words)
- The most introspective essay in MBA admissions. Don't list accomplishments — explore your core values, formative experiences, and genuine motivations.

## Letters of Recommendation

### Who to Ask
- Direct supervisor who knows your work closely (ideal)
- Someone who can speak specifically to your impact (numbers, outcomes, leadership)
- Avoid: Famous names who don't know you; skip the CEO if they can't describe your daily contributions

### How to Brief Your Recommenders
1. Share your WHY MBA story and target schools
2. Suggest 2–3 specific qualities you'd like emphasized
3. Provide concrete examples (STAR format) they can draw from
4. Give them at least 4–6 weeks lead time

## MBA Interview Preparation

### Common Questions & Frameworks

**"Walk me through your resume"**
→ Past → Present → Future (3-part narrative, 2–3 min)

**"Why this school?"**
→ Minimum 3 specific reasons (particular course, professor, club, employer relationship, cohort)

**"Tell me about a failure"**
→ Real failure + Genuine learning + Applied change

**"Describe your leadership style"**
→ STAR example: Situation, Task, Action (your specific decisions), Result

### Interview Types

| Format | Characteristic | Preparation |
|--------|----------------|-------------|
| Behavioral (most common) | "Tell me about a time when…" | Prepare 8–10 STAR stories |
| Case discussion (HBS) | Discuss a business scenario | Practice thinking aloud |
| Informational / Blind | Only your resume, no application | Know your resume cold |
