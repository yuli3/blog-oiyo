## Key Astro Events — June 2026

June wraps the first half of 2026 and pivots into the second. The biggest astrological event: **Jupiter moves from Gemini into Cancer** around June 9, beginning roughly one year of home, emotion, and inner-growth luck across all signs.

| Event | Date | Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Jupiter enters Cancer | ~Jun 9 | Home, family, and inner-life luck begins |
| Summer Solstice | Jun 21 | Sun enters Cancer — energy peaks |
| Capricorn Full Moon | Early Jun | Career and goals check-in |
| Mercury in Gemini | Mid Jun | Communication and contracts favored |

<Callout type="tip">
**June Theme**: Home, family, and emotional depth. Use this month to review your first half and set clear intentions for the second — Jupiter in Cancer rewards exactly that kind of thoughtful planning.
</Callout>

## Aries ♈ (Mar 21–Apr 19)

### Overall
Jupiter entering Cancer activates your **4th House (home, roots, security)**. After months of outward action, June asks you to come home — literally and emotionally. Recharge, renovate, and reconnect with family.

### Love
Domestic and nurturing qualities become your most attractive traits. Inviting a partner into your home world deepens the connection.

### Money
Home-related expenses are likely. Budget for them. An unexpected income opportunity may arrive near month's end.

### Career
Remote work and home-office improvements boost productivity. June is preparation for the ambitious H2 push.
