## Maturity by Rank

- **Page:** "Curious Child." Starting stage, learning attitude, pure but immature. Plays the role of delivering messages.
- **Knight:** "Energetic Young Adult." Energy rushing towards a goal. Fast and powerful, but sometimes reckless and hasty.
- **Queen:** "Embracing Mother." Inner maturity, emotional understanding, power to maintain and manage. Strong Water tendency.
- **King:** "Ruling Father." Authority, responsibility, social success, control. Strong Fire or Air tendency, being dominant.

## Practical Reading Tips

"Knight of Cups within Love Luck!" -> Someone might make a romantic proposal or approach like a Prince Charming.
"Queen of Swords within Career Luck!" -> A piece of advice to exclude personal feelings and handle work coldly and professionally.
