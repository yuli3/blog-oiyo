## The Core Investment Principle in a K-Shaped Economy

There's one key insight to a K-shaped economy: **your investment outcome depends less on the direction of the economy as a whole, and more on which group within the economy you belong to.**

### Positioning at the Top of the K

This strategy focuses on the asset classes and sectors sitting at the "top" of the K.

**Big Tech and AI stocks:** less sensitive to the rate cycle, with structural growth that keeps going.

**Real estate (selectively):** enter during rate-cutting periods, take profits when rates rise — a tactical approach that rides the rate cycle.

**High-quality, high-dividend stocks:** as income inequality deepens in a K-shaped economy, stable cash-flow assets become more valuable.

### Betting on a Change in the K-Shape

The more extreme the K-shape gets, the greater the political pressure — and eventually, redistributive policy follows. It's worth holding a position that prepares for this "reset scenario."

**Small-cap index ETFs:** benefit from both rate cuts and redistribution policy at once.
**Consumer staples:** defensive in a downturn, with stable demand even as the K-shape widens.

### The Core Principle

Diversify, but keep watching which side of the K is currently benefiting. Use policy signals — rates, taxes, subsidies — as leading indicators.
