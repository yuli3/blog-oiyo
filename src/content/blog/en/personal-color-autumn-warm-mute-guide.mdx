**Autumn Warm Mute** is the palette of the forest after the bright colors have been absorbed back into the earth — the olive of lichen on stone, the terracotta of dried leaves, the mustard of late grasses, the warm grey-green of sage. This is not the vivid autumn of October's peak colors, but the deeper, dustier autumn that comes after — quieter, richer, and in its own way more profound.

## What Is Autumn Warm Mute?

Autumn Warm Mute combines **warm undertone** (golden-yellow in the skin) with **lower saturation and medium contrast**. Unlike the vivid autumn types, Autumn Mute's colors are muted — earthy, dusty, slightly greyish warm tones rather than bright, clear warm colors.

**Characteristic features:**
- Skin: warm beige, golden-olive, or warm ivory with slight yellowish or olive quality; typically deeper than spring types
- Eyes: warm hazel, golden brown, amber, or deep warm brown; often complex multi-colored eyes
- Hair: warm brown, auburn, dark warm brown, or chestnut; typically has warmth and depth
- Overall impression: rich, earthy, quietly sophisticated; warm but not vivid

**The key**: the warm-dusty quality. Autumn Mute colors are all warm at the base but muted — they have that earthy, organic quality of natural materials (wood, clay, moss, stone).

## Your Signature Colors

### Power Colors

**Olive Green** — the signature Autumn Mute color. This yellow-grey-green exists almost nowhere in the human complexion except on this type, where it appears completely natural. Other types often cannot wear olive at all; Autumn Mute wears it like skin.

**Terracotta** — the orange-red of clay, of pottery, of desert earth. Not the vivid orange-red of Autumn Vivid, but the slightly dusty, earthy version with more brown in it.

**Mustard Yellow** — golden yellow pushed toward amber; slightly muted. This is distinct from the bright yellow of Spring — Autumn Mute's yellow is richer, heavier, more golden.

**Warm Camel** — the light tan of natural leather; warm and golden-beige. A staple neutral.

**Rust** — orange-brown, the color of dried iron; one of Autumn's most distinctive colors.

**Warm Forest Green** — dark green with warmth; the green of deep moss and pine shadows.

### Neutrals

- **Dark Warm Brown** — the Autumn Mute version of black; used for depth and grounding
- **Camel** — the primary neutral; versatile and unfailingly flattering
- **Warm Greige** — grey-beige with yellow-warm undertone

### Colors to Approach Carefully

- **Cool colors** (cool pink, lavender, blue-grey) — the warm undertone requirement means these will look slightly discordant
- **Very bright, high-saturation colors** — the muted palette is overwhelmed by vivid clarity
- **Black** — can work but loses the warmth that makes this type glow; substitute dark warm brown

## Makeup

### Foundation and Base
**Warm-toned foundations** with golden or olive undertone. Autumn Mute skin often has a slightly olive quality that makes neutral foundations appear too pink. Look for foundations labeled "warm," "golden," or "olive." The right shade makes skin look healthy and luminous; the wrong shade (too pink, too neutral) creates a grey undertone.

### Eyes

**Eyeshadow**: The Autumn Mute eye palette is extraordinarily rich — warm brown, terracotta, bronze, olive, golden, warm taupe, muted copper. Eye looks in these earthy warm tones appear completely natural and deeply flattering on Autumn Mute eyes (which are themselves typically warm amber or hazel).

**Eyeliner**: Dark warm brown and bronze-black are ideal. Black works but can be slightly cold; warm brown is more natural.

**Mascara**: Brown-black or warm black.

### Cheeks

Warm peach-brown and terracotta blush are the Autumn Mute signatures — these earthy warm tones blend seamlessly with the golden-olive skin and create a sun-kissed depth rather than a pink flush.

### Lips

- **Terracotta** — the most distinctively Autumn Mute lip color; earthy, warm, uniquely flattering
- **Warm nude** — the golden-beige nude that is deeper and warmer than Spring nude
- **Muted warm coral** — warm coral with enough brown to mute it
- **Warm brick red** — deeper, earthier version of red; more brown-orange than blue-red
- **Avoid**: cool pink, cool berry, clear bright red (these pull cool against warm skin)

## Fashion and Wardrobe

### Building the Autumn Warm Mute Wardrobe

The Autumn Mute wardrobe is perhaps the most coherent and wearable of all personal color wardrobes — the colors all share an earthy warmth that makes everything coordinate naturally, and the overall effect is of organic, sophisticated richness.

**Core wardrobe colors:**
- Olive (the anchor color; extraordinary on this type)
- Camel / warm tan
- Terracotta
- Mustard
- Warm forest green
- Rust
- Dark warm brown
- Warm burgundy (muted, not vivid)
- Warm cream / warm off-white

**The Autumn Mute capsule wardrobe** photographs beautifully in natural settings — it looks completely organic in warm natural light, in forested environments, near natural materials. The wardrobe has the quality of belonging to the earth.

**Pattern notes**: Earth-toned prints (botanical prints, abstract warm patterns), animal prints in warm earth tones (leopard, warm-toned snake). The warmth and richness of the palette suits organic, non-geometric patterns particularly well.

**Metal jewelry**: Gold — specifically warm, slightly matte or antique gold. Rose gold also works. Silver and cool metals look slightly discordant.

**Fabric suggestions**: Natural, matte fabrics suit this palette well — linen, suede, matte leather, cashmere, raw silk. These materials share the organic quality of the colors.

## Hair Color

**Most flattering:**
- Natural warm brown (chestnut, mahogany)
- Auburn / warm red-brown
- Dark brown with warm highlights
- Golden-brown

**Approach carefully:**
- Cool ash tones — fight the warm undertone
- Very bright, vivid red — can overwhelm the muted palette
- Platinum blonde — creates a discordant contrast with the warm, deep features

Autumn Warm Mute is the palette of the earth itself — the colors of ground and bark and seed, the colors that were there before anything grew and will remain after everything falls. There is a reliability to this palette, a depth that doesn't fade or trend because it is not fashion: it is nature.

When Autumn Mute wears its colors, the effect is of someone who is completely at home in themselves — not trying, not performing, simply present in a warmth that is entirely their own. Olive on the right person doesn't look like a choice. It looks like home.
