## The Future is Not Fixed

If a Tarot reading says "You will be rich", it feels good, but it builds a passive attitude. A healthy reading is a \*\* like "Your current mindset helps attract wealth".

## Daily Draw Routine

Draw one card every morning.

- \*\* "What mindset do I need today?"
- \*\* What catches your eye first in the image? How does that symbol relate to my situation?
- \*\* Write down intuitive thoughts in a notebook. This is your inner voice.

## Synchronicity

The experience where a randomly drawn card fits my situation perfectly. Carl Jung called this Synchronicity. It's like a wink from the universe.
