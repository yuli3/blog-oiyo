## The Future is Not Fixed

If a Tarot reading says "You will be rich", it feels good, but it builds a passive attitude. A healthy reading is a **a reading that opens a question** like "Your current mindset helps attract wealth".

## Daily Draw Routine

Draw one card every morning.

- **Ask a question:** "What mindset do I need today?"
- **Look at the image:** What catches your eye first in the image? How does that symbol relate to my situation?
- **Write it down:** Write down intuitive thoughts in a notebook. This is your inner voice.

## Synchronicity

The experience where a randomly drawn card fits my situation perfectly. Carl Jung called this Synchronicity. It's like a wink from the universe.
