## Salary Increase Benchmarks (Global Reference, 2024)

| Category | Average Raise |
|---|---|
| Large corporations (global average) | 3–6% |
| Mid-size companies | 2–5% |
| High-growth startups | 10–20%+ |
| Job change negotiation | **+15–30% over current** is achievable |
| Consumer price inflation (2024 global) | ~2.5–3.5% |

*Ranges vary significantly by country, industry, and seniority level.*

<Callout type="tip">
**Job change = biggest raise**: Statistically, changing jobs yields an average 20% salary increase — far above the typical 3–5% internal raise. Use job offers as a strategic negotiation lever.
</Callout>

## Negotiation Preparation Checklist

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Market rate research | LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Levels.fyi, local job boards |
| Quantify achievements | List contributions you can back with numbers |
| Secure your BATNA | A competing offer or clear willingness to move |
| Set a target range | Floor / target / ideal — three tiers |
| Timing | Right after a performance review or project completion |

## Negotiation Language: What to Say

**Weak approach**  
*"I'd like a raise — living costs are getting high."*

**Strong approach**  
*"In last quarter's project I contributed to a 15% revenue increase and an 8% improvement in customer retention. Based on market data, the going rate for my role and experience level is [X]. I'd like to request a [Y]% increase from my current salary."*

<Callout type="note">
Take-home pay is reduced by social insurance contributions (pension, health, unemployment) and income tax. Exact amounts vary by country — typically 75–85% of gross salary reaches your bank account.
</Callout>
