## Growing Pains of 7-Year Cycles

While orbiting once (approx. 29.5 years), Saturn checks us by forming 90-degree (Square) and 180-degree (Opposition) angles.

- \*\* Losing baby teeth, going to school, experiencing first socialization (discipline).
- \*\* Puberty, pain of rebellion and ego formation.
- \*\* First independence as an adult and fear of entering society.
- \*\* Major surgery to live "my real life".

## Saturn's Gift: Structure

We feel pressure when Saturn comes. But pressure is needed to create diamonds. Saturn is a grateful architect who removes vain bubbles and builds a sturdy frame.
