## What Is ACCA?

**ACCA (Association of Chartered Certified Accountants)** is one of the world's largest and most respected accountancy bodies, headquartered in the UK. With members in over 180 countries and approximately 240,000 members plus 550,000+ students globally, ACCA is the premier international accountancy qualification.

**Why ACCA?**
- Globally recognized, especially in the UK, Ireland, Hong Kong, Singapore, Dubai, and across Africa
- Pathway into Big 4 (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG) global offices
- Comprehensive coverage of financial reporting, management accounting, audit, tax, and corporate law

---

## ACCA Exam Structure

ACCA comprises three levels and 13 papers.

### Applied Knowledge — 3 Papers

| Paper | Code | Content |
|-------|------|---------|
| Business Technology | BT | IT, organizations, ethics |
| Management Accounting | MA | Cost accounting, budgeting |
| Financial Accounting | FA | Financial accounting fundamentals |

### Applied Skills — 6 Papers

| Paper | Code | Content |
|-------|------|---------|
| Corporate & Business Law | LW | Contract law, company law |
| Performance Management | PM | Management control, KPIs |
| Taxation | TX | UK taxation (country variants available) |
| Financial Reporting | FR | IFRS financial reporting |
| Audit and Assurance | AA | Audit principles and procedures |
| Financial Management | FM | Corporate finance, cost of capital |

### Strategic Professional — 4 Papers

**2 Compulsory papers**:
- **Strategic Business Reporting (SBR)**: Advanced IFRS, group accounts
- **Strategic Business Leader (SBL)**: Integrated business strategy case study

**Choose 2 Optional papers** from:
- Advanced Financial Management (AFM)
- Advanced Performance Management (APM)
- Advanced Taxation (ATX)
- Advanced Audit and Assurance (AAA)

## ACCA Membership Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|------------|--------|
| Exams | Pass all 13 papers |
| Work experience | 3 years of relevant practical experience |
| Online module | Ethics and Professional Skills (EPS) completion |
| Annual subscription | Maintain ongoing membership |

## Exemptions: Fast-Tracking Your ACCA

One of ACCA's most valuable features is its **exemption system**:
- Relevant degree holders: up to 9 paper exemptions
- Holders of other professional qualifications (e.g., CIMA, ICAEW, KICPA): specific exemptions available under Mutual Recognition Agreements

## Global Career Value

ACCA opens doors in:
- **Big 4 global offices**: Standard credential for international transfers
- **Financial services**: Investment banks, asset managers, insurance companies in London, Singapore, Hong Kong
- **Industry**: CFO-track roles at multinationals
- **Public sector**: Government finance, development organizations (World Bank, IMF)

Average ACCA-qualified accountant salary ranges:
- UK: £35,000–£65,000 (newly qualified to senior manager)
- Hong Kong / Singapore: HK\$450,000–HK\$900,000 / S\$70,000–S\$120,000

---

## Recommended Study Order

**Beginner path**: BT → MA → FA → (Applied Skills in order) → Strategic Professional

**Time to complete**: Typically 3–5 years for most students working full-time

**Pass rates**: Applied Knowledge ~80%; Applied Skills ~40–55%; Strategic Professional ~30–50%
