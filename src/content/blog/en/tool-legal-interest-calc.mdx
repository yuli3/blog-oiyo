# Korea Legal Interest Calculator

Use this when no interest rate is specified in a contract, or when calculating delay damages in a legal dispute.

## Korea's Statutory Interest Rates

| Category | Rate | Legal Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Civil statutory rate | 5% per annum | Civil Act Article 379 |
| Commercial statutory rate | 6% per annum | Commercial Act Article 54 |
| Litigation special rate | 12% per annum | Special Act on Promotion of Litigation, Article 3 |

**Which rate applies when?**

- **Civil**: Loans between individuals, unjust enrichment repayment, etc.
- **Commercial**: Transactions between business entities, commercial debt/credit
- **Litigation special**: Delay interest on amounts unpaid after a court judgment (12% since June 2019)

## Common Scenarios

| Situation | Applicable Rate |
|---|---|
| Personal loan not repaid (individuals) | Civil 5% |
| Unpaid trade receivables (businesses) | Commercial 6% |
| Delay in payment after court judgment | Litigation special 12% |
| Unpaid wages or severance pay | Minimum civil 5% (may vary by precedent) |
| Tort damages (delay interest) | Civil 5% from incident → 12% after judgment |
