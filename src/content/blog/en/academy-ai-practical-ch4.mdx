## Chapter 4 Overview: How AI Is Transforming Cover Letter Writing

One of the areas where job seekers can use AI most effectively is **writing a cover letter**. AI can analyze a job posting, help you frame your experience, and craft sentences that raise your odds of getting hired.

<Callout type="info">
**Goal of this lecture**: use AI to identify the key requirements in a job posting, connect them to your own experience, and write a cover letter with a strong chance of landing you an interview.
</Callout>

---

## 1. Job Posting Analysis — Get the Gist in 5 Minutes with AI

### Job posting analysis prompt

```
Prompt:
"Analyze the job posting below.
1. The top 5 skills this company clearly values most
2. Keywords that repeat frequently
3. Which items are 'preferred' vs. 'required'
4. What this role is likely expected to accomplish in the first 3 months
5. Keywords that describe this company's culture

[Paste the full job posting]"
```

**How to use the analysis:**
- Top 5 skills → use as subheadings for each section of your cover letter
- Keywords → weave naturally throughout the letter
- Company culture → reflect in your "why I'm applying" paragraph

---

## 2. Organizing Your Experience — Using AI with the STAR Framework

### Extracting experience with the STAR framework

```
Prompt:
"Help me organize my experience using the STAR framework
(Situation-Task-Action-Result).

My experience: [describe freely]

Connect this experience to the key skills from the job posting
above and organize it using STAR. If I don't have exact numbers,
suggest reasonable estimates."
```

**Example STAR structure:**

| Component | Content |
|---|---|
| **S** (Situation) | A team project was two weeks behind schedule |
| **T** (Task) | Responsible for getting the timeline back on track and rebuilding team morale |
| **A** (Action) | Introduced daily stand-ups, re-prioritized the backlog |
| **R** (Result) | Schedule normalized within 3 days; team satisfaction rose 40% |

---

## 3. Drafting the Cover Letter

```
Prompt:
"Based on the job posting analysis and my STAR experience above,
write the 'personal growth' section of my cover letter (about
150 words).

Requirements:
- Naturally include the company's keywords
- Include specific numbers
- End with a sentence connecting to why I'm applying here
- No exaggeration — everything must be fact-based"
```

---

## 4. Feedback and Revision

After drafting, ask AI to critique your own work.

```
Prompt:
"Evaluate the cover letter above from the perspective of a hiring
manager.
1. Which parts are least persuasive
2. What specific details would strengthen it
3. Which clichés should be cut
4. An overall score (out of 10) and how to improve it"
```

---

## 5. Preparing for Likely Interview Questions

Once your cover letter is done, use AI to get ahead of likely interview questions.

```
Prompt:
"Based on my finished cover letter and the job posting,
suggest 10 questions I'm likely to be asked in the interview,
and outline how I should approach answering each one."
```

---

## A Word of Caution on AI Cover Letters

<Callout type="warning">
**Never submit AI-generated text as-is**: any cover letter AI drafts for you must be revised in your own words, grounded in your real experience. Hiring managers can often recognize the telltale style of AI-written text.
</Callout>

**The right way to use AI:**
- AI = structure, direction, and a source of feedback
- The actual content, emotion, and experience = written by you
