# Jeonse ↔ Monthly Rent Converter

Use this when converting from jeonse to a partial-deposit monthly rent arrangement (반전세), or when calculating the equivalent jeonse deposit for a given monthly rent. Useful for both landlords and tenants.

## The Conversion Formula

**Monthly rent = (Jeonse deposit gap × conversion rate) ÷ 12**

<Callout type="tip">
**Legal conversion rate cap**: Under Article 7-2 of Korea's Housing Lease Protection Act, the maximum conversion rate is **Bank of Korea base rate + 2 percentage points**. At a base rate of 3.5% (as of 2024), the cap is **5.5%**.
</Callout>

**Example**: Converting ₩300M jeonse → ₩100M deposit + monthly rent  
- Amount converted: ₩200M  
- At 5.5%: ₩200M × 5.5% ÷ 12 = **approx. ₩917,000/month**

## Jeonse vs. Monthly Rent Comparison

| Factor | Jeonse | Monthly Rent |
|---|---|---|
| Upfront cost | Very high | Low |
| Monthly cash outflow | None | Ongoing rent |
| Use of lump sum | Tied up | Free to invest |
| Sensitivity to interest rates | High (if financed) | Lower direct impact |
| Best when | Large lump sum available, rent > loan interest | No lump sum, or rates are high |

## Break-Even Analysis

Compare jeonse loan interest vs. monthly rent:

- **Jeonse loan interest** = Loan amount × interest rate ÷ 12
- **Monthly rent** = Negotiated monthly amount

If jeonse loan interest < monthly rent → **Jeonse is cheaper**  
If jeonse loan interest > monthly rent → **Monthly rent (invest the deposit) is cheaper**
