### Introduction: Dreaming of Your Next Escape

Life is short, and the world is vast. One of the best ways to maintain [mental health and vitality](file:///Users/seuncho/coding/blog-oiyo/src/content/blog/en/mental-health-quotes.mdx) is to break the routine and immerse yourself in a new environment. However, timing is everything in travel. Visiting a paradise during its monsoon season or a festival city after the celebrations have ended can lead to disappointment.

To help you plan the perfect journey, we have curated a **12-month global travel calendar.** Here are the absolute best places to visit from January to December, ensuring that you find yourself in the right place at the right time.

---

### The Oiyo Global Travel Calendar

#### **January: Kyoto, Japan**

While many flock to tropical beaches in January, there is a unique, quiet beauty in Kyoto's snowy temples. Experience the **Hatsumode** (first shrine visit of the year) and enjoy the serene atmosphere of Kinkaku-ji (The Golden Pavilion) against a crisp white backdrop. It's a time for reflection and new beginnings.

#### **February: Venice, Italy**

February is the month of the **Carnival of Venice.** The city transforms into a mysterious, 18th-century masquerade ball. The misty canals and the elaborate masks create an atmosphere of pure magic and historical romance.

#### **March: Jeju Island, South Korea**

March marks the beginning of spring in East Asia. Jeju Island is covered in vibrant yellow **Canola flowers** and delicate cherry blossoms. The weather is perfect for hiking Mount Hallasan or walking along the Olle trails before the summer crowds arrive.

#### **April: Amsterdam, Netherlands**

April is the peak of the **Tulip season.** A visit to the Keukenhof Gardens is a bucket-list experience. Millions of flowers bloom in a riot of color, and the city's canals are perfect for breezy spring boat tours.

#### **May: Santorini, Greece**

May is the 'Goldilocks' month for the Greek Isles—not too hot, and not too crowded. The Aegean Sea is a deep, sparkling blue, and the iconic white-and-blue architecture of Oia provides the perfect backdrop for a restorative Mediterranean escape.

#### **June: Interlaken, Switzerland**

June is when the alpine meadows are at their most lush and green. It's the ideal time for paragliding over the lakes or taking a scenic train ride through the Jungfrau region. The air is fresh, and the mountain peaks are still capped with snow.

#### **July: Bali, Indonesia**

While much of Southeast Asia is experiencing the rainy season, July is the dry season in Bali. It's the perfect time for surfing in Uluwatu, exploring the rice terraces of Ubud, or finding spiritual peace in ancient water temples.

#### **August: Edinburgh, Scotland**

August is the month of the **Edinburgh Festival Fringe,** the world's largest arts festival. the city is alive with street performers, avant-garde theater, and comedy. The cool Scottish summer provides a welcome relief from the global heatwaves.

#### **September: Munich, Germany**

September marks the start of **Oktoberfest.** Experience the ultimate celebration of Bavarian culture, traditional music, and world-class beer. The early autumn weather is also perfect for exploring the nearby Neuschwanstein Castle.

#### **October: Quebec City, Canada**

For the ultimate **fall foliage** experience, head to Quebec. The maple forests turn into shades of fiery red and burnt orange. The French-influenced architecture and cobblestone streets of Old Quebec feel cozy and romantic in the autumn air.

#### **November: Chiang Mai, Thailand**

November is when the **Yi Peng (Lantern Festival)** takes place. Thousands of paper lanterns are released into the night sky, creating a breathtaking scene of light and hope. The weather is cool and dry, making it the best time to explore Northern Thailand.

#### **December: Rovaniemi, Finland (Lapland)**

Celebrate the end of the year in the **official hometown of Santa Claus.** Experience the magic of the Northern Lights, go on a husky sled ride, and enjoy the ultimate winter wonderland. It is a place that brings out the inner child in everyone.
