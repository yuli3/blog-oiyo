# Essential Business Administration Memorization: Major Scholars and Theories Matching Guide

The most fundamental yet core part of business administration exams (civil service, public enterprises, labor attorneys, etc.) is the accurate matching of **'Scholar - Theory - Keyword'**. This guide is a 'memorization master list' organizing major scholars who have appeared in exams at least once, categorized by era and field.
