## 1. Introduction: The Night Theatre

We spend 33% of our lives asleep. In that time, we travel to worlds where we can fly, talk to the dead, and lose our teeth.
Science says it is "memory consolidation."
Mystics say it is "soul travel."
Who is right?

---

## 2. Korean Dream Culture

In Korea, dreams are **prophetic currency**. You can even "buy" and "sell" dreams.

- **Taemong (Conception Dreams)**: If a pregnant woman dreams of a dragon, tiger, or shiny fruit, it predicts the gender and destiny of the unborn child. (e.g., A golden carp = A great boy).
- **Pig Dreams**: Pigs symbolize wealth. If you dream of a pig, you buy a lottery ticket immediately.
- **Buying Dreams**: If someone had a good dream, you can pay them (symbolically) to transfer the luck to you.

## 3. Jungian Analysis: Archetypes

Carl Jung saw dreams as the Unconscious trying to balance the Conscious mind.

- If you are too arrogant in the day, you dream of falling in shit. (Compensatory).
- **Big Dreams**: Dreams with gods, monsters, or mandalas. These are not about your personal life, but about the Collective Unconscious.

## 4. Lucid Dreaming: Taking Control

**Lucid Dreaming** is the art of waking up _inside_ the dream.
"I am dreaming right now."
Once aware, you can control the narrative. You can fly, summon objects, or ask the dream characters: "What do you represent?"
It is the ultimate virtual reality simulator.

---

## 5. How to Interpret

Don't use a generic dictionary.
If you dream of a dog:

- To a dog lover, it means loyalty.
- To a mailman, it means danger.
  **Context is King.**
  Ask: "How did I _feel_ in the dream?" The emotion is the truth; the image is just the costume.

## 6. Wrapping Up: The Unopened Letters

Dreams are unopened letters from your Soul.
If you have a recurring nightmare, it is a letter you haven't read yet. It will keep coming until you open it.
Keep a pen by your bed. The morning erases the night quickly. Catch the message before it fades.
