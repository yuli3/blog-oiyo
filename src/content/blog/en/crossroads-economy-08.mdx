## An Unprecedented Conflict: President vs. Central Bank

In U.S. history, a president publicly criticizing the Fed chair was once unheard of. That precedent was broken in Trump's first term, and the conflict deepened further in his second.

### What Fed Independence Means

Fed independence is the institutional guarantee that lets the central bank pursue its dual mandate — price stability and maximum employment — free of political pressure.

What happens when that independence is shaken?
- Pressure grows to cut rates for political reasons ahead of elections
- The risk of reigniting inflation rises
- Confidence in the dollar declines
- Treasury yields rise

### What the Market Really Fears

What markets truly fear in a White House–Fed clash isn't who wins. **The conflict itself raises uncertainty, and uncertainty raises the risk premium.**

The more the conflict escalates, the more a pattern emerges: a weaker dollar, stronger gold, and stronger TIPS (inflation-protected bonds).

### Hedging Strategies for Investors

- **Gold:** a hedge against institutional uncertainty
- **Short-term bonds:** avoid long-term rate uncertainty
- **International diversification:** spread out dollar risk
