## What Actually Builds Compatibility

Unlike name-based fun scores, research has identified real factors that increase relationship satisfaction:

| Factor | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Shared values** | Agreement on money, children, and family priorities |
| **Communication style** | Talking vs. withdrawing when conflict arises |
| **Attachment style** | Anxious / avoidant / secure combinations |
| **Growth mindset** | Believing the relationship can improve with effort |
| **Empathy** | Willingness to understand a partner's emotions |

## The 5 Love Languages

According to Gary Chapman's research, people give and receive love in distinctly different ways:

| Love Language | Expression |
|---|---|
| Words of Affirmation | Praise, appreciation, encouragement |
| Quality Time | Focused, undivided attention |
| Receiving Gifts | Small tokens of affection |
| Acts of Service | Showing love through helpful actions |
| Physical Touch | Connection through hugs and physical closeness |
