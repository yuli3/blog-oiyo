## Battle royale, one match at a time

Battle royale is a genre where a large group of players drops onto one map and fights until **only one player or team is left standing.** PUBG, Fortnite, and Apex Legends are the best-known examples. Once you know the core rules, your first match won't feel like chaos.

---

## The basic flow of a match

```
1. Boarding: Start in a plane (or a designated starting point)
2. Drop: Land wherever you choose
3. Looting: Collect weapons, armor, and items from buildings
4. Survive and fight: Move with the shrinking zone, engage enemies
5. Final circle: The last shrinking area decides the winner — last player/team standing wins
```

The exact term for "winning" varies by game (PUBG famously calls it a "chicken dinner").

---

## The most important thing — the Zone

<a id="zone"></a>

```
The zone: a safe circle that shrinks over time.
- Being outside it deals continuous damage → eventually death
- Damage outside the zone increases as the match goes on
→ "Always move toward the next circle before you're forced to" is the core survival rule
```

Managing the zone well is half of what it takes to be good at battle royale.

---

## Looting — what to grab

| Category | Examples | Priority |
|------|-----|---------|
| **Weapons** | Two primary weapons (one close-range, one mid-range) | Top priority |
| **Armor** | Helmet, vest, backpack | High |
| **Healing** | Med kit, bandages, energy drinks | High |
| **Attachments** | Scope, grip, magazine | Medium |
| **Ammo** | Matched to your weapon caliber | Essential |

```
The first minute after landing: weapons → armor → healing, in that order, fast.
Getting caught in a fight empty-handed is the most dangerous spot to be in.
```

---

## Beginner survival strategy

```
1. Drop on the outskirts: land somewhere with fewer fights early on to loot and adjust
2. Use cover: avoid open ground — hug trees, rocks, and buildings
3. Listen: footsteps, gunfire, and engine sounds reveal enemy positions (headset recommended)
4. Play as a team (duo/squad): communicate with pings, move together
5. Avoid unnecessary fights: the goal is survival — staying alive to the end
```

---

## Common modes

```
Solo: alone / Duo: 2 players / Squad: 4 players
TPP (third-person) / FPP (first-person): different camera perspectives
```

---

## Key takeaways

1. A genre where you fight until only one player or team remains.
2. Flow: drop → loot → move with the zone, fighting as needed → final circle.
3. Managing **the zone** (the shrinking safe area) is the core survival skill.
4. Loot in order after landing: weapons → armor → healing, and do it fast.
5. Beginners should focus on outskirt drops, cover, sound awareness, and avoiding unnecessary fights.
