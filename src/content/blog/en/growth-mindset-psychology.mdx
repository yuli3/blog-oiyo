## Neuroplasticity

The brain is muscle-like. It changes with experience.
Failure is not a reflection of your ontology ("I am stupid"); it is feedback for your process ("I need to try a different strategy").

## Praising Effort

Do not praise intelligence ("You are so smart"). Praise effort ("You worked really hard").
Praising intelligence creates a fixed mindset and fear of failure.
