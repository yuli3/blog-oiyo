## Final Strategy Summary by Section

| Section | Key Strategy | Common Mistake |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| Reading | Map structure, then follow questions | Reading every word start to finish |
| Listening | Note-taking: topic within first 30 sec | Focusing on tiny details |
| Speaking | Templates + natural delivery | Sounding like a memorized script |

## Score Improvement Roadmap

### 70s → 80s
- Reading: Focus on Inference question type
- Listening: Train to capture lecture topic in opening 30 seconds
- Speaking: Automate transition words
- Writing: Master Integrated Task template

### 80s → 90s
- Reading: Ace Prose Summary (3-point questions)
- Listening: Improve accuracy on Function/Attitude questions
- Speaking: Control pace and use natural pauses
- Writing: Hit 200+ words on Academic Discussion

### 90s → 100+
- Balance all four sections
- Complete 5+ full-length practice tests
- Intensive focus on weakest section

## Official Practice Test Strategy

**ETS-provided tools**:
1. **TOEFL Practice Online (TPO)**: Identical to real exam conditions; most accurate score prediction
2. **Free Practice Test**: Available on ETS website (one free test)
3. **Official TOEFL iBT Tests vol. 1 & 2**: Contains actual retired test questions

**How to use practice tests**:
- Start 4–6 weeks before test day
- Always simulate real conditions: quiet room, timer, no interruptions
- Spend **2× the test time** analyzing errors afterward

## Exam Day Playbook

### Night Before
- 7–8 hours sleep (non-negotiable)
- Review only your personal note sheet — no new learning
- Confirm test center address, ID (passport), and confirmation email
- Prepare what you need for the next day (ID, snack for break)

### Morning Of
- Arrive 30 minutes early
- Eat a light meal 90 minutes before start time
- Earplugs are allowed — bring your own if you prefer

### During the Test — Section Tips

**Reading**:
- First paragraph: read carefully to establish thesis
- Vocabulary: always infer from context before guessing
- Prose Summary: do last within the passage set

**Listening**:
- Note topic immediately when lecture begins
- Don't go back to change answers — trust your first impression
- Audio plays only once — stay focused

**Speaking**:
- Task 1: state your position directly in the first sentence
- Maintain consistent distance from microphone
- If you finish early, extend with an additional example

**Writing**:
- Task 1: target 3–4 paragraphs in 20 minutes
- Task 2: include a unique perspective not mentioned by either student in the discussion prompt
