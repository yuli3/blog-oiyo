## What Are the Heavenly Stems?

The Heavenly Stems classify **heavenly energy into 10 categories**. Positioned at the top of the four pillars, they represent psychological, conscious, and extroverted energy.

The Heavenly Stems are composed of **Five Elements (Wood·Fire·Earth·Metal·Water) × Yin/Yang**.

| Element | Yang Stem | Yin Stem | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wood (木) | 甲 (Gab) | 乙 (Eul) | Gab = tall tree; Eul = grass/vine |
| Fire (火) | 丙 (Byeong) | 丁 (Jeong) | Byeong = the sun; Jeong = candlelight |
| Earth (土) | 戊 (Mu) | 己 (Gi) | Mu = mountain/earth; Gi = farmland |
| Metal (金) | 庚 (Gyeong) | 辛 (Sin) | Gyeong = axe/iron; Sin = jewel |
| Water (水) | 壬 (Im) | 癸 (Gye) | Im = river/sea; Gye = dew/rain |

## Wood Stems

### 甲 Gab-Wood — The Towering Tree

**Nature image**: A straight, strong tree like a pine, oak, or bamboo

**Energy characteristics**:
- Straightforward with powerful drive
- Unyielding will and self-respect
- Pioneer spirit that opens new paths
- Strong sense of justice; protective of the vulnerable

**Psychological patterns**:
- Wants to lead but tends to carry burdens alone
- Dislikes compromise; very faithful to principles
- Energy peaks when recognized and appreciated

**Strengths**: Drive, leadership, decisiveness, independence
**Weaknesses**: Stubbornness, rigidity, lack of consideration for others

```
Gab-Wood relationship principles:
✓ Im/Gye Water (Water feeds Wood) → nurturing presences
✓ Byeong/Jeong Fire (Wood feeds Fire) → those who receive your energy
✗ Gyeong Metal (Metal cuts Wood) → the greatest source of stimulation/challenge
```

### 乙 Eul-Wood — The Swaying Vine

**Nature image**: A flexible, growing plant like ivy, vine, or ornamental flower

**Energy characteristics**:
- Flexible with excellent situational adaptability
- Delicate sensitivity and empathy
- Values partnership; prefers collaboration
- Resilience that maintains roots through storms

**Psychological patterns**:
- Tendency to lean on the strong
- Soft on the outside, but with clear inner goals
- Prefers supporting from the side rather than leading from the front

**Strengths**: Adaptability, sociability, sensitivity, artistry
**Weaknesses**: Indecisiveness, dependency, inconsistency

---

## Fire Stems

### 丙 Byeong-Fire — The Sun at Noon

**Nature image**: Intense, brilliant fire like the sun or a great blaze

**Energy characteristics**:
- Bright and lively; illuminates those around them
- Natural charisma and sociability
- Enthusiastic and optimistic temperament
- Loves attention; richly expressive

**Psychological patterns**:
- Strong desire to be recognized
- Burns energy quickly; susceptible to burnout
- Broad social network, but deep relationships take time

**Strengths**: Passion, expressiveness, optimism, charisma
**Weaknesses**: Scattered focus, lack of persistence, tendency to exaggerate

### 丁 Jeong-Fire — The Steady Candleflame

**Nature image**: A constant, burning fire like a candle, hearth, or star

**Energy characteristics**:
- Outstanding concentration and precision
- Keen sensitivity and excellent intuition
- Tendency to focus deeply on one thing
- Perfectionist; strong in detail

**Psychological patterns**:
- Does not reveal inner feelings at first
- Once trust is built, highly devoted
- Remembers hurts for a long time

**Strengths**: Insight, perfectionism, focus, artistry
**Weaknesses**: Sensitivity, suspicion, self-criticism

---

## Earth Stems

### 戊 Mu-Earth — The Vast Terrain

**Nature image**: Broad, stable earth like a mountain, plain, or desert

**Energy characteristics**:
- Strong capacity for inclusion; accepts everything
- Presence that provides stability
- Responsible and trusted
- Prefers stability over change

**Psychological patterns**:
- Tends to take on the role of pillar for others
- Inclined to carry heavy burdens alone
- Takes time to adapt to change

**Strengths**: Reliability, inclusiveness, responsibility, steadiness
**Weaknesses**: Stubbornness, resistance to change, excessive responsibility

### 己 Gi-Earth — The Fertile Farmland

**Nature image**: Detailed, fertile soil like a field, paddy, or garden

**Energy characteristics**:
- Careful and meticulous nurturing ability
- Good interpersonal skills; can mediate atmospheres
- Analytical and considerate
- Maximizes ability in a stable environment

**Psychological patterns**:
- Prone to worry and anxiety
- Sensitive to others' emotions
- Tendency to underestimate oneself

**Strengths**: Consideration, analytical ability, thoroughness, sociability
**Weaknesses**: Excessive worry, indecisiveness, self-deprecation

---

## Metal Stems

### 庚 Gyeong-Metal — Forged Steel

**Nature image**: Hard, sharp metal like an axe, sword, or iron ore

**Energy characteristics**:
- Values principles and rules
- Decisiveness in cutting away the unnecessary
- Direct and clear communication style
- Fast execution toward goals

**Psychological patterns**:
- Logic and principles take precedence over emotions
- Dislikes compromise; points out what is wrong
- Feels lonely but hates showing weakness

**Strengths**: Decisiveness, principle, execution, courage
**Weaknesses**: Coldness, self-righteousness, poor emotional expression

### 辛 Sin-Metal — The Shining Gem

**Nature image**: Refined and beautiful metal like a diamond, pearl, or crafted jewelry

**Energy characteristics**:
- Well-developed aesthetic sense; pursues beauty
- Strong in delicate and precise work
- Values dignity and refinement
- Perfectionist; maintains high standards

**Psychological patterns**:
- Pursues outward perfection but carries many inner wounds
- Remembers hurts long and rarely shows them
- Sensitive to others' evaluations

**Strengths**: Aesthetic sense, perfectionism, sensitivity, sharp judgment
**Weaknesses**: Oversensitivity, arrogance, vulnerability

---

## Water Stems

### 壬 Im-Water — The Great River and Sea

**Nature image**: Free, expansive water like a river, ocean, or lake

**Energy characteristics**:
- Free-spirited; dislikes constraint
- Outstanding strategic thinking and crisis response
- Sees the big picture; strategic thinker
- Enjoys varied experiences and change

**Psychological patterns**:
- Spreads wide, sometimes lacking depth
- Tends to choose freedom over duty and responsibility
- Does not want to be tied down in relationships either

**Strengths**: Strategy, adaptability, big-picture thinking, creativity
**Weaknesses**: Scattered focus, irresponsibility, lack of direction

### 癸 Gye-Water — Dewdrops and Rain

**Nature image**: Delicate, deep water like dew, rain, mist, or a spring

**Energy characteristics**:
- Highly developed intuition
- Deep empathic ability and sensitivity
- Drawn to art, philosophy, and the mysterious
- Rich inner world

**Psychological patterns**:
- Feels a great deal internally but struggles to express it
- Prone to anxiety and depression
- Emotional stability is paramount

**Strengths**: Intuition, empathy, artistry, depth
**Weaknesses**: Anxiety, secretiveness, melancholy
