# The Fundamentals of Digital Productivity: Typing Speed Is Output Speed

We spend most of our working day at a keyboard — writing emails, drafting documents, writing code, taking meeting notes. Every piece of work passes through the bottleneck of your fingertips. If you can go from 40 WPM to 80 WPM, you've cut your text input time in half — with the same mental effort.

## 2. How to Get Faster

1. **Accuracy before speed**: Every backspace you hit costs more time than typing slowly and cleanly. Aim for 100% accuracy at a comfortable pace — speed will follow naturally.
2. **Touch typing**: Learn to type without looking at the keyboard. Once your fingers know where to go without visual confirmation, your brain is freed up entirely for your content — and you enter a state of flow.
3. **Consistent rhythm**: Develop an even, metronomic cadence. Uneven bursting and stopping increases fatigue over long sessions.

<Callout type="info">
**Digital Agility**: True agility isn't about raw speed — it's about minimizing the latency between a thought in your mind and its appearance on screen. The lower that lag, the more your thinking and your output feel like one continuous act.
</Callout>
