## The Present Bias

Would you prefer \$100 today or \$110 tomorrow? Most say \$100 today.
Would you prefer \$100 in 30 days or \$110 in 31 days? Most say \$110 in 31 days.

Logically, the difference is the same (waiting 1 day for $10). But our brain applies a massive "discount" to anything that is not _right now_.
