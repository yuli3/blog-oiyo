# Calorie Calculator

Weight management comes down to the balance of **calories in vs. calories out**. But the exact daily calorie target is different for every person. Your age, gender, weight, and activity level all shape that number — a generic "2,000 calories" guideline won't cut it.

## BMR vs. TDEE

| Term | Definition | Key Detail |
|---|---|---|
| BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) | Calories burned at complete rest | Breathing, heartbeat, temperature regulation |
| TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure) | Actual daily calories burned | BMR × activity multiplier |

**Calculation formula (Revised Harris-Benedict)**

- Men: BMR = 88.36 + (13.4 × weight kg) + (4.8 × height cm) − (5.7 × age)
- Women: BMR = 447.6 + (9.25 × weight kg) + (3.10 × height cm) − (4.33 × age)

<Callout type="tip">
**TDEE activity multipliers**: Sedentary ×1.2 / Light exercise (1–3×/wk) ×1.375 / Moderate (3–5×/wk) ×1.55 / Heavy daily exercise ×1.725 / Physical labor + exercise ×1.9
</Callout>

## Calorie Targets by Goal

| Goal | Calorie Adjustment | Expected Weekly Change |
|---|---|---|
| Gradual weight loss | TDEE − 250 kcal | ~0.25 kg lost |
| Standard weight loss | TDEE − 500 kcal | ~0.5 kg lost |
| Weight maintenance | TDEE | — |
| Lean muscle gain | TDEE + 250–300 kcal | ~0.25 kg gained |

<Callout type="warning">
**Never go below 1,200 kcal/day**. Eating less than your BMR causes muscle loss, nutritional deficiency, and immune suppression. A small, consistent deficit is far healthier than severe restriction.
</Callout>
