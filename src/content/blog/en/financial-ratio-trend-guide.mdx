## What Is Financial Ratio Analysis?

Financial ratio analysis expresses relationships between financial statement items as numbers, enabling assessment of a company's profitability, safety, and efficiency. **3+ year trend analysis is far more meaningful than a single-year snapshot.**

## The 8 Key Financial Ratios

### Safety Ratios

**Current Ratio**
> Current Assets / Current Liabilities × 100
- Meaning: Short-term debt repayment capacity
- Benchmark: 200%+ (healthy)
- Low: Short-term liquidity risk

**Debt-to-Equity Ratio**
> Total Liabilities / Equity × 100
- Meaning: Degree of debt reliance
- Benchmark: 100% or below
- High: Financial leverage risk

**Equity Ratio**
> Equity / Total Assets × 100
- Meaning: Proportion of assets financed by equity
- Benchmark: 50%+

### Profitability Ratios

**ROA (Return on Assets)**
> Net Income / Total Assets × 100
- Meaning: How efficiently assets generate profits
- Benchmark: 5%+

**ROE (Return on Equity)**
> Net Income / Equity × 100
- Meaning: Return on shareholders' capital
- Benchmark: 10%+
- Warren Buffett's key metric

**Operating Margin**
> Operating Income / Revenue × 100
- Meaning: Profitability from core operations
- Benchmark: 10%+

**Net Profit Margin**
> Net Income / Revenue × 100
- Meaning: Overall profitability
- Benchmark: 5%+

### Efficiency Ratio

**Asset Turnover**
> Revenue / Total Assets
- Meaning: How efficiently assets generate revenue
- Benchmark: 1.0x+

<Callout type="info">
DuPont Analysis: ROE = Net Margin × Asset Turnover × Financial Leverage
Even a high ROE needs scrutiny — is it from operational excellence or excessive debt?
</Callout>

## Key Caveats in Trend Analysis

**1. Industry-Specific Benchmarks**
Asset turnover in manufacturing vs. IT services, and operating margins in retail vs. pharmaceuticals differ structurally. Always compare within the same industry first.

**2. One-Time Items**
Years with large asset disposals, M&A activity, or extraordinary gains/losses will show anomalous ratios.

**3. Denominator Effects**
A sudden jump in ROE may reflect declining equity (share buybacks) rather than genuine profit growth.
