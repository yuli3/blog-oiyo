# Investment Decision Interactive Lab

Every investment decision ultimately translates to **present value**.
NPV is the most powerful language for pulling future cash flows back to today.

---

## 1. Calculate NPV Yourself

Adjust the initial investment, discount rate, and cash flows.
Feel how small number changes can completely flip the investment decision.

<NPVCalculator client:load locale="en" />

<Callout type="tip">
**Observation point**: as the discount rate rises, the present value of future cash flows shrinks rapidly.
In other words, as **risk grows, present value falls**.
</Callout>
