## A Cognitive Shift

Astronauts go to space as technicians and return as humanitarians. Seeing Earth hanging in the void of space creates an overwhelming feeling of unity and protective responsibility.
This is the **Overview Effect**. It is the realization that national borders are imaginary lines and that we are all crew members on the same spaceship.

## Spaceship Earth

You don't need a rocket to experience this. The lesson is simple: Zoom out. Your daily stresses—the traffic, the rude email, the lost wallet—are microscopic specks on a microscopic planet.
We are all breathing the same recycled air. The only borders that matter are the thin layer of atmosphere protecting us from the void.
