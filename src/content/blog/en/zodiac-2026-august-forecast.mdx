## Key Astro Events — August 2026

August is the month ruled by **Leo's Sun**, brimming with self-expression, creativity, leadership, and joy. After August 22, when the Sun moves into Virgo, energy shifts toward detail and practicality.

| Event | Date | Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Sun in Leo | Aug 1–22 | Self-expression, leadership & creativity maximized |
| Sun enters Virgo | Aug 22 | Energy shifts to detail, health & efficiency |
| Full Moon in Aquarius | Early Aug | Individual vs. community balance |
| New Moon in Virgo | Late Aug | Fresh start for health & routines |
| Jupiter in Cancer direct | All month | Home & emotional luck continues |

<Callout type="tip">
**August Theme**: Invest in creativity and self-expression. Show your talents and boldly ask for what you want.
</Callout>

## Aries ♈ (Mar 21–Apr 19)

### Overall
**5th House (creativity, love, pleasure)** energy explodes. Peak of summer — a month to relish life's joys. Creative projects, hobbies, romance, and time with children are all abundant.

### Love
A hot summer romance is within reach. A confident approach draws others in. With an existing partner, inject spark with an exciting date.

### Money
Opportunity to monetize creative talent. Ideas for turning hobbies into a business begin to surface.

### Career
The ideal month to pitch ideas and demonstrate leadership. Especially prominent in creative industries.
