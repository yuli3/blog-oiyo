When we read a horoscope, we usually look at just one thing: our "Sun sign" — the zodiac sign tied to our birthday. But a full astrological reading is built from your **natal chart**, calculated from your exact birth time and place. This guide walks you through how to read and interpret one.

## What Is a Birth Chart?

A birth chart is a snapshot of the sky, as seen from the exact place you were born, at the exact moment you were born. It's laid out as a circle and built from three core components:

1. **Planets**: Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto
2. **Signs**: the 12 zodiac signs
3. **Houses**: the 12 areas of life

**Generating your chart**: free tools include Astro.com, AstroSage, and Café Astrology.

<Callout type="info">
**Why an accurate birth time matters**: calculating a birth chart requires a precise birth time. Even an hour's difference can meaningfully shift your Ascendant and house placements.
</Callout>

## The Three Core Signs

### Sun Sign — Your Conscious Self

Determined by your birthday, this is the sign most people already know. It represents **who you are** — your conscious identity.

It answers the question: "Who am I?"

### Moon Sign — Your Unconscious Self

The sign the Moon occupied at your birth. The Moon shifts signs roughly every 2.5 days. It represents your **emotions, instincts, unconscious reactions, and inner needs**.

It answers the question: "What do I feel? What do I need?"

| Moon Sign | Emotional Style | What It Needs |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Aries | Immediate, direct | Independence, autonomy |
| Taurus | Steady, sensory | Safety, comfort |
| Gemini | Intellectual, changeable | Communication, variety |
| Cancer | Deeply feeling, nurturing | Connection, care |
| Leo | Dramatic, creative | Recognition, self-expression |
| Virgo | Analytical, practical | Order, usefulness |
| Libra | Harmonizing, graceful | Balance, partnership |
| Scorpio | Deeply felt, intense | Depth, transformation |
| Sagittarius | Optimistic, philosophical | Freedom, meaning |
| Capricorn | Restrained, responsible | Achievement, stability |
| Aquarius | Independent, innovative | Freedom, originality |
| Pisces | Sensitive, spiritual | Connection, peace |

### Ascendant/Rising Sign — Your Outer Self

The sign that was rising on the eastern horizon at the moment you were born. It represents **how others perceive you at first glance** and your outward style of engaging the world.

It answers the question: "How do I come across? How do I approach things?"

## The 12-House System

The houses represent 12 areas of life. Which sign and which planets fall in a given house shapes how that area of your life plays out.

| House | Domain | Keywords |
|--------|------|--------|
| 1st House | Self, appearance | Identity, first impressions |
| 2nd House | Money, values | Resources, possessions |
| 3rd House | Communication, short trips | Siblings, learning |
| 4th House | Home, roots | Family, origins |
| 5th House | Creativity, romance | Pleasure, children |
| 6th House | Health, daily life | Service, routine |
| 7th House | Partnership | Marriage, business partners |
| 8th House | Transformation, death | Shared resources, deep psychology |
| 9th House | Philosophy, long-distance travel | Education, travel, beliefs |
| 10th House | Career, social standing | Goals, reputation |
| 11th House | Friends, dreams | Community, hopes |
| 12th House | The unconscious, spirituality | Hidden things, karma |

## Reading a Sample Chart

**Example**: Sun = Scorpio (8th House), Moon = Taurus (2nd House), Ascendant = Leo

**Interpretation**:
- Sun/Scorpio/8th House: drawn to deep transformation and psychological depth. Self-expression is intense and often private. Identity is bound up with shared resources and themes of life and death.
- Moon/Taurus/2nd House: emotional needs center on stability and material security. A grounded, physical sense of safety provides emotional footing.
- Ascendant/Leo: a confident, creative first impression — someone who appears self-assured in how they express themselves.
