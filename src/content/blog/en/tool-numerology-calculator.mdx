# Numerology Calculator

Numerology is an ancient philosophical tradition holding that numbers carry unique energies and meanings. Attributed to Pythagoras (c. 570–495 BC), it uses the digits of your birth date to explore life direction and personality patterns.

## How to Calculate Your Life Path Number

Add all digits in your birth date repeatedly until you reach a single digit (or a Master Number).

**Example: May 23, 1990**  
1+9+9+0 + 0+5 + 2+3 = 29 → 2+9 = **11** (Master Number)  
Or reduce further: 1+1 = **2**

<Callout type="tip">
**Master Numbers (11, 22, 33)**: These two-digit numbers are kept intact — they symbolize greater potential and greater challenges. In most readings, both the Master Number and its single-digit reduction (2, 4, 6) are considered.
</Callout>
