## What Is Four Pillars of Destiny?

**Four Pillars of Destiny (四柱命理學, Saju Myeongnihak)** is an Eastern wisdom system that analyzes the energy embedded in the **four pillars of a person's birth year, month, day, and hour** to read their personality, talents, and life trajectory.

'Saju (四柱)' means four pillars; 'Myeongni (命理)' means the principle of destiny. Rather than simple fortune-telling, it is **a systematic study of the relationship between cosmic time-energy and human beings**.

<Callout type="note">
**Modern perspective**: Four Pillars of Destiny starts from the premise that the cosmic energy pattern at the moment of birth influences the individual — a philosophical foundation similar to Western Astrology.
</Callout>

## Historical Background

### Origins and Development

| Era | Key Development |
|---|---|
| Yellow Emperor period (c. 2700 BC) | Foundation of Yin-Yang and Five Elements thought |
| Spring and Autumn / Warring States (770–221 BC) | Completion of the *I Ching*, philosophical systematization |
| Han Dynasty (206 BC–220 AD) | Development of Five Elements theory; establishment of the Ganzhi (stem-branch) system |
| Tang Dynasty (618–907 AD) | Li Xuzhong establishes the three-pillar system of year, month, and day |
| Song Dynasty (960–1279 AD) | **Ziping Method** completed — day master-centered analytical system |
| Ming and Qing Dynasties | Classic texts such as *Sanming Tonghui* and *Dishui Suiyu* compiled |

The **Ziping Method** was perfected by Xu Ziping of the Song Dynasty and forms the basis of the modern Saju analysis system in which the day stem represents "the self."

## Yin-Yang and Five Elements: The Basic Language of Saju

### Yin (陰) and Yang (陽)

This philosophy holds that everything in the universe is made up of **the opposition and harmony of Yin and Yang**.

| | Yang (陽) | Yin (陰) |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Active, extroverted, bright | Receptive, introverted, dark |
| Examples | Sun, male, daytime, summer | Moon, female, nighttime, winter |
| In Saju | Yang stems (甲·丙·戊·庚·壬) | Yin stems (乙·丁·己·辛·癸) |

### Five Elements (五行)

The Five Elements are **5 streams of energy** that make up the universe. They maintain balance through relationships of generation (生) and control (剋).

| Element | Wood (木) | Fire (火) | Earth (土) | Metal (金) | Water (水) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Symbol | Tree, growth | Fire, passion | Soil, stability | Metal, decisiveness | Water, wisdom |
| Season | Spring | Summer | Transition | Autumn | Winter |
| Direction | East | South | Center | West | North |
| Color | Blue-green | Red | Yellow | White | Black |

**Generation Cycle (相生) — mutually nurturing**:
```
Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood
(Wood feeds Fire → Fire enriches Earth → Earth bears Metal → Metal collects Water → Water grows Wood)
```

**Control Cycle (相剋) — mutually restraining**:
```
Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → Wood
(Wood breaks Earth → Earth absorbs Water → Water douses Fire → Fire melts Metal → Metal cuts Wood)
```

## The Ganzhi (干支) System

### 10 Heavenly Stems (十天干)

The Heavenly Stems classify heavenly energy into 10 categories.

| Stem | 甲 | 乙 | 丙 | 丁 | 戊 | 己 | 庚 | 辛 | 壬 | 癸 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pronunciation | Gab | Eul | Byeong | Jeong | Mu | Gi | Gyeong | Sin | Im | Gye |
| Element | Wood | Wood | Fire | Fire | Earth | Earth | Metal | Metal | Water | Water |
| Polarity | Yang | Yin | Yang | Yin | Yang | Yin | Yang | Yin | Yang | Yin |

### 12 Earthly Branches (十二地支)

The Earthly Branches classify earth energy into 12 categories.

| Branch | 子 | 丑 | 寅 | 卯 | 辰 | 巳 | 午 | 未 | 申 | 酉 | 戌 | 亥 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reading | Ja | Chuk | In | Myo | Jin | Sa | O | Mi | Sin | Yu | Sul | Hae |
| Animal | Rat | Ox | Tiger | Rabbit | Dragon | Snake | Horse | Goat | Monkey | Rooster | Dog | Pig |
| Element | Water | Earth | Wood | Wood | Earth | Fire | Fire | Earth | Metal | Metal | Earth | Water |

### 60 Jiazi (六十甲子)

Combining 10 Heavenly Stems × 12 Earthly Branches in sequence produces 60 combinations. The same stem-branch combination repeats every 60 years.
- Gabia (甲子), Eulchuk (乙丑), Byeongin (丙寅)… Gyehae (癸亥) → back to Gabia (甲子)

## The Structure of the Eight Characters

Saju consists of **4 Pillars × 2 characters (heavenly stem + earthly branch)** = **8 characters (八字)**.

```
        Year Pillar  Month Pillar  Day Pillar  Hour Pillar
Stem:      甲           丙           壬           庚
Branch:    子           寅           午           申
```

| Pillar | Meaning | What It Represents |
|---|---|---|
| Year Pillar (年柱) | Ancestors, parents, early environment | Family background, social context |
| Month Pillar (月柱) | Parents, siblings, youth | Career environment, sociability |
| Day Pillar (日柱) | The self, spouse | Core personality, marriage |
| Hour Pillar (時柱) | Children, old age, dreams | Later years, achievement goals |

<Callout type="tip">
**Key point**: The heavenly stem of the Day Pillar (the Day Master, 日干) symbolizes "the self." The core of Saju analysis is understanding how the remaining seven characters relate to the Day Master.
</Callout>

## Levels of Saju Analysis

Saju analysis deepens step by step:

1. **Basic**: Identify the Day Master → understand your elemental energy
2. **Intermediate**: Pattern analysis (格局) → life patterns and direction
3. **Advanced**: Derive the Lucky Element (用神) → the energy you need
4. **In-depth**: Apply Major and Annual Luck Cycles → predict the flow of time
