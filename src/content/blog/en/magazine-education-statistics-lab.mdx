# Statistics Interactive Lab

Statistics isn't about "right answers" — it's about **measuring the size of uncertainty**.  
Play with sampling error hands-on, and you'll develop a visceral sense of why sample size matters.

---

## 1. Sampling Error Lab

Adjust the population proportion and the sample size.  
You'll see with your own eyes that **even from the same population, sample estimates inevitably vary**.

<SamplingErrorLab client:load />

<Callout type="tip">
**What to watch**: As sample size grows, the average stabilizes and variability shrinks.  
Put simply, **more information means less uncertainty**.
</Callout>
