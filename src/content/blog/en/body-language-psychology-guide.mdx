According to research by Albert Mehrabian, when we communicate emotion and attitude, the words themselves account for only 7% of the message. Tone of voice carries 38%, and **nonverbal signals — body language — carry 55%**. We say far more with our bodies than we do with our words. This guide breaks down the body language signals you'll run into every day.

<Callout type="info">
**A key caveat**: Body language always depends on context. Crossed arms can mean "defensive," but they can just as easily mean "cold" or "comfortable." Never read a single signal in isolation — look at the overall pattern.
</Callout>

## Eyes and Gaze

### Eye Contact

| Signal | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Natural eye contact** (60–70% of the time) | Confidence, sincerity, interest |
| **Prolonged staring** (near 100%) | Threat, dominance, fixation |
| **Avoiding eye contact** | Anxiety, dishonesty, submission — or cultural respect |
| **Frequent blinking** | Anxiety, tension |
| **Slow blinking** | Relaxation, calm |

### Pupils

- **Dilated pupils**: interest, arousal — or simply a dark environment
- **Constricted pupils**: displeasure, tension — or simply a bright environment

### Direction of Gaze (note: the scientific evidence here is limited)

| Gaze direction | Some claims |
|---------|--------|
| Up and to the left | Constructing a visual memory |
| Up and to the right | Imagining a new image |
| Down and to the left | Internal dialogue |
| Down and to the right | Emotion |

## Facial Expressions

Psychologist Paul Ekman's research found that seven basic emotions produce the same facial expressions across every culture studied.

### The Seven Universal Expressions

| Emotion | Key muscle signals |
|------|-------------|
| Happiness | Raised outer eye corners, crow's feet, lifted mouth corners |
| Sadness | Inner eyebrows drawn together, mouth corners pulled down |
| Anger | Lowered brows, narrowed eyes, clenched teeth |
| Fear | Raised, drawn-together eyebrows, wide-open eyes |
| Surprise | Sharply raised eyebrows, open mouth |
| Disgust | Wrinkled nose, raised upper lip |
| Contempt | One mouth corner raised |

### Microexpressions

Microexpressions flash across the face in less than 1/25th of a second and reveal the true emotion someone is trying to hide. Spotting them reliably takes dedicated training.

## Posture and Body Orientation

### Open vs. Closed Posture

**Open posture** (confidence, openness):
- Arms relaxed at the sides
- Body leaning toward the other person
- Palms visible
- Chest open, back straight

**Closed posture** (defensiveness, anxiety):
- Crossed arms
- Body leaning back
- Crossed or pressed-together legs
- Shoulders hunched

### The "Power Pose"

Amy Cuddy's research — which has drawn some controversy — suggests that holding a power pose for two minutes can boost feelings of confidence.

**The power pose**: feet planted wide, hands on hips, chest open.

### Spatial Positioning

- **Same eye level**: signals an equal relationship
- **Standing higher**: authority, dominance
- **Standing lower**: submission, humility
- **Sitting beside someone**: cooperative
- **Sitting across from someone**: competitive or formal

## Arms and Hand Gestures

### Crossed Arms

| Situation | Interpretation |
|------|------|
| Arms suddenly cross mid-conversation | Shift into defensiveness |
| Arms crossed from the start | Could just mean cold, or a habit |
| Crossed arms + clenched fists | Hostile defensiveness |
| Crossed arms + smiling | Simply a comfortable posture |

### Hand Position

| Gesture | Meaning |
|--------|------|
| **Palms up** | Openness, sincerity |
| **Palms down** | Authority, restraint |
| **Pointed finger** | Directing, emphasizing — often reads as aggressive |
| **Hiding the hands** | Tension, concealment |
| **Touching the neck** | Anxiety, discomfort |

### Handshakes

| Style | Meaning |
|---------|------|
| **Palm turned down** | Dominant |
| **Palm turned up** | Submissive |
| **Two-handed handshake** | Warmth, sincerity |
| **Overly firm grip** | Overt display of dominance |
| **Overly weak grip** | Lack of confidence |

## Legs and Feet

Legs and feet are the body parts we control the least — which is exactly why they often reveal our true feelings.

| Signal | Meaning |
|------|------|
| **Feet pointed at the exit** | Wanting to leave |
| **Feet pointed toward you** | Interest, liking |
| **Nervous leg bouncing** | Anxiety, tension |
| **Legs crossed toward someone** | Interest |
| **Legs crossed away from someone** | Blocking, discomfort |

## Mirroring

Mirroring is the unconscious habit of matching another person's body language.

**Meaning**: mirroring signals rapport and empathy. If someone starts mirroring your posture mid-conversation, it's a sign they feel connected to you.

**How to use it**: if you want to build rapport, try naturally matching the other person's pace, tone, and posture.

## Managing First Impressions

First impressions form in about 0.1 seconds — and they color every judgment that follows.

**Body language that builds a strong first impression**:
1. **Confident posture**: shoulders back, spine straight
2. **A genuine smile**: one that reaches the corners of the eyes
3. **Eye contact**: natural, at 60–70% of the time
4. **A steady handshake**: firm pressure, 2–3 seconds
5. **Body orientation**: leaning slightly toward the other person

<Callout type="tip">
**Job interview tip**: Use natural mirroring with your interviewer. If they lean forward, lean forward too; if you match their pace, it creates an unconscious impression of "we click."
</Callout>
