## Why Index ETFs?

For most individual investors, **low-cost index ETFs** are the optimal investment vehicle.

**ETF advantages**:
- Instant diversification across hundreds or thousands of holdings
- Ultra-low expense ratios (0.01–0.20%)
- Traded like stocks (flexible)
- No fund manager needed — track the market automatically

**Index fund vs. active funds**:
- Over 20 years, less than 10% of active funds beat their benchmark after fees
- High fees compound into dramatically lower wealth over time

## Top Index ETFs

### US Total Market
- **VTI** (Vanguard Total Stock Market ETF): 0.03% expense ratio, 4,000+ stocks
- **VTI** is the broadest US market exposure available

### S&P 500
- **VOO** (Vanguard S&P 500 ETF): 0.03%, top 500 US companies
- **IVV** (iShares Core S&P 500): 0.03%, same exposure

### International
- **VEA** (Vanguard FTSE Developed Markets): Europe, Japan, Australia
- **VWO** (Vanguard FTSE Emerging Markets): China, India, Brazil

### Bonds
- **BND** (Vanguard Total Bond Market ETF): investment-grade US bonds
- Adds stability and reduces portfolio volatility

## Portfolio Building Guide

### Age-Based Stock/Bond Allocation

| Age | Stocks | Bonds/Cash |
|-----|--------|-----------|
| 20s | 85–95% | 5–15% |
| 30s | 75–85% | 15–25% |
| 40s | 65–75% | 25–35% |
| 50s+ | 55–65% | 35–45% |

### Simple 3-Fund Portfolio

- **US stocks**: 60% (VTI or VOO)
- **International stocks**: 25% (VEA + VWO)
- **Bonds**: 15% (BND)

This simple allocation has outperformed the majority of actively managed funds over the past 20 years.

## Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)

**DCA**: Invest a fixed amount at regular intervals regardless of price.

**Advantages**:
- Eliminates need to time the market
- Automatically buys more shares when prices are low
- Reduces emotional decision-making

**Implementation**:
1. Set up automatic monthly contributions at your brokerage
2. Invest right after each paycheck (automation removes willpower requirement)
3. Maintain for minimum 3–5 years; ideal 10+ years
