# In the exam room, let your notes talk — not your memory

Walking into an exam room is stressful enough that it's easy to forget the symptom that actually matters. That's why the real value of a pre-visit checklist isn't medical knowledge — it's **accurate communication.**

## Example symptom log

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Onset | Since the night of June 10 |
| Location | Lower right abdomen |
| Character | Sharp, stabbing; worse with movement |
| Accompanying symptoms | Mild fever, reduced appetite |
| Medication taken | 1 tablet of acetaminophen |

<Callout type="warning">
For emergency symptoms — sudden chest pain, difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, paralysis, or severe bleeding — skip the checklist and go straight to the nearest emergency room or call your local emergency number.
</Callout>
