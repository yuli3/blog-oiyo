## Where the "Sell America" Argument Came From

In 2025, some market participants began arguing for "Sell America" — selling U.S. stocks, the dollar, and Treasuries all at once.

Why?
- Trump's tariff policy and threats to Fed independence
- A surge in the U.S. fiscal deficit and debate over the debt ceiling
- Challenges to dollar dominance (talk of a BRICS currency)

### American Exceptionalism: Still Valid?

"American exceptionalism" is the view that the U.S. economy and capital markets hold a structural edge fundamentally different from other countries.

The case for it remains strong.
- The world's leading innovation ecosystem (Silicon Valley)
- The dollar's status as the world's reserve currency
- Rule of law and property rights protection
- A relatively young population structure

### The Lesson 2025 Left Behind

Neither argument was entirely wrong. The dollar weakened, but it was still the reserve currency. U.S. stocks corrected, but they remained strong relative to the rest of the world.

The takeaway: trim U.S. asset exposure, but abandoning it entirely is premature. **Adjusting your diversification weighting** is the right answer.
