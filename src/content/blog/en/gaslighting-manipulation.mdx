## "That Never Happened"

The abuser denies reality. "You are crazy." "You are too sensitive."
Over time, the victim loses trust in their own perception and relies entirely on the abuser's version of reality.

## Origin

From the 1938 play _Gas Light_, where a husband dims the gas lights but tells his wife she is imagining it.
