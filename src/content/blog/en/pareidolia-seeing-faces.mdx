## Evolutionary Survival

Why do we see faces everywhere?
Because missing a face (a predator or enemy) in the jungle could mean death. Seeing a face where there is none (a rock) is a cheap mistake.
Better to be safe than sorry.

## Meaning Making

This extends beyond faces. We see patterns in stock markets, "lucky" streaks in gambling, and shapes in stars (constellations).
We are pattern-seeking machines in a chaotic universe.
