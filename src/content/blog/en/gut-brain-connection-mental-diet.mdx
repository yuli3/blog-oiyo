## Clear Gut, Clear Brain

The old saying 'A clear gut makes a clear brain' is a scientific fact. Did you know that 90% of serotonin, the happiness hormone, is produced in the gut, not the brain? Gut microbes constantly communicate with the brain via the vagus nerve to regulate mood.

## Foods That Ruin the Brain vs. Save It

- **Foods that harm the brain:** Sugar, processed foods, excessive gluten. They cause gut inflammation, and these inflammation signals are sent to the brain, causing depression and brain fog.
- **Foods that help the brain**
- **Fermented foods:** Kimchi, Yogurt, Natto (Supply beneficial bacteria)
- **Prebiotics:** Garlic, Onion, Jerusalem Artichoke (Food for beneficial bacteria)
- **Omega-3:** Perilla oil, Salmon (Protect brain cells)

## Mindful Eating

'How' you eat is as important as what you eat. Don't watch TV or your phone; focus entirely on the taste and texture of the food and chew slowly. Good digestion leads to a comfortable mind.
