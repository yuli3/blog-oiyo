## Key Deadlines Summary

```
Administrative appeal:          30–60 days from final action.
Judicial review (APA default):  60 days from final order.
Tort statute of limitations:    2–3 years from discovery.
                                (outer limit: varies by state)
General contract SOL:           4–6 years (state law).
Commercial contract (UCC):      4 years.
Agent disqualification:         3 years.
Stay / injunction pending review: while litigation is active.

For bound-action refusals:
  Seek mandamus / obligation-to-act remedy.
```

## Top 10 High-Frequency Error Points

```
① Presumption of regularity = valid until set aside
   (void = no presumption)
② Administrative appeal = unlawful + improper;
   judicial review = unlawful only
③ Appeal period ≠ judicial review period
   (verify each statute)
④ Abuse of discretion = unlawful (reviewable)
⑤ Void action = no time limit to challenge
⑥ Agents may NOT appear in court;
   may assist with administrative appeals
⑦ Rescission = retroactive; termination = prospective
⑧ Respondeat superior = within scope of employment
⑨ Contract rescission = retroactive voidance
   (same effect as void)
⑩ Official document date = write out in full
```

## Key Concept Cards

**Agent Scope = Appeals Assistance · Document Drafting** ★★★★★
: Court representation is prohibited. Administrative appeal drafting and filing is within authorized scope.
*Memory tip: Agent = appeals YES · litigation NO.*

**Three Core Principles of Administrative Law** ★★★★★
: (1) Principle of Legality (no action without law); (2) Supremacy of Law (no action contrary to law); (3) Proportionality (means must not exceed what is necessary).
*Memory tip: Legality – Supremacy – Proportionality.*

**Agency Action Validity = Presumption + Finality** ★★★★☆
: Presumption of regularity: valid until set aside. Finality: once review period expires, action cannot be contested.
*Memory tip: Presumption = until overturned; finality = after deadline.*

## Practice Quiz (Comprehensive)

**Q. How do administrative law and civil law intersect in exam questions?**
> Agency action defect doctrine parallels civil-law void/voidable concepts. Government liability for wrongful agency action is governed by federal tort claims / state tort statutes (applying negligence principles). Government contracts are subject to specialized procurement rules, but civil contract law doctrines (offer, acceptance, breach, damages) still apply. Studying both subjects together is highly efficient.

**Q. What are the most important elements for the Part 2 official document drafting question?**
> (1) Legal authority: cite the exact statute and subsection. (2) Clear facts: specify dates, locations, and acts precisely. (3) Specific prayer for relief: state exactly what outcome is sought. (4) Formal compliance: proper date format, official agency name, authorized signature. Examiners most commonly deduct points for format errors rather than substantive errors, so precision in form is critical.
