## The True Cost of an MBA

Before committing, understand the full financial picture.

### Annual Cost at US Top MBA Programs (2025)

| Item | Cost (USD) |
|------|-----------|
| Tuition | \$70,000–\$85,000 |
| Living expenses (Boston/NYC) | \$25,000–\$35,000 |
| Books, activities, travel | \$5,000–\$10,000 |
| **Annual total** | **\$100,000–\$130,000** |
| **2-year program total** | **\$200,000–\$260,000** |

Adding opportunity cost (foregone salary for 2 years) can bring the true economic investment to \$350,000–\$500,000+.

## MBA Scholarship Types

### Merit-Based Fellowships
- Awarded by the school to attract competitive applicants
- Based on GMAT/GRE score, GPA, leadership, and potential contribution
- Examples: Wharton Joseph Wharton Fellowship ($40,000/year), Columbia Business School Dean's Fellowship

### Diversity Fellowships
- Targeted at underrepresented groups: women, veterans, URM candidates, international students from specific regions

### Loan Forgiveness Programs
- For graduates entering public sector, non-profit, or social enterprise
- HBS, Wharton, Booth, and others offer low-income protection plans

### External Scholarships
- Forte Foundation (women in business)
- Consortium Program (underrepresented minorities in management)
- Reaching Higher Foundation

## Financing Options

### US Federal Loans
| Loan Type | Rate (~2025) | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Graduate PLUS Loan | ~8.05% | Up to full COA; no asset-based credit check |
| Unsubsidized Stafford | ~7.05% | Annual limits apply |
| Private Loans | 5–12% | Credit-dependent; may require US co-signer |

### International Student Options
- Many US private lenders now offer loans to international students (with US co-signer)
- Some MBA programs have dedicated international student loan programs
- Home country education loans may offer lower rates

## MBA ROI Calculation

### Simple Payback Period

**Payback period = Total Cost ÷ Annual Salary Increase**

### Example Calculation

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Total cost (tuition + living) | $230,000 |
| Pre-MBA salary | $80,000 |
| Post-MBA first-year salary (consulting/IB) | $175,000 |
| Net annual salary increase | +$95,000 |
| **Payback period** | **~2.4 years** |

### Median First-Year Salaries Post-MBA (2024)

| School Tier | Median Total Compensation |
|-------------|--------------------------|
| M7 (HBS/Wharton/Stanford) | \$175,000–\$200,000+ |
| Top 10 (Booth/Kellogg/etc.) | \$150,000–\$175,000 |
| Top 25 | \$120,000–\$150,000 |
