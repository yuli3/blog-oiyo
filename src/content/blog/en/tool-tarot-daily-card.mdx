# Tarot Daily Card

Tarot originated in 14th-century Europe as a 78-card deck. The **22 Major Arcana** cards represent life's great themes and archetypal energies. Draw one card to explore the energy around today.

## The 22 Major Arcana

| No. | Card | Core Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | The Fool | New beginnings, freedom, adventure |
| I | The Magician | Will, creativity, skill |
| II | The High Priestess | Intuition, inner wisdom, mystery |
| III | The Empress | Abundance, creation, nature |
| IV | The Emperor | Authority, stability, structure |
| V | The Hierophant | Tradition, education, belief |
| VI | The Lovers | Choice, love, values |
| VII | The Chariot | Victory, willpower, control |
| VIII | Strength | Courage, patience, inner power |
| IX | The Hermit | Reflection, inner search, solitude |
| X | Wheel of Fortune | Change, cycles, destiny |
| XI | Justice | Fairness, truth, balance |
| XII | The Hanged Man | New perspective, sacrifice, waiting |
| XIII | Death | Transformation, endings and beginnings |
| XIV | Temperance | Balance, harmony, patience |
| XV | The Devil | Attachment, materialism, constraint |
| XVI | The Tower | Sudden change, upheaval, revelation |
| XVII | The Star | Hope, inspiration, healing |
| XVIII | The Moon | Illusion, anxiety, the unconscious |
| XIX | The Sun | Joy, success, vitality |
| XX | Judgement | Renewal, awakening, a new calling |
| XXI | The World | Completion, integration, achievement |

<Callout type="note">
Tarot is not a prophetic or supernatural tool. Psychologically, tarot functions as a **projection** medium — the card imagery helps surface thoughts and emotions from within. Never follow a card reading blindly when making important decisions.
</Callout>
