# The Global Horizon: Global Business and Future Trends

This chapter introduces international entry modes, digital change, and ESG considerations. It is chapter 9 of a continuing series, not its final chapter. These topics require separate analysis of the market, operating constraints, and evidence; an overview cannot establish readiness to manage a global business.

## 3. The New Bottom Line: ESG and Sustainability

In the past, the only goal was "Profit Maximization." Today, a new standard has emerged: <mark><strong>"Doing Well by Doing Good."</strong></mark>

  title="The Three Pillars of ESG"

<Callout type="important">
  **The Stakeholder Era**: Firms are moving from "Shareholder Primacy" (serving only owners) to
  "Stakeholder Capitalism"—creating value for employees, customers, suppliers, and the environment
  alike.

## 4. Scope and further study

This overview does not complete the management curriculum or a course in AI ethics. To turn an entry-mode comparison into a decision, specify the investment, contractual responsibilities, operational capabilities, and relevant uncertainties. Identify which claims are observed and which remain assumptions.
