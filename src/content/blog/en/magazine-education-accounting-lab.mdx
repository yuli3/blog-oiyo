# Cost Accounting Interactive Lab

The break-even point is not just a number — it is a **structure**.
The goal of this lab is to help you feel the CVP relationship not as a formula, but as a **living, moving structure**.

---

## 1. CVP Experiment: Where Does the Break-Even Point Come From?

Adjust the price, variable cost, and fixed cost sliders.
Watch the intersection on the graph shift in real time.

<Callout type="tip">
**Key insight**: as contribution margin grows, the break-even point moves **to the left**.
In other words, it's the "margin" — not the volume — that reshapes the structure.
</Callout>

## 2. When the Break-Even Point Becomes a Warning Signal

The break-even point is not a target.
In business, it should be read as a **risk boundary**.

  headers={["Situation", "Interpretation", "Recommended Strategy"]}

<Quiz>
**Q.** If the contribution margin ratio increases, what happens to the break-even point?
**A.** It decreases — a higher contribution margin means fixed costs are covered with fewer units sold.
</Quiz>

CVP is no longer a formula to memorize — it is a **map to navigate by**.
Hold onto one sensation: when you change the numbers, the structure changes.
