## Beyond Causality

Jung saw that there are events in the world that cannot be explained by causality (A→B). He named the phenomenon where two events occur simultaneously in time and are meaningfully connected **Synchronicity**.

## Example: Dream of the Scarab Beetle

Jung's famous case: The moment a patient talked about a "golden scarab beetle dream," a real scarab beetle flew in from outside the window. This is not coincidence, but a moment when the unconscious and the external world resonated.

## Synchronicity in Daily Life

- A call from the person you were thinking about
- The book you need appears in front of you by chance
- Seeing repeating numbers (11:11, 222)

This is a signal the universe sends saying "Pay attention to this, right now."

## How to Experience Synchronicity

1. **Open Your Mind:** Don't dismiss everything as coincidence
2. **Record:** Write meaningful coincidences in your journal
3. **Act:** If you receive a signal, take a step in that direction
