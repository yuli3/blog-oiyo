**Winter Cool Deep** is the most powerful palette in the seasonal system — not loudest, but most commanding. The deep cool palette is built for depth: navy that holds the eye like the ocean floor, burgundy-plum that is neither warm nor light but cool and rich simultaneously, black that reads as natural rather than stark. On the right type, these colors don't just look good. They create a presence.

## What Is Winter Cool Deep?

Winter Cool Deep combines **cool undertone** (pink or blue in the skin) with **deep, rich coloring**. Unlike Winter Cool Bright (high-contrast vivid), Winter Cool Deep has a deeper, more saturated overall quality — the richness of deep jewel tones rather than the clarity of vivid brights.

**Characteristic features:**
- Skin: deep cool brown, medium-dark cool complexion, or cool olive with depth
- Eyes: very dark brown, black-brown, deep cool dark eyes with depth and richness
- Hair: very dark brown, black, or deep cool-toned dark hair
- Overall impression: commanding, rich, cool; a depth of feature that creates natural authority

**The key**: depth and coolness. Winter Deep colors must be both cool (no yellow undertone) AND deep (rich, saturated, not pale or light). Pale colors lack the depth; warm colors fight the undertone.

## Your Signature Colors

### Power Colors

**Deep Navy** — the darkest, richest blue with cool undertone; neither the powder blue of summer nor the vivid royal blue of Winter Bright, but a deep, cool navy that is this type's most versatile non-black color. Endless in wearability, always flattering.

**Deep Burgundy (Cool)** — the cool version of burgundy; red-purple with blue undertone rather than brown undertone. This is different from Autumn's warm burgundy — this one leans toward plum.

**Plum** — deep purple-red; cool, dark, jewel-toned. One of Winter Deep's most distinctive and flattering colors.

**Deep Emerald** — rich, deep green with cool undertone; darker and cooler than the vivid emerald of Winter Bright.

**Cool Charcoal** — dark grey with cool undertone; the neutral that provides depth without the starkness of pure black.

**Icy Jewel Tones** — deep versions of cool jewel colors: deep sapphire, deep amethyst, deep cool teal.

### Neutrals

- **True Black** — the most important neutral; natural and flattering as a base
- **Deep Cool Navy** — the blue alternative to black; equally flattering
- **Cool Charcoal** — for when pure black is too stark

### Colors to Approach Carefully

- **Warm colors** — the cool undertone requirement is non-negotiable
- **Light, airy colors** — the depth of coloring requires colors with matching depth; pastels lack the weight
- **Bright, vivid colors without depth** — these belong to Winter Bright; Winter Deep needs its vividness combined with richness

## Makeup

### Foundation and Base
**Cool-toned foundations** at medium-to-full coverage in the appropriate depth. Winter Cool Deep skin has richness and depth — the foundation must match both the tone (cool) and the depth (not too light, not mismatched). Full-coverage formulas often work best to create a smooth, even base.

### Eyes

**Eyeshadow**: The Winter Cool Deep eye looks best with deep, cool, rich tones — dark plum, deep charcoal grey, deep navy, cool dark brown, black with cool shimmer. Smoky eyes on this type are spectacular — the depth of feature carries the depth of color completely.

**Eyeliner**: Black — always. Cool dark navy, deep purple also work. The sharp definition of black liner against Winter Deep features creates extraordinary drama.

**Mascara**: Black. Always.

### Cheeks

Cool berry, deep cool rose, or soft plum blush. The depth of Winter Deep skin can support more pigmented blush than lighter types. The placement can be more defined and structured.

**Bronzer**: Cool bronzer or a neutral bronzer without orange undertone; warm bronzers will look out of place.

### Lips

- **Deep burgundy/plum** — the signature Winter Deep lip; cool, dark, commanding
- **Cool red** — classic; striking against deep cool skin
- **Deep berry** — dark, cool, sophisticated
- **Plum** — distinctly Winter Deep; this color at its darkest is exclusively available to this type
- **Avoid**: warm nude, coral, warm brown, orange-red

## Fashion and Wardrobe

### Building the Winter Cool Deep Wardrobe

The Winter Cool Deep wardrobe is built on depth and cool richness. The overall effect should be commanding, effortless, and authoritative.

**Core wardrobe colors:**
- True black (the foundation of everything)
- Deep navy
- Deep burgundy (cool)
- Plum
- Deep emerald
- Cool charcoal
- Deep sapphire
- Cool white (for contrast)
- Cool dark teal

**The Winter Deep capsule wardrobe** has a naturally sophisticated, monochromatic quality — everything in deep cool tones coordinates naturally, creating a look of effortless authority. The person who discovers they are Winter Deep often finds that their existing wardrobe was already mostly right: everything dark and cool that they naturally reached for was always correct.

**The all-black outfit**: Winter Cool Deep wears all-black more naturally than almost any other type. The cool, deep undertone of the skin makes black look intentional and powerful rather than harsh.

**Pattern notes**: Bold geometric patterns, graphic prints in deep cool colors, strong contrasts. Large-scale, dramatic prints in cool palettes.

**Metal jewelry**: Cool silver, white gold, platinum. The cool depth of this type makes warm metals look slightly jarring; the precision of cool metals is entirely harmonious.

**Fabric suggestions**: Rich, structural fabrics — matte silk, cashmere, leather, velvet in deep cool tones. The richness of texture complements the richness of color.

## Hair Color

**Most flattering:**
- Natural very dark brown to black (the most common natural color for this type)
- Deep cool brown
- Blue-black (the cool sheen)
- Cool-toned dark brown with deep highlights

**Approach carefully:**
- Auburn or warm red — the warm undertone fights the cool skin
- Platinum blonde — creates a dramatic contrast that may overwhelm the deep features
- Warm golden highlights in dark hair

---

Winter Cool Deep is the palette of deep winter — the hour when the sky is the darkest possible blue before it becomes black, the moment when snow appears luminous against the night. This is not the palette of warmth or softness. It is the palette of depth, command, and the kind of beauty that requires no performance because it simply is.

People who discover they are Winter Cool Deep often have a particular experience: putting on a deep navy or plum and feeling, for the first time, that they are wearing something that belongs to them — not on them, but to them. That belonging is personal color at its most powerful.

It doesn't add anything to you. It just stops taking things away.
