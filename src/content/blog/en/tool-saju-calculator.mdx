# Four Pillars (사주팔자) Calculator

The Four Pillars of Destiny (四柱八字) — known as *Saju* in Korean and *BaZi* in Chinese — is an East Asian astrological system that reads a person's character and life patterns from the **year, month, day, and hour** of birth. Each of the four pillars combines one Heavenly Stem (天干) and one Earthly Branch (地支), producing eight characters total: hence "Eight Characters" (八字). The system cycles on a **60-year Sexagenary** basis.

This calculator derives your Four Pillars using **pure mathematical calculation** — no AI involved.

## Understanding the Four Pillars

| Pillar | Represents | Domain |
|---|---|---|
| Year Pillar (年柱) | Birth year | Ancestry, family background, social environment |
| Month Pillar (月柱) | Birth month | Parents, upbringing, career luck |
| Day Pillar (日柱) | Birthday | **Your core self (Day Master)** · Spouse palace |
| Hour Pillar (時柱) | Birth hour | Children, late-life luck, inner desires |

<Callout type="tip">
**The Day Master is the key.** The Heavenly Stem of the Day Pillar represents "you." All analysis in Four Pillars astrology is anchored to the Day Master.
</Callout>

## Reading the Five Elements Distribution

The Five Elements — Wood (木), Fire (火), Earth (土), Metal (金), Water (水) — each appear across the eight characters. The balance (or imbalance) reveals which energies dominate your chart.

| Element | Strength Keywords | Challenge Keywords | Lucky Colors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wood (木) | Growth, creativity, humanitarianism | Indecision, idealism | Green, teal |
| Fire (火) | Passion, charisma, leadership | Impulsiveness, volatility | Red, purple |
| Earth (土) | Stability, trust, practicality | Conservative, slow to decide | Yellow, brown |
| Metal (金) | Decisiveness, justice, structure | Rigid, uncompromising | White, gold |
| Water (水) | Wisdom, adaptability, insight | Anxiety, over-thinking | Black, dark blue |

## FAQ

**Q. What if I don't know my birth time?**  
Select "unknown" — the calculator will default to noon (午時). The three remaining pillars still give a solid basic reading.

**Q. Can I enter a lunar calendar birthday?**  
This calculator uses the solar calendar. Convert a lunar date to solar first, then enter.

**Q. Is the Four Pillars system scientifically proven?**  
No. Saju is a traditional philosophical and cultural system from East Asia with no scientific validation. Use it as a tool for self-reflection and cultural exploration.

**Q. What is the Sexagenary Cycle?**  
60 unique combinations from 10 Heavenly Stems × 12 Earthly Branches (LCM = 60) that repeat on a 60-year cycle: 甲子 (Gap-Ja), 乙丑 (Eul-Chuk), … 癸亥 (Gye-Hae).
