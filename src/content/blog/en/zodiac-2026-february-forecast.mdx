## Key Astro Events — February 2026

February is the month of love and transition. Aquarius season (Jan 20–Feb 18) closes while Pisces season (Feb 19–Mar 20) opens — and Valentine's Day lands right in this emotionally charged shift.

| Event | Date | Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Full Moon in Leo | Feb 12 | Dramatic love energy, creative peak |
| Valentine's Day | Feb 14 | Venus energy amplified |
| Sun enters Pisces | Feb 19 | Intuition, romance, and spiritual depth |
| New Moon in Pisces | Feb 28 | Set intentions for inner growth |

<Callout type="tip">
**Valentine's 2026**: With Venus moving through Pisces around Valentine's Day, the love energy this year is exceptionally romantic, idealistic, and spiritually charged.
</Callout>
