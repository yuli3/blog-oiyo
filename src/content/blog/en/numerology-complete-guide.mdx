## What Is Numerology?

**Numerology** is a system that treats numbers as carriers of universal energy, calculating specific figures from your name and birth date to analyze personality and destiny.

Pythagoras (c. 570–495 BCE) is credited with laying the foundations of Western numerology, though number symbolism has an equally long history across Asian traditions.

## Core Numerology Calculations

### 1. Life Path Number

**The single most important number in your chart.** Add every digit of your birth date together until you reach a single digit.

**Example**: May 15, 1990
```
1+9+9+0 = 19 → 1+9 = 10 → 1+0 = 1 (year)
0+5 = 5 (month)
1+5 = 6 (day)

Life Path Number = 1 + 5 + 6 = 12 → 1+2 = 3
```

**Exception**: 11, 22, and 33 are Master Numbers and are never reduced further.

## Calculating Your 2026 Personal Year

Calculate your **2026 Personal Year number** to understand the energy the year holds for you.

**Formula**: Birth month + birth day + the digit sum of 2026 (2+0+2+6=10 → 1+0=1)

**Example**: Born May 15
```
5 + 15 + 1 = 21 → 2+1 = 3
→ 2026 Personal Year = 3
```

## Name Numerology

Each letter of your name is assigned a number to calculate your **Expression Number**.

```
A,J,S = 1   B,K,T = 2   C,L,U = 3
D,M,V = 4   E,N,W = 5   F,O,X = 6
G,P,Y = 7   H,Q,Z = 8   I,R   = 9
```

**Expression Number**: The sum of every letter in your full name
**Soul Urge Number**: The sum of the vowels only
**Personality Number**: The sum of the consonants only
