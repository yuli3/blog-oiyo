## 20-Question Mock Exam

**Q1 (FAR).** Which inventory method is NOT permitted under US GAAP?
A) FIFO  B) Weighted average  C) LIFO  D) Specific identification  E) None — all are permitted

**Q2 (FAR).** Under ASC 842, how does a lessee account for an operating lease?
A) No balance sheet recognition  B) Recognize ROU asset and lease liability  
C) Recognize only interest expense  D) Recognize only depreciation

**Q3 (FAR).** The General Fund in governmental accounting is used for:
A) Long-term capital projects  B) Debt service payments  
C) General operating activities  D) Special restricted purposes

**Q4 (FAR).** Step 3 of the ASC 606 five-step revenue model is:
A) Identify the contract  B) Identify performance obligations  
C) Determine the transaction price  D) Allocate the transaction price

**Q5 (AUD).** Audit risk formula:
A) AR = IR + CR + DR  B) AR = IR × CR × DR  
C) AR = (IR+CR)/DR  D) AR = IR × DR

**Q6 (AUD).** An auditor who also prepares the client's financial statements faces what independence threat?
A) Self-interest  B) Self-review  C) Advocacy  D) Familiarity

**Q7 (AUD).** Requesting bank balance confirmation from a third party is:
A) Inquiry  B) Observation  C) Confirmation  D) Inspection

**Q8 (AUD).** Financial statements with material and pervasive misstatements receive:
A) Unmodified  B) Qualified  C) Adverse  D) Disclaimer

**Q9 (REG).** The 2024 standard deduction for a Single filer is:
A) \$12,400  B) \$13,850  C) \$14,600  D) \$15,000

**Q10 (REG).** The C-Corporation federal income tax rate under TCJA is:
A) 15%  B) 21%  C) 25%  D) 35%

**Q11 (REG).** Partnership income is taxed:
A) At the entity level at 21%  B) As pass-through income to partners  
C) Only when distributed  D) At the partner's election

**Q12 (REG).** Which is NOT a required element for a valid contract?
A) Offer  B) Acceptance  C) Consideration  D) Written form

**Q13 (FAR).** Under US GAAP, research and development costs are:
A) Capitalized as intangible assets  B) Development phase only is capitalized  
C) Expensed immediately in the period incurred  D) Amortized over product life

**Q14 (FAR).** Under US GAAP, goodwill is:
A) Amortized over 40 years  B) Subject to annual impairment testing  
C) Expensed immediately  D) Written off when the acquired entity is sold

**Q15 (AUD).** PCAOB auditing standards apply to audits of:
A) All US companies  B) Publicly registered companies  
C) Non-profit organizations  D) Government entities

**Q16 (REG).** The default tax treatment of an LLC is:
A) C-Corp taxation  B) Pass-through to members  C) Exempt from taxation  D) 20% flat rate

**Q17 (FAR).** Under US GAAP, investment property is measured using:
A) Fair value method only  B) Cost method only  
C) Either fair value or cost  D) Net realizable value

**Q18 (AUD).** Analytical procedures are most appropriately used in:
A) Tests of controls only  B) Planning and final review stages  
C) Physical asset inspections  D) Confirmation procedures

**Q19 (REG).** S-Corporation eligibility does NOT allow:
A) US domestic corporation  B) Maximum 100 shareholders  
C) Only US citizen/resident shareholders  D) Multiple classes of stock

**Q20 (FAR).** Under the accrual basis of accounting:
A) Revenue recognized when cash is received  
B) Revenue recognized when earned; expenses when incurred  
C) Both based on cash flows  D) Management determines timing

## Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|---|--------|------------|
| 1 | E | All four methods (FIFO, LIFO, weighted avg, specific ID) are permitted under GAAP. LIFO is prohibited under IFRS. |
| 2 | B | ASC 842: operating leases → ROU asset + lease liability on balance sheet |
| 3 | C | General Fund = primary fund for general government operations |
| 4 | C | ASC 606 Step 3: **Determine the transaction price** |
| 5 | B | AR = IR × CR × DR (multiplication) |
| 6 | B | Preparing AND auditing = **self-review threat** |
| 7 | C | Bank balance request = **confirmation procedure** |
| 8 | C | Material AND pervasive = **Adverse opinion** |
| 9 | C | 2024 Single standard deduction = **$14,600** |
| 10 | B | C-Corp: **21%** flat federal rate (Tax Cuts and Jobs Act 2017) |
| 11 | B | Partnership: **pass-through** (Form 1065 → K-1 to partners) |
| 12 | D | Written form NOT required for all contracts (oral contracts are valid) |
| 13 | C | GAAP: R&D **expensed immediately** (IFRS allows development cost capitalization) |
| 14 | B | Goodwill: no amortization; **annual impairment test** (ASC 350) |
| 15 | B | PCAOB governs audits of **publicly registered** companies (SOX 2002) |
| 16 | B | LLC default = **pass-through to members** |
| 17 | B | US GAAP investment property: **cost model only** (unlike IFRS) |
| 18 | B | Analytical procedures: **planning stage and final review** |
| 19 | D | S-Corp = **only one class of stock** allowed |
| 20 | B | Accrual basis: revenue earned when performance complete; expense when incurred |

## Post-Exam: CPE and License Maintenance

- **Most states**: 40–80 CPE hours every 2 years
- Ethics hours typically required (2–4 hours minimum)
- AICPA membership is voluntary but beneficial for professional development
