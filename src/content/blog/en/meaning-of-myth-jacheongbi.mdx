Jacheongbi is an important female divinity in the Jeju shamanic myth "Segyeong Bonpuri." Her story holds agriculture and grain, love and trials, clever disguise, and return. Jacheongbi is also well known through her relationship with Mundoryeong, but she is not simply the heroine of a romance. She becomes a Segyeong divinity, an agricultural deity who brings the seeds of grain and the order of farming into the human world. Details vary by transmission, but her core is the power of a wise woman who sustains life.

## Core Myth — Representative Episodes

Jacheongbi forms a bond with Mundoryeong by dressing as a man or using quick wit to overcome difficult situations. Her story unfolds not as the story of a woman who waits passively, but as the story of a woman who learns, moves, and chooses for herself. Love is an important motive, but that love appears as a long process that must pass through social rules and divine trials.

Her relationship with Mundoryeong involves misunderstanding, separation, death and resurrection, and reunion. Jacheongbi sometimes undergoes extreme ordeals, then sets out again to find her way. The order and emphasis of scenes differ by transmission, but one point remains steady: Jacheongbi does not lose her wisdom amid hardship. She is the protagonist of emotion and, at the same time, a being who opens the order of livelihood.

At last, Jacheongbi brings the seeds of grain into the human world and takes her place as a deity of agriculture. This ending matters because it links a love story to the question of how people eat and live. The myth says that individual desire and communal survival are not separate, and Jacheongbi is the bridge that joins the two.

## Reading as an Archetype — A Jungian View

From a Jungian perspective, Jacheongbi is a figure in whom the archetypes of the **wise maiden** and the **life-giver** overlap. She pursues love, but she is not confined to love alone; through her own talents, she brings the order of grain to the community. In modern terms, she resembles a person who creates productivity in their own field through learning, movement, negotiation, and emotional labor.

The Jacheongbi archetype keeps femininity from being read only as obedience. She disguises herself, tests, chooses, and at times crosses social boundaries. For that reason, Jacheongbi is a symbol who grows through love but ultimately moves toward an order of life wider than love itself.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. What kind of deity is Jacheongbi?**

A. In Jeju shamanic myth, she is understood as a Segyeong divinity who governs agriculture and the order of grain. A love story and an agricultural myth are intertwined in her narrative.

**Q. Why is the story of Jacheongbi so complex?**

A. Because it was transmitted as an oral shamanic song, its details changed according to region and performance context. The core is the ordeal of a wise woman and the acquisition of agricultural order.
