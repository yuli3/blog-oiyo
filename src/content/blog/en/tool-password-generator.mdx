## Password Strength Guide

| Strength | Length | Composition | Crack Time (brute force) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Very weak | ≤6 chars | Numbers only | Instant |
| Weak | 8 chars | Lowercase only | Minutes |
| Fair | 10 chars | Upper + lower + numbers | Months |
| Strong | 12 chars | Upper + lower + numbers + special | Years |
| Very strong | 16+ chars | All character types + random | Centuries+ |

## Password Security Principles

| Principle | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Unique per site** | One breach exposes everything if reused |
| **12+ characters** | Minimum 12 chars; 16+ for sensitive accounts |
| **Rotate periodically** | Change important account passwords every 6 months |
| **Use a password manager** | 1Password, Bitwarden, KeePass, etc. |
| **Enable 2FA** | Authenticator app (e.g., Google Authenticator) over SMS |

## What to Avoid

- Name, birthdate, phone number
- Sequential numbers/letters: 1234, abcd
- Keyboard patterns: qwerty, asdf
- Simple word + number: password123
- Reusing previous passwords
