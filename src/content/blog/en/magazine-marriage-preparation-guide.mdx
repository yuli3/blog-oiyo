## The Reality of Getting Married

Average wedding costs (US, 2024):

- Venue + catering + photography + attire + flowers: **roughly $25,000–$35,000**
- Down payment or first/last month's rent on a new home: **$5,000–$30,000+ depending on city**
- Furniture and appliances for the new home: **$5,000–$20,000**

**Total funds needed**: In major metro areas, couples commonly spend $50,000–$100,000 all-in (new home costs included).

Starting without a realistic budget plan means stress and conflict before the wedding day even arrives.

## Wedding Preparation Timeline

### 12–18 Months Out

- **Set a tentative wedding date** (coordinate with both families)
- **Discuss the budget** (who pays for what, overall spending cap)
- **Start your home search** (research mortgage pre-approval or rental options)

### 6–12 Months Out

- **Book the venue** (popular venues fill up 12+ months in advance)
- **Book photographer, videographer, and florist**
- **Reserve honeymoon flights** (book 6+ months ahead for peak season)

### 3–6 Months Out

- **Send save-the-dates and invitations**
- **Purchase furnishings and appliances** (timed to move-in date)
- **Finalize the new home** (sign lease or close on purchase)
- **Shop for wedding and engagement rings**

### 1–3 Months Out

- **Confirm honeymoon itinerary**
- **Schedule a wedding rehearsal**
- **Gather documents for the marriage license**

### Within 1 Month

- **Final confirmation of day-of timeline**
- **Meet with officiant and emcee**
- **Apply for the marriage license** (most US states require this 3–30 days before the ceremony)

## Photography, Dress, and Hair & Makeup — The Most Complex Area

In the US, the big three visual vendors are **photographer/videographer**, **wedding dress**, and **hair & makeup artist**. You can book them as a package through a full-service wedding planner or hire each separately.

### Package vs. Individual Booking

**Package (through a wedding planner or venue)**: Convenient, but watch for upsells and locked-in vendors.
**Individual booking**: More control over quality, but requires more coordination.

### What to Confirm Before Signing

**Photographer/Videographer**:
- What locations are included (indoor, outdoor)?
- Do you receive all raw/edited files, or only a curated selection?
- How many edited images are included, and what is the album cost?
- Confirm the specific photographer assigned — portfolio samples may be from a different shooter.

**Wedding Dress**:
- How many fittings are included?
- What are the policies for stains or damage?
- Is alteration cost included?

**Hair & Makeup**:
- Confirm the specific artist (assistants sometimes take over on the day)
- Is a touch-up kit or mid-day touch-up included?
- Is a trial run available?

### Contract Checklist

- **Cancellation and refund terms** — read every clause before signing
- **Get the specific artist's name** in the contract
- **Pay by credit card**, not cash (protection for disputes)
- US consumer protection tip: many states have cooling-off periods for certain contracts; check your state's rules.

## Choosing a Venue

### Venue Types

| Type | Characteristics | Approximate Cost |
|------|----------------|-----------------|
| Hotel ballroom | Elegant, full service, easy parking | $10,000–$50,000+ |
| Dedicated wedding venue | Efficient, all-inclusive packages | $5,000–$20,000 |
| Intimate event space / loft | Smaller, unique, city vibe | $2,000–$8,000 |
| Outdoor / barn / garden | Scenic, weather-dependent | $2,000–$15,000 |

### Venue Contract Watch-outs

- **Per-head catering cost**: A $20 difference per guest multiplied by 150 guests is $3,000.
- **Guest minimum guarantee**: You may be charged for 150 guests even if only 100 attend.
- **Outside vendor restrictions**: Some venues require you to use their preferred caterer, DJ, or florist.
- **Alcohol policy**: Is a bar package included, or is there a corkage fee?
- **Parking**: Is there on-site parking, or will guests need to arrange transportation?

## Setting Up Your First Home

### Homebuyer and Renter Programs for Newlyweds

**FHA Loans**: Low down-payment (3.5%) option for first-time buyers with moderate credit.

**USDA Loans**: Zero-down-payment option for homes in eligible rural and suburban areas.

**State first-time homebuyer programs**: Many states offer down-payment assistance or below-market mortgage rates for first-time buyers — search "[your state] first-time homebuyer program."

**HUD-approved housing counseling**: Free guidance on the homebuying process (hud.gov).

### What to Prioritize When Choosing a Home

- **Commute times for both partners** — a 30-minute asymmetry in daily commutes compounds over years
- **School districts** (if children are in the plan)
- **True monthly cost**: mortgage/rent + HOA fees + utilities + insurance
- **Light, noise, and layout**: orientation, floor level, proximity to busy roads

## Furnishings and Rings

### Home Furnishing Priorities

**Essential appliances** (needed immediately):
1. Refrigerator
2. Washer and dryer
3. Air conditioning (window unit or central)
4. TV

**Quality-of-life additions** (when budget allows):
- Dishwasher (huge daily impact)
- Air purifier
- Robot vacuum

**Furniture**: Invest most in the mattress — you'll spend a third of your life on it. For sofas and dining tables, live in the space for a few weeks before buying so you understand the actual layout.

### Rings

**Engagement ring**: Average US spend is around $5,500 (The Knot, 2023), but what matters is what feels right for your relationship and budget.

**Wedding bands**: Simple bands typically cost $300–$1,500 each.

Practical advice: **Agree on expectations openly before shopping.** Misaligned expectations about rings are a surprisingly common source of early tension.

## Honeymoon Planning

### Popular Honeymoon Destinations

| Destination | Highlights | Approximate Cost (per person) |
|-------------|-----------|-------------------------------|
| Maldives | Overwater bungalows, snorkeling | $4,000–$8,000 |
| Bali | Nature, culture, excellent value | $2,000–$4,000 |
| Italy / France | Romance, history, food | $4,000–$9,000 |
| Mexico (Riviera Maya) | Easy from US, all-inclusive options | $1,500–$4,000 |
| Hawaii | No passport needed, stunning scenery | $2,500–$6,000 |

### Honeymoon Planning Checklist

- **Flights**: Book 6+ months in advance for summer (June–August) and winter holiday travel
- **Accommodation**: Compare all-inclusive packages vs. booking independently
- **Travel insurance**: Essential — covers medical emergencies, trip cancellation, and lost luggage
- **Credit cards**: Use a card with no foreign transaction fees

## After the Wedding: What to Do

1. **File for a marriage license / marriage certificate** (timing varies by state — typically before or shortly after the ceremony)
2. **Update your address** (USPS change of address, driver's license, voter registration)
3. **Update health insurance** (add spouse during the Special Enrollment Period within 30–60 days)
4. **Update beneficiaries** on life insurance, 401(k), and bank accounts
5. **Name change** (if applicable): Social Security Administration first, then DMV, then passport
6. **Review tax filing status**: Married filing jointly often reduces total tax burden

Marriage is not an event — it's a partnership. Building a solid financial foundation together matters more than a single spectacular day.
