The basic CVP analysis of [Cost Accounting chapter 13](/en/academy-cost-accounting-ch8/) assumed one product, pre-tax profit and known demand. Real companies sell several products, pay tax and cannot be sure of their sales volume. This chapter relaxes these three assumptions one by one to fit CVP analysis to real decisions.

## 1. Multiple products and the sales mix

Company BN sells product A (price ₩100,000, variable cost ₩60,000) and product B (price ₩80,000, variable cost ₩60,000) in a unit ratio of 3 : 1. Common fixed costs are ₩14 million.

<FormulaBox
  label="Weighted-average unit contribution margin"
  formula="\overline{CM} = \frac{3 \times 4 + 1 \times 2}{3 + 1} = 3.5, \qquad Q_{BEP} = \frac{1{,}400}{3.5} = 400\text{ units}"
  description="In ₩10,000. Splitting 400 units 3 : 1 gives a break-even point of 300 units of A and 100 of B. Calculating with 100 'packages' (3 A + 1 B, contribution margin 14) gives the same answer."
/>

### A. When the sales mix changes

If the mix shifts to 1 : 1, the weighted-average contribution margin becomes $(4 + 2)/2 = 3$ and break-even rises to about 467 units. Even at the same total volume, profit falls as the share of low-margin B grows. Tying sales incentives only to revenue can tilt the mix toward easy-to-sell, low-margin products.

## 2. After-tax target profit

With income tax, a target **after-tax** profit is converted to pre-tax profit.

<FormulaBox
  label="Units for an after-tax target profit"
  formula="Q = \frac{F + \dfrac{\text{Target after-tax profit}}{1 - t}}{\overline{CM}} = \frac{1{,}400 + \dfrac{560}{1 - 0.2}}{3.5} = \frac{1{,}400 + 700}{3.5} = 600\text{ units}"
  description="At a 20% tax rate, keeping ₩5.6 million after tax needs ₩7 million before tax. The break-even point is unaffected by tax because profit there is zero."

## 3. Sensitivity analysis

Look at how much the result moves when assumptions change. If BN cuts A's price by 10% (from ₩100,000 to ₩90,000), A's contribution margin becomes ₩30,000, the weighted-average contribution margin $(3 \times 3 + 2)/4 = 2.75$ and break-even about 509 units. A 10% price cut raises break-even by 27%. Unless you are confident the cut will lift volume by more than 27%, it reduces profit.

## 4. Uncertainty and expected value

When volume is uncertain, compute expected profit from the probability of each scenario. BN is considering spending ₩3 million on a new advertising campaign. Without it, sales are 500 units with 50% probability and 700 with 50%. With it, 600 units with 50% and 900 with 50%.

- No advertising: expected volume 600 units, expected profit $600 \times 3.5 - 1{,}400 = 700$ (₩7 million)
- Advertising: expected volume 750 units, expected profit $750 \times 3.5 - 1{,}700 = 925$ (₩9.25 million)

The expected incremental profit from advertising is ₩2.25 million. In the worst scenario (600 units), however, profit is ₩4 million, only ₩500,000 more than the worst case without advertising (500 units, ₩3.5 million). A risk-averse manager looks not only at expected value but also at the variance of profit and the worst outcome.

<Callout type="warning">
A multi-product break-even point is a single number only **on the assumption that the sales mix holds**. The actual mix keeps shifting with seasons, competition and sales strategy. And unless fixed costs are split into common and product-specific costs, product-level break-even points cannot be calculated. CVP analysis is a linear approximation, so do not extrapolate far beyond the planning range.
</Callout>

## Check your understanding

Company BO sells products P (contribution margin ratio 50%) and Q (30%) in a revenue ratio of 60 : 40. Fixed costs are ₩21 million and the tax rate is 25%. What are break-even sales and the sales needed for an after-tax profit of ₩9 million?

The weighted-average contribution margin ratio is $0.6 \times 50\% + 0.4 \times 30\% = 42\%$. Break-even sales are $2{,}100 / 0.42 = 5{,}000$ (₩50 million). The pre-tax profit for ₩9 million after tax is $900 / 0.75 = 1{,}200$ (₩12 million), so required sales are $(2{,}100 + 1{,}200) / 0.42 ≈ 7{,}857$, about ₩78.57 million.
