## Table tennis comes down to 11-point games and the serve

Table tennis is fast, but its rules are simple. Learn the 11-point scoring system and the serve rules, and that's really all you need.

---

## Scoring — games to 11

```
Game:   First to 11 points, winning by a margin of at least 2.
Match:  Usually best of 5 games, or best of 7 in higher-level play.
Deuce:  At 10-10, play continues until one side leads by 2
        (e.g. 12-10).
Serve:  The serve switches every 2 points (every 1 point once
        the game reaches deuce).
```

---

## Serve rules

```
- Toss the ball nearly straight up, at least 16cm, from an
  open palm
- Serve with your hand above the table surface, behind your
  own end line
- The serve must be an "open serve" — the ball can't be
  hidden from the receiver's view
- The ball must bounce once on your own side, clear the net,
  and bounce once on your opponent's side
- If the serve clips the net but still lands correctly, it's
  a "let" and is simply replayed
```

---

## Rally rules

```
- Return the ball after it bounces once on your own side —
  you can't volley it before it bounces
  → It must then clear the net and bounce once on your
  opponent's side
- A ball that clips the table's edge (not the side) counts as "in"
- If the ball hits the net and doesn't go over, you lose the point
- Touching the table with your free hand (the one not holding
  the paddle) loses you the point
```

---

## Doubles rules

```
- The serve must go diagonally — from your own right half
  of the table to your opponent's right half
- Teammates must alternate hits (A → B → opponent A →
  opponent B, in a fixed rotation)
- Hitting out of turn loses the point
```

---

## Key takeaways

1. Games go to 11 points (win by 2), usually decided over a best-of-5 match.
2. The serve alternates every 2 points — every 1 point once the score reaches deuce.
3. A legal serve requires a 16cm toss and must be an open serve.
4. Returns must come after the ball bounces once on your side (no volleying); a ball clipping the table edge is "in."
5. Doubles requires a diagonal serve and a fixed, alternating hitting order between teammates.

> This guide follows International Table Tennis Federation (ITTF) rules.
