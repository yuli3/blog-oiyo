The two planets that matter most for money in 2026 are **Mars** and **Jupiter**. Mars spends an extended stretch of 2026 in Taurus (the sign of material wealth), stirring up material-world energy. Jupiter, in Gemini, activates a variety of income channels.

## The Overall Money Energy of 2026

| Period | Money energy | Strategy |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Jan–Mar | Planning and cleanup | Set financial goals, cut unnecessary spending |
| Apr–Jun | A period of growth | Explore new income sources, negotiate and sign contracts |
| Jul–Sep | Peak material energy | Investing, real estate, sensory spending |
| Oct–Dec | Settling up and reaping rewards | Review this year's earnings, plan for next year |
