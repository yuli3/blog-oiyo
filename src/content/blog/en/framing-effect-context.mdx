## 90% Lean vs. 10% Fat

We do not make decisions in a vacuum. We react to how information is presented (the frame).

- **Positive Frame**: "This surgery has a 95% survival rate." (Patients say yes).
- **Negative Frame**: "This surgery has a 5% mortality rate." (Patients say no).

## Loss Aversion

Framing works because losing feels twice as bad as winning feels good.
To persuade someone, frame the choice as _avoiding a loss_ rather than _gaining a benefit_.
Don't say: "Buy this insulation to save money."
Say: "You are losing money every day through your walls."
