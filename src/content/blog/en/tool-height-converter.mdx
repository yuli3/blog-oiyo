## Conversion Formulas

| Conversion | Formula |
|---|---|
| cm → inches | cm ÷ 2.54 |
| inches → cm | inches × 2.54 |
| feet → cm | feet × 30.48 |
| cm → feet | cm ÷ 30.48 |
| 1 foot | = 12 inches = 30.48 cm |

<Callout type="tip">
**Quick mental shorthand**: 170 cm ≈ 5'7". Divide cm by 30.48 to get feet, then multiply the decimal remainder by 12 to get inches. 170 ÷ 30.48 ≈ 5.577 → 5 ft + 0.577 × 12 ≈ 6.9 in ≈ 5'7".
</Callout>
