## Key Astro Events — May 2026

May completes Taurus season and launches Gemini season — a shift from steady groundwork to energetic communication and multi-channel exploration. Family themes and deep connections run strong through the month.

| Event | Date | Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Full Moon in Scorpio | May 12 | Deep emotional release and transformation |
| Sun enters Gemini | May 21 | Communication, variety, and mental agility |
| New Moon in Gemini | May 27 | Plant seeds of new conversations and learning |

### Overall
A values and finances review month. What's truly worth your energy and money? This is the time to recalibrate.

### Love
Relationship quality versus quantity becomes clear. Invest deeply in what genuinely matters, release what doesn't.

### Money
Income-to-spending ratio gets optimized. Finding efficiencies now amplifies the income surge coming in June.

### Career
Your market value assessment leads to a pivotal decision. Know what you're worth before the next negotiation.
