## The Purpose of Insurance

Insurance is **not an investment** — it is risk transfer for events that are:
- **Low probability** but **high financial impact**
- Beyond your ability to self-insure with savings

**Insurance you need**:
- Major medical expenses
- Death of a primary income earner (with dependents)
- Liability from car accidents

**Insurance you probably don't need**:
- Extended warranties on small electronics
- Credit card insurance
- Whole/universal life insurance in most cases

---

## The 5 Essential Insurance Policies

### 1. Health Insurance
- Most critical insurance — a single hospital stay can cost $50,000+
- Priority: Minimize deductible + use HSA if enrolled in HDHP
- Open enrollment: use carefully; review plan annually

### 2. Term Life Insurance
- Pays a fixed death benefit for a set period (10, 20, or 30 years)
- **20–30x annual income for 20-year coverage** is a common guideline
- A healthy 30-year-old can get $500,000 coverage for ~$25–$35/month
- **Avoid whole life**: 5–10x more expensive; investment returns are weak

### 3. Disability Insurance
- **Most underrated insurance**: 1 in 4 Americans becomes disabled before retirement
- Covers 60–70% of income if you can't work
- Employer LTD policies exist but are often insufficient; consider supplemental

### 4. Auto Insurance
- Carry **liability limits of at least 100/300/100** (bodily injury/total/property)
- State minimums are dangerously low
- Comprehensive/collision: Skip if car value < $5,000

### 5. Renters/Homeowners Insurance
- Cheap (~$150–$400/year for renters), covers belongings and liability
- Required by most landlords; critical protection

---

## Whole Life vs. Term Life

| Feature | Term Life | Whole Life |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| Cost (same coverage) | $30/month | $200–$400/month |
| Duration | 10–30 years | Lifetime |
| Cash value | None | Grows slowly |
| Investment return | N/A | ~1–3% after fees |
| Best use case | Pure protection | Very rare exceptions |

**Bottom line**: Buy term, invest the difference.
