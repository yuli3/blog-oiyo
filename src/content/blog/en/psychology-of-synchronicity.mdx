## Tip of Acausal Connection

Carl Jung defined 'Synchronicity' as the simultaneous occurrence of two events that cannot be explained by physical causality (cause and effect) but are meaningfully connected. For example, a golden scarab beetle flying into the window while discussing a golden scarab beetle story.

## Why Does It Happen?

Jung believed it's because our unconscious is actually connected through a vast \*\*. Just as islands floating on a huge ocean are all connected land beneath the sea, our minds are connected to the material world at a deep level.

## How to Use Synchronicity

Synchronicity gives us a message: "You are not alone, you are connected to the universe." Look for the hidden meanings in events that seem like coincidences. They can awake your intuition and become a compass showing the direction of your life.\*\*
