## Chunking

Try to remember: M Y C A T I S B I G. (10 letters, hard).
Now try: MY CAT IS BIG. (4 words, easy).
This is **Chunking**. We break information into chunks to bypass the limit.

## Simplicity

If you give someone 10 instructions, they will forget the first 3. Keep it simple. Respect the limit.
