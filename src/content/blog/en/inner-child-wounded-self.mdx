## What is the Inner Child?

The 5-year-old, 10-year-old, 15-year-old me still lives within us. Especially the me from moments of hurt remains in my unconscious with time stopped. This is called the **Inner Child**.

## Signals of a Wounded Inner Child

- Overreacting to trivial matters (anger, tears)
- Clinging to relationships for fear of abandonment
- Unable to believe praise even when received
- Suffering from perfectionism

## Inner Child Healing Practice

**Step 1:** Look at childhood photos and write a letter to that me.

**Step 2:** Say "The feelings you had then were natural. It's not your fault."

**Step 3:** Imagine the adult me now hugging the child me.

## Reparenting

Give yourself what your parents couldn't give. Practice saying "Good job", "It's okay", "I love you" to yourself.
