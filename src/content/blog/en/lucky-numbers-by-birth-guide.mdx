Your birth date carries a distinct energy. Numerology holds that numbers each carry their own vibrational frequency, and that this energy connects to patterns in your life. This guide walks you through calculating and interpreting the **lucky numbers, colors, direction, and Life Path Number** tied to your birth date.

## How to Calculate Your Life Path Number

Your Life Path Number is the single most foundational figure in numerology.

**How to calculate it**: add together every digit of your birth date, repeating the process until you reach a single digit (1–9).

**Example**: born July 15, 1990

1+9+9+0 + 7 + 1+5 = 32 → 3+2 = **5**

→ Life Path Number: **5**

<Callout type="info">
**Master Numbers**: 11, 22, and 33 are left as they are rather than reduced further. These are called "Master Numbers," and they carry an especially potent energy and potential.
</Callout>

## Practical Ways to Put Your Luck to Work

### Using Your Numbers

- Choose dates for important contracts that include your lucky numbers
- Look for your lucky numbers in phone numbers or license plates
- Keep a slip of paper with your lucky numbers in your wallet

### Using Your Colors

- Wear your lucky color to important meetings or interviews
- Bring your lucky color into your bedroom or office decor
- Choose a wallet or bag in your lucky color

### Using Your Direction

- Position your desk to face your lucky direction
- Sit facing your lucky direction when making important decisions
- Place wealth, love, or health items on the side of your home aligned with your lucky direction
