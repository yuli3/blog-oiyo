## What Are Planets in Astrology?

In astrology, a **planet** symbolizes a specific energetic principle. Each planet represents a type of energy that defines "what is acting and how."

Planets fall into two broad groups:

| Group | Planets | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| **Personal planets** | Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars | Move quickly; represent personal traits |
| **Social/generational planets** | Jupiter, Saturn | Mid-speed; represent social patterns |
| **Transpersonal planets** | Uranus, Neptune, Pluto | Move slowly; represent generational influence |

---

## Personal Planets

### ☀️ Sun — The Core Self

**Symbolizes**: Ego, will, conscious identity, father
**Ruling sign**: ♌ Leo
**Orbital period**: 1 year (moves ~1° per day)

The Sun represents **"Who am I?"** The 'zodiac sign' most people know is the Sun sign.

The Sun's position in the birth chart indicates:
- The central purpose and direction of life
- Conscious self-identity
- Relationship with father and authority figures

**Strong Sun**: Confidence, leadership, powerful self-expression
**Challenged Sun**: Work on self-worth; may depend on others' approval

### 🌙 Moon — Emotions and the Unconscious

**Symbolizes**: Emotions, unconscious, mother, habits, instinct
**Ruling sign**: ♋ Cancer
**Orbital period**: 27.3 days (moves ~13° per day)

The Moon represents **"How do I feel?"** The Moon sign reveals emotional response patterns and unconscious instincts.

<Callout type="tip">
**The importance of the Moon sign**: Many people behave more like their Moon sign in daily life than their Sun sign. How you act in a comfortable setting and your emotional reactions follow your Moon sign.
</Callout>

### ☿ Mercury — Communication and Thought

**Symbolizes**: Thinking style, communication, language, learning, movement
**Ruling signs**: ♊ Gemini, ♍ Virgo
**Orbital period**: 88 days

Mercury represents **"How do I think and communicate?"**

Why Mercury retrograde gets attention: When Mercury is retrograde, communication errors, technology issues, and contract problems are said to arise.

### ♀ Venus — Love and Beauty

**Symbolizes**: Love, beauty, relationships, values, pleasure
**Ruling signs**: ♉ Taurus, ♎ Libra
**Orbital period**: 225 days

Venus represents **"What do I love and how do I form relationships?"**

Venus's role in romance:
- What type of partner you're attracted to
- How you express and receive love in relationships
- What you find beautiful

### ♂ Mars — Action and Desire

**Symbolizes**: Drive, desire, passion, competition, anger
**Ruling sign**: ♈ Aries (co-ruler: ♏ Scorpio)
**Orbital period**: 687 days

Mars represents **"How do I act and what do I desire?"**

Mars in romance: Physical attraction, sexual energy, how you pursue in a relationship

## Social Planets

### ♃ Jupiter — Expansion and Luck

**Symbolizes**: Expansion, luck, philosophy, education, optimism
**Ruling sign**: ♐ Sagittarius (co-ruler: ♓ Pisces)
**Orbital period**: 12 years (~1 year per sign)

Jupiter represents **"Where do I grow and encounter good fortune?"**

The sign and house where Jupiter is located marks the **area of life where expansion flows most easily**.

### ♄ Saturn — Structure and Discipline

**Symbolizes**: Limitation, responsibility, structure, discipline, time
**Ruling sign**: ♑ Capricorn (co-ruler: ♒ Aquarius)
**Orbital period**: 29.5 years

Saturn represents **"Where am I tested and where do I grow?"**

**The Saturn Return at ages 29–30**: The period when Saturn returns to its natal position. It symbolizes a restructuring of life and entry into true adulthood.

<Callout type="note">
**Saturn's paradox**: Saturn may seem to bring hardship, but the deepest growth happens in that very area. Those who overcome Saturn's challenges become masters of that domain.
</Callout>

## Transpersonal Planets

### ♅ Uranus — Innovation and Freedom

**Symbolizes**: Revolution, originality, freedom, change, technology
**Ruling sign**: ♒ Aquarius
**Orbital period**: 84 years (~7 years per sign)

Uranus is a generational planet. People born in the same era share the same Uranus sign. It symbolizes social revolution and generational change.

### ♆ Neptune — Spirituality and Dissolution

**Symbolizes**: Spirituality, illusion, art, compassion, dissolution
**Ruling sign**: ♓ Pisces
**Orbital period**: 165 years (~14 years per sign)

Neptune is the energy of dissolving boundaries and reaching toward transcendence. It symbolizes spirituality, art, and fantasy — and at times, confusion and deception.

### ♇ Pluto — Transformation and Regeneration

**Symbolizes**: Destruction and rebirth, power, depth, transformation
**Ruling sign**: ♏ Scorpio
**Orbital period**: 248 years (~12–30 years per sign)

Pluto carries the most powerful transformational energy. Where Pluto passes, **complete breakdown and regeneration** occur.

## Planetary Strength: The Dignity System

Each planet operates with greater or lesser strength depending on which sign it occupies.

| State | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Domicile** | In its ruling sign; strongest | Sun in Leo |
| **Exaltation** | Expresses at its most powerful | Sun in Aries |
| **Detriment** | In the opposite of its ruling sign; weakened | Sun in Aquarius |
| **Fall** | Opposite of exaltation; weakest | Sun in Libra |

<Callout type="tip">
**Planetary strength** requires synthesizing: which sign it's in + aspects (angular relationships) + house position. Being in domicile is not automatically ideal.
</Callout>
