# Tip Calculator

Navigating tipping in the US or Canada can feel daunting if you're not used to it. How much is appropriate? How do you split it among a group? This calculator handles it in seconds.

## Tipping Culture by Country

| Country | Custom | Appropriate Amount |
|---|---|---|
| **United States** | Effectively mandatory | Restaurant 18–22%, Taxi 15%, Hotel housekeeping $2–5/night |
| **Canada** | Near-mandatory | Restaurant 15–20%, similar to US otherwise |
| **United Kingdom** | Customary | Restaurant 10–15%, optional at bars |
| **Most of Europe** | Optional | Leave change or 5–10% |
| **Japan** | Can be seen as rude | No tipping culture; some may refuse |
| **South Korea** | None | No tipping culture |
| **Southeast Asia** | Growing | 5–10% at upscale restaurants, optional |

<Callout type="tip">
**US tip shortcut**: Calculate 15–20% of the pre-tax subtotal. Multiply the tax line by 2 for a quick ~18–20% tip estimate (average US sales tax ~9%).
</Callout>

## Recommended Tips by Service Type

| Service | Minimum | Standard | Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sit-down restaurant | 15% | 18–20% | 25%+ |
| Takeout / counter | None | 10% | 15% |
| Taxi / rideshare | 10% | 15% | 20% |
| Hotel room service | 15% | 18–20% | 25% |
| Housekeeping | $2/night | $3–5/night | $10/night |
| Bar (per drink) | $1 | $1–2 | $2–3 |
| Hair / nail salon | 15% | 18–20% | 25% |

<Callout type="note">
**Auto-gratuity**: US restaurants typically add an automatic 18–20% gratuity for parties of 6 or more. Check the bill for a "gratuity" or "service charge" line before adding an additional tip.
</Callout>
