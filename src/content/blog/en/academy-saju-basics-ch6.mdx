## The Structure of Luck Cycles

In Four Pillars, **luck (運)** is the flow of energy that changes with time. If the natal chart is the foundation, luck cycles are the temporal energy overlaid upon it.

```
Life = Natal Chart (foundation) + Major Luck Cycle (10-year) + Annual Luck (1-year) + Monthly Luck (1-month)
```

When all three luck cycles are positive simultaneously, it is the **peak of your life**; when all are negative, it is the **lowest point**.

---

## Major Luck Cycle (大運) Analysis

### Calculating the Major Luck Cycle

The Major Luck Cycle progresses **forward or backward from the Month Pillar**.

**Forward sequence (Yang males · Yin females)**:
- Males born in a Yang year
- Females born in a Yin year
- Progresses in order from the next stem-branch after the Month Pillar

**Reverse sequence (Yin males · Yang females)**:
- Males born in a Yin year
- Females born in a Yang year
- Progresses in reverse order from the stem-branch before the Month Pillar

**Calculating the start age**:
```
Forward: Days from birth to the next solar term (節入) ÷ 3
Reverse: Days from birth back to the previous solar term ÷ 3
(Remaining days are rounded)
```

<Callout type="note">
**Example**: Born in Gabia year, Gabin month; 24 days to the next solar term
→ 24 ÷ 3 = Major Luck Cycle begins at age 8
→ Changes at ages 8, 18, 28, 38…
</Callout>

### Sample Major Luck Cycle Sequence

For a forward Major Luck Cycle with Month Pillar **Gabin (甲寅)**:
```
1st cycle: Eulbyo (乙卯)
2nd cycle: Byeongjin (丙辰)
3rd cycle: Jeongsa (丁巳)
4th cycle: Muo (戊午)
…
```

### Core Principles of Major Luck Cycle Interpretation

**Major Luck Cycle Stem**: Psychological and interpersonal changes in the first 5 years
**Major Luck Cycle Branch**: Environmental and material changes in the latter 5 years

| Luck Cycle Ten God | Main Theme | Actual Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Bijep cycle | Competition, independence | Starting a business, career change, restructuring relationships |
| Siksang cycle | Expression, creation | New creative work, children, career changes |
| Jaeseong cycle | Wealth, reality | Increased income or sudden major expenses |
| Gwanseong cycle | Challenges, growth | Changes in social standing; watch health |
| Inseong cycle | Learning, support | Academic study, parental support, steady growth |

## Annual Luck (歲運) Analysis

### What Is Annual Luck?

Annual Luck is determined by **the stem-branch of that year**. For example, 2026 is the year of **Byeong-O (丙午年)**.

Byeong-Fire (丙) and O-Fire (午) of that year interact with everyone's chart.

### Annual Luck × Natal Chart Interaction

**Combination (合) of the Annual Luck Stem and Day Master**:
```
Gabgi combination (甲己合): If Gab-Wood Day Master has a Gi-Earth Annual Luck → Earth combination activates
Eulgyeong combination (乙庚合): Eul-Wood Day Master with Gyeong-Metal Annual Luck → Metal combination activates
```

When a combination forms, the energy of that element activates strongly.

**Clash (沖) between Annual Luck Branch and Natal Chart Branch**:
```
Ja (子) Annual Luck year + O (午) in natal chart → Ja-O clash activates
→ A year of relocation, career change, or major shift
```

### Criteria for Judging Annual Luck

**Characteristics of a favorable Annual Luck**:
1. Annual Luck element is your Lucky or Favorable Element
2. Annual Luck combines harmoniously with Major Luck Cycle
3. Annual Luck fills a vacancy (空亡) in the natal chart
4. No or weak clashes, conflicts, or breaches

**Annual Luck requiring caution**:
1. Annual Luck element is your Unfavorable or Harmful Element
2. Annual Luck clashes with Major Luck Cycle
3. A year when Conflicts (刑) activate
4. Annual Luck clashes with an important character in the natal chart

## Monthly Luck (月運) Analysis

### What Is Monthly Luck?

Monthly Luck is the **stem-branch of that month**. It provides a more refined timing picture within the Annual Luck.

**Timing important decisions**:
- Job offer / career change contracts: Lucky Element month
- Investment / business decisions: When Jaeseong monthly luck is favorable
- Marriage / starting cohabitation: Gwanseong or Jaeseong monthly luck
- Starting a new project: Siksang or Bijep monthly luck

### Monthly Fortune Intensity Table

```
Best: Major Luck Lucky Element + Annual Lucky Element + Monthly Lucky Element (triple favorable)
Good: 2 or more Lucky Elements overlapping
Average: Lucky and Unfavorable Elements mixed
Caution: Major Luck + Annual + Monthly all Unfavorable
```

## Case Study: Comprehensive Major and Annual Luck Analysis

### Hypothetical Case

**Chart**: Eul-Wood (乙木) Day Master, currently in **Byeong-Fire (丙火) Major Luck Cycle**
**Lucky Element**: Water (strong Eul-Wood; needs to be controlled)

**Major Luck Cycle Analysis (Byeong-Fire)**:
- Byeong-Fire is Sikshin (食神) for Eul-Wood, draining Wood energy
- A period of channeling Eul-Wood's energy outward
- Favorable for creative activity and entering society
- However, Lucky Element is Water but Fire Luck Cycle → slightly unfavorable direction

**2026 Annual Luck Analysis (Byeong-O year)**:
- Annual Luck Byeong-Fire → Sikshin for the Eul-Wood Day Master
- Annual Luck branch O-Fire (午) → adds additional Fire energy
- Fire doubly reinforced → unfavorable for Lucky Element Water

**Advice**:
- In 2026, be cautious of excessive activity and expenditure
- Strengthen collaboration with friends/partners who have Water (水) energy
- Focus important decisions in months when Water energy strengthens in the latter half of the year

## What Changes When You Know Your Luck Cycles?

| Without knowing luck cycles | With knowledge of luck cycles |
|---|---|
| Don't understand why things aren't working now | Understand the reason for difficulty and prepare |
| Hard to judge whether it's a good opportunity | Know when to move actively |
| Vague anxiety | Build a strategy fitted to the timing |
| Miss golden opportunities | Maximize peak periods when they arrive |

<Callout type="tip">
**Core of using luck cycles**: When a bad cycle comes you can't avoid it, but you can prepare and act defensively. When a good cycle comes, you can move more actively to maximize it. Knowing your luck cycles means knowing the timing of your life.
</Callout>
