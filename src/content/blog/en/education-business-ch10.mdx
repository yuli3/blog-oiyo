### (1) Market Segmentation Criteria Variables ★

| Classification | Variables | Role |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| **Customer Behavioral Variables (Primary Role)** | Benefits sought, usage situation, usage rate, brand loyalty, customer lifetime value | Core of identifying market segments |
| **Demographic Variables (Secondary Role)** | Age, gender, income, occupation, region, family composition | Market description and reach |
| **Psychographic Variables (Secondary Role)** | Lifestyle, personality | In-depth customer understanding |

**4 Conditions for Effective Segmentation**: Measurability / Substantiality / Accessibility / Intra-segment homogeneity & Inter-segment heterogeneity

### (3) Seven Positioning Approaches

1. **Attribute-based**: "World's largest theme park — Shanghai Disneyland"
2. **Benefit-based**: "Specialized in dandruff — Head & Shoulders shampoo"
3. **Use-occasion-based**: "Rough roads and safe winter driving — SUV commercials"
4. **User-based**: "For those who love adventure — Timex"
5. **Competitor-based**: "We're No.2. But we try harder — AVIS"
6. **Product category-based**: Positioning as an 'energy drink' rather than a cola
7. **Price/Quality-based**: Highest quality ↔ Reasonable price

## 6. ★ Pricing Strategy

### (1) Four Pricing Methods

| Method | Basis | Disadvantages |
| ------------------ | ------------------ | --------------------- |
| Cost-plus pricing | Cost + standard markup | Completely ignores customers and competitors |
| Target-return pricing | Achieving target ROI | Difficult to reflect changes in demand |
| Competitor-based pricing | Competitor prices | Ignores customer-perceived value |
| Perceived-value pricing | Value perceived by customers | Most desirable method |

### (3) Price Discrimination Strategy

Charging different prices to different customer segments for the same product.

**Conditions for Success**: Arbitrage (buying cheap and selling high) must be impossible / No legal issues / No negative customer emotions
