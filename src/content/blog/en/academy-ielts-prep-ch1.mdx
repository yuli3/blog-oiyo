## What Is IELTS?

**IELTS (International English Language Testing System)** is co-owned by British Council, IDP: IELTS Australia, and Cambridge Assessment English. Accepted by more than 11,000 organizations in over 140 countries, IELTS is the world's most widely taken English proficiency test.

---

## Two IELTS Test Types

| Type | For | Distinguishing Feature |
|------|-----|----------------------|
| **Academic** | University admission, professional registration | Academic reading and writing tasks |
| **General Training** | Immigration, work visas, vocational training | Practical, everyday content focus |

---

## IELTS Exam Structure

| Section | Duration | Score |
|---------|----------|-------|
| Listening | 30 min + 10 min transfer | Band 0–9 |
| Reading | 60 min | Band 0–9 |
| Writing | 60 min (2 tasks) | Band 0–9 |
| Speaking | 11–14 min (live examiner) | Band 0–9 |
| **Total** | **~2 hr 45 min** | **Overall Band 0–9** |

**Key feature**: Speaking is conducted face-to-face with a certified examiner, often on a different day.

## University Score Requirements

| Institution Type | Overall Band | Section Minimum |
|----------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Top UK universities (Imperial, UCL) | 6.5–7.0 | 6.0 each |
| Oxford / Cambridge | 7.0–7.5 | 7.0 each |
| Top Australian universities | 6.5–7.0 | 6.0 each |
| UK immigration/visa | 5.5–6.5 | Varies |

## IELTS vs. TOEFL

| Feature | IELTS | TOEFL |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Speaking format | Live 1:1 examiner interview | Microphone recording |
| UK university preference | Strong | Accepted |
| US university preference | Accepted | Strong |
| Accent | British English prominent | American English focus |
| Test availability | Weekly most locations | Periodic schedule |

## IELTS Academic Writing Tasks

### Task 1 (20 min, min 150 words)
Describe a graph, chart, diagram, map, or process objectively.
**Rule**: No personal opinion — report and summarize data only.

### Task 2 (40 min, min 250 words)
Write an essay on a given argument or issue.
**Important**: Task 2 carries more weight — approximately 2/3 of the Writing score.
