## The Jam Study

A grocery store display with 24 jams attracted many people, but only 3% bought jam.
A display with 6 jams attracted fewer people, but 30% bought jam.
Sales were 10x higher with fewer choices.

## Analysis Paralysis

When faced with too many options, we fear making the wrong choice, so we make no choice at all.
Limit your options to set yourself free.
