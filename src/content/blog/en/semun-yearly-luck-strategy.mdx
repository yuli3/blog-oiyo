## Harmony of Heaven, Earth, and Man

If Daewoon is 'Season (Environment)', Semun is a 'Guest (Event)' visiting that year. You must dress for the season and welcome the guest.

## Clash and Harmony

If this year's character clashes with characters in my chart (Chung), changes or movements occur. This is not bad but an \*\*. Conversely, if there is Harmony (Hap), collaborators appear or stability is found.

## No Bad Luck, Only Bad Attitude

If the fortune says "Be careful this year", don't be afraid but set a strategy: "Ah, I should look before I leap this year." A rain forecast means prepare an umbrella, not stay home.\*\*
