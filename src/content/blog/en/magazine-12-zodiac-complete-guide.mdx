## What Is the Zodiac?

The **Zodiac** divides the Sun's yearly path through the sky (the ecliptic) into 12 sections. Systematized in ancient Mesopotamia and Greece, this system of signs has become the most widely recognized astrological language in the world today.

In Western astrology, a sign isn't just an astronomical position — it carries a philosophical view that the energy pattern at the moment of your birth shapes your personality, temperament, and life direction. This isn't a scientifically verified claim, of course; it's best treated as a cultural tradition and a tool for self-understanding.

The 12 signs are organized by **four elements** (Fire, Earth, Air, Water) and **three modalities** (Cardinal, Fixed, Mutable), and this combination gives each sign its distinct energetic character.

### The four elements

| Element | Signs | Core energy |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 Fire | Aries, Leo, Sagittarius | Passion, drive, action, leadership |
| 🌍 Earth | Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn | Realism, stability, patience, practicality |
| 💨 Air | Gemini, Libra, Aquarius | Intellect, communication, ideas, freedom |
| 💧 Water | Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces | Emotion, intuition, depth, empathy |

## ♈ Aries — March 21 to April 19

**Ruling planet**: Mars | **Element**: Fire | **Modality**: Cardinal

The first sign of the zodiac. Born alongside the start of spring, Aries carries a natural pioneering energy. Bold and driven, with an instinct for leadership, Aries thrives on starting new things and welcoming a challenge.

**Strengths**: courage, drive, honesty, passion, independence
**Weaknesses**: impulsiveness, impatience, self-centeredness, easily bored
**Best matches**: Leo, Sagittarius, Gemini, Aquarius
**Lucky keywords**: red, Tuesday, the number 9, diamond

---

## ♉ Taurus — April 20 to May 20

**Ruling planet**: Venus | **Element**: Earth | **Modality**: Fixed

Taurus has finely tuned senses and a love of beauty and abundance. Known for seeing things through to the end and a strong pull toward stability, Taurus is dependable and grounded, valuing what's practical and real.

**Strengths**: persistence, reliability, practicality, sensory awareness, patience
**Weaknesses**: stubbornness, resistance to change, possessiveness, slow to decide
**Best matches**: Virgo, Capricorn, Cancer, Pisces
**Lucky keywords**: green, Friday, the number 6, emerald

---

## ♊ Gemini — May 21 to June 20

**Ruling planet**: Mercury | **Element**: Air | **Modality**: Mutable

Gemini stands out for intellectual curiosity and communication skill. With wide-ranging interests and a fast learning curve, Gemini's sociability lets them connect quickly with almost anyone. Versatile, but sometimes prone to scattered focus.

**Strengths**: communication, intellectual curiosity, flexibility, wit, versatility
**Weaknesses**: inconsistency, distractibility, moodiness, tendency toward surface-level relationships
**Best matches**: Libra, Aquarius, Aries, Leo
**Lucky keywords**: yellow, Wednesday, the number 5, agate

---

## ♋ Cancer — June 21 to July 22

**Ruling planet**: The Moon | **Element**: Water | **Modality**: Cardinal

Emotionally rich and deeply devoted to home and family, Cancer picks up on others' feelings through remarkable empathy and intuition. Protective and devoted, though prone to mood swings and holding onto hurt for a long time.

**Strengths**: empathy, intuition, devotion, creativity, memory
**Weaknesses**: mood swings, attachment, overprotectiveness, indirect communication
**Best matches**: Scorpio, Pisces, Taurus, Virgo
**Lucky keywords**: white/silver, Monday, the number 2, pearl

---

## ♌ Leo — July 23 to August 22

**Ruling planet**: The Sun | **Element**: Fire | **Modality**: Fixed

Naturally charismatic and a born leader, Leo carries a presence that lights up the room like the star of the show. Generous and warm-hearted, Leo inspires the people around them. Recognition matters deeply to Leo, who will give everything for the people they love.

**Strengths**: charisma, generosity, creativity, loyalty, leadership
**Weaknesses**: self-centeredness, stubbornness, a need to show off, sensitivity to criticism
**Best matches**: Aries, Sagittarius, Gemini, Libra
**Lucky keywords**: gold, Sunday, the numbers 1 and 5, ruby

---

## ♍ Virgo — August 23 to September 22

**Ruling planet**: Mercury | **Element**: Earth | **Modality**: Mutable

Virgo brings sharp analysis and a perfectionist streak. Practical and logical, Virgo handles anything with meticulous care. A strong sense of service, real interest in health, and an excellent instinct for cutting out what isn't needed.

**Strengths**: analytical skill, attention to detail, practicality, service-mindedness, health awareness
**Weaknesses**: over-criticism, perfectionism-driven stress, excessive worry
**Best matches**: Taurus, Capricorn, Cancer, Scorpio
**Lucky keywords**: brown/beige, Wednesday, the number 6, sapphire

---

## ♎ Libra — September 23 to October 22

**Ruling planet**: Venus | **Element**: Air | **Modality**: Cardinal

Libra seeks balance and harmony above all. With a natural sense for beauty and fairness, Libra excels at diplomacy and mediation, smoothing over conflict. Partnership matters deeply, and Libra grows through relationships.

**Strengths**: diplomacy, fairness, aesthetic sense, cooperation, communication skill
**Weaknesses**: indecisiveness, conflict avoidance, dependence on others, seeking surface-level peace
**Best matches**: Gemini, Aquarius, Aries, Leo
**Lucky keywords**: pastel blue/pink, Friday, the numbers 6 and 9, opal

---

## ♏ Scorpio — October 23 to November 21

**Ruling planet**: Pluto (traditional: Mars) | **Element**: Water | **Modality**: Fixed

Scorpio carries deep, intense energy. Known for piercing insight into what lies beneath the surface, and an unrelenting focus once committed to something. Loyal and deeply emotional, Scorpio embodies the energy of transformation and rebirth.

**Strengths**: insight, focus, loyalty, capacity for transformation, deep analysis
**Weaknesses**: suspicion, vindictiveness, excessive need for control, secretiveness
**Best matches**: Cancer, Pisces, Taurus, Virgo
**Lucky keywords**: black/red, Tuesday, the number 8, topaz

---

## ♐ Sagittarius — November 22 to December 21

**Ruling planet**: Jupiter | **Element**: Fire | **Modality**: Mutable

A philosopher and explorer at heart, Sagittarius loves freedom and adventure. Optimistic and open-minded, Sagittarius sees the world through a wide lens and holds a genuine hunger for knowledge and truth. Direct and honest, always wanting the freedom to live life on their own terms.

**Strengths**: optimism, philosophical thinking, adventurousness, honesty, wisdom
**Weaknesses**: irresponsibility, exaggeration, impatience, freedom without direction
**Best matches**: Aries, Leo, Gemini, Libra
**Lucky keywords**: purple/turquoise, Thursday, the number 3, turquoise

---

## ♑ Capricorn — December 22 to January 19

**Ruling planet**: Saturn | **Element**: Earth | **Modality**: Cardinal

The symbol of steady discipline, Capricorn moves toward goals with unwavering consistency. Ambitious and highly self-disciplined, Capricorn reaches high targets through responsibility and patience. Careful and realistic, Capricorn gets stronger with time.

**Strengths**: patience, self-discipline, responsibility, realistic ambition, reliability
**Weaknesses**: excessive caution, difficulty expressing emotion, rigidity, workaholism
**Best matches**: Taurus, Virgo, Cancer, Scorpio
**Lucky keywords**: dark green/brown, Saturday, the number 8, garnet

---

## ♒ Aquarius — January 20 to February 18

**Ruling planet**: Uranus (traditional: Saturn) | **Element**: Air | **Modality**: Fixed

A forward-looking, innovation-driven thinker who values freedom. Aquarius carries a real desire to change the world through original ideas and humanitarian values. Independent and unconventional, Aquarius never loses their individuality even within a group.

**Strengths**: originality, humanitarianism, intelligence, independence, future-oriented thinking
**Weaknesses**: emotional distance, stubborn individualism, excessive idealism
**Best matches**: Gemini, Libra, Aries, Leo
**Lucky keywords**: electric blue, Saturday, the numbers 4 and 11, amethyst

---

## ♓ Pisces — February 19 to March 20

**Ruling planet**: Neptune (traditional: Jupiter) | **Element**: Water | **Modality**: Mutable

The last of the 12 signs. Said to carry a trace of every other sign's energy, Pisces has boundless empathy and artistic sensitivity. Moving freely between reality and dreams, Pisces offers healing and comfort through deep spiritual and emotional understanding.

**Strengths**: empathy, creativity, spiritual sensitivity, intuition, tolerance
**Weaknesses**: escapism, excessive self-sacrifice, difficulty setting boundaries, indecisiveness
**Best matches**: Cancer, Scorpio, Taurus, Virgo
**Lucky keywords**: sea green/lavender, Thursday, the numbers 7 and 12, aquamarine
