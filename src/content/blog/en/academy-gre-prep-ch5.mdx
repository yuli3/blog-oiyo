## Final Strategy Summary

| Section | Core Strategy |
|---------|--------------|
| Verbal TC/SE | Full context → predict word → match choices |
| Verbal RC | Map structure → follow questions → "must be true" |
| Quant QC | Test special values (0, 1, fractions, negatives) |
| Quant PS | Backsolve, estimate, simplify |
| AWA Issue | Position → 2–3 reasons + examples → counterargument → conclusion |
| AWA Argument | Identify logical flaws, not personal opinion |

## Score Improvement Roadmap

### Verbal 158+
- **Flashcard system**: 500–1000 GRE-specific vocabulary words (Magoosh GRE Vocabulary app)
- **Daily RC practice**: 2 passages + questions per day
- **Track error patterns**: Keep a log of which question types you miss

### Quantitative 160+
- **QC special values automatization**: 0, 1, −1, fractions, large numbers
- **Formula memorization**: Quadratic formula, special triangles, circle/cone/sphere
- **Speed training**: Practice 25% faster than the actual test pace

### AWA 4.5+
- **Pre-write 2–3 essays**: Use actual ETS prompt pool for practice
- **Study scored sample essays**: ETS publishes 6-point and 4-point samples with commentary

## Statement of Purpose (SOP) Framework

The SOP is as important as your GRE score for competitive programs.

```
Paragraph 1 (Hook + Research Interest):
A specific moment, question, or experience that drew you to this field

Paragraphs 2–3 (Research/Experience Background):
Specific projects, publications, internships with concrete outcomes

Paragraph 4 (Research Plan):
Your specific research questions or direction for the program

Paragraph 5 (Why This Program):
Name specific faculty + lab + program resources that fit your plan

Paragraph 6 (Conclusion):
How your work will contribute to the field
```

## Recommendation Letter Strategy

For graduate school (especially PhD programs):
- **Faculty letters > Professional letters**: A letter from a research supervisor who knows your intellectual abilities is gold
- **Brief your recommenders** on your research interests and target programs
- Request letters at least **6–8 weeks** in advance

## GRE Exam Day Tactics

### Section Order
- AW → Verbal → Quantitative is the default order
- Place your weakest section last to preserve peak concentration for strong sections

### Time Management
- Verbal: ~1.5 min/question
- Quant: ~1.75 min/question
- AWA: 5 min brainstorm + 22 min writing + 3 min review

## ScoreSelect: Strategic Score Sending

The GRE's **ScoreSelect** feature lets you choose which test dates' scores to send:
- Most recent only
- All scores
- Any specific test date

You may take the GRE **up to 5 times in 12 months** (no lifetime limit). Use ScoreSelect strategically: if your second attempt is significantly better, you don't have to show the first.
