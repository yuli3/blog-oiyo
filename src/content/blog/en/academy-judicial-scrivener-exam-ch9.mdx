## Contract Law — Common Mistakes

```
① Rescission vs. Termination
   → Rescission = retroactive unwinding + restitution (treated as never formed)
   → Termination = prospective only (lease, employment, ongoing services)

② Disclaimer Deadline = 9 Months
   → Written qualified disclaimer under IRC § 2518 within 9 months of death

③ Forced / Elective Share
   → Surviving spouse only: typically 1/3 to 1/2 of augmented estate
   → Children have NO forced share under US law

④ Tort Statute of Limitations = Discovery Rule (commonly 2–3 years)
   → Do not confuse with general contract SOL (commonly 4–6 years)

⑤ Respondeat Superior = Employee Acting in Scope of Employment
   → Personal frolic outside job duties = NOT employer liability
```

## Recording Law — Common Mistakes

```
⑥ Recording = Notice, Not Validity Guarantee
   → Recording gives constructive notice; recorder does NOT warrant title

⑦ No Warranty of Title by Recorder
   → Formal review of instrument only; title insurance fills the protection gap

⑧ Lis Pendens = Priority Notice (Relates Back on Final Judgment)
   → Subsequent deeds recorded after lis pendens take subject to outcome

⑨ Entity Existence = Articles Filed AND Approved
   → Drafting articles of organization alone does NOT create the entity

⑩ Amendment Filing = Within Statutory Period (commonly 30–90 days)
   → Name change, registered agent change, officer change all require timely filing
```

## Civil Procedure — Common Mistakes

```
⑪ Claim Preclusion Applies to Final Judgment Only (not interim rulings)
⑫ Federal Appeal Deadline = 30 Days; State courts vary (14–60 days)
⑬ Enforcement Tools = Writ, Garnishment, Lien, Levy
   → Writ of execution, wage garnishment, recorded judgment lien, bank levy
⑭ Attachment = Monetary; Injunction/TRO = Non-Monetary
⑮ Closing Escrow = Funds + Title Transfer Simultaneously
```

## Business Entity Law — Common Mistakes

```
⑯ Entity Legally Exists = Secretary of State Approves Filing
⑰ Corporate Supermajority = 2/3 of votes cast (common default); charter may vary
⑱ Director Term = Typically 1 year (annual election); charter may stagger
⑲ Derivative Suit Threshold = Shareholder must have owned shares at time of wrong
⑳ Merger = Surviving Entity Assumes All Liabilities of Merged Entity
```

## Key Concept Cards

**Rescission = Retroactive; Termination = Prospective** ★★★★★
: Rescission treats the contract as never having existed; termination ends obligations going forward.
*Memory hook: rescind = retroactive; terminate = prospective*

**Enforcement = Writ / Garnishment / Lien** ★★★★★
: Three primary tools to collect on a money judgment.
*Memory hook: collect = writ/garnish/lien*

**Entity Existence = Filing Approved** ★★★★☆
: The entity comes into existence only upon state approval of the articles.
*Memory hook: entity = state approval*

## Practice Quiz

**Q. What is the practical significance of claim preclusion (res judicata) applying only to the judgment, not the court's reasoning?**
> Claim preclusion bars re-litigation of the same claim after a final judgment on the merits. Because it applies to the claim — not every factual finding in the opinion — a plaintiff whose claim was dismissed may still have related claims that were not fully litigated. For example, a dismissed breach-of-contract claim does not bar a separate tort claim arising from the same facts if that tort was not raised. This distinction matters in litigation strategy when deciding which claims to pursue first.

**Q. In what service areas do document preparers and attorneys compete?**
> Document preparers dominate: real property recording, LLC/corporate filings, routine probate petitions for pro se filers. Attorney-only work: courtroom representation, legal advice, signing as counsel of record. Competitive overlap: complaint drafting for pro se litigants, business formation counseling, and estate planning documents (where UPL rules vary by state). Cost advantage favors document preparers for high-volume, standardized recording work.
