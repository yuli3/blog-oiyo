# Apartment Subscription Score Calculator

Korea's apartment subscription point system (청약 가점제) allocates a portion of new housing units in regulated zones based on priority scores. Knowing your exact score helps you assess your chances before applying.

## Score Breakdown

| Category | Max Points | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Homeless period** | 32 pts | 32 pts for 15+ years, 2 pts for under 1 year |
| **Number of dependents** | 35 pts | 35 pts for 6+ dependents, 5 pts for none |
| **Subscription savings account tenure** | 17 pts | 17 pts for 15+ years, 1 pt for under 6 months |
| **Total** | **84 pts** | |

<Callout type="tip">
**Homeless period**: Counted from age 30 or the date of marriage (based on the household head). Proof requires a resident registration document showing no prior home ownership.
</Callout>

## Win Probability by Score (Seoul, 2023–2024)

| Score | Eligible Areas | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| 70+ | Gangnam 3 districts, premium locations | Top 1% |
| 60–69 | Central Seoul | Hard |
| 50–59 | Outer Seoul / capital region | Moderate |
| 40–49 | Outer capital region | Possible |
| Below 40 | Focus on lottery-based allocations | Luck-dependent |

## First-Priority Subscription Conditions

| Zone | Requirements |
|---|---|
| Speculative / overheated zones | 2+ years account tenure + 24+ payment installments |
| Capital region | 1+ year + 12+ installments |
| Non-capital region | 6+ months + 6+ installments |

<Callout type="note">
**2024 rule changes**: Newlywed special supply ratios have been expanded and first-time buyer income thresholds relaxed. Always check the official announcement on Korea's Cheongyanghome portal (apply.lh.or.kr) before submitting an application.
</Callout>
