# The outer planets make more sense as systems than as places to land

Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune are hard to picture if you imagine them the way you'd imagine Earth — as a solid surface you could stand on. They're better understood as **miniature planetary systems in their own right**, each built around an enormous atmosphere, rings, a powerful magnetic field, and dozens of moons.
