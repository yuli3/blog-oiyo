## The Courtroom Study

Researchers analyzed 1,100 judicial rulings.
Parole probability at 9 A.M.: ~65%.
Parole probability at 4 P.M.: ~0%.
The judges were not malicious. They were tired. The safe default option is "deny parole."

## Steve Jobs' Turtleneck

Why did Jobs wear the same clothes every day? To save decision energy.
Don't waste your limited willpower on picking socks. Save it for big decisions.
