## 20-Question Final Mock Exam

### Quantitative (Q1–7)

**Q1.** A rectangle's length is twice its width. If the perimeter is 36, what is the area?
A) 36  B) 54  C) 72  D) 81  E) 96

**Q2.** The average height of 5 people is 170cm. A 190cm person joins. What is the new average?
A) 173.3cm  B) 174.3cm  C) 175cm  D) 176.3cm  E) 177cm

**Q3.** What is the sum of the roots of x² − 5x + 6 = 0?
A) 1  B) 2  C) 3  D) 5  E) 6

**Q4.** How many ways can 3 people be selected from a group of 6?
A) 18  B) 20  C) 24  D) 30  E) 36

**Q5.** A price is discounted 10%, then discounted another 10%. What is the total % discount?
A) 19%  B) 20%  C) 21%  D) 22%  E) 25%

**Q6.** What is the area of a circle with radius 6?
A) 12π  B) 24π  C) 36π  D) 48π  E) 72π

**Q7.** Which of the following is always true about prime numbers?
A) All prime numbers are odd  
B) 8 is a prime number  
C) 1 is a prime number  
D) 2 is the only even prime number  
E) All odd numbers are prime

### Verbal (Q8–13)

**Q8 (Assumption).** "Remote workers are more productive than office workers. Therefore, all employees should work remotely." The argument assumes:

A) All employees are suited for remote work  
B) Productivity is the only criterion for employment decisions  
C) Office environments are unnecessary  
D) Remote work reduces costs  
E) Both A and B

**Q9 (Weaken).** "Crime rates rose in this city. Police budgets also rose. Therefore, more police cause crime increases."

A) The budget increase was decided before the crime increase was observed  
B) Other cities saw crime rates fall  
C) An economic recession occurred in the same period  
D) More police do not always reduce crime  
E) The city's population grew

**Q10 (Inference).** "All citizens of Country A have passports. Ji-Young does not have a passport."

A) Ji-Young is not a citizen of Country A  
B) Ji-Young does not like to travel  
C) Countries other than A don't require passports  
D) Ji-Young is a citizen of another country  
E) Both A and D

**Q11 (Strengthen).** "90% of customers who used Product X were satisfied. Therefore Product X is high quality." Which strengthens this most?

A) Only 50% of customers who did NOT use Product X were satisfied  
B) Product X is cheaper than competitors  
C) Product X is environmentally friendly  
D) Customer satisfaction is an accurate proxy for product quality  
E) Product X comes with a 10-year warranty

(1) x² = 9  
(2) x + 5 > 3

A) (1) alone  B) (2) alone  C) Both together  D) Either alone  E) Neither

**Q13 (DS).** Is n even?

(2) n/2 is an integer

A) (1) alone  B) (2) alone  C) Both  D) Either alone  E) Neither

**Q14 (DS).** Is xy > 0?  
(1) x > y  
(2) y < 0

A) (1) alone  B) (2) alone  C) Both  D) Either  E) Neither

**Q15 (Table).** A table shows Revenue and Expenses for 3 companies in 2023 and 2024. Without seeing the actual numbers, which type of statement CAN be answered from Revenue and Expenses data?

A) Which company has the highest market share  
B) Which company had the highest profit margin in 2023  
C) Which company has the strongest brand recognition  
D) Which company will grow fastest in 2025  
E) None of the above

## Exam Day Playbook

### The Night Before
- 7–8 hours of sleep (non-negotiable)
- Review your formula sheet one final time — no new material
- Confirm test center location, required ID, and allowed materials

### Exam Morning
- Eat a real meal 90 minutes before your start time
- Arrive 30 minutes early
- During the section order selection: put your strongest section last (to preserve peak focus)

### During the Exam
- First 10 questions: maximum focus, normal pacing (don't rush AND don't dawdle)
- If stuck: eliminate, guess, move on — never leave a question unanswered
- Use scratch paper actively — writing prevents careless errors

### Score Preview & Cancellation
- You see your unofficial scores immediately after the exam
- You have 2 minutes to Accept or Cancel before scores are recorded
- If you cancel, the score disappears from your record (schools won't see it)
- Retake: available 16 days later; max 5 times/year, 8 times lifetime
