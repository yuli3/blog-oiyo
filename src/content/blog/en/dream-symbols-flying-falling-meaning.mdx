## 1. Flying Dream

Symbolizes \*\*. You dream this when you regained control in life or overcame obstacles. But if you were scared flying too high, it might be a warning that you are losing touch with reality.

## 2. Falling Dream

Means \*\*. Often appears when fearing the collapse of support systems (job, relationship). They say it means growing taller, but for adults, it's a signal to "let go and rest".

## 3. Teeth Falling Out

Teeth are tools for chewing (aggression) and part of appearance. Falling teeth symbolize ** or regret over words spoken.**
