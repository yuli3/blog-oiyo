## What Is Lorem Ipsum?

"Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet..." derives from Cicero's Latin philosophical work *De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum* (45 BC). Printers in the 1500s began using a scrambled version as a font specimen, which spread through Letraset dry-transfer sheets in the 1960s and was eventually adopted as the standard in modern design software.

<Callout type="tip">
**Non-Latin fonts**: For testing CJK (Chinese, Japanese, Korean) font rendering and text layout, actual native-language dummy text is far more accurate than Lorem Ipsum. Use the Korean option in this generator for Korean UI projects.
</Callout>
