Enneagram Type 2 is known as **The Helper** or **The Giver**. Deep in their core sits a conviction: *"I must help others to be loved."* The need to feel loved and to be seen as needed is the central driving force of their lives.

## Type 2 Core Profile

| Attribute | Description |
|------|------|
| Type Name | The Helper / The Giver |
| Basic Desire | To be loved; to be seen as needed |
| Basic Fear | Being unloved; becoming unnecessary |
| Core Motivation | Meeting others' needs and receiving love in return |
| Center | Feeling (Heart) Center |
| Worldview | "I am most valuable when I'm helping others" |

## Personality Traits

### Healthy Type 2 (Levels 1–3)

- **Unconditional love**: Genuinely cares for others without expecting anything in return
- **Self-love**: Recognizes and meets their own needs
- **Healthy boundaries**: Can say "no" without guilt
- **Authenticity**: Serves others without hidden agendas or unspoken expectations

### Average Type 2 (Levels 4–6)

- Excessively dependent on others' praise and gratitude
- Cannot ask for what they want directly — communicates indirectly ("I'm fine, I just want you to be happy")
- Worries about what will happen to others without them
- Strongly absorbs others' emotions and moods as their own

### Unhealthy Type 2 (Levels 7–9)

- Demands reciprocity for help and is deeply wounded when rejected
- Uses their sacrifices to induce guilt in others
- Overly intrudes in others' lives
- Creates manipulative and co-dependent relationships

## Wing Types

### 1w2 (One Wing)

- More principled and ethical
- Enhanced qualities of educator and counselor
- Compulsion for help to be given the "right" way
- More introverted and deliberate

### 3w2 (Three Wing)

- More extroverted and sociable
- Places greater value on success and recognition
- High-energy and active
- Helps through social influence and charm

## Stress & Security Directions

| State | Direction | Characteristics |
|------|------|------|
| Under Stress | → Type 8 (Challenger) | Suddenly becomes aggressive and controlling |
| Feeling Secure | → Type 4 (Individualist) | Recognizes and expresses their own emotions and needs |

## Relationship Patterns

### Relationship Strengths

- Attentively cares for a partner and anticipates their needs
- Connects at a deep emotional level
- Wholly devoted to the relationship

### Relationship Challenges

- Suppresses their own needs until a breaking point
- May become overly dependent on a partner — or make the partner dependent on them
- Highly vulnerable to rejection

### Ideal Partner

Someone who cherishes Type 2's care while also creating space for them to voice their own needs.

## Growth Guide for Type 2

1. **Practice saying "No"**: You can be loved without accepting everything.
2. **Notice your own needs first**: Like an airplane oxygen mask — take care of yourself before helping others.
3. **Separate giving from getting**: Helping unconditionally, or choosing not to help — both are healthy choices.
4. **Receive gratitude gracefully**: Instead of saying "It was nothing," let yourself hear "Thank you."

**To Type 2**: You are worthy of love simply by existing. Helping is your choice — not a measure of your worth.
