## What Is the Six Relationships Theory?

**The Six Relationships Theory (六親論)** analyzes which family members — parents, siblings, spouse, children — each Ten God component symbolizes in a chart, and what relational patterns emerge based on that Ten God's strength or weakness.

When a specific Ten God is strong or weak in a chart, characteristic patterns tend to appear in the corresponding family relationship.

---

## Ten Gods and Family Correspondence Table

### For Male Charts

| Ten God | Symbolizes (for men) |
|---|---|
| Bijeon / Geomjae | Brothers, colleagues, competitors |
| Sikshin / Sanggwan | Children, creative output, students |
| Pyeonjae | Father, lover, the opposite sex |
| Jeongjae | Wife, wealth |
| Pyeongwan | Children (sons), pressure, superiors |
| Jeonggwan | Children (daughters), honor, workplace |
| Pyeonin | Stepmother, aunt, a special elder figure |
| Jeongin | Mother, academics, mentor |

### For Female Charts

| Ten God | Symbolizes (for women) |
|---|---|
| Bijeon / Geomjae | Sisters, colleagues, competitors |
| Sikshin / Sanggwan | Children, creative output |
| Pyeonjae | Father, mother-in-law |
| Jeongjae | Father, wealth |
| Pyeongwan | A potential husband, lover |
| Jeonggwan | Husband, workplace, honor |
| Pyeonin | Stepmother, a special elder figure |
| Jeongin | Mother, academics |

<Callout type="note">
**Note**: The family correspondences above reflect the traditional perspective of classical Saju. Rather than applying them literally to modern family structures, it is more useful to understand them as psychological patterns.
</Callout>

## Analyzing the Parental Relationship

### Mother — Jeongin (正印)

**Strong Jeongin**: Abundant maternal love and care. An environment of academic support. Grew up in a stable home.

**Weak or absent Jeongin**: Emotional distance from mother; often becomes independent early. Must forge one's own learning path.

**Jeongin under clash or control**: Possible mother's health issues or relational conflict.

**Excessive Jeongin**: Excessive dependency; difficulty separating from mother.

### Father — Pyeonjae (偏財) or Jeongjae (正財)

The father is represented by **Jaeseong (財星)** for both men and women.

**Strong Jaeseong**: Father is socially active with an entrepreneurial character. Abundant financial support.

**Weak or absent Jaeseong**: Emotional distance from father, father's absence, or financial hardship.

## Analyzing the Spousal Relationship

### Male's Spouse — Jeongjae (正財)

**Strong and favorable Jeongjae**: A stable spouse; a marriage that provides financial support; a realistic partner.

**Weak Jeongjae**: Marriage may come late, or there may be wealth-related conflict with the spouse.

**Jeongjae under clash or control**: Potential for marital conflict; be mindful of divorce.

**Multiple Jeongjae**: Tendency to pursue multiple relationships simultaneously, or the spouse may change.

### Female's Spouse — Jeonggwan (正官) or Pyeongwan (偏官)

**Favorable Jeonggwan**: A socially successful and stable spouse. An honorable marriage.

**Only Pyeongwan**: Drawn to strong, charismatic partners.

**No Gwanseong**: Indifference to marriage, or a preference for independent life.

**Excessive Gwanseong**: Complicated relationships with men, or feeling much pressure in married life.

<Callout type="tip">
**Modern interpretation**: The absence of Gwanseong in a woman's chart does not mean she cannot marry. Modern Saju interprets this as a pattern of independent, self-directed living.
</Callout>

## Analyzing the Relationship with Children

### Children — Siksang (食傷) [men also include Gwanseong]

**Strong Siksang**: Rich, creative relationship with children. Ability to raise children well.

**Weak or absent Siksang**: Emotional distance from children; may feel difficulty in parenting.

**Siksang under clash**: Possible difficulties with children's health or the relationship.

### Siblings and Colleagues — Bijep (比劫)

**Much Bijep**: Many siblings, or an environment full of competitors. Strong independence; strives to protect one's own territory.

**No Bijep**: May be an only child or emotionally distant from siblings. Independent but potentially lonely.

## Assessing Marriage Timing

Marriage tends to come when the following conditions overlap:

**For men**:
- Jaeseong Major Luck Cycle or Annual Luck
- Gwanseong or Inseong cycle (stable environment)
- A year when Jaeseong Branch forms a combination (合)

**For women**:
- Gwanseong Major Luck Cycle or Annual Luck
- A year when Gwanseong Branch forms a combination
- A period when Bijep is suppressed and Gwanseong activates

<Callout type="note">
**Important**: Saju indicates the *direction* of marriage — it does not mean things will *necessarily* happen that way. Personal will and effort can change fate.
</Callout>

## Basics of Compatibility (宮合)

Saju compatibility compares two people's charts comprehensively.

**Basic compatibility checklist**:

1. **Five Element complementarity**: Best when each person's Lucky Element is the other's Favorable Element
2. **Day Master combination (合)**: Combinations like Gabgi, Eulgyeong (where Day Masters form a pair) are favorable
3. **Clash (沖) between Day Branches**: Frequent relational conflict if Day Branches clash
4. **Five Element harmony**: When one person's deficient element is provided by the other

<Callout type="tip">
**The truth about compatibility**: Even when Five Elements or charts don't seem to match well, mutual understanding and effort can build a wonderful relationship. Compatibility charts are reference tools, not absolute standards.
</Callout>
