## 2. How to read the result

MBTI is not a verdict. It is a **directional snapshot**.  
Remember these four axes for a practical interpretation:

1. **E / I**: How you recharge energy
2. **S / N**: How you process information
3. **T / F**: How you decide
4. **J / P**: How you manage plans and deadlines
