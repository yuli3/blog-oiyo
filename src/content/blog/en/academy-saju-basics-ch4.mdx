## What Are the Ten Gods?

The Ten Gods classify **the relationship between the Day Master (the self) and the other seven characters** into 10 categories. In Saju analysis, the Ten Gods are the core tool for understanding **personality, talents, human relationships, and life themes**.

Knowing the Ten Gods lets you interpret the numbers and characters of a chart as a meaningful story.

---

## How the Ten Gods Are Calculated

They are determined by the **Five Element generation/control relationship + whether Yin and Yang match or differ** between the Day Master and the other stems/branches.

| Relationship | Same Yin/Yang | Different Yin/Yang |
|---|---|---|
| **Same element as the self** (比和) | Bijeon (比肩) | Geomjae (劫財) |
| **Element the self generates** (泄氣) | Sikshin (食神) | Sanggwan (傷官) |
| **Element the self controls** (財星) | Pyeonjae (偏財) | Jeongjae (正財) |
| **Element that controls the self** (官星) | Pyeongwan (偏官) | Jeonggwan (正官) |
| **Element that generates the self** (印星) | Pyeonin (偏印) | Jeongin (正印) |

<Callout type="note">
**Example**: For a Gab-Wood (甲木) Day Master —
- Gab-Wood (甲木) = Bijeon / Eul-Wood (乙木) = Geomjae
- Byeong-Fire (丙火) = Sikshin / Jeong-Fire (丁火) = Sanggwan
- Mu-Earth (戊土) = Pyeonjae / Gi-Earth (己土) = Jeongjae
- Gyeong-Metal (庚金) = Pyeongwan / Sin-Metal (辛金) = Jeonggwan
- Im-Water (壬水) = Pyeonin / Gye-Water (癸水) = Jeongin
</Callout>

## 1. Bijep (比劫) — Same Energy as the Self

### Bijeon (比肩): A Peer Shoulder to Shoulder

Bijeon is an element **with the same Five Element and same Yin/Yang as the Day Master**.

**Psychological meaning**:
- Independence, self-direction, competitiveness
- Protecting one's own territory and self-respect
- Prefers independence over cooperation

**Bijeon in life**:
- As actual people: competitors or colleagues similar to oneself
- Too much Bijeon: independent but stubborn; may find it hard to cooperate with others

### Geomjae (劫財): The Sibling Who Takes

Geomjae is an element **with the same Five Element but different Yin/Yang as the Day Master**.

**Psychological meaning**:
- Strong drive and competitiveness
- Willingness to use any means to achieve results
- Speculative tendencies, strong winning instinct

**Geomjae in life**:
- Too much Geomjae: repeated experiences of losing wealth, or conversely, the temperament of a powerful entrepreneur

---

## 2. Siksang (食傷) — Energy Generated by the Self

### Sikshin (食神): Blessings Enjoyed Through Eating and Drinking

Sikshin is the element **generated by the Day Master with the same Yin/Yang**.

**Psychological meaning**:
- Creativity, desire for expression, interest in food and pleasures
- Easy-going and optimistic temperament
- Interest in art, cooking, education
- Joyfully expressing one's talents

**Strengths**: Creativity, expressiveness, patience, blessings
**Life theme**: Expressing and enjoying your talents
**Representative careers**: Chef, artist, educator, social worker

### Sanggwan (傷官): The Free Spirit Who Breaks Rules

Sanggwan is the element **generated by the Day Master with different Yin/Yang**.

**Psychological meaning**:
- Rebellious, creative, boundary-crossing temperament
- Challenges rules and authority
- Exceptional expressiveness and artistry
- Innovator who breaks existing frameworks

**Strengths**: Originality, exceptional expressiveness, innovation, intellectual challenge
**Watch out for**: Rebellious temperament, relational conflict, ignoring rules
**Life theme**: Breaking existing frameworks and creating the new

<Callout type="tip">
**Sikshin vs Sanggwan**: Where Sikshin expresses creativity within the flow, Sanggwan crosses boundaries to create innovation. In modern society, Sanggwan's originality is often a major strength.
</Callout>

## 3. Jaeseong (財星) — Energy Controlled by the Self

### Pyeonjae (偏財): Flowing Money

Pyeonjae is the element **controlled by the Day Master with the same Yin/Yang**.

**Psychological meaning**:
- Desire for big money, investment, and business
- Free flow of money
- Generosity in interpersonal relationships
- Symbolizes father, and women (for men)

**Strengths**: Business acumen, interpersonal skills, generosity
**Life theme**: Large-scale wealth; dealing with many people
**Representative careers**: Entrepreneur, investor, financial professional

### Jeongjae (正財): Stable Income

Jeongjae is the element **controlled by the Day Master with different Yin/Yang**.

**Psychological meaning**:
- Stable and consistent income
- Realistic and steady wealth management
- Accumulates wealth through principled, honest methods
- Symbolizes spouse (wife for men)

**Strengths**: Diligence, steadiness, wealth management ability
**Life theme**: Steadily building stable assets
**Representative careers**: Accountant, financial professional, civil servant, stable employee

---

## 4. Gwanseong (官星) — Energy That Controls the Self

### Pyeongwan (偏官): Chilsal (七殺), Intense Stimulation

Pyeongwan is the element **that controls the Day Master with the same Yin/Yang**. Also called Chilsal (Seven Killings, 七殺).

**Psychological meaning**:
- Intense pressure, challenge, competition
- Strict self-control and discipline
- Intense charisma
- An obstacle that must be overcome

**Strengths**: Toughness, charisma, crisis response, commanding ability
**Watch out for**: Excessive pressure, impulsiveness, explosive temperament
**Life theme**: Growth through trials; leadership that leads through strength

### Jeonggwan (正官): Proper Discipline and Honor

Jeonggwan is the element **that controls the Day Master with different Yin/Yang**.

**Psychological meaning**:
- Emphasizes social norms and order
- Honor, face, social recognition
- Attains status through proper means
- Symbolizes husband (for women)

**Strengths**: Morality, honor, social recognition, leadership
**Life theme**: Stability through social standing and honor
**Representative careers**: Civil servant, legal professional, politician, manager

<Callout type="note">
**Too much Gwanseong**: Tendency to be bound by rules and obligations. Too little: may develop a tendency to disregard norms. The right amount of Gwanseong supports social success.
</Callout>

---

## 5. Inseong (印星) — Energy That Generates the Self

### Pyeonin (偏印): Original Thinking

Pyeonin is the element **that generates the Day Master with the same Yin/Yang**.

**Psychological meaning**:
- Original and unusual interests
- Drawn to religion, philosophy, and the mysterious
- Outstanding intuition and inspiration
- Tends to start well but struggle to finish

**Strengths**: Originality, intuition, versatility, philosophical thinking
**Watch out for**: Lack of persistence, fickleness, detachment from reality
**Life theme**: Living through original knowledge and inspiration

### Jeongin (正印): A Mother's Love

Jeongin is the element **that generates the Day Master with different Yin/Yang**. Symbolizes mother and orthodox learning.

**Psychological meaning**:
- Interest in academic study, knowledge, and formal education
- Conservative and stable temperament
- Desire for learning and growth
- The warmth of a mother's care

**Strengths**: Academic ability, stability, deep thinking, reliability
**Life theme**: Growth through knowledge and study
**Representative careers**: Professor, researcher, doctor, professional

## Effects of Excess or Deficiency in Each God

| Ten God | When in Excess | When Deficient |
|---|---|---|
| Bijep | Stubborn; clashes with others | Lack of confidence; dependent |
| Siksang | Talks too much; over-expressive | Poor expressiveness; feels stifled |
| Jaeseong | Obsessed with money or scattered | Difficulty managing wealth |
| Gwanseong | Stress, suppression | Indulgence, disregard for rules |
| Inseong | Laziness, over-protection | Refusing to learn; impulsive behavior |

## Career Aptitude by Ten Gods

| Career | Key Ten Gods Needed |
|---|---|
| Entrepreneur / Executive | Strong Pyeonjae, Bijep |
| Artist / Creator | Strong Sikshin / Sanggwan |
| Civil servant / Professional | Strong Jeonggwan / Jeongin |
| Researcher / Scholar | Strong Inseong |
| Leader / Military / Police | Strong Pyeongwan |
| Finance professional | Balanced Jeongjae / Pyeonjae |

<Callout type="tip">
**Practical tip**: Choosing a career that leverages the strong Ten Gods in your chart lets you naturally express your ability. Use a partner or team to compensate for the areas where your Ten Gods are weak.
</Callout>
