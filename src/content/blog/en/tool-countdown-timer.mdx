# Countdown Timer

Watch the days, hours, minutes, and seconds to your target date tick down in real time. Exams, certification tests, birthdays, travel departures, product launches — count down to any moment that matters.

There is a psychological difference between simply knowing a date and watching the seconds physically shrink toward it. Research shows that a visible countdown increases goal commitment and focus. Students especially benefit from setting a countdown screen as their background, keeping the pressure tangible every day.

Set a date and time to start the countdown immediately.

## Use Cases

|---|---|
| Exam preparation | University entrance exams, professional certifications |

| Events | Product launches, concerts, sports matches |

<Callout type="tip">
**The psychology of countdowns**: Seeing a concrete number triggers a sense of urgency, letting you reverse Parkinson's Law. When the deadline is visible, your brain activates and focus increases.
</Callout>

## Timer vs. Countdown: What's the Difference?

| Tool | Direction | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Timer | From a set duration → 0 | Focus sessions, cooking, workouts |
| Countdown | Now → a future target date | Event anticipation, D-day |
| Stopwatch | 0 → elapsed time | Recording performance |
