## What is Bagua (八卦)?

Bagua means 8 trigrams, and in Feng Shui, this is overlaid on the floor plan of a house to analyze which area is responsible for which part of life.

## 8 Life Areas

1. **Wealth:** Southeast. Abundance and prosperity.
2. **Fame:** South. Social recognition and success.
3. **Relationships:** Southwest. Love and partnership.
4. **Children:** West. Creativity and joy.
5. **Helpful People:** Northwest. Mentors and travel.
6. **Career:** North. Life path and purpose.
7. **Knowledge:** Northeast. Learning and spirituality.
8. **Family:** East. Health and roots.

## Activation Methods

- **Wealth Area:** Plants, fountains, purple/gold decorations
- **Relationship Area:** Paired items, pink/red
- **Career Area:** Water element, black/blue

## Caution

What if the bathroom or storage is in an important area? Keep that space clean and keep the door closed. There is no perfect house. Intention is most important.
