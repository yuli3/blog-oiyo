### Introduction: Something More Than Suppressing Symptoms

For a long time, when we said psychiatric treatment, we only thought of taking medications such as antidepressants or anti-anxiety drugs, or solving psychological problems through counseling. Although these methods are important, they had limits in solving the fundamental cause of 'metabolic disorder' we looked at earlier.

Dr. Christopher Palmer emphasizes through **Brain Energy** that we now have to move to a new paradigm called 'Metabolic Treatment.' It is a method of fundamentally reversing the disease by normalizing the energy of brain cells beyond simply managing symptoms.

---

### 1. What You Eat is Your Mood: Power of Diet

One of the most powerful tools of metabolic treatment is 'food.' Especially the 'Ketogenic Diet,' which reduces carbohydrate intake and increases healthy fats, has been used to treat epilepsy since 100 years ago.

Dr. Palmer clinically proved that this diet shows amazing effects also for patients with schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and refractory depression. Ketone bodies provide clean and efficient fuel to brain cells, increase the number of mitochondria, and play a role in reducing inflammation. What we eat becomes a process of directly repairing the chemical factory of the brain.

### 2. Sleep and Exercise: Recharging and Strengthening Mitochondria

Sleep is not just resting time. If the state of insufficient brain energy continues, our brain cannot perform the work of cleaning up broken mitochondria and restoring neurocircuits. Quality sleep is itself the best metabolic treatment.

Exercise is the same. When muscles contract and relax, signals promoting the production of mitochondria are generated in our body. Regular exercise increases the number of powerhouses in brain cells and develops the power to withstand stress.

### 3. Integrative Approach: Multi-faceted Metabolic Management

Metabolic treatment is not a process of finding single secret. It is an 'integrative approach' that includes diet, exercise, and sleep, as well as stress management and management of metabolic side effects of medications.

If necessary, medications should be used concurrently, but the goal should not be to depend on medications for life, but to restore metabolic health so that the brain can regulate energy by itself without medications.

---

### Conclusion: Sovereignty of Healing in Your Hands

The greatest advantage of metabolic treatment is that the patient changes from a passive object of treatment to a 'subject of healing.' Beyond just swallowing the medicine prescribed by the doctor, you learn how to manage your own brain energy by yourself through daily life.

**Mental illness is not a curse you have to carry for life. It is an urgent signal sent by your body and brain, and when we restore metabolic health in response to that signal, we can gain true freedom.**
