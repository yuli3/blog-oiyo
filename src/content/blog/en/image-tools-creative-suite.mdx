## Grayscale Conversion Methods Compared

| Method | Formula | Characteristics |
|--------|---------|----------------|
| Average | (R+G+B)/3 | Simple, fast |
| Luminance-based | 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B | Optimized for human vision |
| Desaturation | (max+min)/2 | Midpoint method |

**Recommendation**: For converting photos to black and white, the **luminance-based** method produces the most natural results. The human eye is most sensitive to green and least sensitive to blue — this formula reflects that perceptual reality.

## Tips for Creating Pixel Art

1. **Use a large pixel size**: The bigger the pixel block, the more retro the result
2. **Choose the right source image**: Simple, high-contrast images convert to pixel art more effectively than complex ones
3. **Limit the color palette**: Fewer colors create a more authentic pixel art aesthetic

## EXIF Metadata and Privacy

Before sharing photos on social media or any public platform, we recommend checking the metadata.

Smartphone photos frequently contain **GPS location data**. If you'd rather not expose your home or workplace coordinates, check and remove the EXIF data before uploading.
