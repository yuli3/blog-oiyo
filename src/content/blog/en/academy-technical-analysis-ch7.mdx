## Overview: Reading the Market Through Time and Balance

Ichimoku Kinko Hyo (一目均衡表) was created by Goichi Hosoda (pen name Ichimoku Sanjin) in 1930s Japan. The name literally means **"a one-glance (一目) view of the market's balance."** Where other indicators focus on price, Ichimoku adds the axis of **time** to show trend in three dimensions.

<Callout type="info">
**Goal of this lesson**: Accurately understand the calculation and meaning of Ichimoku's five lines and the Cloud (spans). Practical trading comes in Lesson 8.
</Callout>

---

## 1. The Philosophy of Ichimoku

Ichimoku is a vast system of three theories (wave, time, and price-level), but what appears on the chart is essentially **five lines and the cloud**. Two premises underlie it.

- **The market seeks balance** — price may lean one way temporarily, but returns to balance (the center).
- **Time creates change** — not just price, but the variable of "how many days ahead" creates turning points.

<Callout type="tip">
The default settings **9, 26, 52** come from Hosoda's analysis of historical Japanese markets (when a month ≈ 26 trading days under a six-day week). They remain the standard today, though some optimize them per market or instrument.
</Callout>

---

## 2. The Five Lines

| Line | Calculation | Character |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| **Tenkan-sen (Conversion)** | (9-period high + low) ÷ 2 | Short-term, 9-day center |
| **Kijun-sen (Base)** | (26-period high + low) ÷ 2 | Mid-term, 26-day center |
| **Senkou Span A** | (Tenkan + Kijun) ÷ 2, plotted **26 ahead** | Cloud edge |
| **Senkou Span B** | (52-period high + low) ÷ 2, plotted **26 ahead** | Cloud edge |
| **Chikou Span (Lagging)** | Today's close plotted **26 back** | Current vs. past position |

### Tenkan and Kijun
They look like moving averages but differ. An MA is the "average of closes"; Tenkan and Kijun are the **midpoint of the period's high and low** — the "balance price" of that span.

- Tenkan above Kijun → short-term bullish bias
- Price above a rising Kijun → mid-term trend is healthy

<Callout type="note">
**The Kijun is the spine of the trend.** A flat Kijun means no trend (ranging); a rising Kijun acts as the centerline of an uptrend. It often serves as strong support when price corrects.
</Callout>

---

## 3. The Cloud (Kumo) — The Heart of Ichimoku

The space **between Senkou Span A and B**, filled with color, is the Cloud (Kumo). Because both lines are plotted **26 periods into the future**, the cloud is drawn ahead of price at the right edge of the chart.

```
       ░░░░░░░  ← Cloud (spans plotted 26 ahead)
price /░░░░░░
    /
─────────────────→ time
```

### What the Cloud Tells You

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Price **above** the cloud | Uptrend — cloud is support |
| Price **below** the cloud | Downtrend — cloud is resistance |
| Price **inside** the cloud | Unclear trend — stand aside |
| **Thick** cloud | Strong support/resistance (hard to break) |
| **Thin** cloud | Weak support/resistance (easy to break) |

### Bullish and Bearish Clouds
- **Bullish cloud (Span A > Span B)**: bias upward
- **Bearish cloud (Span A < Span B)**: bias downward
- Where the two spans cross is the **cloud twist**, a clue to a trend change.

<Callout type="warning">
Ichimoku's greatest weapon is that the cloud **shows 26 periods of the future in advance** — you can see upcoming support/resistance ahead of time. But when price enters the cloud, direction is lost, so **avoid trading inside the cloud.**
</Callout>

---

## 4. Chikou Span — The Simplest Yet Powerful Line

The Chikou Span simply plots **today's close 26 periods back.** The simplest to compute, yet Hosoda valued it highly.

- Chikou **above** the price of 26 periods ago → bullish confirmation
- Chikou **below** the price of 26 periods ago → bearish confirmation

It compares current strength directly with the past — a final check of whether a trend is real.

---

## 5. The Five Lines Across Time, at a Glance

| Line | Time position |
|------|---------------|
| Chikou Span | 26 periods **past** |
| Tenkan, Kijun | **Present** |
| Senkou Span A/B (Cloud) | 26 periods **future** |

Past, present, and future on one screen — this is Ichimoku's identity, and the source of its name "one glance at balance."

---

## Key Takeaways

1. Ichimoku adds the axis of "time" to price (settings 9·26·52).
2. Tenkan (9) and Kijun (26) are midpoints of period highs/lows (balance prices); the Kijun is the trend's center.
3. The **Cloud**, formed by Spans A and B, is plotted 26 ahead and shows upcoming support/resistance.
4. Price above the cloud = up, below = down, inside = stand aside; a thicker cloud is stronger.
5. The Chikou Span plots the close 26 back to confirm current strength against the past.
