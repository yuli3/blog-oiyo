## The Invisible String of Hearts

Sometimes, even when we are far apart, our hearts are joined by an **invisible string**.

### Magic Pulling

Imagine you and your best friend are holding a long, magic string that no one else can see. Even if your friend is on the other side of the world, if they tug the string, you feel it instantly!

#### You are Not Alone

You are like a star in a big, beautiful sky made of light.

- When you are happy, the air around you gets happy too.
- when you smile, you send a "heart-tug" to everyone you know.

### The Mirror Magic

This "heart-string" magic is why we feel what others feel. Your heart sings the same song as the people you love.

> "To know who you are, look at all the friends you've made and all the tugs you've felt."

### Harmony

The world is like a big game where everyone is holding hands. No matter how big the world seems, you are always connected to the magic field of love.
