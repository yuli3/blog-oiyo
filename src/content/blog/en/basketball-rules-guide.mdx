## Basketball comes down to the clock and the score

Basketball moves fast, but the rules underneath it are simple. Once you understand **how scoring works, the time limits, and fouls**, the whole game becomes easy to follow.

---

## The basics — players and time

```
Players: 5 per team on the court. Substitutions are unlimited.
Time:    FIBA: four 10-minute quarters / NBA: four 12-minute
         quarters. A tie goes to a 5-minute overtime period.
Winner:  whichever team has more points when time expires
         (there are no ties in basketball).
```

---

## Scoring — 2-pointers, 3-pointers, and free throws

<a id="scoring"></a>

```
2 points: a field goal made from inside the 3-point line
3 points: a field goal made from beyond the 3-point line
1 point:  a free throw — awarded after being fouled during
          a shooting motion, among other situations
```

---

## Time-limit rules (violations)

The real charm of basketball is in its tight, layered time limits.

| Rule | What it means |
|------|------|
| **24-second clock** | The offense must attempt a shot that hits the rim within 24 seconds, or they lose possession |
| **8-second rule** | The team must advance the ball past halfcourt within 8 seconds of gaining possession |
| **5-second rule** | A closely guarded player can't hold the ball for 5 seconds or more without passing, dribbling, or shooting |
| **3-second rule** | An offensive player can't stand in the opponent's painted area (the key) for more than 3 seconds |

## Common fouls and violations

```
Violations (minor infractions → possession simply switches):
- Traveling (taking 3 or more steps while holding the ball)
- Double dribble (stopping your dribble, then starting again)
- Backcourt violation, and others

Fouls (illegal physical contact):
- Accumulating personal fouls (5 in FIBA, 6 in the NBA)
  results in disqualification for that player
- A foul during a shooting motion → free throws
- Accumulating team fouls triggers bonus free throws for
  the opponent
- Technical or flagrant fouls → free throws plus possession
```

## Positions

```
1 Point Guard (PG):   directs the offense, primary passer
2 Shooting Guard (SG): perimeter scoring
3 Small Forward (SF):  versatile on both ends
4 Power Forward (PF):  interior play, rebounding
5 Center (C):          rim protection, interior scoring
```

---

## Terms worth knowing

```
Rebound: grabbing the ball after a missed shot
Assist:  a pass that directly leads to a score
Steal:   taking the ball away from the opponent
Block:   deflecting or stopping a shot attempt
Fast break: a quick transition from defense to offense
```

---

## Key takeaways

1. 5 players per side, 4 quarters — whoever scores more when time runs out wins (no ties).
2. Points come from 2-pointers, 3-pointers, and free throws (worth 1 point each).
3. The 24-, 8-, 5-, and 3-second rules are what give the game its pace.
4. Traveling and double dribble simply turn over possession; physical contact is a foul.
5. Racking up too many personal fouls (5 or 6, depending on the league) disqualifies a player.

> FIBA and NBA rules differ in some details, including quarter length and foul limits.
