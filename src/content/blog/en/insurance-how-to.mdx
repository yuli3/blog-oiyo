Korean insurance has three main categories that most people should understand — health (실손/silson), life insurance, and auto insurance. Policies are complex and often sold aggressively through agents, so knowing what to check before signing matters. This guide covers the essentials.

---

## 1. Universal Principles Before Enrolling

### Use Official Comparison Tools First
- **Insurance Damoa**: [e-insmarket.or.kr](https://www.e-insmarket.or.kr) — Operated by Korea's Financial Supervisory Service. Compare life and non-life insurance premiums for free
- **Korea Life Insurance Association**: [klia.or.kr](https://www.klia.or.kr)
- **Korea Non-Life Insurance Association**: [knia.or.kr](https://www.knia.or.kr)

### Three Questions to Ask Before Signing Any Policy
1. **Coverage scope**: What exactly is covered and how much can I receive?
2. **Exclusions**: What situations are explicitly NOT covered?
3. **Renewable vs. non-renewable**: Will my premium increase as I age?

---

## 2. Silson Medical Insurance (실손의료보험) — Get This First

### What Is Silson?
Silson (실손, literally "actual loss") reimburses your real out-of-pocket medical expenses — especially non-covered treatments that Korea's national health insurance (NHIS) doesn't pay for.

### Policy Generations (as of 2024)
| Generation | Characteristics | Self-Pay Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| 1st Gen (before 2009) | Broadest coverage, lowest premiums. If you have this, don't cancel it. | 0–10% |
| 2nd Gen (2009–2017) | Mid-level coverage | 10–20% |
| 3rd Gen (2017–2021) | Higher out-of-pocket | 20–30% |
| 4th Gen (2021–present) | Covered and non-covered treatments billed separately, high self-pay | Covered: 20%, Non-covered: 30% |

> New enrollees can only buy 4th generation. If you have an older policy, think carefully before canceling.

### Key Items to Check
- **All 4th gen policies are renewable** — premiums increase with age
- **Waiting period**: Most policies exclude specific conditions for the first 90 days
- **Pre-existing condition disclosure**: You are legally required to disclose existing conditions — failure to disclose can result in claim denial

### Enrollment Steps
1. Compare premiums at Insurance Damoa (e-insmarket.or.kr)
2. Enroll online directly through the insurer's website or app (often cheaper than going through an agent)
3. Fill in health disclosure accurately — include all existing conditions and medications
4. Set up payment method (monthly or annual)

## 3. Life Insurance — Term, Whole Life & CI

### Main Product Types
| Type | What It Covers | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| Term Life (정기보험) | Death coverage for a set period. Low premiums. | Working-age adults with dependents |
| Whole Life (종신보험) | Lifetime death coverage. High premiums. | Estate and inheritance planning |
| CI (Critical Illness) | Lump sum on diagnosis of major illness (cancer, stroke, heart attack) | Those with family history of major illness |
| Cancer Insurance (암보험) | Specialized coverage for cancer diagnosis and treatment | Additional cancer protection |

### Critical Checks for Life Insurance
- **Designate a beneficiary**: Death benefits require a named beneficiary — otherwise they're paid to legal heirs via probate
- **Payment vs. coverage period**: Understand the structure (e.g., pay for 20 years, covered for life)
- **Surrender value**: Canceling early returns only a fraction of what you paid — especially painful in the first few years
- **Renewable CI/cancer policies**: Premiums spike steeply with age → always compare with non-renewable alternatives

### Enrollment Steps
1. Compare using Insurance Damoa or insurer comparison sites
2. For complex products, a licensed insurance agent consultation is recommended
3. Complete the health disclosure form (청약서) accurately
4. Use the **free-look period**: Within 15 days of enrollment, you can cancel for a full premium refund

## 4. Auto Insurance — Mandatory + Comprehensive

### Auto Insurance Structure
| Coverage | What It Covers | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| 대인배상 Ⅰ (Bodily Injury I) | Minimum mandatory bodily injury liability | **Required** |
| 대인배상 Ⅱ (Bodily Injury II) | Additional bodily injury above Type I limits | Strongly recommended |
| 대물배상 (Property Damage) | Damage to other vehicles/property | Required (min. ₩20M) |
| 자기신체사고 (Personal Injury) | Covers your own injuries | Optional |
| 자기차량손해 (Own Vehicle Damage) | Covers repairs to your own car | Optional |
| 무보험차상해 (Uninsured Driver) | Covers you if hit by an uninsured driver | Recommended |

### Ways to Reduce Your Premium
- **Buy online directly**: 10–15% cheaper than going through an agent
- **Mileage discount**: Low annual mileage qualifies for significant discounts
- **Dash cam discount**: Having a dashcam (블랙박스) registered with your insurer earns a discount
- **Safe driving app**: Link an insurer's app — safe driving scores unlock premium reductions

### Enrollment Steps
1. Go to Insurance Damoa → auto insurance comparison (results in 5 minutes)
2. Select insurer → enroll directly through their app or website
3. Enter vehicle plate number and driver information
4. Receive your insurance certificate via email or app
5. **New car**: Enroll the day before your scheduled pick-up date

---

## After Enrolling — Things You Must Do

- **Store your insurance certificate**: Keep in a place your family can find, or save in an app
- **Know how to file a claim**: Save your insurer's emergency claims number before you ever need it
- **Annual review**: Revisit your coverage and premiums every year at renewal time
