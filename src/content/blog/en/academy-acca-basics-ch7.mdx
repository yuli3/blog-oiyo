## SBR: The Pinnacle of the ACCA Qualification

SBR is one of two mandatory Strategic Professional papers and tests the application of advanced IFRS in complex, real-world scenarios. Long-form case-style questions replace the short exercises seen in Applied Skills.

## IFRS 3: Business Combinations

**Acquisition Method** — the only permitted method under IFRS 3:

1. Identify the **acquirer**
2. Determine the **acquisition date**
3. Measure **consideration transferred**
4. Measure **identifiable assets acquired and liabilities assumed** at fair value
5. Recognise **goodwill** (or bargain purchase gain)

**Goodwill**:
Goodwill = Consideration + NCI − Fair value of net identifiable assets

**Step acquisitions**:
Pre-existing interest is remeasured to fair value at acquisition date → difference goes to P&L.

## Complex Group Structures

### Vertical Groups
A → B → C (A controls B, B controls C)

**A's effective interest in C** = A's stake in B × B's stake in C

### Mixed Groups
A directly owns both a majority of B AND a minority of C; B also owns shares in C.

## Foreign Currency Translation (IAS 21)

### At Entity Level
- Foreign currency transactions: Recorded at spot rate on transaction date
- Monetary items: Retranslated at closing rate → exchange differences to P&L
- Non-monetary items (at historical cost): Stay at original rate

### Translating Foreign Operations
- Assets and liabilities: **Closing rate**
- Income and expenses: **Average rate**
- **Translation differences → Other Comprehensive Income (OCI)**

## Fair Value Measurement (IFRS 13)

**Fair value** = price received to sell an asset (or paid to transfer a liability) in an orderly transaction between market participants at measurement date

### Three-Level Hierarchy

| Level | Input Type | Reliability |
|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Level 1 | Quoted prices in active markets (identical asset) | Highest |
| Level 2 | Observable market data (similar assets, adjusted) | Medium |
| Level 3 | Unobservable inputs (entity's own assumptions) | Lowest |

## Professional Skills in SBR

SBR rewards not just technical accuracy but **professional judgment**:

**ACCA Professional Skills**:
- Analysis and Evaluation
- Commercial Acumen
- Communication (clear, structured writing)
- Professional Scepticism and Judgement

Marks are awarded for structure, clarity, and appropriate use of judgment — not just knowing the rule.
