## Legal Marriage Age in Korea

| Category | Standard |
|---|---|
| Minimum legal marriage age | **18 years old** (amended 2023, same for both sexes) |
| Previous standard | Men 18, Women 16 (old law) |
| Underage marriage | Completely prohibited as of July 2023 |

## Trend: Average First Marriage Age in Korea (Statistics Korea)

| Year | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| 2000 | 29.3 | 26.5 |
| 2010 | 31.8 | 28.9 |
| 2020 | 33.2 | 30.8 |
| 2023 | **34.0** | **31.5** |

First marriage ages continue to rise each year.

<Callout type="tip">
**Wedding preparation timeline**: Venue bookings typically need 12–18 months' advance notice, and housing contracts 6–12 months ahead. In-season (spring/fall) wedding halls can fill up 2 years in advance.
</Callout>
