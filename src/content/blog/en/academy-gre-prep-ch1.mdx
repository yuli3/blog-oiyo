## What Is the GRE?

The **GRE (Graduate Record Examinations)**, administered by ETS, is the most widely accepted standardized test for graduate school admissions worldwide. It is used for Master's and PhD programs across virtually all disciplines — science, engineering, humanities, social sciences, and increasingly for MBA programs as well.

---

## GRE Structure (Post-2024 Revision)

| Section | Questions | Time | Score |
|---------|-----------|------|-------|
| Verbal Reasoning | 27 (2 sections) | 41 min | 130–170 |
| Quantitative Reasoning | 27 (2 sections) | 47 min | 130–170 |
| Analytical Writing | 1 task | 30 min | 0–6 |
| **Total** | | **~1 hr 58 min** | |

The 2024 revision cut the exam from ~3 hours 45 minutes to under 2 hours.

---

## Section Characteristics

### Verbal Reasoning
- **Reading Comprehension**: Passage understanding and inference
- **Text Completion**: 1–3 blank fill-ins (heavy vocabulary focus)
- **Sentence Equivalence**: Select two words that complete a sentence with equivalent meaning

**Key insight**: GRE Verbal requires significantly stronger vocabulary than GMAT — this is the defining characteristic.

### Quantitative Reasoning
- Quantitative Comparison (compare two quantities)
- Problem Solving
- Data Interpretation (graphs and tables)

**Key insight**: High school-level math + logical application. Built-in calculator available (unlike GMAT Quant).

### Analytical Writing
- One essay task (Issue or Argument)
- Tests ability to construct and evaluate arguments in written English

---

## GRE Scoring

**Combined score**: Verbal + Quantitative, each 130–170

| Score | Percentile |
|-------|-----------|
| 170 | 99% |
| 163–169 | 90%+ |
| 155–162 | 75%+ |
| 148–154 | 50–74% |

ETS and GMAC provide official GRE-to-GMAT score comparison tools on their websites.

---

## When to Choose GRE

| Situation | Recommended |
|-----------|-------------|
| STEM Master's / PhD | GRE |
| Humanities / Social Science grad school | GRE |
| MBA programs that accept GRE | GRE or GMAT |
| Top MBA with heavy quant focus | GMAT preferred |
| Vocabulary/reading strength | GRE |
| Math/logical reasoning strength | GMAT |
