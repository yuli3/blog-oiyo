## Stars Don't Go Backwards

Planets don't actually go backwards; it's an optical illusion caused by speed difference with Earth. In astrology, communication, travel, electronics, and contracts ruled by Mercury are seen to have setbacks during this period.

## Re-view, Re-do, Re-connect

Retrograde is the time to **to review, redo and reconnect**.

- **Re-view:** Double-check contracts or documents carefully.
- **Re-connect:** Good for reconnecting with forgotten friends or old ties.
- **Re-do:** Stop the brain overload and rest for a while.

## Caution

It is better to postpone important contracts or new beginnings until after the retrograde. However, it is the best time to correct and supplement things already started.
