## Key Astro Events — July 2026

July is the first complete month after Jupiter fully settles into Cancer. **Home, emotion, and inner life** energy flows abundantly through every sign, and with summer vacation in full swing, this is the month of family, rest, and recharging.

| Event | Date | Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Jupiter stable in Cancer | All month | Home, emotion & inner growth maximized |
| Sun Cancer → Leo | July 22 | Energy shifts from inward to outward |
| Full Moon in Capricorn | Early July | Balance goals vs. feelings |
| New Moon in Leo | Late July | Plant seeds of self-expression & creativity |

<Callout type="tip">
**July Theme**: Invest in home and family. With Jupiter in Cancer, everything related to your living space carries extra luck this month.
</Callout>

---

## Aries ♈ (Mar 21–Apr 19)

### Overall
**4th House (home, roots, emotional security)** energy peaks. A month to discover that time with family is true happiness. Home renovations, moving, and family trips all yield great results.

### Love
Your domestic side looks attractive to a potential partner. Introducing a lover to your family or attending family events together deepens the relationship.

### Money
Real-estate-related investment or improving your living situation increases long-term value. Prepare for unexpected family-related expenses toward month's end.

### Career
Improving your home office boosts work efficiency. Focus on building internal capabilities — this is preparation for the leap forward after August.

---

## Taurus ♉ (Apr 20–May 20)

### Overall
**3rd House (communication, learning)** energy. Acquiring new skills and taking short trips bring fresh stimulus. A great month to start summer reading, online courses, or a new certification.

### Love
Romance potential while traveling. Sharing a learning or exploring experience deepens a connection. You're drawn to intellectually stimulating partners.

### Money
Investment in knowledge and skills pays back as income later. Plan your summer travel budget in advance to reduce impulse spending.

### Career
Perfect timing to start a work-related certification or skill upgrade. Progress ideas through collaboration and open communication.

---

## Gemini ♊ (May 21–Jun 20)

### Overall
After Jupiter's move, **2nd House (money, self-worth)** energy kicks into high gear. You're entering a roughly one-year cycle of rising financial luck. Time to reassess your value and expand income streams.

### Love
You start seeking a partner whose values and lifestyle match yours. Right now you weigh values and financial stability over appearances.

### Money
Income-growth opportunities arrive. All pathways — new revenue models, salary negotiations, side hustles — look favorable. Finalize your savings and investment plan.

### Career
Ask for what you're worth. Expect good results from salary negotiations, freelance rate increases, and similar moves.

---

## Cancer ♋ (Jun 21–Jul 22)

### Overall
The **Sun and Jupiter are both in Cancer** — a special window until July 22. The most energetically abundant, confident stretch of your year. Pursue what you want actively.

### Love
Your magnetism overflows — you attract attention everywhere. First meetings leave strong impressions; in existing relationships, a partner's love feels deeper.

### Money
Large financial movements are possible. Real estate, major investment, and business expansion all look favorable. Prioritize data-driven decisions over emotional ones.

### Career
Propose, request, and announce your goals now. Bosses, clients, and partners are all well-disposed toward you. Opportunities go to whoever reaches for them first.

## Leo ♌ (Jul 23–Aug 22)

### Overall
The **Sun enters Leo on July 22**, kicking off birthday season. Energy charges up and your presence shines. The 1st House activates for fresh starts and self-expression.

### Love
Leo's summer is a romantic explosion. Glamorous, memorable dates and heartfelt confessions. Your confidence itself is magnetic.

### Money
Opportunity to generate income from creative ideas. Self-branding and visibility activities convert directly to earnings.

### Career
Your moment to lead. Drive the team, pitch ideas, and move proactively. This month's performance will directly affect your year-end review.

---

## Virgo ♍ (Aug 23–Sep 22)

### Overall
**12th House (retreat, recharge)** energy. The month calls for a full recharge through summer vacation. Take solo time in a quiet place and listen to your inner voice.

### Love
Some time alone actually keeps the relationship healthy. Give your partner space while enjoying your own personal time. An honest conversation about buried feelings heals.

### Money
Check for money leaking from invisible places. Audit unnecessary subscriptions, auto-payments, and overall spending structure.

### Career
A quiet preparation phase. Store energy for the new cycle beginning in August. Focus on completing current work rather than forcing new challenges.

## Libra ♎ (Sep 23–Oct 22)

### Overall
**11th House (networks, dreams, community)** energy. The people you meet this summer will play important roles in the second half of the year. A month to announce your dreams and find companions.

### Love
New connections emerge from your friend group. Special meetings at social events, reunions, or clubs. Old friends can become something more.

### Money
Opportunities from joint investments or group funds. Ideas for earning through social media or communities start taking shape.

### Career
Your network becomes opportunity. Unexpected offers can arrive at summer parties, industry meetups, or alumni networking.

## Scorpio ♏ (Oct 23–Nov 21)

### Overall
**10th House (career, reputation)** energy is strong. Even in summer, meaningful career progress unfolds. Time to receive recognition based on first-half results.

### Love
Career success registers as attractiveness. A month to achieve both work and love — balance is key.

### Money
Income increase opportunities tied to career growth. Right time to request compensation for your performance.

### Career
The month you stand out to bosses and clients. Wrap up important projects and actively communicate your results.

## Sagittarius ♐ (Nov 22–Dec 21)

### Overall
**9th House (exploration, philosophy, abroad)** energy. Summer travel, new philosophical thinking, and higher-education plans enrich your life. All horizon-expanding activities bring luck.

### Love
You're drawn to partners with multicultural backgrounds. Life-changing encounters are possible while traveling. Plan adventurous dates.

### Money
Foreign investment, global funds, and travel-related spending all carry long-term value. A month it's fine to budget generously for travel.

### Career
Everything favors overseas opportunities, remote work, and global projects. The ideal time to start studying a new field.

## Capricorn ♑ (Dec 22–Jan 19)

### Overall
**8th House (depth, transformation, shared resources)** energy. A month to look into life's depths. Psychological growth, financial restructuring, and forming deeper relationships are the keywords.

### Love
You want depth, not surface connections. Deep conversations with a partner elevate the quality of your relationship.

### Money

### Career
Bold change to existing methods is needed. Don't fear change — innovate strategically.

### Overall
**7th House (partnerships, contracts)** energy. One-on-one relationships and contracts are central. A month to formalize partnerships — in business or in life.

### Love

### Money
Income opportunities through partnerships. Good conditions for collaborative contracts and finding investment partners. A bigger picture emerges when you work with others.

### Career

## Pisces ♓ (Feb 19–Mar 20)

### Overall
**6th House (health, routine, service)** energy. Health comes first. Building healthy habits this summer will change your energy level for the rest of the year.

### Love

### Money

### Career
