## Incident Response

### PICERL

1. **Preparation**: policy, tools, drills
2. **Identification**: detect and size the event
3. **Containment**: stop spread
4. **Eradication**: remove the cause
5. **Recovery**: return to service
6. **Lessons learned**: what to change

<Callout type="tip">
**Contain first.** Powering a box off can destroy evidence. Isolate (pull it off the network), then collect.
</Callout>

## Digital Forensics

Collect, analyze, preserve.

**Integrity**: hash at collection (SHA-256). Detect later change.

**Chain of custody**: who held it, when, in what state — a continuous record.

**Order of volatility**:
- **Volatile first**: RAM, live connections, running processes
- **Then non-volatile**: disks, logs

---

## SIEM

Central collection, analysis, and correlation of security events.

**Functions**:
- Logs from firewalls, IDS, OS, databases
- Near-real-time anomaly detection
- Alerts
- Compliance reports

**Examples of products**: Splunk, IBM QRadar, Microsoft Sentinel — names, not a buying list.

## BCP and DRP

**BCP**: keep the business running through a disaster.

**DRP**: restore IT systems and infrastructure.

### Metrics

**RPO**: maximum acceptable data loss.

```
RPO = 4 hours → back up at least every 4 hours
```

**RTO**: maximum acceptable time to restore.

```
RTO = 2 hours → the system must be up within 2 hours
```

### Recovery sites

| Strategy | Cost | RTO |
|------|------|-----|
| Cold site | Low | Days to weeks |
| Warm site | Medium | Hours to days |
| Hot site | High | Minutes to hours |
| Cloud DR | Flexible | Can be minutes |

---

## Key Concept Cards

**Incident response** ★★★★★  
: Prepare → identify → contain → eradicate → recover → lessons. Containment first.

**RPO vs RTO** ★★★★★  
: RPO sets backup frequency. RTO sets the DR strategy.

**Hot / warm / cold** ★★★★☆  
: Hot = fast and expensive. Cold = slow and cheap. Match RPO/RTO.

## Practice Quiz

**Q1. Ransomware is detected. What is the first IR action?**  
> Containment. Isolate infected hosts so the malware cannot move laterally. Forensics matter, but spread comes first.

**Q2. RPO = 1 hour, RTO = 4 hours. What backup and recovery fit?**  
> Backup or replicate at least hourly (RPO). Restore within 4 hours (RTO). Warm site or cloud DR can hit that. A cold site that takes days cannot.
