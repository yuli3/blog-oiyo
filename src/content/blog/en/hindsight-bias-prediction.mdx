## The Historian's Fallacy

Before the 2008 financial crash, almost no one predicted it. After it happened, everyone said, "It was obvious! Look at the housing bubble!"
This is **Hindsight Bias**. Once we know the outcome, we rewrite our memory to believe we saw it coming. We connect the dots backwards and assume the line was straight.

## The Danger

"I knew it" is a dangerous phrase. It stops you from asking, "Why didn't I actually predict it?" It makes you think the world is predictable, when it is actually chaotic.
Keep a journal of your predictions. You will be shocked at how wrong you usually are.
