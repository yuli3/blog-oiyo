# Word Counter

Cover letters, social media posts, news articles — counting characters manually every time is tedious. Paste your text and get instant results.

This counter displays characters with and without spaces simultaneously, plus word count and sentence count. Korean cover letters typically use **character count with spaces** as the standard, while English essays and international submissions count **words** — so both are shown at a glance.

**Instant use cases:**
- Korean job application essays (500, 800, 1,000 char limits per section)
- Instagram caption (2,200 chars), X / Twitter (280 chars)
- Blog post titles (60 chars for major Korean platforms)
- Academic abstract (200–250 words for English submissions)

## Platform Character Limits Reference

| Platform | Limit |
|---|---|
| X (Twitter) | 280 chars (Latin), 140 (CJK) |
| Instagram caption | 2,200 chars |
| Instagram bio | 150 chars |
| Facebook post | 63,206 chars |
| YouTube title | 100 chars |
| YouTube description | 5,000 chars |
| KakaoTalk message | 10,000 chars |
| Naver Blog title | 60 chars |

## Cover Letter Length Guide (Korea)

| Section | General Companies | Public Sector |
|---|---|---|
| Background & upbringing | 500–800 chars | ~500 chars |
| Motivation for applying | 500–700 chars | ~500 chars |
| Relevant skills & experience | 700–1,000 chars | ~700 chars |
| Future goals at the company | 400–600 chars | ~500 chars |

<Callout type="tip">
**Spaces included vs. excluded**: Korean cover letters typically use **characters with spaces**. English writing typically counts **words**. This tool displays both so you can match whichever standard applies.
</Callout>
