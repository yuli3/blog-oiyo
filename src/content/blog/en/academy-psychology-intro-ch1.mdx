## Psychology Is Not Mind Reading

Psychology is not a skill for guessing what's going on in someone's head. It's a scientific discipline that observes and explains the behavior, thoughts, emotions, and relationships of humans and animals. In everyday conversation, people tend to narrow psychology down to personality typing or relationship advice, but the actual field is much broader.

Psychology asks why people make the choices they make, why memory gets distorted, how stress shapes behavior, and how judgment shifts inside a group.

## What "Scientific" Means Here

| Element | Meaning |
|------|------|
| Observation | Systematically watching real behavior and reactions |
| Hypothesis | Forming a tentative, testable explanation |
| Measurement | Recording emotion and behavior as precisely as possible |
| Verification | Checking whether the data support the hypothesis |
| Revision | Updating the explanation as new evidence arrives |

A scientific approach doesn't mean treating people coldly. If anything, it's an attitude that resists snap judgments and bias in favor of more careful understanding.

## Major Branches of Psychology

Psychology includes cognitive, developmental, social, personality, biological, clinical, and industrial-organizational psychology, among others. Some branches focus on the brain and behavior, some on memory and thought, and some on relationships and groups.

At the introductory level, it's more useful to learn "at what level are we explaining this behavior" than to memorize every term.

## Example: Fear of Public Speaking

Fear of public speaking can be viewed from several angles at once. Biological psychology looks at heart rate and tension responses, cognitive psychology looks at thoughts like "one mistake and it's over," and social psychology looks at the pressure of being evaluated by an audience. A single behavior can have several valid levels of explanation.

## Key Takeaways

- Psychology is not a technique for guessing minds — it's a science of behavior and mental life.
- The scientific approach includes observation, hypothesis, measurement, verification, and revision.
- Psychology splits into many branches covering the brain, cognition, development, personality, and social relationships.
- Any single behavior can be explained at multiple levels at once.
