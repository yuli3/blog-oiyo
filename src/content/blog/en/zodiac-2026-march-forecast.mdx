| Full Moon in Virgo | Mar 14 | Health, routine, practical results check |
| Spring Equinox / Sun enters Aries | Mar 20 | New astrological cycle begins |
| New Moon in Aries + Solar Eclipse | Mar 29 | Powerfully amplified fresh start |
