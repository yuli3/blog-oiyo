## Why Dividend Investing?

Dividends provide **cash flow regardless of stock price movements** — the bedrock of income-focused investing.

**Benefits**:
- Regular cash income from portfolio
- Companies that consistently raise dividends typically have strong fundamentals
- Reinvested dividends compound powerfully over decades
- Psychological stability during market downturns

---

## Core Dividend Metrics

### Dividend Yield
**Yield = Annual Dividend Per Share / Share Price × 100**

- 1.5–3%: Growth-oriented payers
- 3–5%: Income-focused; common in mature industries
- >5%: May signal a struggling company cutting dividend ahead

### Payout Ratio
**Payout Ratio = Annual Dividends / Net Income × 100**

- &lt;50%: Sustainable; room to grow
- 60–75%: Monitor carefully
- >85%: Risk of dividend cut (unless stable utility/REIT)

### Dividend Growth Rate
Annual percentage increase in dividend payment. A 7–10% annual growth rate doubles the dividend every 7–10 years.

## Dividend Aristocrats

Companies in the S&P 500 that have **increased their dividend for 25+ consecutive years**.

**Notable Aristocrats**:
| Company | Ticker | Consecutive Years of Increases |
|---------|--------|-------------------------------|
| Coca-Cola | KO | 61+ years |
| Johnson & Johnson | JNJ | 61+ years |
| Procter & Gamble | PG | 67+ years |
| Colgate-Palmolive | CL | 60+ years |

**Dividend Kings**: 50+ consecutive years of increases (even stricter)

## The Math of Dividend Reinvestment (DRIP)

Reinvesting dividends into additional shares creates a compounding engine.

**Starting investment**: $10,000 in a portfolio with 3.5% yield + 7% annual price appreciation + 7% annual dividend growth

| Year | Portfolio Value (DRIP) | Portfolio Value (No DRIP) |
|------|----------------------|-------------------------|
| 10 | ~$21,000 | ~$18,500 |
| 20 | ~$46,000 | ~$34,000 |
| 30 | ~$100,000 | ~$63,000 |

The DRIP difference grows exponentially over time.
