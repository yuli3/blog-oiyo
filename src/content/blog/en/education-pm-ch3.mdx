# Chapter 3. Resource and Budget: Fueling the Mission

A perfect schedule is useless without the money to pay for it and the people to execute it. <mark><strong>Resource and Budget Management</strong></mark> is the process of identifying, acquiring, and managing the resources needed for successful project completion.

## 3. Resource Leveling: The Human Factor

People are not machines. You cannot assign 200% workload to an engineer for three weeks and expect quality. <mark><strong>Resource Leveling</strong></mark> is the technique of adjusting the start and finish dates of tasks to address resource over-allocation.

- **The Trade-off**: Leveling often results in a **longer project duration** as you spread the work out to avoid burning out your team.
- **Resource Smoothing**: Adjusting tasks _only_ within their free time (float) so the critical path doesn't change.

<Callout type="important">
  **The Two Reserves**: Never confuse your reserves. **Contingency** is for things you know *might*
  happen (like a shipment delay). **Management** is for things you didn't even imagine (like a
  global pandemic).
</Callout>
