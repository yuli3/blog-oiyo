# A Black Hole Is Best Understood as an "Inescapable Boundary," Not "Suction"

Thinking of a black hole as a cosmic vacuum that sucks in everything nearby leads to misunderstanding. The real concept is escape velocity. Inside a certain boundary, gravity becomes so strong that not even light can get out. That boundary is called the event horizon.

## Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Event horizon | The boundary beyond which escape becomes impossible |
| Accretion disk | Matter orbiting and heating up around a black hole |
| Singularity | The core region current theory struggles to describe |
| Gravitational waves | Ripples in spacetime produced by the violent motion of massive objects |

<Callout type="info">
Because black holes themselves are invisible, scientists study "how the surroundings move." The skill that matters in science is inferring an unseen object from indirect evidence.
</Callout>
