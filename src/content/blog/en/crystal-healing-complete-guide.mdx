Crystal healing is an energy practice that cultures around the world have drawn on for thousands of years. Ancient Egyptians used lapis lazuli as a protective talisman, while jade has long been treasured across East Asia as a symbol of vitality and prosperity. Today, interest in crystals is rising worldwide alongside the broader mindfulness and wellness movement.

## How Crystal Healing Is Said to Work

Crystals are minerals formed over millions of years in the earth. Each one has a distinct crystalline structure, and that structure is said to produce a particular vibrational frequency.

**The physical angle**: quartz has a genuine piezoelectric effect and is used in real-world electronics like watches — evidence that crystals really can convert energy from one form to another.

**The energetic angle**: crystal healers describe each stone as resonating with the body's energy systems — the chakras, the flow of chi — to support balance and healing.

<Callout type="info">
**Where the science stands**: clinical research on the effectiveness of crystal healing remains limited. That said, many people use crystals as meditation aids, anchors for setting intentions, and physical symbols of mindfulness, and report real psychological benefits from doing so.
</Callout>

## How to Cleanse a Crystal

Always cleanse a crystal before using it. Crystals can absorb the energy of whatever they've come into contact with previously.

### Moonlight Cleansing (the gentlest, most universal method)

- Place the crystal by a window or on a balcony on a full-moon night
- Let it sit under the moonlight overnight and bring it in the next morning
- Safe for use with most crystals

### Sunlight Cleansing (a strong energetic charge)

- Leave the crystal in morning sunlight for 1–2 hours on a clear day
- Caution: colored crystals such as amethyst and rose quartz can fade in direct sun

### Running Water Cleansing

- Rinse briefly under natural or filtered water
- Caution: never use this method on crystals that dissolve or degrade in water, such as selenite or malachite

### Smoke Cleansing (smudging)

- Burn white sage or palo santo and let the smoke surround the crystal
- Set an intention as you do it, such as, "I cleanse all negative energy from this crystal"

### Cleansing with Clear Quartz

- Place other crystals on top of a clear quartz cluster
- Leave them there for 24 hours

## Activating a Crystal and Setting an Intention

Once cleansed, set an intention for the crystal.

1. Hold the crystal in your hand and close your eyes
2. Take a deep breath in, then out
3. Clearly visualize what you want this crystal to support
4. Say — aloud or silently — "This crystal is now activated for [your purpose]"
