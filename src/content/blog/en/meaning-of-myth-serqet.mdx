Serqet is an Egyptian goddess associated with scorpions, venom, healing, and funerary protection. She is depicted as a woman with a scorpion on her head and was regarded as a sacred protector who guarded people from poison. The real danger of scorpions in the desert and around homes made Serqet an urgently relevant goddess in everyday life.

## Core Myth — Representative Episodes

Serqet's central power is the handling of venom. A scorpion's poison threatens life, but Serqet appears as a goddess who understands and controls that danger. In ancient Egyptian healing spells, her name was used as protective language to ward off or treat attacks by venomous creatures.

Serqet was also important in funerary tradition. She was one of the four protective goddesses who guarded canopic jars and the body of the deceased. The fact that a goddess standing on the boundary of poison and death protects the body on its way to the afterlife shows that the one who understands danger can become the deepest kind of protector.

## Reading as an Archetype — A Jungian View

From a Jungian perspective, Serqet is the archetype of the **healer of wounds** and the **guardian of dangerous knowledge**. She is not someone who speaks of danger from a safe distance, but a being who can heal because she knows the nature of deadly venom. In modern terms, she resembles emergency medicine, toxicology, crisis counseling, and specialists who help others after having passed through trauma themselves.

The key to reading Serqet is that knowledge of what is frightening can become protection. If danger is denied, it cannot be prepared for; if one is overwhelmed by danger, healing becomes impossible. The Serqet archetype symbolizes that when poison is named and handled through proper ritual and knowledge, its deadliness can be transformed into wisdom that protects life.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is Serqet a scorpion goddess?**

A. Yes. She symbolizes scorpions and venom, but her core meaning is not the spreading of poison. It is the power to protect from poison and to heal.

**Q. Why is Serqet connected with funerals?**

A. She was regarded as one of the protective goddesses who guarded the body of the deceased and the canopic jars. Her character as a guardian of dangerous boundaries connects her with funerary protection.
