# Fortune Tools

Three tools drawing on Eastern and Western wisdom traditions — all in one page. Tarot, dream interpretation, and daily fortune each offer a different doorway to self-reflection. While none are scientifically grounded, psychology recognizes these tools as aids to **projection** — the process of externalizing thoughts and emotions onto symbols in order to gain clearer self-awareness.

## Tarot Spread Guide

| Position | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Card 1 (Past) | The background and causes that shaped the present |
| Card 2 (Present) | The current situation and energy around you |
| Card 3 (Future) | The likely direction if things continue as they are |

## Common Dream Symbols

| Dream | Traditional Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Flying | Desire for freedom, new possibilities |
| Teeth falling out | Worry or anxiety, fear of change |
| Water (ocean / river) | Emotions, the unconscious, change |
| Being chased | Real-life pressure, avoidance |
| Pig (East Asian tradition) | Wealth luck, abundance |
