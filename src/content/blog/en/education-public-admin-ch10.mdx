# Chapter 10: Civil Service Systems: Spoils and Merit

The criteria for recruiting public officials is a key factor in determining the democracy and professionalism of the administration.

---

## 1. Spoils System

The practice of appointing public officials based on political loyalty or contribution.

- **Pros**: Strengthens democratic control (reflects election results), develops party politics, ensures administrative accountability.
- **Cons**: Lowers administrative professionalism, induces corruption, undermines administrative continuity and neutrality.

---

## 2. Merit System

The practice of recruiting officials based on individual ability, qualifications, and performance. The Pendleton Act (1883) in the U.S. is the cornerstone of the modern merit system.

- **Pros**: Ensures professionalism and neutrality, guarantees equality of opportunity, provides job security.
- **Cons**: Can lead to institutional rigidity, lowers political responsiveness (officials may become a privileged class).

---

## 3. Career Civil Service

A system that recruits young, healthy talents and allows them to dedicate their entire lives to public service.

- **Requirements**: Established merit system, closed-entry recruitment, rank-in-person orientation, high job security.

---

## 4. Representative Bureaucracy

A system that recruits officials in a way that reflects the demographic composition of society to address the limits of the merit system, which may exclude the socially disadvantaged. (e.g., gender quotas, mandatory employment for disabled persons).

| Feature                  | Merit System                   | Representative Bureaucracy                           |
| :----------------------- | :----------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------- |
| **Core Value**           | Efficiency, Individual ability | Equity, Social representation                        |
| **Recruitment Criteria** | Test scores                    | Background, Quota systems                            |
| **Criticism**            | May fix social inequality      | Reverse discrimination, potential drop in efficiency |

---

## Key Checklist

- [ ] Which system appoints officials based on their political support to promote the development of party politics? (Answer: Spoils System)
- [ ] Which system seeks to overcome the limits of the merit system and increase the social representation of the bureaucracy? (Answer: Representative Bureaucracy)
- [ ] Which U.S. act served as the catalyst for the introduction of the modern merit system? (Answer: Pendleton Act)
