### Introduction: "The Eighth Wonder of the World"

Albert Einstein reportedly called compound interest "the eighth wonder of the world," saying: "He who understands it, earns it; he who doesn't, pays it."

Tiles that begin as small, seemingly insignificant 2s and 4s — when they merge and double repeatedly — suddenly become 1,024 and then 2,048. That process mirrors how wealth actually accumulates over a lifetime. Today, let's feel that thrilling principle of exponential growth through the classic game of **2048**.

---

## 1. Experience the Magic: Play 2048

Swipe tiles up, down, left, or right to merge matching numbers. Reaching the tile labeled 2048 represents achieving financial freedom! (Use arrow keys on desktop.)



---

## 2. Three Economic Principles Hidden Inside the Game

### ① The Timing of Investment (Early Start)
Carefully using the empty spaces in the early game to position your tiles mirrors what happens when you invest even a small amount early in your career. The more open space (time) you have, the more room there is later for bigger numbers to fit.

### ② The Snowball Effect
Going from 2 to 4 feels slow. But going from 512 to 1,024 in a single move adds an enormous amount in an instant. Compound interest has exactly this "J-curve" shape — the graph stays relatively flat for years and then accelerates sharply upward as time goes on.

### ③ Risk Management (Space Management)
The most common cause of game over is running out of space. In investing, over-extending yourself (going "all in") leaves you with no room to maneuver when the market makes even a small move against you.

---

## 3. How to Make Your Financial Life Look Like 2048

The method for making compound interest work in real life is simple to understand but hard to sustain.

1. **Make time your ally**: Investment duration matters more than rate of return.
2. **Reinvest your returns**: Just as tiles merge on the board, reinvesting interest and dividends back into your principal creates exponential growth.
3. **Survive the boring middle**: The hardest moment is when your portfolio sits at the equivalent of 128 or 256 — seemingly going nowhere. That plateau is often right before explosive growth kicks in.

## Conclusion: 2048 Is a Beginning, Not an End

Reaching the 2048 tile doesn't end the game — you can keep going toward 4,096, 8,192, and beyond. Successful wealth management works the same way. Maintaining good habits after reaching a goal is the real secret to lasting wealth.

Don't forget the feeling this game gave you. May your small savings and investments one day grow into a great forest.
