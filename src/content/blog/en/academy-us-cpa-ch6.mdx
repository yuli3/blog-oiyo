## CPA License Requirements

Passing the exam is just the first step. A CPA license requires:

1. **Pass all 4 CPA exam sections** (score 75+ each)
2. **150 semester hours** of education (most states)
3. **1–2 years** of supervised work experience under a licensed CPA
4. **AICPA Ethics Exam**: pass the Professional Ethics course

## Big 4 Career Ladder (US Market)

**The Big 4**: Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG

| Level | Experience | Typical Salary (US) |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|
| Associate/Staff | 0–2 years | \$60,000–\$80,000 |
| Senior Associate | 2–5 years | \$75,000–\$100,000 |
| Manager | 5–8 years | \$100,000–\$130,000 |
| Senior Manager/Director | 8–12 years | \$130,000–\$180,000 |
| Partner | 12+ years | \$200,000–\$500,000+ |

**Big 4 services**: Audit, Tax, Advisory/Consulting

## Corporate Finance Career Paths

CPA is not limited to public accounting:

| Role | Typical Experience | Salary Range |
|------|------------------|-------------|
| Financial Controller | 5–10 years | \$120,000–\$180,000 |
| VP Finance | 10–15 years | \$150,000–\$250,000 |
| CFO (mid-size company) | 15+ years | \$200,000–\$500,000+ |
| Internal Audit Director | 8–15 years | \$130,000–\$200,000 |
| FP&A Director | 8–12 years | \$120,000–\$180,000 |
