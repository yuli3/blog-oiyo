Neith is one of Egypt's very ancient goddesses, a complex being who combines war and hunting, weaving, creation, and wisdom. She appears through the sign of a shield and crossed arrows, and she was worshiped as the principal goddess of Sais. Neith presents a distinctive union: a warrior carrying weapons, and also a weaver who weaves the fabric of the world.

## Core Myth — Representative Episodes

Neith's important character lies in her independence as a creator goddess. In some traditions, she is described as a primordial goddess who existed by herself, a power that gives birth to the world or begins order. The image of a creator not subordinated to a male consort makes Neith distinctive even among Egyptian goddesses.

Another representative scene is her role, mentioned almost like a mediator, in the kingship dispute between Horus and Set. Neith is a powerful war goddess, but she does not simply incite violence. Instead, she offers judgment and balance. The symbol of weaving is also important. She is a goddess who interlaces the lines of war, the threads of creation, and social order into a single cloth.

## Reading as an Archetype — Jungian Perspective

From a Jungian perspective, Neith is the archetype of the **warrior goddess** and the **weaving creator**. She can fight, but she does not make the world through conflict alone. In modern terms, she resembles a female leader with strong expertise, a mediator who weaves together complex interests, and a creator who handles both strategy and making.

The key point when reading Neith is that independence is not isolation. She stands by her own power, yet she weaves the threads of the world and mediates disputes. For that reason, the Neith archetype symbolizes mature creativity that builds relationships and structures without losing its own authority.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q. Is Neith a goddess of war?**

A. Yes. She is a goddess of war and hunting with the symbols of shield and arrows, but she also carries the qualities of weaving, creation, and wisdom.

**Q. Why is Neith associated with weaving?**

A. Weaving is the act of joining threads to make form. It can be understood as a symbol that shows Neith's creativity and her power to weave order.
