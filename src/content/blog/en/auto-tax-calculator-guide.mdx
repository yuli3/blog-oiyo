## What Is Vehicle Tax?

Vehicle tax is a local tax levied annually on vehicle owners. The standard schedule requires payment **twice a year — in June and December** — but applying for **annual prepayment** at the start of the year earns a discount of up to 7%.

## Vehicle Tax Assessment Criteria

### Non-Commercial Passenger Vehicles (Most Common)

Tax is assessed based on engine displacement.

| Displacement | Tax per cc |
|--------------|------------|
| Up to 1,000 cc | $0.07 |
| 1,001–1,600 cc | $0.12 |
| 1,601–2,000 cc | $0.17 |
| 2,001–2,500 cc | $0.19 |
| 2,501 cc and above | $0.19 |

**Example**: A 2,000 cc vehicle → 2,000 × $0.17 = **$340/year** ($170 each in June and December)

### Electric and Hydrogen Vehicles

Because they have no engine displacement, a flat annual rate of **$100** applies (as of 2025).

### Vehicle Age Reduction

Starting from the third year after manufacture, the tax is reduced by 5% per year, up to a maximum of 50%.

| Years Since Manufacture | Tax Reduction |
|-------------------------|---------------|
| 1–2 years | 0% |
| Year 3 | 5% reduction |
| Year 5 | 15% reduction |
| 10+ years | 50% reduction (maximum) |

## Save on Vehicle Tax with Annual Prepayment

Annual prepayment applications are accepted in **January, March, June, or September**.

| Application Month | Discount Rate |
|-------------------|---------------|
| January | 7% |
| March | 5% |
| June | 2.5% |
| September | 1.25% |

**Best choice**: Apply for annual prepayment every January → save 7%

For a vehicle with $340 in annual tax, January prepayment saves **$24**.

## How to Pay Vehicle Tax

1. **Local Tax Portal (WETAX)**: wetax.go.kr — online payment
2. **Smart WETAX App**: mobile payment
3. **Auto-debit**: set up automatic payment within the payment period
4. **Card payment**: local tax card payment portal

---

## Pro-Rated Refund After Selling Your Vehicle

If you sell or scrap your vehicle mid-year, you can receive a pro-rated refund of the remaining period on any vehicle tax already paid.

Refund application: Tax Division of your local city/county/district office, or online via WETAX
