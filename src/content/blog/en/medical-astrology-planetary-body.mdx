## Introduction: As Above, So Below

Hippocrates of ancient Greece is called the father of modern medicine, but he was actually an outstanding astrologer. He even said, **"A physician without a knowledge of astrology has no right to call himself a physician."**
Medical Astrology starts from the premise that the human body (Microcosm) is a miniature of the solar system (Macrocosm). Each planet and sign governs specific body organs and physiological processes.

## Core Concept: 12 Signs and Body Parts (Zodiac Man)

The human body is assigned from head (Aries) to feet (Pisces) in the order of the 12 signs.

1.  **Aries:** Head, brain, face, eyes. (Headaches, fevers)
2.  **Taurus:** Throat, vocal cords, thyroid. (Sore throat, neck issues)
3.  **Gemini:** Shoulders, arms, hands, lungs. (Bronchitis, fractures)
4.  **Cancer:** Chest, stomach, breasts. (Indigestion, gastritis)
5.  **Leo:** Heart, spine, upper back. (Cardiovascular disease, back pain)
6.  **Virgo:** Abdomen, small intestine, nervous system. (IBS, nervous breakdown)
7.  **Libra:** Lower back, kidneys, skin. (Lumbago, nephritis)
8.  **Scorpio:** Genitals, excretory system, rectum. (Urological diseases)
9.  **Sagittarius:** Hips, thighs, liver. (Sciatica, liver disease)
10. **Capricorn:** Knees, bones, teeth, skin. (Rheumatism, arthritis)
11. **Aquarius:** Calves, ankles, circulation. (Varicose veins)
12. **Pisces:** Feet, lymphatic system, immune system. (Plantar fasciitis, addiction)

## Planetary Physiology

If signs are 'parts', planets are 'functions'.

- **Sun:** Vitality, heartbeat, overall stamina.
- **Moon:** Body fluids, hormones, digestion, reproductive cycle. Body rhythm.
- **Mercury:** Nerve transmission, respiration, hand dexterity.
- **Venus:** Veins, kidneys, desire for sweets, diabetes.
- **Mars:** Muscles, red blood cells, inflammation, surgery, heat.
- **Jupiter:** Liver, growth hormone, obesity, excess growth (tumors).
- **Saturn:** Bones, teeth, skin, aging, hardening (sclerosis), stones.
- **Uranus:** Seizures, spasms, sudden nervous disorders.
- **Neptune:** Addiction, paralysis, mysterious illnesses, allergies.
- **Pluto:** Regeneration, genetic diseases, cancer, major surgery.

## Deep Dive: Reading the Timing of Disease

The highlight of Medical Astrology is predicting "When will I get sick?" and "When will I recover?"

### 6th House and 8th House

- **6th House:** Acute illnesses, occupational diseases, lifestyle diseases. (Curable with care)
- **8th House:** Chronic illnesses, genetic disorders, serious diseases related to death.

### Influence of Transit Saturn

When Transit Saturn forms a Square (90°) or Opposition (180°) to your Sun or Moon, **vitality is at its lowest**. Pushing yourself now guarantees trouble. Chronic conditions worsen or bone/teeth issues arise.

### Influence of Transit Jupiter

When Jupiter passes the 6th House or aspects the Sun, healing power increases. You meet good doctors or medicine works well.

## Practical Application: Health Regimen by Constitution

The **strongest Element** in your chart determines your constitution.

- **Fire Constitution (Aries/Leo/Sag):** Hot and dry. Watch heart/blood pressure.
  - _Rx:_ Meditation, cooling foods, yoga instead of intense cardio.
- **Earth Constitution (Taurus/Virgo/Cap):** Cold and dry. Watch digestion/joints.
  - _Rx:_ Warm foods, regular meals, massage.
- **Air Constitution (Gemini/Libra/Aq):** Warm and moist. Watch nervous system/lungs.
  - _Rx:_ Fresh air, breathing exercises, talking to relieve stress.
- **Water Constitution (Cancer/Scorp/Pisces):** Cold and moist. Watch fluids/lymphatic circulation.
  - _Rx:_ Half-bath, diuretic teas, emotional cleansing.

## Wrapping Up: Body is the Memory of Stars

Your back pain might not be random; it could be your Libra screaming. Indigestion might be Cancer's emotions getting stuck.
Medical Astrology is not just a technique to cure diseases. It is wisdom to understand **how my body resonates with the rhythm of the universe.** When you listen to the voice of the stars, healing begins.
