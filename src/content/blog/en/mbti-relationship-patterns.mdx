## Dating Style by MBTI Group

### NT (Intuition + Thinking) — The Analysts
**INTJ · INTP · ENTJ · ENTP**

The NT group prioritizes **intellectual connection and independence** above everything else, even in relationships.

| Shared traits | Ideal partner | Source of conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Logic over emotion | An intellectual equal | Limited emotional expression |
| Values independence | A partner to grow with | Can come across as cold |
| Holds high standards | Someone with their own inner world | Perfectionist expectations |

---

### NF (Intuition + Feeling) — The Idealists
**INFJ · INFP · ENFJ · ENFP**

The NF group pursues **deep connection and meaningful relationships**.

| Shared traits | Ideal partner | Source of conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Craves emotional depth | An authentic partner | Tendency to idealize |
| Values connection | Someone with soul-level depth | Sharp disillusionment after disappointment |
| Values-driven | A partner aligned in values | Difficulty facing hard realities |

---

### SJ (Sensing + Judging) — The Guardians
**ISTJ · ISFJ · ESTJ · ESFJ**

The SJ group wants **stable, committed relationships**.

| Shared traits | Ideal partner | Source of conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Responsible and loyal | A dependable, stable partner | Resistance to change |
| Values tradition | Someone who values home and family | Can seem too conservative |
| Deeply devoted | A partner who notices their effort | Limited emotional expression |

---

### SP (Sensing + Perceiving) — The Explorers
**ISTP · ISFP · ESTP · ESFP**

The SP group values **fun, freedom, and the present moment**.

| Shared traits | Ideal partner | Source of conflict |
|---|---|---|
| Present-focused | A free-spirited, energetic partner | Struggles with long-term planning |
| Expresses through action | Someone to share experiences with | Awkward at putting feelings into words |
| Values independence | A partner who gives them space | Commitment can look harder than it is |

## Relationship Patterns for All 16 Types

### INTJ — The Strategic Perfectionist in Love

INTJ approaches relationships **strategically and selectively**.

**Relationship traits:**
- Chooses a partner carefully, then commits deeply once chosen
- Awkward with emotional expression, but feels deeply underneath
- Independence matters enormously — respects a partner's space too
- Craves intellectual stimulation

**Ideal partner**: Someone intelligent and independent — a partner who keeps their own world while sharing a life together.

**Source of conflict**: Limited emotional expression; the gap between "putting in enough effort" and "actually expressing it."

**Good advice for INTJ**: Say "I love you" in words, not just actions.

---

### INFJ — The Idealist Seeking Deep Connection

INFJ wants **deep, meaningful connection** above everything else.

**Relationship traits:**
- A romantic idealist searching for a soulmate
- Wants to deeply understand a partner and help them grow
- Tends to give everything to a relationship
- Risks loving a partner's future potential more than who they actually are right now

**Ideal partner**: Someone authentic and deep, with a genuine drive to grow.

**Good advice for INFJ**: Accept your partner as they are, and voice your own needs too.

---

### ENFP — Passionate Love, Free Spirit

ENFP wants both **excitement and deep connection**.

**Relationship traits:**
- Early-relationship passion is explosive
- Tends to idealize a partner
- Feelings run intense and sincere, but can shift over time
- Caught in the paradox of wanting both freedom and closeness at once

**Ideal partner**: Someone intellectually stimulating with real emotional depth.

**Good advice for ENFP**: The relationship can deepen even after the initial spark fades — it just takes sustained effort.

---

### INFP — The Deep, Romantic Idealist

INFP searches for **genuine love and meaning**.

**Relationship traits:**
- Wants a deep, serious relationship
- Feels intensely but hesitates to express it
- Feels things strongly internally but struggles to show it
- Searches a long time for someone aligned in values

**Ideal partner**: A partner who shares their values and accepts them exactly as they are.

---

### ISTJ — Steady, Faithful Relationships

ISTJ builds relationships on **trust and responsibility**.

**Relationship traits:**
- Approaches things slowly and seriously
- Once committed, unwavering
- Shows love through action rather than words
- Prefers stable, predictable relationships

**Good advice for ISTJ**: Putting feelings into words, not just actions, helps a partner feel far more loved.

---

### ISFJ — Warm, Devoted Caretaking

ISFJ expresses love through **care and devotion**.

**Relationship traits:**
- Notices and tends to a partner's needs first
- Often struggles to voice their own needs
- Seeks a stable, harmonious relationship
- Sacrifices a great deal for a partner, and it hurts when that goes unrecognized

**Good advice for ISFJ**: Voicing your own needs isn't selfish. A healthy relationship requires both people's needs to be met.

---

### ENTJ — Ambition and Leadership in Partnership

ENTJ wants a **partnership built around shared goals**.

**Relationship traits:**
- Tends to manage the relationship efficiently, almost like a project
- Wants to help a partner grow and rise together
- Leans on plans and execution more than emotion
- Prefers a clear, decisive relationship

**Good advice for ENTJ**: Remember that a partner sometimes needs emotional connection more than a plan or a goal.

---

### ESFJ — The Warm Center of Community

ESFJ finds happiness through **harmonious relationships**.

**Relationship traits:**
- Oriented toward family and community
- Wants to introduce a partner to their social world
- Expresses emotion openly and wants recognition in return
- Finds conflict especially difficult

**Good advice for ESFJ**: Talking honestly instead of avoiding conflict actually deepens the relationship.

## Deeper Compatibility: Cognitive Function Pairing

The real key to MBTI compatibility isn't the type label itself — it's the **order of cognitive functions**.

### Best matches: same functions, different order

**Example: INTJ + ENFP**
- INTJ: Ni > Te > Fi > Se
- ENFP: Ne > Fi > Te > Si

Each person's dominant function lines up with the other's inferior function — meaning they complement and grow each other.

### More challenging matches: entirely different functions

**Example: ISTJ + ENFP**
- Fundamentally different values and decision-making styles
- But often the pairing where each partner has the most to learn from the other

<Callout type="tip">
**The real key to MBTI compatibility**: each person's **level of psychological health** matters more than their type label. A healthy ISTJ and a healthy ENFP can overcome type differences and build a great relationship.
</Callout>
