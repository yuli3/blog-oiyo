## Mismatch Theory

Why do we crave sugar? Because on the savanna, calories were rare.
Why are we anxious? Because a rustle in the grass meant a lion.
Today, a rustle is just an email, but our body reacts the same way. We are living in an alien environment.

## Adaptation Lag

Evolution is slow. Technology is fast. We are trying to run modern software on Stone Age hardware.
