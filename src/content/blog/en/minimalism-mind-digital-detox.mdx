## Objects are Visual Noise

Clutter on the desk doesn't speak, but our brain burns energy constantly scanning and processing it. This is called 'Visual Noise'. Empty space (Yeobaek) is not just interior design, but a resting space for the brain.

## Don't Be a Digital Hoarder

Thousands of unorganized photos, unread emails, and numerous notification badges make us unconsciously anxious.

- **Clear your desktop:** Keep your computer and phone backgrounds clean. A fresh start leads to a clear process.
- **Turn off notifications:** Turn off all notifications except essential ones (calls, texts). Don't lose control of your attention to your smartphone. Looking when you want to, not when it rings, is a proactive life.
