## Consistency

Request 1: "Can I put this small sticker in your window?" (Yes).
Request 2: "Can I put this giant ugly billboard on your lawn?" (Likely Yes).
Why? Because we want to be consistent with our past actions. "I am the kind of person who supports this cause."
