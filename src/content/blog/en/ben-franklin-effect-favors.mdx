## The Rare Book Trick

Benjamin Franklin had a rival legislator who hated him. Franklin didn't try to be nice. Instead, he asked to borrow a rare book from the rival's library.
The rival, flattered, lent it. When Franklin returned it with a thank-you note, the rival became a lifelong friend.

## Rationalizing Kindness

This works because of **Cognitive Dissonance**.
Brain: "I did a favor for Franklin."
Brain: "I hate Franklin."
Conflict!
Resolution: "I must not hate Franklin. In fact, I must like him, since I helped him."

To get someone to like you, let them help you. It makes them feel invested in your success.
