## The Major Astrological Events of 2026

2026 is a year when two major planetary moves signal significant astrological change. The movements of Saturn and Jupiter shape a powerful current of energy across all 12 signs.

### Saturn's move

In 2026, Saturn is completing its settlement into **Pisces after leaving Aquarius**, and by the second half of the year it's approaching entry into **Aries**. Saturn shifts signs roughly every 2.5 years, bringing tests, structure, and responsibility to the sign it occupies and its opposite. In the first half of 2026, Saturn's pressure concentrates on the Pisces-related signs (Pisces, Virgo, Gemini, Sagittarius); from the second half, new challenges begin for the Aries-related signs (Aries, Libra, Cancer, Capricorn).

### Jupiter's move

Jupiter, the planet of luck and expansion, changes signs roughly once a year. In 2026, Jupiter moves **from Gemini into Cancer** (around June), bringing intellectual expansion, communication, and diversity to the fore in the first half, and home, emotion, and inner growth in the second half. The sign Jupiter is passing through tends to see the greatest growth and opportunity of the year.

### Major 2026 Retrogrades

- Mercury retrograde: roughly 3 times (Jan–Feb, May–Jun, Sep–Oct) — watch communication, contracts, travel
- Venus retrograde: second half of the year (roughly Sep–Nov) — reconsider romance, aesthetic judgment, and money matters
- Mars retrograde: part of the first half — reduced drive, watch for burnout

## Aries (Mar 21 – Apr 19)

### 2026 overall fortune

As Saturn enters Aries in the second half of 2026, Aries faces **its first direct Saturn test in roughly 29–30 years**. This is a period when everything you've built gets checked, and what's genuinely solid gets separated from what's shaky. Through the first half you can still express your energy fairly freely, but once the second half begins, irresponsible choices can carry a real cost. There's no need to fear this — used well, this period becomes a powerful opportunity to build a genuinely stable foundation for yourself.

### Love

New romantic opportunities open up in the first half of the year. This is a period when Aries' natural vitality shines, and a proactive approach pays off. Aries already in a relationship need to make an effort to keep things fresh. After Saturn enters in the second half, seriousness and commitment become the central question in relationships. A relationship that started casually may need to find direction — either deepening or coming to an end.

### Career

The first half favors starting new projects and taking on challenges. Aries' pioneering energy is strong, favoring you to step first into territory others hesitate to enter. In the second half, results come with accountability attached, so building a solid foundation matters more than reckless expansion. If you're employed, banking achievements in the first half helps you stay stable in the second.

### Money

Overall in 2026, financial returns track effort fairly closely. Speculation and get-rich-quick thinking are best avoided, especially in the second half when Saturn's influence makes reckless investment more likely to result in losses. It's wise to build your financial foundation through saving and frugality in the first half.

### BEST months: March, April / WORST months: September, October

### Lucky keywords: building the foundation, courageous beginnings, authenticity

As **Jupiter moves from Gemini into Cancer**, Taurus gets a lift from communication, learning, and financial planning in the first half, and finds deep satisfaction in home and inner stability in the second. Overall, this is a stable year defined more by steady growth than dramatic change — a time when Taurus' signature patience and staying power really shine.

Emotional bonds deepen especially in the second half (thanks to Jupiter in Cancer), as feelings become richer. Single Taurus are likely to find natural connections through shared hobbies or everyday life. Couples strengthen their bond by investing in time together and a comfortable shared environment. Taurus' style of building trust slowly rather than pushing hard is exactly the right approach for love in 2026.

### Career

This is a good year to build or advance professional skills and certifications. Time invested in learning and gathering information in the first half turns into concrete results in the second. Taurus in real estate, food, art, or finance may find especially good opportunities. New collaborations or partnerships could be the key to professional growth.

Taurus' financial fortune is solid in 2026. Expect steady income growth or a rise in the value of existing assets. During the Mercury retrograde in the first half, though, review financial contracts or major purchases carefully and don't rush. Long-term investing and saving deliver far better results than short-term speculation.

### BEST months: May, November / WORST months: February, August

### Lucky keywords: consistency, sensory satisfaction, building trust
