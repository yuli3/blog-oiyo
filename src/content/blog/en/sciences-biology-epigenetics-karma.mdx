## Introduction: End of Genetic Determinism

"Cancer runs in my family." "I'm born stupid."
We believed DNA was absolute fate. Living and dying according to blueprint from parents.
But new biology, **Epigenetics**, asks:
"Blueprint is same, why does one get sick and another stay healthy?"

## Core Concept: Switch Above DNA

Genes are not 'Fixed Commands' but 'List of Possibilities'.
What decides which to execute (Expression) is not the gene, but the **Switch (Epi-genome) above the DNA**.
And what toggles that switch is **Environment** and **Your Mind**.

- Feel fear -> 'Disease gene' turns ON.
- Feel love -> 'Healing gene' turns ON.

## Deep Dive: Biological Mechanism of Karma

Biblical verse "Sins of fathers visited upon children to 3rd and 4th generation" or Buddhist Karma has scientific basis.
Experiments show fear response of traumatized mice is inherited to children and grandchildren (without DNA sequence change).
Grandmother's war terror or starvation is stored in your genes as **Chemical Tags (Methylation)**.
This is **Biological Karma**. Ancestors' pain breathes inside you.

## Practical Application: Rewriting Destiny

Fortunately, this switch can be changed. This is the great gospel of Epigenetics.

1.  **Shift in Consciousness:** Dr. Bruce Lipton said "Belief changes biology." If you deeply believe "I am healthy", "I am loved", DNA switches rearrange at cellular level.
2.  **Cleanse Environment:** Cut toxic food, toxic relationships, toxic news. Good environment turns on good genes.
3.  **Ancestral Healing:** Comfort and let go of ancestors' pain through meditation or family constellation. If you heal, the burden is not passed to descendants. **You are the Hero to break family Karma.**

## Conclusion: You Are Not a Victim of Genes

You are not a machine living as genes dictate.
The moment you change your perception, your Blood changes, your Destiny changes.
Science proved it. Mind over Genes.
