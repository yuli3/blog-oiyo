## The Ego Shield

Success has many fathers; failure is an orphan.
When stock prices go up, traders say "I am a genius."
When they go down, they say "The market is irrational."

## Taking Responsibility

To grow, you must overcome this bias. You must accept that your failures are your fault. Only then can you fix them.
