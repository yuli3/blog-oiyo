Feng Shui — literally "wind and water" — is an East Asian practice for studying how the natural flow of energy moves and accumulates in a space. For thousands of years it has shaped architecture, city planning, and interior design across East Asia, and today it's used as a practical lifestyle tool for optimizing the energy of your surroundings.

## Core Concepts of Feng Shui

### Chi (Qi)

At the heart of Feng Shui is **chi** — the life force said to flow through everything. Where good chi gathers, health, prosperity, and happiness are said to follow naturally.

**Signs of good chi in a space**:
- Plenty of natural light and good ventilation
- Tidy, uncluttered surroundings
- A space people find comfortable and want to linger in

**Signs of poor chi in a space**:
- Dark, damp, poorly ventilated
- Cluttered and dusty
- A space that feels uncomfortable, one you want to leave quickly

### Yin and Yang Balance

Balancing yin and yang inside your home matters.

| Yin | Yang |
|-------|-------|
| Dark colors, cool materials | Bright colors, warm materials |
| Quiet spaces | Active spaces |
| The water element | The fire element |

### The Five Elements and Space

| Element | Colors | Direction | Use in a Space |
|------|------|------|---------|
| Wood | Green, brown | East, southeast | Growth and creative energy |
| Fire | Red, orange | South | Recognition and reputation energy |
| Earth | Yellow, beige | Center | Stability and health energy |
| Metal | White, gold | West, northwest | Wealth and support energy |
| Water | Blue, black | North | Career and wisdom energy |

## Direction and Feng Shui in the Home

### Entryway/Front Door

The entryway is your home's "mouth" — the first gateway through which energy enters.

**Good entryway Feng Shui**:
- Keep it bright and clean
- Store shoes properly; don't let clutter build up at the entrance
- Position mirrors so they don't directly face the door
- Add plants or flowers for lively energy

**What to avoid**:
- Letting broken items pile up near the entrance
- Placing a mirror directly opposite the door (it pushes incoming energy back out)
- Dim lighting

### Living Room

The living room is where the family gathers, so **vibrant yang energy** matters most here.

**Good living-room Feng Shui**:
- Place the sofa with a solid wall behind it for a sense of security
- Adjust curtains to let natural light in
- Keep living plants (never dead ones — living energy matters)
- Put the TV in the living room, not the bedroom

**Elements that boost wealth energy**:
- Prosperity plants (jade plants, money trees) in the southeast corner
- Touches of gold or purple
- A fish tank (flowing water = the flow of wealth)

### Bedroom

The bedroom is a space of rest and renewal centered on **yin energy**.

**Bed placement**:
- Headboard against a solid wall (for stability)
- The door should be visible from the bed (a sense of control)
- Avoid lining the bed up directly opposite the door
- Never place the bed directly under a window

**What to avoid in the bedroom**:
- Charging a TV, computer, or phone nearby (the yang energy of electronics disrupts sleep)
- A mirror facing the bed (said to absorb your sleeping energy)
- Storing things under the bed (it blocks the circulation of energy)

### Kitchen

The kitchen is where **fire and water** coexist.

**Good kitchen Feng Shui**:
- Keep the stove (fire) and sink (water) from facing each other directly (to avoid clashing)
- Never place a mirror behind the stove
- Keep it clean at all times — the foundation of wealth energy
- Keep food fresh

### Bathroom

Water drains out of the bathroom, which makes managing its energy important.

**Bathroom Feng Shui tips**:
- Always keep the toilet lid closed (to prevent wealth from "draining away")
- Keep the bathroom door closed
- Use plants to absorb moisture and add life
- Keep it clean and well-ventilated

## The Bagua Map and Dividing Your Home

Feng Shui uses the **Bagua** map, which divides a home into nine zones.

| Zone | Direction | Life Area | How to Strengthen It |
|------|------|------|---------|
| Kan | North | Career | Blue, the water element |
| Gen | Northeast | Knowledge, self-development | Green, plants |
| Zhen | East | Family, health | Green, wood |
| Xun | Southeast | Wealth, prosperity | Purple, plants |
| Li | South | Recognition, reputation | Red, candles |
| Kun | Southwest | Love, relationships | Pink, paired objects |
| Dui | West | Creativity, children | White, metal |
| Qian | Northwest | Helpful people, travel | Gray, metal |
| Center | Center | Health, balance | Yellow, stability |

## Practical Feng Shui to Boost Wealth

1. **Strengthen your wealth corner (southeast)**: prosperity plants, purple accents, crystals
2. **Use a fish tank**: a goldfish tank in the southeast of your living room (goldfish symbolize wealth)
3. **Keep the entryway clean**: keep the gateway through which all energy enters bright and tidy
4. **Replace broken items right away**: chipped dishware or a stopped clock creates stagnant energy
5. **Strengthen the north side of your home**: keep the space that influences career luck neat and clear
