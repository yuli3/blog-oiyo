# Blood Type Personality & Compatibility

Blood type personality theory originated in early 20th century Japan and remains widely popular in Korea and East Asia. While it has no scientific basis, it serves as a fun icebreaker and a lighthearted framework for self-reflection.

## Key Traits by Blood Type

| Blood Type | Keywords | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Type A** | Detail-oriented, diligent | Perfectionism, consideration, precision | Worry-prone, overly mindful of others |
| **Type B** | Free-spirited, direct | Honesty, creativity, focus | Self-centered, moody |
| **Type O** | Leadership, sociable | Inclusiveness, drive, social skills | Stubborn, jealous |
| **Type AB** | Dual nature, rational | Cool analysis, adaptability | Unpredictable, emotionally distant |

<Callout type="note">
**Scientific fact**: Decades of worldwide research have found no statistically significant correlation between blood type and personality. Blood type personality theory is a classic example of the **Barnum Effect** — the tendency to believe vague descriptions apply specifically to oneself.
</Callout>
