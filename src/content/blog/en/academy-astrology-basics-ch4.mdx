## What Is a House?

A house represents **a specific domain of life**. If a planet says 'what' and a sign says 'how,' a house answers **'where'** that energy operates.

Houses are determined by birth time and place. They begin at the **Ascendant (Rising Sign)** and extend counterclockwise through all 12 positions.

---

## The Ascendant (Rising Sign)

The **Ascendant** is the zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at the exact moment of your birth. It marks the beginning of the 1st House and is one of the most important points in the birth chart.

| Element | Symbolizes |
|---|---|
| Sun sign | Inner essence, conscious self |
| **Ascendant** | **External impression, first appearance, interface with the world** |
| Moon sign | Emotional response, the unconscious |

People with the same Sun sign can give completely different impressions depending on their Ascendant.

<Callout type="tip">
**The Ascendant changes every ~2 hours**. People born on the same day but at dawn versus midday have entirely different Ascendants.
</Callout>

## Complete Analysis of the 12 Houses

### House 1 — Self and First Impressions
**Keywords**: Appearance, self-awareness, first impressions, physical body
**The house where the Ascendant is located**

House 1 is "you." It represents how you present yourself to the world and what impression others receive when they first meet you.

Planets in this house strongly influence appearance and first impressions.

---

### House 2 — Wealth and Values
**Keywords**: Money, material possessions, personal values, talents
**Ruling sign**: Taurus

House 2 is what you own and cherish. It shows how you earn and spend money, and the value you place on your own abilities.

---

### House 3 — Communication and Learning
**Keywords**: Communication, siblings, short trips, learning, neighbors
**Ruling sign**: Gemini

House 3 is everyday communication and learning. It reveals how you learn, how you express yourself, and your relationships with your immediate environment (siblings, neighbors).

---

### House 4 — Home and Roots
**Keywords**: Family, home, roots, psychological foundation, mother
**Ruling sign**: Cancer / **The lowest point of the chart (IC — Imum Coeli)**

House 4 is the most private realm. It represents family environment, the relationship with your mother, and your psychological foundation of safety.

---

### House 5 — Creativity and Joy
**Keywords**: Creativity, romance, children, play, self-expression
**Ruling sign**: Leo

House 5 is the domain of pure joy. It reveals how you express creativity, how you behave in romance, and your relationship with children.

---

### House 6 — Work and Health
**Keywords**: Health, daily work, service, routines, pets
**Ruling sign**: Virgo

House 6 is daily work and health. It shows how you work, how you manage your health, and your spirit of service.

---

### House 7 — Partnership
**Keywords**: Marriage, partnerships, contracts, public relationships
**Ruling sign**: Libra / **Opposite the Ascendant (Descendant, DC)**

House 7 is one-on-one relationships. It represents how you relate to a romantic partner, spouse, or business partner.

<Callout type="note">
**The 7th House paradox**: House 7 represents your partner, but it simultaneously represents the shadow you project. Traits that strongly attract or repel you in a partner may actually exist within your own inner world.
</Callout>

---

### House 8 — Depth and Transformation
**Keywords**: Death and rebirth, shared assets, psychological depth, other people's money
**Ruling sign**: Scorpio

House 8 is the most profound realm. It represents transformation, inheritance, shared resources, and psychological depth.

---

### House 9 — Philosophy and Exploration
**Keywords**: Higher education, philosophy, travel, religion, foreign lands
**Ruling sign**: Sagittarius

House 9 is the realm of broadening horizons. It reveals how you expand your worldview, your beliefs and philosophy, and experiences of the foreign.

---

### House 10 — Career and Honor
**Keywords**: Career, fame, social status, father
**Ruling sign**: Capricorn / **The highest point of the chart (MC — Midheaven)**

House 10 is social achievement. It shows which career brings success and how you become known in society.

---

### House 11 — Networks and Dreams
**Keywords**: Friends, groups, social ideals, hopes
**Ruling sign**: Aquarius

House 11 is community and future vision. It reveals which groups you belong to, what dreams you hold, and how you build your network.

---

### House 12 — Solitude and Reflection
**Keywords**: Retreat, unconscious, solitude, self-sacrifice, spiritual inquiry
**Ruling sign**: Pisces

House 12 is the most hidden realm. It represents unconscious patterns, spiritual exploration, and hidden strengths and weaknesses.

---

## House Strength: Angular, Succedent, and Cadent

| Type | Houses | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| **Angular** | 1, 4, 7, 10 | Strongest influence; key life domains |
| **Succedent** | 2, 5, 8, 11 | Moderate strength |
| **Cadent** | 3, 6, 9, 12 | Softest influence; more internal |

People with many planets in Angular houses tend to be highly visible socially and exert a strong impact on the world.

---

## House 12 — Hidden Strengths

House 12 is often known as the 'difficult house,' but it is also **a place where deep inner strength is hidden**.

People with many planets in House 12:
- Have an exceptionally rich inner world
- Carry hidden spiritual or artistic talents
- Recharge through solitary time
- Contribute to the world through service and self-giving
