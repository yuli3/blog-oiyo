# Frameworks: Choose Your Weapon

Every major achievement in human history—from building the Pyramids to launching a spaceship—required <mark><strong>Project Management.</strong></mark> In the modern business world, a project is a temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product or service. To succeed, you must first choose the right framework.

Choosing a methodology is like choosing between a blueprint and a compass. One provides a fixed path; the other helps you navigate as you go.

## 3. When to Pick Which?

There is no "One size fits all" in project management.

- **Use Waterfall if**: The requirements are fixed, the technology is stable, and the project is highly regulated (e.g., Construction, Aerospace).
- **Use Agile if**: The market is changing fast, the requirements are evolving, and the end product is digital (e.g., Software Development, Marketing Campaigns).
- <mark><strong>Hybrid Approach</strong></mark>: Many modern organizations use a "Waterfall-Agile Hybrid," using Waterfall for long-term budgeting and Agile for day-to-day execution.

<Callout type="important">
  **The Iron Triangle**: Remember that every project is balanced by three constraints: **Scope,
  Time, and Cost.** If you change one, at least one other must be adjusted to maintain quality.
</Callout>

## 4. Conclusion: The Foundation of Execution

Frameworks are not religious doctrines; they are <mark><strong>"Tools for Success."</strong></mark> By understanding the strengths and weaknesses of each, you can design a management process that empowers your team instead of suffocating them.
