# A Simple Map for Learning the Universe

Space feels difficult because the vocabulary arrives all at once: Sun, planet, moon, asteroid, comet, star, nebula, galaxy, black hole. The easier path is to organize everything by **scale, gravity, and energy**.

## 1. What the Solar System Is

The Solar System is not centered on Earth. It is centered on the **Sun**, whose gravity holds planets, moons, dwarf planets, asteroids, and comets in a shared system.

| Object | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Star | A body that produces light through fusion | Sun |
| Planet | A large body orbiting a star | Earth, Mars, Jupiter |
| Moon | A body orbiting a planet | Moon, Europa |
| Dwarf planet | Planet-like body that has not cleared its orbit | Pluto |
| Comet | Icy body that may grow a tail near the Sun | Halley's comet |

## 2. Two Kinds of Planets

The inner planets, Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars, are rocky and relatively small. The outer planets are giants: Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants, while Uranus and Neptune are ice giants.

This one split explains why some worlds can be landed on while others are studied through atmosphere, rings, magnetic fields, and moons.

## 3. Why the Sun Shines

The Sun is not a burning fireball. It is a star powered by **nuclear fusion**. In its core, hydrogen nuclei fuse into helium and release energy. That energy becomes the light and heat that drive weather, ocean circulation, photosynthesis, and the rhythm of life on Earth.

## 4. Stars Have Life Cycles

Stars form inside clouds of gas and dust. Gravity pulls matter inward, pressure and temperature rise, and fusion begins. The most important variable is mass: massive stars burn brighter and die faster.

A star may end as a white dwarf, neutron star, or black hole depending on its mass and history.

## 5. What a Black Hole Is

A black hole is not a cosmic vacuum cleaner. It is a region where gravity is so strong that, inside a boundary called the **event horizon**, even light cannot easily escape.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Event horizon | The boundary beyond which escape becomes impossible |
| Singularity | The theoretical center where density becomes extreme |
| Accretion disk | Hot material orbiting around the black hole |

Black holes are detected indirectly through the motion of nearby stars, glowing disks of material, and gravitational waves.

## A Good Study Order

Start with Earth and the Moon, then move to the Solar System, stars, galaxies, black holes, and finally cosmology. Science does not remove mystery. It gives mystery a sharper language.
