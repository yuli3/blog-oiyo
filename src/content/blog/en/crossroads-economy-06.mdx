## Can Fiscal Policy Fix the K-Shape?

If monetary policy has a paradoxical tendency to deepen the K-shape, can fiscal policy ease it instead?

In theory, yes. Collect more revenue from high earners through progressive taxation, redistribute it to welfare for low-income households, and the K-shaped gap narrows. But reality is far more complicated.

### The Dilemmas of Fiscal Policy

**Problem 1: The debt ceiling**
Since COVID-19, governments around the world are carrying debt equal to 130–140% of GDP, leaving limited room for additional fiscal spending.

**Problem 2: Timing**
Because fiscal policy has to move through legislation and implementation, its effects take one to two years to show up — later than the business cycle itself.

**Problem 3: Political conflict**
Political fights over tax cuts versus expanded welfare get in the way of effective fiscal policy execution.

### The Fiscal Policy Direction Investors Should Watch

The direction fiscal policy takes has a direct effect on specific sector investments.
- **More infrastructure spending:** benefits construction, materials, and energy
- **Expanded welfare:** benefits healthcare and education
- **Higher corporate taxes:** squeezes profits → downward pressure on stock prices
- **Subsidy policy:** benefits semiconductors, EVs, and renewable energy
