While personal colors might seem like a fleeting trend, the experience of discovering the colors that actually suit you is far more powerful than you might think. That moment when you put on a certain colored clothing item in front of the mirror and your face brightens, your gaze becomes sharp, and your skin glows translucently — that is the exact moment you meet your "true color."

Let's take a deep dive into **Spring Warm Light**, commonly known as the **peach blossom type**, which is considered the brightest and most vibrant among the 12 personal color types.

## What is the Personal Color System?

Personal Color is a color analysis system that finds the color groups that suit an individual best by analyzing their skin undertone, eye color, hair color, and skin contrast.

It fundamentally originates from the **4-season system (Spring/Summer/Autumn/Winter)**, and currently, the **12-type system**, which divides each season into 3 sub-types, is widely used. Each season is further broken down by a combination of undertones (warm/cool) and brightness (light/mute/vivid/deep).

The spring season is entirely based on **Warm undertones**, among which Spring Warm Light has the brightest and most translucent energy.

## What Kind of Type is Spring Warm Light?

### Physical Characteristics

The most prominent feature of the Spring Warm Light type is its **overall transparency and brightness**. Skin, eyes, and hair are all soft and bright without darkness or strong contrast, giving a gentle impression.

**Skin Tone**
- Peach, ivory, or pale golden base
- Cheeks naturally flush into a pink-peach color
- Skin that isn't pale and carries a warm glow
- Warm beige to golden beige foundations work well

**Eyes**
- Light brown, amber, honey-brown
- A somewhat brighter and warmer tone than black pupils
- A warm and soft feeling in the gaze itself

**Hair**
- Golden brown, copper, light khaki brown
- Gray hairs tend to come in golden rather than silver
- Light brown tones suit more naturally than dark brown or black

### Overall Image

It gives a bright, innocent, and **spring peach blossom-like impression**. It carries a childlike purity and loveliness, naturally brightening the atmosphere with an unforced radiance.

## Best Color Palette for Spring Warm Light

The color groups that suit the Spring Warm Light type best are **bright, warm, and moderately saturated colors**. If they are too muted (muted colors mixed with gray), too cold (blue-based), or too dark (deep colors), the face looks dull or the yellow undertones in the skin stand out.

### Recommended Colors

**Warm Family**
- Peach Pink — A core color that naturally revives the face like blush
- Coral Red — A passionate yet soft warm red
- Warm Yellow — A golden yellow one tone warmer than chick yellow or lemon yellow
- Salmon Orange — Between peach and orange, adds radiance to the face
- Ivory — A creamy white warmer than pure white, a perfect base for spring warm

**Point Colors**
- Mint Green — A bright mint that adds spring freshness
- Light Coral — A soft and bright coral
- Pastel Peach — The essence of spring warm
- Warm Rose — A warm rosy hue rather than a cold pink

**Achromatic and Basics**
- Camel — Warm camel suits far better than cool gray
- Oatmeal Beige — The foundation of warm neutrals
- Light Gold — Golden accessories suit well

### Colors to Avoid

Colors that make the face look dull or washed out when worn by the Spring Warm Light type:

- **Dark Navy, Black**: The contrast is too strong, making the face look pale or dull
- **Deep Burgundy, Dark Wine**: Dark red tones make the face look heavy
- **Deep Purple, Violet**: Cool purple tones make the yellowish undertones in the skin stand out
- **Ash Gray**: Grayish mute colors kill the skin tone
- **Cool White**: Warm ivory/cream is much more natural than blue-based pure white
- **Olive Green, Khaki**: Muddy green tones are also warm, but too heavy for spring light

## Makeup Guide

### Foundation & Base
Warm beige-toned foundations are the baseline. Avoid leaning too far toward pink undertones or overly dark oak shades. **Y201–Y221 series** (based on serum foundations) or brands' "peach beige" or "warm ivory" shades fit well.

### Blush
A core makeup point for Spring Warm Light. Lightly dab **coral pink or peach pink** family blush on the high points of the cheekbones. Too-dark red blushes look unnatural and can appear overdone. Utilizing a highlighter rather than shading makes this type's bright skin shine even more.

### Lip Makeup
**Coral, peach, and warm rose** families look the most natural and radiant. Especially, a single peach coral lipstick exerts enough powerful effect to complete the entire makeup look. If you want a nude lip, choose a peach nude one tone brighter than your skin. Dark wine lips and cold burgundy look awkward on warm lights.

### Eye Makeup
Warm-toned shadows are the baseline. **Golden brown, peach beige, and terracotta** family shadows bring out the gaze. Brown eyeliner melts in more naturally than black eyeliner. For mascara, brown-black families suit this type's soft impression better than black.

### Hair Color
Golden brown, honey bronze, and copper red families are the top choices. Ash gray, blue-black, and cool dark brown can make the face look dull.

## Fashion Styling

### Fabric Selection
**Light and soft fabrics** suit Spring Warm Light well. Flowing lightweight materials like chiffon, linen, cotton, and silk harmonize with the type's light energy. Thick wool or heavy materials weigh down this type's bright impression.

### Patterns
Soft and feminine patterns like floral patterns, small dots, and paisley suit well. Natural curved patterns match the type's soft image better than strong geometric patterns or thick stripes.

### Accessories
**Golden (Gold) materials** are the baseline. Yellow gold suits much better than silver or rose gold. Delicate and lightweight jewelry looks more natural with this type's cute and dainty image than large pieces of jewelry.

## Spring Warm Light Self-Diagnosis Checklist

If you check many of the items below, you are likely a Spring Warm Light:

- [ ] Skin has a peach/ivory/golden base
- [ ] Cheeks easily flush pink-peach
- [ ] Pupils are light brown or amber
- [ ] Gray hairs tend to come in golden rather than silver
- [ ] Wearing peach, coral, or ivory clothing brightens your face
- [ ] Wearing dark navy or black makes your face look dull
- [ ] Brown eyeliner looks more natural than black
- [ ] Gold accessories suit you better than silver accessories
- [ ] You always choose the brightest warm beige foundation

If 5 or more items apply to you, we recommend confirming through a professional personal color diagnosis.
