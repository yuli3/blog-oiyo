## Key Dividend Investing Terms

| Term | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Ex-dividend date** | Must hold shares by this date to receive the dividend |
| **Payment date** | The actual date dividends are deposited |
| **Dividend yield** | Annual dividend ÷ share price × 100% |
| **Payout ratio** | Percentage of net income distributed as dividends |

<Callout type="tip">
**Watch out for the ex-date drop**: On the ex-dividend date, share prices typically fall by roughly the dividend amount. Short-term trades aimed solely at capturing dividends can result in a net loss after taxes and commissions.
</Callout>
