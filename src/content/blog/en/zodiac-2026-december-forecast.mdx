## Key Astro Events — December 2026

December is the month to wrap up the year and prepare for 2027. **Sagittarius's free-spirited and optimistic energy** blends beautifully with the holiday season, while Jupiter in Cancer adds the warmth of home and family.

| Event | Date | Influence |
|---|---|---|
| Sun in Sagittarius | Dec 1–21 | Optimism, freedom, exploration & vision |
| Winter Solstice | Dec 21 | Sun enters Capricorn — new cycle begins |
| New Moon in Sagittarius | Early Dec | Plant seeds of new vision and dreams |
| Full Moon in Cancer | Mid-Dec | Home & emotional energy peaks |
| Jupiter in Cancer | All month | Final home-luck energy of 2026 |

<Callout type="tip">
**December Theme**: Close out the year with gratitude and paint your 2027 dreams in vivid detail. Jupiter's final stretch in Cancer makes time with family and home the greatest gift of the season.
</Callout>
