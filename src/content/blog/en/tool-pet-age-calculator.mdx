# Pet Age Calculator

"My dog is 10 — what is that in human years?" The old rule of multiplying by 7 is now outdated. Recent research shows that the rate at which dogs age varies significantly by size and life stage.

## Dog Age Conversion (by size)

**Small dogs (under 10 kg)**

| Dog Age | Human Age Equivalent |
|---|---|
| 1 year | ~15 years |
| 2 years | ~24 years |
| 5 years | ~36 years |
| 10 years | ~56 years |
| 15 years | ~76 years |

**Large dogs (25 kg+)** — age faster than small dogs

| Dog Age | Human Age Equivalent |
|---|---|
| 1 year | ~15 years |
| 5 years | ~40 years |
| 10 years | ~66 years |
| 13 years | ~96 years |

<Callout type="tip">
**UCSD 2020 study**: DNA methylation analysis revealed that dogs age very rapidly in their first 1–2 years (equivalent to a human adolescence), then slow down. The "× 7" formula is inaccurate and misleading.
</Callout>

## Cat Age Conversion

Cats have less size variation between breeds, so a single scale is used.

| Cat Age | Human Equivalent | Life Stage |
|---|---|---|
| 1 year | ~15 years | Adolescent |
| 2 years | ~24 years | Young adult |
| 6 years | ~40 years | Early middle age |
| 10 years | ~56 years | Late middle age |
| 15 years | ~76 years | Senior |
| 20 years | ~96 years | Very old |

## Health Care by Life Stage (small dogs)

| Stage | Age | Key Care |
|---|---|---|
| Puppy | 0–1 year | Complete vaccines, spay/neuter, socialization |
| Young adult | 1–3 years | Annual check-ups, dental care |
| Middle age | 4–7 years | Weight management, heart and joint monitoring |
| Senior | 8–11 years | Twice-yearly vet visits, prescription diet review |
| Geriatric | 12+ years | Pain management, cognitive decline monitoring |
