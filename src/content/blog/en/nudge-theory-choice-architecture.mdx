## Choice Architecture

There is no such thing as a neutral design.
Every form, every menu, every environment influences our decisions.
A "Nudge" alters behavior in a predictable way without forbidding any options (Libertarian Paternalism).

## Defaults

The most powerful nudge is the **Default Option**. Most people stick with the default because they are lazy (Status Quo Bias).
