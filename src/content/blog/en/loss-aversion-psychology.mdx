## 2:1 Ratio

Psychologically, losses loom larger than gains.
This explains why people stay in bad jobs (fear of losing security) rather than risk a better career.
It also explains why stock market crashes cause panic selling.

## Framing

"You will save $50" is less persuasive than "You are losing $50 every month."
