# Korea Reconstruction Profit Calculator

The core of a Korean reconstruction or redevelopment investment is **the gap between your rights value and the new unit price**. If the new apartment is worth more than your rights value plus any additional assessment, you profit.

## How the Profit Is Calculated

**Rights value** = Appraised property value × Proportional ratio (비례율)  
**Additional assessment** = New unit price − Rights value  
**Expected profit** = New apartment market value − Original investment − Assessment

<Callout type="tip">
**Proportional ratio (비례율)**: The profitability index for a reconstruction project. Above 100% means the union members gain; below 100% means losses. Prime Seoul reconstruction projects typically run 100–120%.
</Callout>

## Reconstruction Process Stages

| Stage | Contents | Typical Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Safety inspection | Must receive D or E grade | 6 months – 1 year |
| Improvement zone designation | Local government announcement | 1–2 years |
| Union formation | 75% landowner consent | 1–2 years |
| Project implementation approval | Design and construction plan finalized | 2–3 years |
| Management and disposition approval | Assessment amounts confirmed | 1–2 years |
| Relocation / demolition / groundbreaking | Construction begins | 3–4 years |
| Completion / move-in | New apartment delivered | — |

## Key Risks to Watch

- Regulatory changes during the process (stricter safety inspections, reconstruction surplus collection)
- Interest costs if the project is delayed
- Need for relocation funds after management disposition approval
- 2-year actual residency requirement (speculative-zone allocations)

<Callout type="warning">
Korean reconstruction projects can take 10–20 years and are highly sensitive to regulatory shifts. Consult a real estate specialist before committing to investment.
</Callout>
