## Comparación de Cargas Salariales en Europa

| País | Cotizaciones empleado (~) | Cotizaciones empresa (~) | Neto / Bruto |
|---|---|---|---|
| **España** | 6,4% | 30,9% | ~75–80% |
| **Francia** | 22–23% | 40–45% | ~75–78% |
| **Alemania** | 20–21% | 20–21% | ~62–68% |
| **Países Bajos** | 17–20% | 18–22% | ~65–75% |
| **Suecia** | 7% | 31,4% | ~68–75% |
