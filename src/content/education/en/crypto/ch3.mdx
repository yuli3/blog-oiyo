# Chapter 3. Tokenomics: The Economic Heartbeat of Crypto

A blockchain project is more than just code; it is a mini-economy. <mark><strong>"Tokenomics"</strong></mark> (Token + Economics) is the study of how a token functions within its ecosystem. A well-designed tokenomics model can drive a project to success, while a poor one can lead to its collapse regardless of the technology.

---

## 1. The Supply Side: Scarcity and Math

In crypto, <mark><strong>"Math is the new Monetary Policy."</strong></mark> Unlike central banks, code determines exactly how many tokens will exist.

## 2. Demand Side: Why Hold the Token?

Supply is only half the story. A token must have a reason to be held.

- **Utility**: Used to access a service (e.g., Gas for transactions).
- **Governance**: Used to vote on the future of the project (e.g., Uniswap’s UNI).
- **Staking**: Locked up to secure the network in exchange for rewards.
- **Value Catchment**: Does the success of the platform actually increase the demand for the token?

## 4. Conclusion: Designing Incentives

Tokenomics is ultimately about <mark><strong>"Aligning Incentives."</strong></mark> The goal is to create a system where the selfish actions of individuals (trying to make a profit) lead to the collective benefit of the entire network.
